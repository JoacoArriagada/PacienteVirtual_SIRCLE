**Acute reactive airway disease exacerbation**

Edward is a 15mo old former 34w preemie with reactive airway disease presenting with acute onset severe respiratory distress and wheezing responsive to albuterol therapy that developed in the setting of an upper respiratory illness. Pt is well appearing, afebrile, in mild respiratory distress with lung exam significant for symmetric expiratory wheezing without crackles or rhonchi. Dx studies significant for normal wbc and CXR showing hyper-inflation.

Presentation consistent with reactive airway disease exacerbation most likely triggered by viral URI. Bronchiolitis is also a consideration given pt’s age and illness, time course, and peribronchial thickening seen on CXR though rapid improvement with albuterol therapy and lack of crackles make it less likely. Foreign body aspiration is a consideration given pt’s chronic history of coughing and gagging with liquids, though less likely with no identified aspiration event and onset of symptoms long after last meal. The infiltrate seen on CXR more likely represents atelectasis.

Pt significantly improved following albuterol therapy. Requires hospitalization due to need for frequent albuterol administration and RAD teaching.
