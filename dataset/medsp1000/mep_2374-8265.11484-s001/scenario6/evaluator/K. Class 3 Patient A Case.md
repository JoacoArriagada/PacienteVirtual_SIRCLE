Class 3: Patient Case Scenario A

# **WHEN YOU ARE PLAYING THE PHYSICIAN**

You are following up on your patient Howard Li, who is here for a four week follow up after leaving the hospital for management of myocardial infarction.

Here are the updates to the patient you are interviewing.

1. Patient had CBC (complete blood count), lipid panel, blood sugar.
2. Patient had EKG done and diagnosed with myocardial infarction due to blockage of an artery. Patient also diagnosed with hyperlipidemia.
3. Patient had surgery done to remove blockage.

In place of a usual HPI, please **ask for updates on the surgery** along with recovery and complications. Please conduct the rest of the history by **asking for updates on each of the sections** of the full history including but not limited to medications, allergies, family history etc.
