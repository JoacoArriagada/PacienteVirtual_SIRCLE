## **HPI**

You are here for a four week follow up after leaving the hospital for management of myocardial infarction. After your surgery, you were put on Aspirin 81mg (1tab per day) and rosuvastatin 20 mg (1 tab per day). You seem to be having side effects from the statin, noting sore muscle pain in your arms and back. To decrease your levels of stress, you are taking a 3 month break from work. You are considering quitting smoking and have reduced your habit to 4 cigarettes a day now.

You’ve also received your COVID vaccines and have moved out of your apartment and in with your parents in a house in Newport Beach.
