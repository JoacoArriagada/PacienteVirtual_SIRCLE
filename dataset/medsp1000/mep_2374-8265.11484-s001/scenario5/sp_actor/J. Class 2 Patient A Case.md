**History of Present Illness**

She reports that the pain began one week ago and has become more persistent over the past two days. Ms. Wei describes the pain as sharp and cramping. It is unilateral, localized in the upper right quadrant of the abdomen and radiates toward the back. She notices the pain is worse after a meal. Nothing she does makes the pain better. On a pain scale of 0 to 10, she rates the pain as a 7. She does note some nausea along with the pain. Denies any diarrhea or constipation.
