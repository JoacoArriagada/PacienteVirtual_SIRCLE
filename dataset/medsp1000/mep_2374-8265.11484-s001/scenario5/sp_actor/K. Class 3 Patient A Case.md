## **HPI**

You underwent a cholecystectomy at UCI Medical center two weeks ago and are here for a follow up. You were given Cefazolin, an antibiotic, a week before the surgery but you ended up having an allergic reaction to it, breaking out in a skin rash. Otherwise, no other complications occurred. You had some diffuse abdominal pain for 2-3 days after the surgery but you have been taking Tylenol as needed (one tab each day for two days) for the pain and you haven’t had any pain since then. You’ve changed your diet to more home cooked meals recently and have cut out fast food.

Calling your family back in China, you’ve also realized that you have a family history of stroke in your maternal grandmother.
