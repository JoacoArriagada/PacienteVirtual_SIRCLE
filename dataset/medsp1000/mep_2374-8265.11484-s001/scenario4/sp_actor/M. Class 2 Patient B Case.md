**History of Present Illness**

Mr. Li is a 48-year-old Chinese male who presents to the clinic today with a chief complaint of chest pain. He reports that the pain started approximately two hours ago while he was at work. Mr. Li describes the pain as dull and having some tightness in his chest that radiates to his left arm. He rates the intensity of the pain as 7 out of 10. He also mentions feeling shortness of breath and mild anxiety. Mr. Li denies any previous history of similar symptoms. There are no alleviating or aggravating factors. He states that his father has heart disease. His business has been struggling and he has been under increased work pressure over the past few months. There are no associated symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, or dizziness.
