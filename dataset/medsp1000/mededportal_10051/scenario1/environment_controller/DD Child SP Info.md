**Standardized Survivor Profile**

**Father of 5 y.o. boy**

**Setting:**

You have been called to a local emergency room by a nurse and told that your son and wife were there. They

were not able to release any information over the phone. You arrive, you are pleasantly greeted by the triage nurse and asked to wait in the family room and that the doctor will be in to speak with you. When you ask if you can see your son and wife, and if they are all right, you are told that the doctor will be right out to speak with you.

You are escorted to the family room and you wait, alone, for the arrival of the doctor.

The doctor will tell you that your son has died and your wife is in critical condition

**Time of Day:** 2:30pm, weekday

## Your Profile

Name: Alfred Ford

Your Occupation: Administrative Assistant at a nearby University

Your Age: 20s

Your Spouse: Angela Ford, also in her 20’s. She works evenings at a nearby restaurant as a waitress.

Children: one son, Andrew Ford, who is 5 y.o.

Local Support System: Many friends, no religious affiliations, but general belief in God and both spouses were brought up going to church regularly. No immediate family locally.

Son’s Name: Andrew Ford

**What you know prior to arrival to the ER:**

Called by nurse on the phone to come to emergency department. No information given over the phone other than your son and wife are in the ED and you should come immediately.

One hour prior, you spoke with your spouse on the phone, who was on her way to pick up Andrew after school

**Mood, Affect and Demeanor:**

**Demeanor:** Pacing the room, standing when the physician enters the room

**Affect**: Very Anxious. Appropriately concerned. You are expecting bad news. You are sure that he something terrible has happened, and you need to know where things are at so you can “sign consents, or whatever...” You are definitely not expecting your son to be dead. You spoke to your wife within the hour and she seemed fine. You kissed your son goodbye this morning after dropping him off at school as usual. Your wife works evenings, so you take care of getting things together in the mornings usually, and your wife Angela, picks Andrew up from school every day.
