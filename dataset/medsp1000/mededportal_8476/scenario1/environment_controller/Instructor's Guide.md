**Explanation of Resources**

The simulation should first begin by the operators and facilitators opening the simulation outline and familiarizing themselves with the beginning scenario as well as the entire case. The participants will then begin with a general explanation of the case scenario by reading the text located in italics on page one of the outline. The goal of the outline is to allow for an easily observable flowchart showing the operators how the case should be run. Participants should then work their way through the simulation as shown in the outline with lab values and chest x-ray being shown to them when asked for. The goals of the labs are to indicate decompensation of an acute asthmatic, with an increased pCO2. The goal of the chest x-ray is to indicate that there are no other causes of the patient's symptoms, i.e. pneumothorax or pneumonia.

**Practical Implementation**

This case is designed to be run for a wide variety of medical professionals, including medical students, through resident level and faculty trained physicians. Ideally, the simulation requires a simulation room and mannequin, one actor as a nurse and one or more actors acting as a parent(s) as well as one facilitator running the simulation. It is understood that there is a wide range of simulation environments, however, actual monitors, intubating equipment, and x-ray capabilities are ideal in order to make the experience as realistic as possible. The simulation can be run however on as little as two actors, a facilitator and imaginative learners. The simulation should last approximately 15-30 minutes depending on the skill levels involved. Preparation should include a run through by the facilitator and actors prior to the actual simulation experience by learners.
