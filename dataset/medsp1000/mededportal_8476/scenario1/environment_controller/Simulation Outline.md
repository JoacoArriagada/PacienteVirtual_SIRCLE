| **Brief Summary**  **17 year old female presents to the emergency department with her parents in clear respiratory distress. In the ED she quickly deteriorates to loss of airflow. Despite albuterol /ipatropium/magnesium /steroids/epinephrine the patient requires intubation. The PICU is full and the patient needs to be boarded in the ED for a few hours under the correct settings.**  **Text to be shown on screen prior at beginning of case:**  *“17 year old female brought in for dyspnea. The patient is in obvious respiratory distress. Parents are at bedside saying that she has a history of asthma and began feeling short of breath earlier today, receiving albuterol every 4 hours with little help”.* |
