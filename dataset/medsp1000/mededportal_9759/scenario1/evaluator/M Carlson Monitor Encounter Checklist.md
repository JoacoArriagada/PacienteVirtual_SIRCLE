This document is used by monitors to help assess clinical competency of examinees. While none of the items on this list are used on the Grading Rubric, it provides a good source of feedback for examinees. Additionally, portions of the Monitor Encounter Checklist overlap with the Standardized Patient Post-Encounter Checklist and discrepancies can be reviewed for consistency in scoring.
