**V C. SP Training Guidelines: Physical Examination**

**Physical Examination**:

You ache all over, mildly, and when the student palpates your lower back or your right shoulder anywhere or your abdomen, you act as if you hurt more than one should normally hurt. However, if the student distracts you  by asking you  to bend over and pick something up, or you use your right arm to get something, or the student uses a stethoscope to press on your abdomen instead of using hands, you demonstrate markedly less pain behavior.
