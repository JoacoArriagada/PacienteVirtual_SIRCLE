# Example of a scenario.

Harriet, a 62-year-old woman, presents to your office for evaluation of a loud heart murmur discovered during a screening exam. She has always been in good health although she has noticed lately some shortness of breath when climbing stairs. She doesn’t know whether or not she had rheumatic fever as a child. You are thinking about a possible aortic stenosis or a mitral regurgitation. In anticipation of your physical exam of the heart and other related body parts, list the positive sign(s) associate with each diagnostic hypothesis.
