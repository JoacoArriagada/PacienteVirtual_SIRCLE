# Example of a scenario.

Dorothy, 45 years old, sees you because of blurred vision in her left eye. She realized it several weeks ago. The blurriness has been stable until now. Although she has had high blood sugar for the past 13 years, she doesn’t have a regular doctor and has taken various medications on and off. You are thinking of a possible cataract or diabetic retinopathy. In anticipation of your physical exam of the eyes, list the positive sign(s) associated with each diagnostic hypothesis.
