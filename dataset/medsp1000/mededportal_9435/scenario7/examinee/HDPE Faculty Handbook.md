# Example of a scenario.

You are thinking of a possible peripheral facial nerve palsy (Bell’s palsy) or a central facial nerve palsy. In anticipation of your physical exam of the cranial nerves (V, VII, XI and XII), list the positive sign(s) associated with each diagnostic hypothesis.
