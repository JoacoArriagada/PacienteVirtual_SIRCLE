# Example of a scenario.

Andy, 50 years old, sees you because of right leg pain on exertion. The leg has been painful for the last three months and occurs whenever Andy walks at a distance over half a block. You are thinking of a possible peripheral arterial disease (arteriosclerosis obliterans) or spinal stenosis. In anticipation of your vascular exam of the legs, excluding neurological exam, list the positive sign(s) associated with each diagnostic hypothesis.
