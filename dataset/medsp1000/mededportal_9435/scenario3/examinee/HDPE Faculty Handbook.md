# Example of a scenario.

Jamie, 35 years old, came to the clinic because they has had a stuffy and runny nose on and off and worse for the last three days with a touch of a sore throat. S/he also complains of a headache, but does not know if they had a fever. You are thinking of a possible streptococcal pharyngitis or an allergic rhinitis. In anticipation of your physical exam of the nose and lymph nodes, list the positive sign(s) associated with each diagnostic hypothesis.
