**Quick Guide** -Framework and Scripts for SSRD Diagostic Delivery

**ORIENT: Example scripts for different functional symptoms**

**Neurologic:** **numbness, abnormal movements, weakness, inability to walk**

“We can see that his body is strong and reflexes are intact, but his mind and body are not communicating in a healthy way. The body has learned a pattern of abnormal movements that is possibly triggered by environmental factors. This pattern needs to be re-wired, which will take some time.”

**Alarm** = symptoms, nervous system signaling

**Fire/smoke** = trigger (biological, trauma, etc)

**Validating Scripts**

“There is clearly something wrong.”

“I see that this is really affecting his daily life. It must be hard to not be able to participate in [school, sport, activity].”

“This diagnosis can be frustrating for families because there is not a specific test for this condition”

**Analogies**

**Software** = mind, nervous system signaling, symptoms

**Hardware** = body
