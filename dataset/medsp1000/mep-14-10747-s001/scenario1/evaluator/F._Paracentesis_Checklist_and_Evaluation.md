| 6.Palpates the abdomen and selects site | Y | N | * Palpates and notes lateral border of rectus and chooses site 2 fingerbreadths superior and 2 fingerbreadths medial to anterior iliac spine * Unless contraindicated, chooses LLQ. |

| 11.Puts on cap and mask, and then sterile gloves | Y | N | Optional: Applies sterile or non-sterile gown |

| 13.Drapes the area | Y | N |  |

| 15.Uses lidocaine to anesthetize the skin (wheal) | Y | N | * Uses smaller bore needle * Constantly aspirates before injecting |
| 16.Anesthetizes deeper | Y | N | If no peritoneal fluid obtained from initial anesthetic, switches to larger bore needle to anesthetize the peritoneum |
| 17. Using the Yueh catheter/needle complex enters the skin while aspirating | Y | N |  |

| 20.Maintains sterile technique | Y | N | * Does not contaminate sterile field with unsterile equipment * Keeps gloved hands sterile up until tubing is accessed |
| 21.Connects the tubing to a 1 liter vacuum container or the vacuum apparatus connected to low wall suction | Y | N |  |
| 22.Withdraws catheter when drainage stops | Y | N |  |
