| **PATIENT NAME: Mr. S**  **PATIENT AGE: 46 years old**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Abdominal pain and distension** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Mr. S is a 46 year-old male with history of end-stage liver disease secondary to chronic Hepatitis C. He is presenting with worsened abdominal pain and distension despite compliance with home diuretic regimen. The team has decided he needs a diagnostic paracentesis to evaluate for spontaneous bacterial peritonitis, as well as a therapeutic paracentesis to relieve his distension. Participants need to provide patient with informed consent and perform a safe and efficient diagnostic and large volume paracentesis completing all the necessary steps. |
