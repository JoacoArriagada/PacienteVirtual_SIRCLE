**Appendix A: Logistics**

1. Location of activity
   1. Simulation Center
2. Setting for the encounter
   1. Outpatient primary care provider office
3. Materials required
   1. Student instructions with patient vital signs
4. Number of encounters completed by each learner
   1. For this case, each student completed one 15-minute encounter with a standardized patient (SP), one 15 minute SOAP note, and one 3-minute encounter at the closing of the exercise in which the SP provided feedback. This case was one of four medication cases completed by each student.
5. Length of each encounter
   1. Following the 15 minutes with the SP, students had an additional 15 minutes to complete a standard SOAP note and a pharmacokinetic dosing calculation question.
   2. A 3-minute SP-student feedback encounter followed the student’s written SOAP note. This brief encounter allowed the SP to provide generalized feedback to students regarding their performance.
6. Total number of encounters expected in a session for the SP
   1. Four student encounters in the morning, four encounters in the early afternoon, and four encounters in the late afternoon.
7. Start and finish time
   1. 35 minutes per student
8. Staffing requirement
   1. One standardized patient
   2. One “time keeper”
9. Briefing or orientation materials used
    1. Student instructions for part 1 and part 2 of this SP case can be found as Appendix F.
