**Appendix F: Student Instructions**

**STUDENT PART 1**Initial SP-student interaction

You are a family physician in a rural county and are evaluating Michelle Smith, a 55 year old patient. She was brought in to the office today by her family due to excessive sleepiness and confusion.

You are expected to:

1. Perform a focused history and focused physical examination on Mr/Mrs Smith
2. Communicate with Mr/Mrs Smith what you believe is responsible for the current situation.

You have **15 minutes** to complete the encounter – your history, physical, and thoughts to Mr/Mrs Smith.

**PLEASE NOTE: THERE IS A FOLLOW-UP PART FOR THIS CASE WHERE YOU WILL BE ASKED TO ANSWER SOME ADDITIONAL QUESTIONS AND WRITE A RELATED SOAP NOTE.**

**CHART NOTES**

Temperature: **37.4° C**

Pulse: **92 beats/minute and regular**

Respirations: **18/minute**

Blood pressure: **118/78 mmHg**

Weight: **65 kg**

Height: **166 cm**

Vancomycin plasma level (was drawn by the visiting nurse this morning immediately preceding another dose of vancomycin and the report is back from the laboratory). The level was reported as: **110 mcg/mL**

-Based upon your electronic pharmacology resource of choice (e.g. Lexicomp, etc.), you know that vancomycin has a **Vd of 0.7L/kg**

-The patient has been receiving vancomycin at a dose of **40mg/kg/day divided every 12 hours**

**STUDENT PART 2**

You will be writing a SOAP note and answering specific questions about Mr/Mrs. Smith, the 55 year old patient you just saw. You may use a calculator and any additional resources to complete this station.

Write a SOAP-Format Note.

1. What is the underlying problem? Identify at least 3 pertinent positives and negatives elicited from the history and physical that led you to this conclusion.
2. Estimate for how many hours vancomycin must be withheld if the desired plasma level is 15 mcg/ml before administering another dose, based upon the morning laboratory value. Show and describe how you arrived at your answer.
3. What additional laboratory tests do you wish to order to confirm/evaluate the possibilities?

You have **15 minutes** to complete the assignment.

After the 15-minute SOAP note, you will re-enter the SP room. The SP will provide you with feedback.
