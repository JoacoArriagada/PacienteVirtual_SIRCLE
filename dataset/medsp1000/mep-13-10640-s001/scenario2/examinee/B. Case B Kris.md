Presenting Sheet for Case B: Kris

Presenting Sheet

Kris is a 28-year-old intern who is part of the Family and Community Medicine Residency. Over the past few weeks, you and Kris have spent a lot of hours on the service together and have gotten close. You’ve noticed that Kris is a bit timid.

You and Kris are both working in the clinic today. Kris comes to you for advice about a new patient, John Dryer, she saw in clinic. John has a history of recurrent depression and has not had success using antidepressants to reduce the severity of his symptoms. John told Kris that he was reading about Mindfulness-based Cognitive Therapy. John wants to try this treatment, but he does not know where to go to get this type of therapy. Kris went to the attending physician, Dr. Rodriguez, because she was not familiar with this treatment. Dr. Rodriguez told her to prescribe antidepressants and was immediately called away. Kris really wants to discuss the possibility of therapy more with Dr. Rodriguez but is worried about approaching him again.

From your experience working with Dr. Rodriguez, you know that Dr. Rodriguez is generally patient and open to alternative treatments. Your job is to do what you would normally do as a teaching senior resident in this situation. Take advantage of whatever teaching opportunities appear.
