Appendix B

Case B Kris

Addressing Approaching a Faculty Member

Learner Name: Kris

Gender: We will treat Kris as a woman in this resource.

Interpersonal Issue: Lack of confidence and difficulty approaching senior doctors effectively

Type and Level of Learner: Kris is a first-year Family and Community Medicine Resident (i.e., intern) and has been on this rotation for two weeks.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Presenting Situation | Kris is on the Family Medicine Clinic rotation with a senior resident on a busy afternoon. Kris, a first-year resident, has been on this rotation for two weeks. Kris sees a senior resident in the hallway. She is very worried about the patient's treatment. |
| Psychosocial Profile | Kris is an intern in the Family and Community Medicine Residency. Her medical knowledge base is above average but sometimes appears as below average due to her shyness. Kris came from a family with a strong, patriarchal figure that did not tolerate questions. |
| Scenario Development/Situation | At 2:00 pm, Kris was seeing John, whose previous clinic doctor has graduated from the residency program. John had been scheduled for 1:15 pm. Patients earlier in the morning had more complicated cases than usual, which delayed all subsequent appointments. Kris is trying to help out as much as possible and not stall any of the doctors. John had expressed that he was still having serious depressive symptoms even after trying numerous antidepressants. John wanted to try psychotherapy. Kris said she would go ask the attending about possible referrals. Kris went up to Dr. Rodriguez who was very busy. Kris told Dr. Rodriguez that the patient had depression and was seeking treatment, specifically Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy. Kris asked Dr. Rodriguez to recommend any cost-friendly therapists or centers. Dr. Rodriguez said let’s try Wellbutrin first and was called away. Kris runs into the senior resident in the hallway while walking back to room 3 where John is being seen. |
| Emotions | Kris is unsure and worried. Growing up, Kris was not allowed to disagree with her father. If she asked too many questions, her parents would say she was being annoying and send her to her bedroom. Throughout her life, Kris has been influenced by these early experiences. Having had a close friend that was depressed and committed suicide, Kris feels compelled to help the patient, John, as much as possible. |

Possible Dialogue for Case B: Kris

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Senior resident | Kris |
| *Intern opening statement when speaking to senior resident* | Can I ask you a question? |
| Sure, what’s up? | Well, umm…. I wanted to talk to you about a patient. |
| Okay, what patient? | The patient is John Dryer. He has recurrent depression and has tried several antidepressants, I think. |
| What antidepressants has he been on? | Zoloft, Effexor, Lexapro, Wellbutrin, Prozac, and Paxil |
| Okay, so? | *In a more hurried tone:* Well, John doesn’t want any more antidepressants but wants to try this therapy instead. But Dr. Rodriquez said to give him antidepressants. What should I do? |
| Have you told Dr. Rodriquez about this? | Well, no. He seems very busy and I didn’t get a chance to. |
| Well you should probably go tell him. | What should I say? |
| Just tell him what you told me; Dr. Rodriquez is very nice and open to alternative treatments. | Okay… Do you think that maybe you could go with me? |
| Well, usually the doctor is right and antidepressants are effective. | I’m just sure John won’t take them. He practically told me so! |
| Is there a reason you don’t want to approach Dr. Rodriguez? | Well, I don’t want to annoy him. |
| You need to be confident and make a decision. | I guess I’ll go and talk to Dr. Rodriguez. Will you go with me? |
| …Ohkay…. | Maybe that will look bad. What do you think? |

The point here is the senior resident needs to address both the appropriate way to approach Dr. Rodriguez and give Kris encouragement to do so. The resident should have to work a little to get Kris to go and talk to Dr. Rodriguez. Kris is very indecisive.

Close of Encounter:

If it doesn’t come up specifically, Kris specifically asks the resident how to approach Dr. Rodriguez and what to say. The encounter should close with either Kris going to Dr. Rodriguez or deciding not to go based on what the resident’s recommendation calls for.
