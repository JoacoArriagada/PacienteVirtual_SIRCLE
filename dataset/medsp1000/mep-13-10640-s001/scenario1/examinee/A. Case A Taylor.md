Presenting Sheet for Case A: Taylor

Presenting Sheet

Taylor is a 25-year-old, third-year medical student, on a Family Medicine rotation at the Family Health Clinic.

Taylor is about to present his new patient (Mia, a 55-year-old diabetic patient who came to the Family Health Clinic complaining of fatigue and weight gain) to you, a senior resident. You have worked with Taylor before and know he can be argumentative and condescending.

You will hear Taylor’s case presentation about the new patient. Your job is to do what you would normally do as a teaching senior resident in this situation and take advantage of whatever teaching opportunities appear. Focus on issues related to the case and the quality of Taylor’s oral presentation itself.
