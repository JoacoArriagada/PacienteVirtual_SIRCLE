Appendix A

Case A Taylor

Feedback on Incomplete Case Presentation

Learner Name: Taylor

Gender: We will treat Taylor as a man in this resource.

Interpersonal Issue: Arrogant while incompletely reporting on a patient to a senior resident

Type and Level of Learner: Taylor is a third-year medical student and has been on this rotation for two weeks.

Case Objectives:

1. Communicate with a resistant learner in an effective way.
2. Provide constructive feedback for a case presentation.
3. Use effective/positive body language during discussion.
4. Devise a plan moving forward with the learner (e.g. resident tells medical student to go study at home).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Presenting Situation | Taylor is in the outpatient, Family Health Clinic, presenting a new patient to a senior resident. Taylor, a medical student, has been on this rotation for two weeks. |
| Psychosocial Profile | Taylor is a third-year medical student, doing his core clerkship in family medicine. He thinks his medial knowledge is superior to others. When he was previously given feedback by a senior that his oral presentations were not detailed enough, he argued with the resident. Since the encounter, Taylor has ruminated on why that resident was wrong. Taylor doesn’t even want to go into family medicine; he thinks family medicine is for doctors who can't take the pressure of real medicine. |
| Scenario Development/Situation | Taylor just completed a brief history and physical on this new patient, 55-year-old Mia Torres, who came in due to fatigue. Taylor must now present the patient to the senior resident. |
| Emotions | Taylor is a bit annoyed that he is handling these mild complaints in a Family Medicine rotation rather than in a more serious rotation, like surgery. He doesn’t understand why overweight patients don’t just exercise. However, Taylor enjoys showing off his intelligence so he will enjoy giving the presentation. |

Possible Dialogue for Case A: Taylor

Taylor is at the nurse’s station just outside of Mia Torres’s exam room joking with some of the nurses.

The senior resident walks up to Taylor to inquire about Mia Torres’s condition. Taylor gets ready to present this new patient to the senior resident. He makes eye contact, smiles, and smoothly says, “Are you ready?”

Senior resident possible opening statements and Taylor’s responses:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Senior resident | Taylor |
| Yes, tell me about Mia Torres. | Mia Torres presents with fatigue and has a history of diabetes. *Pauses* |
| And.? | I’m getting to the rest. |
| Go ahead and present your patient to me. | Or if senior resident does not ask Taylor to present patient, make sure you ask to present patient. There is a 55-year-old diabetic woman in room 5. She came in today due to fatigue, which has persisted for several months, and she seems concerned that she has gained weight. She takes insulin, quit smoking 15 years ago, and drinks a bit too much alcohol, a 6 pack on Friday and Saturday. All vitals are normal. |

| Pain? *Or any other question related to why she does not exercise or did not follow the doctor’s recommendation.* | The patient has pain in her legs and knees, especially when exercising. |
| *After 4 or 5 questions:* | Jeez, you're really giving me the third degree here. Seems like his is an open and shut case. |
| What do you mean? | Well, I've covered enough to treat her and have a lot more to do today, so let's speed this up. |
| Well, it’s important to be thorough for the patient’s health. *Or any other constructive criticism or probing question.* | Obviously *rolls eyes.* What else do you want to know? I feel like I’ve said more than enough at this point. |

If it doesn’t come up specifically, Taylor should say, “Look, I’ve seen you residents present a case and my presentation was much more detailed. In the real world, we as doctors just don’t have time for the detail required in these trainings.”

The point here is the senior resident should constructively deal with the missing information in Taylor’s case presentation to provide feedback to Taylor even if this means more hostility and blowback from Taylor.
