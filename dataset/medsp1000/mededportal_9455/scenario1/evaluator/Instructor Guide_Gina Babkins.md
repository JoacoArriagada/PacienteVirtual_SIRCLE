**Instructor Guide- Gina Babkins**

**Resource Files**

The Gina Babkins case can be easily reproduced and utilized where standardized patient or OSCE encounters are occurring. There are 7 case specific documents being uploaded with the submission of this case. There is also an instructors guide and introduction to the case being uploaded separately. The Gina Babkins documents include:

1\_Case Document\_Gina Babkins2.pdf

2\_Medical Chart\_Gina Babkins.pdf

3\_Medical Chart Supplement 1\_Medicine Card\_Gina Babkins.pdf

4\_Documentation Exercise\_Gina Babkins.pdf

5\_Performance Checklist\_Gina Babkins.pdf

6\_Documentation Grading Sheet\_Gina Babkins.pdf

7\_Standardized Patient Checklist\_Gina Bapkins.pdf

**Document 1** is the actual case document and should be studied by the standardized patient, encounter facilitator, and encounter grader prior to the encounter taking place. This document should be used for all standardized patient training as a way to ensure accuracy of case portrayal. **Document 2** is the medical chart which should be presented to the student immediately prior to entering the exam room for the encounter. **Document 3** is the medication reminder card for the standardized patient to refer to. This card includes the standardized patients daily medications and dosage. If the student asks for any of that information during the exam, the Standardized Patient is to hand the student the card with the information on it. **Document 4** is the documentation or SOAP note sheet. This should be used by the student following the exam room encounter for SOAP note documentation. The individual assigned to grade this case will use **Documents 5 and 6** to grade the student’s performance on the case in the exam room and on the SOAP note. Finally, **Document 7** will be used by the standardized patient to grade the student’s performance from a humanistic perspective after the encounter has ended.

**Purpose**

The purpose of the Gina Babkins encounter is to challenge students to manage a common geriatric presentation as part of an eight station Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) for third year medical students completing a geriatric clerkship. Students conduct a focused history and physical exam to assess contributing causes of lightheadedness in the patient. The educational objectives of the encounter are to measure students’ ability to do a focused history & physical exam on a patient presenting with lightheadedness, assess students’ ability to recognize dehydration secondary to medications, and to assess students’ ability to create an appropriate plan for treatment of dehydration.

**Practical Implementation**

The Gina Babkins OSCE encounter is limited to 15 minutes in the actual exam room. Students then have an additional 10 minutes to work on their SOAP note following the encounter. The standardized patient should have a medication card, with dosages, on their person during the exam. If the student asks for any of that information during the exam, the standardized patient is to hand the student the card with the information on it.

Faculty members have played, and should play, a major role in this standardized patient encounter. They played a major role in developing the Gina Babkins case to make a true to life geriatric scenario. Our faculty members have received advanced training from experts in the field of geriatric standardized patient case development prior to developing this case. Four faculty members also serve as case raters at the end of each geriatric rotation. Faculty members have also had to endure reliability analysis training, where all faculty members must come together to rate cases until they are rating the cases at 90% agreement. Faculty must be trained on how to uses the recording system that each case is stored in and also how to detect SP portrayal errors. Overall they are a big part of this assessment tool.
