# Additional Scenario Management Information

 Poor or awkward communication with patient and or the nurse

o Example: “Patient” continues to ask unanswered questions “What is going on? What’s wrong with the baby? It hurts!”   
Difficulty understanding role of assistant(s), and using the assistant(s) to help with management

o Management: To alleviate confusion, the “nurse” will be identified prior to beginning of the scenario   
o Management: To solidify the role, the nurse may ask the trainee questions about what he/she could do to aid the trainee during the exercise.

# Simulation Setup for:

Simulator: Obstetrics “Noelle” Simulator.

Room Setup: In the simulation center, the training room should be set up similarly to a delivery or triage room. The simulator is on an examination table, gurney, or bed. The basic equipment listed below should be available. For in-situ testing/simulations on Labor and Delivery, the simulator should simply replace a typical patient and the room used as usual. Videotaping may be considered to assist in training and debriefing, although the data doesn’t support an advantage over traditional debriefing (Ref: Sawyer, 2012).

# General instructions for all scenarios:

• Prior to trainee entering the room

1) Turn on Tocometer. Refer to scenario specific settings.   
2) Turn on the fetal heart rate monitor. Refer to scenario specific settings.   
3) Turn on maternal vital signs. Refer to scenario specific settings.   
4) Set up simulator mannequin

i. Actress providing voice ready with microphone and speakers working   
ii. Place tocometer and FHR monitors   
iii. IV tubing available but IV not yet placed   
iv. Oxygen face mask ready for use

5) Actor playing dad ready, if available   
6) RN or course trainer playing nurse ready   
7) Cameras for video recording properly placed, if available   
8) Video recorder ready to record   
9) Self-evaluation ready for trainee to fill out

• As trainee enters room:

1) The RN provides the SBAR. The RN is ready to answer questions and facilitate scenario.

2) If an RN trainee is present, the RN (course trainer) should make the trainee the primary RN and be available only to answer question but will not administer medications, O2, etc.   
3) Video recorder should be on, if available..   
4) The trainee may enter with progress note, pen and clipboard, if desire.
