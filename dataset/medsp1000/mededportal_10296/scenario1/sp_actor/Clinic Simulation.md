# Clinic Simulation with Case Review

Managing Hemorrhage as a Complication of Uterine Aspiration

# a. Patient:

You are a 29-year-old woman G4P3 (three previous births) now with an 8 wk 5 day early pregnancy loss (intrauterine fetal demise) confirmed by ultrasound. You decided on uterine aspiration for management. Following your procedure you have unexpected bleeding.

# g. Patient’s partner or support person

You are in the exam room with your partner, who has just had a uterine aspiration to manage a miscarriage. You are attempting to help her get off the exam table, but she tells you she feels weak and you notice she is bleeding heavily. You are nervous and concerned.
