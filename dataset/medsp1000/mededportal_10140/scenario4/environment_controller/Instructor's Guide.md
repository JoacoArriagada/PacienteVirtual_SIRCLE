**Implementation Advice:**

This paging curriculum took place during a four-week residency preparation elective for fourth year medical students, already matched in obstetrics and gynecology programs. CNMs from our department paged the students and played the role of clinical bedside nurse. This clinical team model was chosen purposefully. Many labor and delivery units have CNM-staffed obstetrical triage units and/or have a CNM service to which the obstetrician physicians serve as consults. CNMs at our institution are also active teachers of third year medical students during the obstetrics and gynecology clerkship, and were thus accustomed to being very engaged in medical student education. The labor and delivery triage unit at our institution is staffed by CNMs 24 hours a day, and the CNMs in triage volunteered their time for this curriculum. They paged the medical students when there were lulls in patient care activities in triage. The process of paging the student and performing the case took approximately seven minutes per case.

We had a one-hour meeting with the director of the CNM triage unit, and went through the paging scripts for each of the cases. She then trained the 10 CNMs during a two-hour training session. Individual folders, labeled with each student’s name, were placed in the CNM workspace in triage. Each folder contained all eight cases, individually pre-labeled with the student’s name and pager number. The cases contain standardized scripts containing clinical information, with specific clinical details conveyed only if specifically requested by the student.
