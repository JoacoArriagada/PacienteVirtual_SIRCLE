**Obstetrical cases:**

**CASE FOUR Diagnosis: IV no longer needed**

**PAGE: 30 yo G2P2 POD #1 s/p scheduled repeat LTCS, IV has gone sub-cutaneous.**

Scripted info: Nurse wants to know whether IV needs to be replaced.

*If asked, can share:*

Patient is doing well, tolerating regular diet.

Current Vitals: BP 90/60, HR 90, RR 18, temperature 99

I/O: UO: 300cc/8 hours, Total I/O: 1500/750, EBL 1000

Current meds: Percocet 5/325 q 4-6 hours, Motrin 600 mg q 6 hours

Nursing exam: abdomen soft, non-distended, good bowel sounds.

**Key teaching points:** This case is intended to illustrate an example of the more common, non-acute clinical page. The patient’s clinical course, and physical exam findings are all within normal limits, and the student should recognize that the patient does not need the IV replaced.
