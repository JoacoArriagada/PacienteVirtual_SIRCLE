**Gynecologic cases:**

**CASE THREE Diagnosis: Intra-abdominal bleeding**

**Page: 30 year old G3P1 in the PACU after a laparoscopic salpingostomy for an ectopic pregnancy, with decreased urine output.**

Scripted info: Anesthesia has been working on her hypotension in PACU, but how she has only made 20 cc/hour since surgery

*If asked, can share:*

Current Vitals: BP 85/50, RR 20, HR 120

I/O: Urine output has been 20 cc/hour since the surgery ended two hours ago. EBL 50 cc.

Meds: IVF (LR) @100 cc/hour, Morphine 1-2 mg IV q 3 hours PRN

Nursing assessment: Tired, difficult to stay awake

Heart: Tachycardic Rate/Regular Rhythm Lungs: CTAB

Abdomen: Distended, tender. Tight to touch.

**Key teaching points:** The diagnosis of intra-abdominal bleeding is similar to obstetrical case two, however this case is intended to illustrate a higher acuity situation in which the patient needs to be urgently assessed and ultimately brought back to the operating room for active intra-abdominal bleeding.
