**Obstetrical cases:**

**CASE TWO Diagnosis: Post- operative, intra-abdominal bleeding**

**PAGE: 30 yo G3P2 post-operative day #0 after repeat Cesarean Delivery/Bilateral tubal ligation with tachycardia and shoulder pain in post-operative area.**

Scripted Info: patient is trying to breastfeed, but is a bit uncomfortable. Pain moderately controlled. Elevated heart rate in recovery. Has been in recovery for 1 hour

*If asked, can share:*

Current Vitals: BP: 95/50, RR 18, HR 125, EBL 1200 cc.

I/O: Urine output has been 50 cc over last two hours

Current Meds: IVF (LR) @ 80 cc/hour, Morphine PCA

Nursing exam: Alert, oriented, appears uncomfortable, NAD.

Heart: RRR, Lungs: CTAB

Abdomen soft, distended, tympanic. Uterus firm at the umbilicus. Dressing: dried blood.

**Key teaching points:** Hypovolemia should be at the top of the differential diagnosis list for post-operative tachycardia. The shoulder pain is secondary to the referred pain from irritation of the diaphragm from the hemoperitoneum. Important aspects to emphasize in the physical exam findings are the elevated heart rate, and the inadequate urine production Students should understand that post-operative pain needs to be assessed by the surgical team. This case provides an opportunity to discuss the importance of IVF resuscitation in the setting of hypovolemia.
