# Thyroid Storm presenting as Gastroenteritis

# Instructor’s Guide

# How to Use:

. Provide as realistic a setting as possible to simulate an Emergency Department acute care room.   
 Have all equipment and personnel needed as outlined in the Case Template.   
. The case participants should see the “Text to be shown on screen prior to beginning the case” before the case begins.   
 The patient’s history of present illness, past medical history, and social history should be given by the patient and the roommate at the bedside upon request by the case participants, as one would see in a real patient encounter.   
. The initial presentation of the patient to the learners should be as described by the “Initial Presentation of Patient.” This includes vital signs and physical exam as the clinicians make their initial assessment of the patient. Physical exam may need to be relayed by the nurse or other faculty instructing the case as the case participants request. For instance the nurse can relay the pre-tibial skin is thickened when asked by the case participants as the mannequin will not adequately simulate the exam finding. If and when asked about the HEENT exam, the learners may be shown a photo of exophthalmos.   
 Studies are provided after being ordered by the learners including all labs and the ekg strip.   
 Once initial interventions are made, the case will progress to the “transitional period” and the patient should present as described.   
. After the last appropriate intervention is made, the case will then go on to conclusion at which time it is appropriate to discuss the case with the learners and use the provided slide show to highlight learning points. Often this is done in a quick "debriefing" session where feedback on performance can be integrated.

# Purpose:

To teach how to medically manage an emergent hyperthyroid case in a realistic but controlled environment.

# Educational Objectives:

Early recognition of abnormal vital signs, working quickly through broad differential, communication skills with medical consultants Diagnostic tests and therapeutic intervention of Thyroid Storm

# Implementation

You will need the materials as outlined in the Case Template. This case will likely take 30 minutes to one hour to complete and elaborate on learning points. You will need knowledgeable staff to play the role of nurse and consultant as these case proctors may play a vital role in shaping the case.

# Tips:

If the case participants have a difficult time implementing the proper measures to advance to the next stage of the case, then often the acting nurse can push the acting clinicians in the right direction. For instance the nurse may say “don’t we need to give some medication for his heart” if the learners are failing to give the indicated drugs in the beginning of the case. It is important to get feedback from all involved parties at the end of the case, reiterating what was done well and what improvements one might make.
