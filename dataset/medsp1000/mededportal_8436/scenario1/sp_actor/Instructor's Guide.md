# How to Use:

. The case participants should see the “Text to be shown on screen prior to beginning the case” before the case begins.   
 The patient’s history of present illness, past medical history, and social history should be given by the patient and the roommate at the bedside upon request by the case participants, as one would see in a real patient encounter.   
. The initial presentation of the patient to the learners should be as described by the “Initial Presentation of Patient.” Physical exam may need to be relayed by the nurse or other faculty instructing the case as the case participants request.   
 Once initial interventions are made, the case will progress to the “transitional period” and the patient should present as described.   
. After the last appropriate intervention is made, the case will then go on to conclusion.
