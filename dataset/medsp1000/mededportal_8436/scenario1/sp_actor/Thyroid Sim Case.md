# \*History of Present Illness:

Ms. Jones is a 31 year-old white female with 24 hours of nausea, vomiting, abdominal cramping, fevers and worsening confusion. Her symptoms started yesterday afternoon and have continued to worsen. Her abdominal pain is diffuse. She describes the pain as intermittent, crampy in nature and an 8/10 in severity. She has had no appetite and is still feeling nauseous. She has had 8-10 loose, brown, watery stools in the past 24 hours. She denies any hematemesis and/or bloody diarrhea. She also complains of feeling “anxious and jittery” for "months." She denies any chest pain but feels mildly short of breath. She is lightheaded when she stands but has not had any episodes of syncope. She does not see a doctor regularly and has no known medical problems. She admits to no recent travel, camping trips or sick contacts.
