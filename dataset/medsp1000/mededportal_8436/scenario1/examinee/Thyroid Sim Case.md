# Target Audience

Emergency Medicine or Emergency Medicine/Pediatrics Residents

# Text to be shown on monitor screen at bedside prior to beginning of case:

Ms. Jones, a 31 year-old white female, is brought to the Emergency Department by her roommate for 24 hours of abdominal cramping, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, fevers and worsening confusion. She denies any illicit drugs or ingestion of medication. In addition, she has been complaining to her roommate of feeling anxious and “jittery” for several weeks.

(Residents will obtain a full history and physical exam including obtaining or asking the nurse to obtain vital signs at the bedside. The above merely serves as an introduction and should be read by the residents prior to interaction with the patient.)

Scenario Setting: Emergency Department telemetry bed. Acting roommate and nurse are present at the bedside
