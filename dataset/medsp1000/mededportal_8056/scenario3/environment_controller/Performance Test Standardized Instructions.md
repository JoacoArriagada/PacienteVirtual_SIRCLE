Standardized Instructions for Critical Response Simulation Scenarios

You are the surgical intern on call at University of Minnesota, Fairview. Your colleagues here with you will serve as nurses and medical students, able to assist you as you see fit. You will soon be presented with a situation pertaining to one of the patients that you are cross-covering.

The vital signs provided in the situation that follows were obtained two hours ago.  The patient is not currently on any continuous monitoring. There is a peripheral IV in place, with no IV fluids running at this time. As you work-up and manage this patient, please be sure to verbalize to me everything that you intend to do, including components of the physical exam.  I can answer any questions that you may have for the patient or nurse.  You have the ability to obtain and re-assess specific vital signs, examine (and re-examine) the patient as you see fit, place the patient on monitoring, order fluids and medications, obtain labs and imaging, and communicate with more senior level members of the team. I will provide you with results of any tests that you order as they become available.

Do you have any questions before we begin?
