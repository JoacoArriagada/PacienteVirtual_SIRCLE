**Ideal Scenario Flow**

Learner(s) will review the completed CGWEP unintentional weight loss screening tool prior to entering the simulation room- see Appendix. Learner(s) are able to perform a concise and differential focused history and physical exam based on the results of the Weight Loss Tool. Learner(s) should recognize that the patient has a mixture of cognitive impairment, denture problems and oral candidiasis, and possible polypharmacy related to his weight loss. The learner(s) suggest monitored and assisted feedings, nutritional supplements, referral to a dentist and treatment of oral candidiasis, and tapering off of the Zoloft as this could be related to his increased somnolence. The learner(s) should also recognize that he has lost a significant amount of weight which could be secondary to more severe causes, but given the wife’s wishes to avoid intensive intervention and no further cancer screenings there should be a focus on other causes of weight loss.

**Anticipated Management Mistakes**

Failing to utilize weight loss tool properly and failing to recognize the factors that could be contributing to the patient’s weight loss. Utilizing the tool incorrectly and spending extra time focusing on parts of the flow chart that are not pertinent (i.e. attempting a PHQ-9 when the PHQ-2 was negative).
