**Ideal Scenario Flow**

Learner(s) will review the completed CGWEP unintentional weight loss screening tool prior to entering the simulation room- see Appendix. Learner(s) are able to perform a concise and differential focused history and physical exam based on the results of the Weight Loss. Learner(s) should recognize that the patient has a mixture of alcohol dependence, social isolation, concerning oral lesion, and food insecurity that could be contributing to her weight loss. She also has depression (but this is well controlled) and polypharmacy (should consider changing medications- can be done now or at a later date after addressing other issues).

**Anticipated Management Mistakes**

Failing to utilize weight loss tool properly and failing to recognize the factors that could be contributing to the patient’s weight loss. Utilizing the tool incorrectly and spending extra time focusing on parts of the flow chart that are not pertinent (i.e. attempting a PHQ-9 when the PHQ-2 was negative).
