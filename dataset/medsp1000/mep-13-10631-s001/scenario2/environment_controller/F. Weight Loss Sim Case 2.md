**Appendix F: Weight Loss Sim Case 2**

**SIMULATION CASE TITLE:** Geriatric Weight Loss Case #2

**PATIENT NAME:** Valarie Carr  
**PATIENT AGE:** 67-year-old  
**CHIEF COMPLAINT:** weight loss

**Logistics**: This activity occurs in a standardized patient simulation laboratory. The setting for the encounter is a geriatric specialty clinic. The simulation lab should have otoscope and/or penlight used to evaluate the oral cavity and a stethoscope. One standardized patient is needed to play the patient. The learner(s) should review the patient’s responses to the weight loss tool before entering the room. Need one instructor to lead the session and one simulation staff to ensure simulation runs properly and supports issues that may develop.

**Brief narrative description of case**  
Ms. Carr is a 67-year-old female who currently lives with her partner of 20 years. She is alone during the interview. She has a past medical history of depression, breast cancer with a history of bilateral mastectomy and chemotherapy in 2005, and hypertension (HTN). She is being seen in a geriatric specialty clinic for a geriatric evaluation. She was referred by her primary care physician at the patient’s request. When asked why she requested the evaluation she states “to make sure I’m receiving the proper care”.

**Learner Preparation**

- Prior to entering simulation room, the learner(s) will be provided with the patient’s responses to the weight loss tool. They will have 5 minutes to review the results before entering the room.

**Initial vital signs**  
Heart Rate 85, Respiratory Rate 14, Blood Pressure 150/80, Pulse oximetry 97% on room air, weight 117 pounds, height 70 inches

**Overall Appearance**  
Well appearing elderly female, appears older than stated age, thin, appropriately dressed, sitting in chair, resting comfortably, no acute distress

**Actors and roles in the room at case start**  
Female, older than 65, thin to play the main character- Valarie Carr. No other actors needed.

**HPI**  
Ms. Carr is a female who comes in for a geriatric evaluation. She wishes to make sure she is on all of the appropriate medications and is being treated correctly now that she is getting older. It is noted by the nurse who places the patient in the room that the patient has lost roughly 15 pounds in the past four months without trying. Ms. Carr states she is taking all of her medications as prescribed. She is a current smoker (1 pack per week for the past 10 years), drinks 1-2 alcoholic beverages per day (gin cocktails), she lives with her partner of 20 years. When initially asked about her weight loss she states that maybe it is related to being busy and she is concerned about the weight loss as she has not been trying to lose weight.

**Instructor Notes - Changes and CASE Branch Points**

- Prior to starting simulation case, the learner(s) will have 5 minutes to review the CGWEP tool and discuss strategy for interview.
- If learner(s) attempt an oropharyngeal exam, patient hand the learner a picture of the lesion located on the lateral aspect of the tongue.
- If learner(s) explore alcohol use, the source text identifies alcohol dependence as a possible cause of the patient’s weight loss.
- If learner(s) explore social situation, the source text identifies social isolation as a possible cause of the patient’s weight loss.
- If learner(s) explore nutrition and food access, the source text identifies multiple concerns for food insecurity.
- If learner attempts to do a PHQ9, the PHQ9 is negative and the filled out PHQ9 is handed to learner(s) when discussed.
- If learner may want to order lab work, provide: total cholesterol 159 mg/dL, LDL 87 mg/dL, HDL 59 mg/dL, albumin 4.4 g/dL, basic metabolic panel normal with creatinine 0.95 mg/dL, and TSH 1.1 uIU/mL. Other labs can be ordered but will be pending at end of evaluation.
- If learner(s) explore medication-related contributors, the source text lists these effects: ACEI (Lisinopril)- altered taste or smell; Lexapro- anorexia; Aspirin- nausea/vomiting.
- At 20 minutes, instructor notifies learner(s) of 10 minutes remaining.
- At 25 minutes, learners are notified of 5 minutes left.
- At 30 minutes, end of case.
