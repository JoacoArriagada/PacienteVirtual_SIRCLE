Interprofessional Team Training Day

Simulation Scenario

**Participants:** Medical and Nursing Students

**Required Personnel:**

Nursing students, medical students

* 1. Bedside assistant – Stays with patient, monitors VS, maintains IVF, gives meds, reports findings to team
  2. Procedure provider - Performs all procedures, assesses status, reports findings to team
  3. Documenter - Ensures all roles are filled, documents interventions, reports findings to treatment leader/team
  4. Team Leader – Obtains information, maintains awareness of overall situation, makes therapeutic decisions
  5. Runner - Obtains necessary supplies, contacts other resources as needed, interfaces with family members and coordinates transfers as needed

***Part I***

**Case Information:**

*Scenario Given to Participants:*

A previously healthy 52 year-old man arrives in the ED after a construction accident (a steel girder fell on his left thigh). He has left thigh pain and arrives in the ED 20 minutes after the accident.

*Scenario Conditions Initially:*

* Alert and oriented in obvious pain

VS: HR 130, RR 18, BP 88/64, O2 Sat 98% on room air.

* MUSCULOSKELETAL: Large amount of swelling and ecchymosis to the left thigh.
