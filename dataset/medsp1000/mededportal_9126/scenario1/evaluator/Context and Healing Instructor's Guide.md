**Teaching Students to Elicit Contextual Information**

**through a Standardized Patient Encounter**

Instructor’s Guide

**Goals and Objectives:**

The goal of this sequence is to help students develop an understanding of contextual issues that impact clinical outcomes, and to provide experience in using communication processes to elicit contextual information.

By the end of the sequence, students should be able to:

1. Identify commonly occurring patient clues (both verbal and nonverbal) that can signify important contextual information
2. Critique a student physician's communicative process with respect to eliciting contextual information
3. Articulate an understanding of the role of contextual issues in clinical care.
