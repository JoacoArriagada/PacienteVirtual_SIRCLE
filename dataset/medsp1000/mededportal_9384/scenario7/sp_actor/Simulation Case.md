# Covering All Bases: A longitudinal case based approach to teaching critical care fellows

G. Session VII – POD #17: Anoxic brain injury and End of Life Discussion

ii. Scenario background – Provided freely to trainees

a. The patient suffered a cardiac arrest 3 days ago and is failing to show any meaningful recovery of function.   
b. There are no other significant events in the interim.

iii. Initial scenario conditions

a. During morning work rounds, the team convenes and agrees given the current state of affairs their patient is not going to make any further neurological recovery. The fellow is asked to meet with the family when they arrive to discuss the situation.

iv. Scenario branch points

b. The patient’s health care proxy arrives to meet with the fellow and is having a difficult time understanding the nature of the situation.
