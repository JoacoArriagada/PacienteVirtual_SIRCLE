VII. Instructors Notes

A. In order to keep the scenarios flowing:

i. The embedded nurse educator is informed of the goals in a huddle prior to initiating the session with the fellows. The nurse understands what amount of information to divulge and if the trainees are struggling at any point, the nurse divulges necessary information to prod the students and keep the session on track to achieve the stated goals.   
ii. The staff monitoring the situation through a one-way window, including key clinical faculty and technician running the simulator, must pay close attention to the flow of the session and adjust patient

response accordingly. Also, they are able to intervene as necessary if the session is going off course and not achieving the stated goals.

B. Directing the actors:

i. The sessions described in this scenario use a human patient simulator and as needed the technician provides voice via the mannequin’s built in audio system. During large portions of this scenario the patient is intubated and unable to communicate verbally. ii. The nurse, as stated prior, is briefed on the goals of the session and allowed to dictate release of information based upon how the session is unfolding. C. Scenario programming: i. Completed by the simulation lab technician based upon the physiology described for each session. The instructor and technician communicate prior to the session to be certain the mannequin settings accurately reflect the case described and presented to the trainees.
