II. Target Audience: First year critical care fellows but may be adapted to other trainees learning to care for ICU patients.

C. Session III – POD #5: Perioperative Myocardial Infarction

ii. Scenario background – Provided freely to the trainees

a. Patient underwent exploratory laporatomy on POD #1 to relieve abdominal compartment syndrome   
b. On POD#4 in the morning he returned to the operating room for abdominal closure as the swelling resolved   
c. He was extubated in the morning with stable hemodynamics and normal mental status

iii. Initial scenario conditions

a. The patient is showing signs of increased agitation combined with difficulty oxygenating coupled with hypotension   
b. He is complaining of chest discomfort though he is having a difficult time differentiating it from the pain of his abdominal wound
