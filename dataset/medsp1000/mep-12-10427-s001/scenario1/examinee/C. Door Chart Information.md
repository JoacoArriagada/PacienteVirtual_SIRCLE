## **STATION 9**

Informed Consent

You are an M-4 student seeing patients in the Emergency room at UMHS.

Mrs. Caldwell presents with a remote history of cured leukemia (remission>5 years) in otherwise good health, to the ER with a 24-hour history of severe frontal headache and fever.  She states that the headache is becoming increasingly severe and that she has also had a temperature at home of 102F.  Her past medical and surgical history is otherwise negative.  She has felt somewhat lethargic over the past several hours as well.  You now plan to proceed with a lumbar puncture.  You will need to obtain informed consent in order to proceed.
