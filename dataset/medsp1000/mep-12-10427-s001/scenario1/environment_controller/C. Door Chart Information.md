## **STATION 9**

Informed Consent

**This is a 20-minute station.  Please take 5 minutes to read the attached information and 15 minutes to conduct the interview.  The staff member will knock on the door when 5 minutes remain and at the end of the session.**

You are an M-4 student seeing patients in the Emergency room at UMHS.

Mrs. Caldwell presents with a remote history of cured leukemia (remission>5 years) in otherwise good health, to the ER with a 24-hour history of severe frontal headache and fever.  She states that the headache is becoming increasingly severe and that she has also had a temperature at home of 102F.  Her past medical and surgical history is otherwise negative.  She has felt somewhat lethargic over the past several hours as well.  You have already obtained a head CT that is normal and you now plan to proceed with a lumbar puncture.  You will need to obtain informed consent in order to proceed.

**Your task is to:**	1)	Review with the patient the results of the diagnostic tests that the patient has

already undergone.  Explain what these results mean, in an understandable manner.

2)	Discuss the indications and/or benefits of lumbar puncture.

1. Obtain informed consent by having patient sign the attached consent form.
