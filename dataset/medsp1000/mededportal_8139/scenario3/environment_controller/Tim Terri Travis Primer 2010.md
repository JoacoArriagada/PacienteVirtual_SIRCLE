# 1. **Travis**

You are in the Emergency Room at 4am.  Your patient is Travis , a 60-year-old patient with abdominal pain.

The patient’s vital signs are:

# 2. 	**Pulse:**	110

	**BP:**	100/60

	**Resp:**	24

	**Temp:**	101.4 orally

You are to:

**--	Take a relevant history.**

**--	Perform an appropriate physical exam. You do NOT need to recheck vitals.**

**-- 	IF a rectal exam is appropriate, mention the need for the exam to the patient.** 

	**(Do not perform a rectal, pelvic/genital, breast, or corneal reflex examination).**

	**--	Tell the patient what you believe is going on with the patient and what you want to do next.**

You have **15 minutes** to perform these tasks.
