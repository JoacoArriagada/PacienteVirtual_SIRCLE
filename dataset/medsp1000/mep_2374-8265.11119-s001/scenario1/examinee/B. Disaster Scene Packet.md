**Disaster Scene: Sheet 1**

Scenario:

It is Sunday, May 6th at 8:30 pm. There is word that there was an explosion at the large indoor arena downtown during a major sporting event where there are reportedly about 20,000 people in attendance. It is a warm spring evening with temperatures around 60 degrees.

You are the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) set to action.

You will find a map in front of you with information regarding the location of the explosion and nearby structures.

On scene: 1 Advanced Life Support (ALS) truck, 2 Basic Life Support (BLS) trucks

Tasks:

1. Give orders to units to begin responding to the scene and assessing the situation.
2. Establish roles amongst members according to the ICS (Incident Commander, Supporting Roles)
3. Designate triage, treatment, transport, staging, casualty collection/morgue areas
4. You anticipate large masses of people. Where can you send patients and what do you have to consider when sending people there?
