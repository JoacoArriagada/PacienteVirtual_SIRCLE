### Door Note – Case 1

### EXAMINEE INSTRUCTIONS

It is December and you are rotating in the Emergency Department (ED). Sarah, a 14 month-old female has been brought to the ED by her 30 year-old parent. Sarah has a three-day history of cough and trouble breathing. Upon initial assessment of the child you suspect that the parent is a smoker, as you can smell it when you walk in the room. The child is tachypneic and mildly hypoxic, with SPO2 down to 89%. You try a normal saline nebulizer, and while you are monitoring the infant to see her response to the neb, you explain the diagnosis of bronchiolitis to her parent, and explain that Sarah may need to be admitted to the hospital.

### EXAMINEE TASKS

* Interview the parent using motivational interviewing techniques
* Share information and counsel the parent, including referral to the State Smoker’s Quitline. (See provided materials)
* Do not perform a physical exam on Sarah (you have done this already)
