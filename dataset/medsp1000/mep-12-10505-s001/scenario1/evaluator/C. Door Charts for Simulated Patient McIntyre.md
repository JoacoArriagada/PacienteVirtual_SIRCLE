**Practice Door Chart PART A**

Note to faculty facilitators: Upon arrival at their assigned room for **PART A** of the simulated patient interview, students will see a door chart like the one below.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Demographics** | **Care Team and Communications** |
| Name: Karen McIntyre  Age: 35 years old | Primary Care Physician: Dr. Susan Jones |

**Presenting Situation:**

You are a first year OB/GYN resident working in the office of Dr. Margaret Lu. Ms. McIntyre was referred by her PCP for a complaint of irregular periods with plans to become pregnant.

**Your Tasks for Today**:

1. Collect a history of chief complaint of irregular periods, as well as pertinent past medical and social history.
2. Use effective communication skills to address potentially distressing symptoms.
3. Recommend patient get blood work drawn to measure hormone levels. Request that patient return in about 3 weeks for follow-up to discuss the results of these tests.
4. Conduct focused physical exam.

*(For small group session, faculty should recommend to patient that she have a pelvic exam, but do not do exam).*

**Time Limit:**

10 minutes (the proctor will knock on the door at 8 minutes)

*(For practice scenario, faculty should limit this portion of the encounter to 6 minutes as there is no physical exam to complete.)*

**Post-Encounter Activity:**

Once you finish this portion of the encounter with the patient, you will move to the next station to document chief complaint, HPI, physical exam, and assessment and plan.

*(For the practice scenario, faculty will NOT demonstrate documentation).*

**Practice Door Chart PART B**

Note to faculty facilitators: upon arrival at their assigned room for **PART B** the simulated patient interview, students will see a “door chart” like the one below.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Demographics** | **Care Team and Communications** |
| Name: Karen McIntyre  Age: 35 years old | Primary Care Physician: Dr. Susan Jones |

**Presenting Situation:**

You are a first year OB/GYN resident working in the office of Dr. Margaret Lu. Ms. McIntyre was referred by her PCP for a complaint of irregular periods and plans to become pregnant. You saw her for initial evaluation last week and after discussing with your attending physician (Dr. Lu), you recommended collecting laboratory values of two hormones – follicle stimulating hormone (FSH) and estradiol.

This testing revealed the following results:

FSH = 45 IU/L Reference range for adult female = 1 – 30

Estradiol = 20 pmol/L Reference range for adult female = 70 – 600

In discussion with Dr. Lu, your interpretation of these results in the context of the patient’s symptoms suggests a diagnosis of premature ovarian failure. Given this diagnosis, patient will not be able to conceive as she hoped (e.g. she has infertility).

**Your Tasks for Today**:

1. Inform patient of test results and diagnosis of premature ovarian failure.
2. Discuss implications of this diagnosis on fertility and patient’s desire to become pregnant.
3. Use empathic communication skills to support patient’s distress regarding infertility.

**Time Limit:**

10 minutes (the proctor will knock on the door at 8 minutes)

**Post-Encounter Activity:**

Once you finish this portion of the encounter with the patient, you are finished with your SPE.
