**Standardized Survivor Profile**

**Spouse of a Middle Aged Man**

**Setting:**

You have been called to a local emergency room by a nurse and told that your spouse was there. They

were not able to release any information over the phone. You arrive, you are pleasantly greeted by the triage nurse and asked to wait in the family room and that the doctor will be in to speak with you. When you ask if you can see your spouse and if he is all right, you are told that you cannot see him yet and that the doctor will be right out to speak with you. The nurse tells you that your spouse is very ill.

You are escorted to the family room and you wait, alone, for the arrival of the doctor.

The doctor will tell you that your spouse has died.

**Time of Day:** 1:30pm, weekday

## Your Profile

Name: Marilyn White

Your Occupation: Private High School Teacher

Your Age: 50

Children: two, boy age 10; girl age 13

Local Support System: Active in church, mother and sister live nearby.

## Spouse Profile

Spouse Name: Jonathon White

Spouse’s Age: 45

Spouse’s Occupation: Software Engineer for a large corporation

Spouse’s Medical History:

* Insulin Dependent Diabetes
* Episode one year ago with low blood sugar where he had to be hospitalized
* Never had any other medical problems

Spouse’s Family History:

* Father died of heart attack at age 50
* One sister with diabetes and heart disease
* Two brothers with diabetes and heart disease
* One brother collapsed and died suddenly at age 40 without explanation

Spouse’s last doctor’s visit: 2 days ago, told everything fine

Spouse’s doctor: Dr. Smith, you will have Dr. Smith’s card to give to the doctor if they ask for the patient’s doctor

Spouse’s Lifestyle: Health conscious, exercises regularly. Very compliant with physician instructions.

**What you know prior to arrival to the ER:**

Called by nurse in the emergency room to come to emergency department. No information given over the phone.

One hour prior, you spoke with your spouse on the phone at work and you confirmed dinner plans. He stated he felt very nauseated and a little dizzy and thought that his blood sugar might be going low. He stated he was going to eat a piece of candy and get a full lunch after he hung up. He otherwise sounded well.

**Mood, Affect and Demeanor:**

**Demeanor:** Pacing the room, standing when the physician enters the room

**Affect**: Slightly Anxious. Appropriately concerned. You warmly greet the physician; you are not expecting bad news. You are sure that he is in the hospital because his sugar went low, and he had to be hospitalized similar to one year ago, without further complications. You are convinced that he will be all right and that you will go back to the ER and see him, then run to pick the children up from school. You are definitely not expecting him to be dead. You spoke to him within the hour and he was a bit nauseated, but otherwise well.
