**Group 1: Febrile neonate**

She is still waking to eat, but then falls asleep while eating. Normal urine output. She has a 3 year old sister who currently has an upper respiratory infection.

Birth history: Born at 38 weeks to a 25 year old G2P2 Mom by NSVD. GBS negative. Prenatal labs otherwise negative per Mom. No complications during pregnancy.

Physical Exam: T: 38.3° C P 165 R 44 O2 sat: 100% RA

General: Initially sleeping, wakes with exam when unwrapped from swaddle

HEENT: Anterior fontanel open and flat, moist mucus membranes

Neck: Supple

CV: RRR, normal S1 and S2, no murmur, femoral pulses 2+

Lungs: CTA bilaterally, good aeration

Ab: + BS, soft, non-distended, non-tender. No hepatosplenomegaly

Skin: warm, no mottling

Ext: capillary refill < 2 seconds

Neuro: Strong suck, + Moro
