**Group 2: Neonate with fever at home**

A 3 week old girl is brought to the ED for a rectal temperature of 38.2° C (100.7°F) at home. She has been fussier today and Mom feels she is not breast feeding as well as normal. She is still waking to eat, but then falls asleep while eating. Normal urine output. She has a 3 year old sister who currently has an upper respiratory infection. Her Mom did not know the dose of Tylenol so she brought her to the ED.

Birth history: Born at 38 weeks to a 25 year old G2P2 Mom by NSVD. GBS negative. Prenatal labs otherwise negative per Mom. No complications during pregnancy.

Physical Exam: T: 37.6° C P 165 R 44 O2 sat: 100% RA

General: Initially sleeping, wakes with exam when unwrapped from swaddle

HEENT: Anterior fontanel open and flat, moist mucus membranes. + Rhinorrhea

Neck: Supple

CV: RRR, normal S1 and S2, no murmur, femoral pulses 2+

Lungs: No retractions or nasal flaring. CTA bilaterally, good aeration.

Ab: + BS, soft, non-distended, non-tender. No hepatosplenomegaly

Skin: warm, no mottling

Ext: capillary refill < 2 seconds

Neuro: Strong suck, + Moro

1. **What evaluation do you want to perform?**
2. **Will you empirically start antibiotics? If so, which antibiotics?**
3. **Disposition (admit versus discharge)?**
4. **Would your management change if temperature at home had been taken on her forehead?**
5. **Would your management change if temperature at home had been an axillary temperature?**
6. **Would your management change if temperature was a tactile temperature?**
