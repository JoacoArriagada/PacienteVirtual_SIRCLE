PROFESSIONALISM ISSUES OR CHALLENGES

Providers may profile patient and assume that this pregnancy was unplanned/undesired, that patient is still using substances, that there is no paternal involvement in this pregnancy, etc. If these things are assumed, the patient will be very offended, and the rapport will be significantly damaged.
