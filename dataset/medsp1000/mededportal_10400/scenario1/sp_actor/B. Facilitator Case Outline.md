**Appendix B: Facilitator Case Outline**

You are the mother or father to Emma. You are in outpatient clinic for Emma’s 18 months well-child appointment.

Emma is a healthy female with no significant past medical history who is unimmunized. As Emma’s parent, you have some concerns about vaccines. You are concerned about the reports of autism although you recognize that the literature on this has been disproven. You are concerned about the number of immunizations children are given. Emma has a 9 week old sibling (Skylar). You are concerned about the recent measles outbreak. You ask what the ingredients are in the vaccines and side effects.

Past medical history: full term birth, no medical issues, previously unimmunized

Family history: non-contributory

Social history: lives with mother, father, and 9 week old brother who is also unimmunized

**Transcript and questions:**

Parent (Facilitator): *I am here with Emma and I know she’s due for vaccines but I’m not interested in getting them today.*

Questions/concerns:

1. *I’m worried about her receiving all these immunizations at the same time. It’s just too much for her small body to handle.*
2. *Why does she need to receive vaccines if she’s just going to be with at home with me and her little brother? She’s never been immunized and she’s never sick so why should I vaccinate?*
3. *I am concerned about thimerosol. What are the side effects?*
4. *I heard that there are vaccines that are derived from aborted fetal cell lines. Is this true?*
5. *I’ve just heard so many bad things about vaccines, such as long term effects like neurological diseases, multiple sclerosis, and other chronic conditions. What do you think of this?*
6. *I heard about the measles outbreak. I’m not worried since we stay at home, are you?*

Parent (Facilitator): Thank you for your thoughts. I will think about whether Emma will get her immunizations.
