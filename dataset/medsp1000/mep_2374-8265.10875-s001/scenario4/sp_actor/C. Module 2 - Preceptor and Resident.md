**CASE TWO:**

Jamie is a 32-year-old woman with an unremarkable past medical history who presents to your clinic for a routine pap smear. She is accompanied by her boyfriend, Trent who remains in the room during the procedure. You leave the room to give Jamie some privacy to get dressed. When you reenter the room to discuss follow up, she says “Doctor, my boyfriend and I were talking, and we were wondering if he should get a pap smear also?”

In this case Trent is a transgender man who was born with female anatomy, but his gender identity is male. He refers to himself as a man, may take on stereotypically male mannerisms and style of dress, and may also medically or surgically alter his body to affirm his male gender identity.

**Gender affirmation** is the **interpersonal, interactive** process where a person receives social recognition, and support for their chosen gender identity and expression.
