**CASE ONE**:

**Shawna is a 45yo woman who was referred to your primary care clinic after presenting to the emergency department twice, once for a headache and the second time for abdominal pain. She reports that she has no chronic medical problems. During your history, she discloses that she is a transgender male to female person. When reviewing her intake forms, you note a positive depression screen based on Patient Health Questionnaire 9 (PHQ9).**

**CASE ONE continued:**

**She discloses that she is bisexual, and recently got out of a “stressful relationship” with a male partner.**

**CASE ONE continued:**

**Shawna endorses emotional abuse in her past relationship, and she feels safe now that she is no longer in contact with him. She then asks if you could prescribe her cross sex hormonal therapy.**

Cross sex hormones are used to induce or maintain the characteristics of the sex that matches gender identity.
