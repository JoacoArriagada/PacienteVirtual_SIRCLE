Station 1: Standardized Patient

This scenario mostly involves an interaction between the teacher and the student. There may or may not be any questions asked of you. The teacher may or may not wish to demonstrate physical exam technique with you.

The student will present your situation as follows:

The patient presents with shortness of breath. It started this morning. The patient feels like they just can’t catch their breath. The patient has had a cold for the past several days, consisting of a runny nose, sinus congestion, sore throat, etc. The patient is coughing, and has missed the past few days of work. The patient is having trouble breathing with climbing stairs or even walking quickly. The patient is having some associated chest tightness as well, located in the center of the chest. The patient had to sit up in a chair last night. The patient has had a little leg swelling, mostly at night. The patient denies fever, chills, night sweats. The patient has a history of asthma and has been using an inhaler every 4 hours with partial relief. The patient doesn’t see a doctor very regularly.

**PMH:** HTN, hyperlipidemia, asthma.

**MEDS:** HCTZ 25 mg daily, lisinopril 20 mg daily, simvastatin 40 mg daily

**SH:** smokes ½ ppd x 20 years.
