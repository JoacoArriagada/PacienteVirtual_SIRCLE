Logistics

Each group of 4 will rotate through 3 different “stations”, each of which will last 20 minutes. 2 of your scenarios will be at the bedside, with a patient present, and the 3rd will involve a feedback session with a single student.

There are 4 roles (Teacher, Co-Pilot, Verbal Communication Observer, Non-verbal Communication Observer). Please decide among yourselves who will take which role. You should not repeat roles. Since there are only 3 stations, not all 4 of you will have a chance to be the “Teacher”. If you are an “Observer”, feel free to use your sheet as a checklist.

First 7 minutes= enact scenario

Second 7 minutes= discussion, then **teacher selects area to work on**

Next 5 minutes= re-enact scenario

Last 1 minute= brief discussion

Always start the Discussion by asking the teacher how it went. And make sure and ask the student their opinion!!

Station 3:

Setting: ED, patient bedside with new patient

Learner= M3 student, in 6th month of M3 year

For the Verbal Communication Observer:

Your job is to watch carefully. See what the teacher says, and your perception of how effectively it promotes understanding by the learner. Here are some sample observations you might make:

\*Did the teacher obtain a commitment from the learner (i.e. teacher asks question that requires learner to provide a proposed

assessment/diagnosis/treatment)?

\*Did the teacher explore the student’s rationale for their answers?

\*Did the teacher correct mistakes?

\*Did the teacher reinforce or give positive feedback for things done well?

\*Was the teacher able to teach a general principle? (versus specific facts)

\*Your job during the 2nd 7 minutes is to discuss what you observed in terms of verbal communication, and what worked well, and what the teacher might consider adding for the 2nd run-through.

Station 3:

Setting: ED, patient bedside with new patient

Learner= M3 student, in 6th month of M3 year

For the Non-verbal Communication Observer:

Your job is to watch carefully. Observe non-verbal cues, and how that affects the learning climate. Here are some sample observations you might make:

\*Did the teacher make good eye contact with the learner?

\*Did the teacher use hand gestures while speaking?

\*Did the teacher listen, as well as talk?

\*Did the teacher use good facial expression and show lots of energy in the discussion?

\*What was your sense of the overall body language, and how that affected the interaction?

\*Your job during the 2nd 7 minutes is to discuss what you observed in terms of non-verbal communication, and what worked well, and what the teacher might consider adding for the 2nd run-through.
