Station 3: Standardized Patient

This scenario mostly involves an interaction between the teacher and the student. There may or may not be any questions asked of you. The teacher may or may not wish to demonstrate physical exam technique with you.

The student will present your situation as follows:

The patient presents with abdominal pain. The pain started about a week ago, and has gotten progressively worse. The pain seems to be located all over the abdomen. It seems to begin in the morning, and gets worse later in the day. At times it gets quite severe. Curling up seems to make it better. Resting quietly helps too. Moving around makes it worse. The patient has had some nausea, but no vomiting. The patient has lost appetite, and has lost about 4-5#. No bloody stools or black/tarry stools. The patient has felt warm but has not checked their temperature.

**PMH:** Depression, chronic sinusitis

**MEDS:** Zoloft

**SH:** No smoking; Drinks about 2-3 ounces of alcohol 3-4 days/week
