Logistics

Each group of 4 will rotate through 3 different “stations”, each of which will last 20 minutes. 2 of your scenarios will be at the bedside, with a patient present, and the 3rd will involve a feedback session with a single student.

There are 4 roles (Teacher, Co-Pilot, Verbal Communication Observer, Non-verbal Communication Observer). Please decide among yourselves who will take which role. You should not repeat roles. Since there are only 3 stations, not all 4 of you will have a chance to be the “Teacher”. If you are an “Observer”, feel free to use your sheet as a checklist.

First 7 minutes= enact scenario

Second 7 minutes= discussion, then **teacher selects area to work on**

Next 5 minutes= re-enact scenario

Last 1 minute= brief discussion

Always start the Discussion by asking the teacher how it went. And make sure and ask the student their opinion!!

Station 2: Standardized Learner

This scenario deals with an attending physician giving you feedback.

You are an M3. You have just completed a month’s rotation in the ED, and you are now receiving feedback. Try to roll with the discussion; the attending may refer to various things that you did during the month. (feel free to use this sheet as a reference during the roleplay)

**YOUR ROLE** is that you an average M3. You are **defensive** about getting constructive criticism, but not to an extreme. Here is some background, in case it comes up in discussion with the attending:

You have been told in the past that your oral presentations are too brief, so you have been working on lengthening them.

You have been working on reading for an hour each night to improve your knowledge base, which you realize needs work. You have only done 1 outpatient month prior to this, so you realize this is an area of weakness for you.

You would really like to improve your physical exam skills. You would like advice on how to do this.

The attending may comment on you being overconfident or cocky. You realize this may be a defense mechanism for you, because of your lack of confidence about your knowledge base.
