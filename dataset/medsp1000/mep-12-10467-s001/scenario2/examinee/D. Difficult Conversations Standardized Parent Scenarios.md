Second Year Neonatal-Perinatal Fellow Standardized Parent Scenario—Redirection of care

For the fellow:

Baby Boy Jones is a 34 week infant with prenatally undiagnosed trisomy 18 (T18) with dysmorphic features. The infant was born in Small Town, USA to a 43 year old G3P now 2 mother. She had good prenatal care and was expecting a healthy baby. The Outside Hospital called The Children’s Hospital quickly following delivery due to the dysmorphic features and worsening respiratory distress. The Children’s Hospital transport arrived and transported him to The Children’s Hospital. It is now day of life (DOL) 3. You have been told the mother is very invested, sits at the bedside 24 hours a day, jumps on every alarm at the bedside, and very much wants this child. Dad doesn’t visit much. Given the diagnosis and poor prognosis, the care team feels care should be redirected. Your job is to plan this meeting and have this difficult conversation.
