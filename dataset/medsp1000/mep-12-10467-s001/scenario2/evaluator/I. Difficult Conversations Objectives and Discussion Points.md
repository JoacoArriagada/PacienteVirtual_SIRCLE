Neonatal-Perinatal Fellows Standardized Parents: Delivering difficult news

Overall objectives:

* At the end of this module, fellows will demonstrate a 2 point improvement in comfort level score (secondary to increased knowledge and practiced communication skills):
  + Consult at edge of viability
  + Withdrawal/redirection of care
  + Disclosing a medical error
* By the completion of fellowship, fellows will attain a mean Likert scale of 3.5 or above in each of the SPIKES steps

Evaluation needs/prep-work:

* Pre- and post-course self-evaluations
* Fellow Likert scales
* Scenarios
* Pre-reading/guidelines to fellows and facilitators
* 1-2 standardized parents (mother and father preferable)
* Chest x-ray with nasogastric tube in lung for 3rd year scenario

Plan:

First 15-20 minutes: Introductions, pre-test self-evaluation, “what happens in sim, stays in sim,” review readings, review guidelines, answer questions

Scenarios with SP’s—2 fellows at a time, one on-service leaving for the day, one on-call

Redirection of care

* Suggested knowledge-based questions for discussion: What options are there for parents (how things are taken away, memory-making options for family (bath, outside, etc))? What documentation needs to be done following a death?
* Suggested communication questions for discussion: What works well when breaking end-of-life news to parents? What doesn’t? What should be said? How would you handle the situation differently next time? What challenges did you face? How did you use the SPIKES guidelines? What should be done to set up the interview? How did you assess what the family understood about their situation? How did you support the family’s emotions?
