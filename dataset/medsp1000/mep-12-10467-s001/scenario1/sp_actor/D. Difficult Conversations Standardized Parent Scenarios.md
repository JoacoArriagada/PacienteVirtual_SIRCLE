First Year Neonatal-Perinatal Fellow Standardized Parent Scenario—Edge of viability counseling

For the SP Mom:

You are 32 years old. This is your 5th pregnancy—you have lost the first 4 in the first or second trimester. This baby was conceived via in-vitro fertilization and you DESPERATELY want this baby. You have been on bedrest since 19 weeks and followed it strictly. You even got a bedpan so you didn’t have to get up to pee. You came to the hospital today because your on-an-off contractions have increased and the pain is becoming much more intense. You are really upset and angry because you have done everything the doctors told you to do and this pregnancy is not turning out the way you had hoped. Your sister has 6 healthy children—why can’t you have just one? You are a special education teacher so a handicap doesn’t matter to you. You want everything done to save your baby (to be named _____).

For the SP Dad:

You are 33 years old. You and your wife have been married for 10 years and have suffered from miscarriage after miscarriage. You also really want a child, but are a little more hesitant of a severely handicapped child. You are quiet and supportive of your wife.
