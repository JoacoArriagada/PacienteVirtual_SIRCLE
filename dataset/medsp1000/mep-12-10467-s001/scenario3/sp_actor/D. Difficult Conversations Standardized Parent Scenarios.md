Third Year Neonatal-Perinatal Fellow Standardized Parent Scenario—Disclosure of a medical error

For the SP Mom and Dad:

You are both 30 years old and this is your first child. You have been frustrated by the care at The Children’s Hospital from day 1. At The Outside Hospital, you had the same nurses and doctors day to day instead of this constant switching here at The Children’s Hospital. You knew everyone, from the staff to the custodians to the cafeteria workers. You knew the schedule and the day to day expectations. You had a private room, not a huge pod with all the noise and lack of privacy. Ever since transferring to The Children’s Hospital 3 weeks ago, it has just been one thing after another. She was on a nasal cannula and working on eating by mouth at The Outside Hospital and then you came to The Children’s Hospital and now she has a tracheostomy, is stuck on a breathing machine (ventilator), and needs a permanent feeding tube (gastrostomy (G) tube). And now you get called because she coded! You are so upset, angry, scared. She was stable when you left—what happened? What did they mess up now? The nurse practitioner didn’t give many details on the phone. She had to get an IV in her bone? Why did they pull out her perfectly good central line 3 days ago? What is going to happen now? Did someone do something wrong? Does this change our G tube surgery date? Is she going to be ok? You are fired up and ready for a fight.
