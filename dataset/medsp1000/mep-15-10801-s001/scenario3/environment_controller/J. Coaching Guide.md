# Opening

Advise the audience and the actors that the situations presented may be triggering, particularly if individuals have experienced or witnessed similar situations, that they can participate to the extent that feel comfortable, and that there will be time for discussion after the scene is acted out. • Stress the need for confidentiality since people may, in discussion, talk about their own prior experiences Remind that what the actors say/do may or may not be reflective of who they are as people Requires everyone's agreement to create a safe space to discuss these sensitive issues. (Everyone is free to talk outside of the session about the scenario and the method, but not what people say about themselves.) Remind everyone to assume best intentions from all participants Consider – requesting everyone agree explicitly to create a safe space for each other, to speak authentically, and to preserve confidentiality – request each participant say "I agree."
