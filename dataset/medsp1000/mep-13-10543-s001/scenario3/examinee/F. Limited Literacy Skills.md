**Role-play exercise:**

The following role-play exercise involves two characters, a patient’s mother and you. It takes place in the Pediatric Hematology/Oncology Outpatient clinic.

You are a pediatric hematology/oncology fellow taking care of Mary, a 6 year old Caucasian girl diagnosed recently with neuroblastoma. She started treatment 3 weeks ago and is here for a clinic visit. She has missed some appointments, there are concerns about whether medication instructions are being followed correctly, and the team is worried that barriers to care may affect her treatment.

You are going to meet the parent in the clinic to discuss concerns raised by the rest of the medical team as everyone is worried that if the mother is non-compliant so early on in treatment Mary will not get the right care that she needs.
