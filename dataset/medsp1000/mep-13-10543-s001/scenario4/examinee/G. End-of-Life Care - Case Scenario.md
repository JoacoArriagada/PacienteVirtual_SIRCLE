**Role-play exercise:**

The following role-play exercise involves a patient’s parents and a physician. It takes place in the Pediatric Hematology/Oncology inpatient setting.

You are a pediatric hematology/oncology fellow caring for Amina, 14 year old girl from Riyadh with widely metastatic rhabdomyosarcoma that has not responded to multiple chemotherapy regimens. She currently has limited/no mobility and requires high doses of IV pain medications with 24 hour nursing care and continues to clinically decline every day.

You are going to meet with her and her family to discuss palliative care and transition to hospice in the light of dismal prognosis.
