**Role-play exercise:**

The following role-play exercise involves two characters, a patient’s mother/father and you. It takes place in the Pediatric Hematology Outpatient clinic.

You are a pediatric hematology/oncology fellow taking care of Olumide, a 6 year old African-American boy with sickle cell disease. He has had several hospital admissions for vaso-occlusive crises.

You are going to meet the patient and parent in the clinic to discuss concerns of recurrent pain crises and lack of response to therapy prescribed.
