**School Difficulties: Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorders, Learning Disorders, and Anxiety**

***Resident Version***

**Case 1:**

Jaden is a 7 year-old boy who presents to the clinic with his mother. She explains she’s been getting calls almost every day from his teacher complaining he is not completing his work, is running around the classroom, and “talking too much.” His mother states at home he has a lot of energy, but she doesn’t think he has trouble focusing because he can play on the iPad for hours. She does admit she has to ask him up to 5 times to follow a simple direction and that moves constantly from one activity to another. Lastly, his mother is exhausted and claims it can take Jaden up to 3 hours to fall asleep at night and is restless throughout the night, so no one in the family is getting enough rest.
