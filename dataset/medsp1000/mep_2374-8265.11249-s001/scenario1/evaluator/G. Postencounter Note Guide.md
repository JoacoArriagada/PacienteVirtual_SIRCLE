**Appendix G: Post-Encounter Note Guide - Clerkship Orientation Case**

Student Instructions:

Please write a SOAP note documenting this patient encounter.

Expectations:

Students were required to complete a post-encounter Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan (SOAP) note upon completion of their encounter. As this was a low-stakes assessment, the SOAP note was ungraded. However, students were expected to accomplish the following in each the section of the documentation:

Subjective:

* Personal medical history
* Brief family medical history
* Brief social history, including job, housing, support system, gender identity, sexual orientation
* Brief sexual history
* Current concerns such as hormone use and pulmonary issues

Objective:

* Details from the door chart on temperature, heart rate, and blood pressure

Assessment

* Patient’s goal (establish primary care)
* Recognition of uncontrolled asthma
* Hormone use

Plan:

* Counseling about safer sex and STI screening/prevention and preventive care
* Counseling about pregnancy risk (as applicable for current and/or past relationships)
* Recommendation for an albuterol inhaler and pulmonary function tests
* Switching to prescribed medications
  + All patients: albuterol inhaler and pulmonary function tests with monitoring
  + Transgender and gender diverse patients:
    - Transgender men and genderqueer female SAAB – recommendation of prescription testosterone
    - Transgender women and genderqueer male SAAB – recommendation of prescription estrogen and additional discussion/recommendation of an androgen blocker
  + Cisgender patients:
    - Cisgender women – discussion and recommendation of prescription of hormonal birth control
    - Cisgender men – discussion and recommendation of testosterone alternatives
