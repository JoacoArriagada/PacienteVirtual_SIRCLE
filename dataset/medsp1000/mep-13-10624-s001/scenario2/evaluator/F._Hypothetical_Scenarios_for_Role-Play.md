1. Babygirl Naomi is a 7 day-old ex full term female with hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy, a severe brain injury, secondary to prolonged hypoxia during delivery. You learn from the records that the mother and pregnancy were otherwise healthy and uncomplicated. You admitted this baby to the NICU immediately after birth. You reassure the family that you are doing everything you can to help their baby and determine the extent of any possible injuries. However, the MRI revealed severe brain damage. Naomi would almost certainly require tracheostomy for mechanical ventilation, a gastrostomy tube for feeding, and would likely be severely delayed. During a family meeting to discuss Naomi’s prognosis, the attending tells the family, “Your baby will probably never be able to eat with you, speak to you, kiss you, hug you or play.” The parents are expectedly quite tearful. Naomi’s mother repeats, “But my pregnancy was healthy. I can’t believe this happened to us. Do you think I should have pushed for a c-section? I so badly wanted to have Naomi naturally. This is all my fault.”

After several days of discussion and contemplation, Naomi’s family decide to withdraw painful interventions and allow Noelle a natural death. Naomi is extubated and disconnected from IV fluids. She lives another 2 days, and is held in her parents’ arms in her last moments. Naomi’s two older siblings are at bedside, clinging to their new baby sister’s stuffed animal, when the baby stops breathing and becomes asystolic. You turn off the monitor and pronounce the baby dead.

Your pager goes off 5 minutes later with a new admission.

Sample Points for Discussion:

* + - What part(s) of Naomi’s story affect you most?
      * What parts of her care were the most satisfying?
      * What were the most difficult parts?
    - How would you feel, emotionally, after pronouncing a newborn as deceased?
      * Would you feel differently if it were known prenatally that Naomi had a non-survivable condition?
    - How would you feel taking care of other patients immediately after Naomi’s death?
      * Do you think you would or could do your best work?
      * What would you do to help process Naomi’s death?
