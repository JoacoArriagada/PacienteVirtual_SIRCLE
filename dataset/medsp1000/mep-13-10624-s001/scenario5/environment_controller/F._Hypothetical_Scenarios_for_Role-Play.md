1. Jackson is a 4 month old male who was initially admitted severely ill due to sepsis, and was found to have a rare, life-threatening disorder. He requires a bone marrow transplant for cure. Jackson’s father is not involved in his life. Jackson’s mother is addicted to methamphetamine, and rarely is at Jackson’s bedside. One day, during a rare visit, Jackson’s mother says, “I can’t take care of this baby. This is all too much for me. I wasn’t looking to get pregnant, anyway.”

In time, Jackson’s mother relinquishes her parental rights, placing Jackson in the custody of the state. He is medically stabilized and remains hospitalized while awaiting bone marrow transplant. Due to his medical complexity, it is very difficult to find an appropriate foster placement. You make a dedicated effort to read to Jackson every afternoon to give him stimulation and bonding. You note that he bonds indiscriminately to others, but is a very happy child. Jackson’s first birthday party is held in the hospital, attended only by the many nurses, physicians, and other care providers whom have cared for him.

Sample Points for Discussion:

* What was the most difficult aspect of caring for Jackson?
  + Is it difficult to interact with parents that have different mindsets than you?
  + Do you approach patients differently when they are hospitalized for social reasons, rather than medical ones?
* What was the most satisfying part of caring for Jackson?
* What did you learn by caring for him?
