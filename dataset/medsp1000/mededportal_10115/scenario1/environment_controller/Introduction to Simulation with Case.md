**2. Introduction to Simulation Case 1 – Allergic Reaction**

3. Case Description
   1. HPI: A 23 y/o female presents with a rash and some wheezing after eating at a Chinese restaurant 30 minutes ago. She has a known allergy to peanuts but didn’t know the chicken she was eating had them. She has had this before and knows “just what to do”. She did not take any medications prior to arrival.
   2. ROS: Denies chest pain, fevers, cough, throat closing or any other symptoms
   3. Past Medical History: None
   4. Medications: None
   5. Allergies: Peanuts
4. Simulator Settings:
   1. VS initially: HR 110, BP 120/80, RR 20, Sat 97% RA, T 38
   2. Lungs: wheezes bilaterally
   3. Monitor: Sinus Tachycardia
5. Exam:
   1. Lungs: Diffuse wheezing
   2. Heart: tachycardic
   3. Skin: Hives over upper extremities and face, warm
   4. Abdomen: Soft non-tender,
   5. Extremities: No swelling, non-tender, good pulses
6. Trend: The patient will be forthcoming with the peanut allergy. The resident should ask a history and do an exam. If they order medicine the patient will prompt them- “I usually will take Benadryl when this happens” or “ I think I need my epi pen”. The patient will improve with this treatment.
