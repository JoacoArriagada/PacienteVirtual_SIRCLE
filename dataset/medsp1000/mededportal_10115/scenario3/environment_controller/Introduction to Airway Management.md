**Scenario 2: Drug overdose with apnea**

At the end of this session the learner will be able to:

* List the steps to initially approach the airway in an apneic patient
* Describe and properly use basic airway equipment such as the oropharyngeal airway/nasopharyngeal airway, bag valve mask, venti-mask, nasal cannula and laryngoscope

I. Scenario:

A 19-year-old female is brought in with EMS. The patient was dropped off by her friend with a needle in her arm and apneic. Patient is brought to a room. She is cyanotic with a pulse and RR of 4. The resident is asked what is their approach to this patient’s airway while the nurse draws up a Naloxone dose.

II. Exam

VS: RR 4, Sat 75%, HR 50, BP 90/60, temperature 97

Airway: Patent

Lungs: Clear to auscultation bilaterally

Cardiovascular exam: No extra heart sounds, normal exam

Skin: cyanotic, track marks noted

Remainder of exam WNL

III. Case Course:

The learners should position the patient apply O2 via BVM and bag the patient since oxygenation inadequate with patient effort. Preparation for intubation would be appropriate as well. The patient’s saturation will then rise and the nurse will administer the naloxone and the patient will wake up. The learner could also decide to intubate the patient prior to naloxone administration, if so appropriate intubation technique should be emphasized.
