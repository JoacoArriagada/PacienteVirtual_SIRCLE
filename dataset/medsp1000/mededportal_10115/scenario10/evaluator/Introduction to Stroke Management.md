1. Objectives:
   1. Diagnose a patient with post tPA intracranial bleeding
   2. Appropriately treat a tPA induced bleed
   3. Manage blood pressure
   4. Manage the airway of a decompensating stroke patient
2. Critical Actions
   1. Identify a post tPA bleed
   2. Order stat Head CT scan
   3. Treat elevated blood pressure
   4. Order Cryoprecipitate or a reversal medication
   5. Call Neurosurgery immediately
   6. Manage airway
3. Case Overview
   1. This is a case of a patient who received tPA and then decompensates secondary to an active brain bleed. The learner must diagnose the patient with a complication of this medication and initiate proper resuscitation and treatment.

8. Case course:
   1. Patient has an bleed post TPA –
   2. Stop thrombolytic
   3. Stat Head CT must be ordered with labs
   4. Reversal medication (antifibrinolytic) or cryoprecipitate ordered if bleed noted on Head CT
   5. Need to intubate patient recognized
   6. Neurosurgery consulted stat to room. Care can be handed over the Neurosurgeon and Neurology at that point.
9. Debriefing points
   1. If patient deteriorates clinically and intracranial hemorrhage is suspected, discontinue thrombolytic immediately.
   2. STAT Head CT scan is done.
   3. STAT labs include: PT, PTT, fibrinogen, CBC, Platelet count, and type and cross.
   4. If hemorrhage is documented, consider antifibrinolytic or cryoprecipitate.
   5. Consult neurosurgery, neurology and hematology stat.
