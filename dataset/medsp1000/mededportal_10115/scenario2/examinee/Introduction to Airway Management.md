**Scenario 1: Seizure with apnea**

I. Scenario:

Residents are asked to come immediately to the room of a patient who just had a seizure. He is described as a 32-year-old male who has a history of seizures. When the nurse was taking his history he began to have another, it resolved on its own but now patient has sonorous snoring respirations in the bed. He is breathing spontaneously and appears to be sleeping.
II. Exam

Vital signs: RR 8, O2Sat 90%, Pulse 100, BP 150/80, Temp 98.6

Lungs: clear to auscultation bilaterally

Airway: patent but some secretions
