The Development of a PTSD Standardized Patient Encounter

**Implementation of the PTSD SP Encounter**

A simulated hospital suite was modified to mimic an emergency room. The clinical group of 8 students and 1 instructor arrived at the center 30 minutes prior to the start of the simulation. The instructor served as the facilitator and content expert. Students were oriented to the simulation suite, received a student guide and signed consent to be video recorded. Then the instructor assigned student roles. Two students for each simulation were either the primary nurse or the secondary nurse and the other 6 were observers. The encounter was digitally recorded for review in debrief. Each SP simulation was allowed to run a maximum of 20 minutes with an announcement of "5 minutes are remaining" to cue the students to close the encounter. Faculty or students could end the scenario before the full 20 minutes if the learning objectives were successfully achieved. At the end of the scenario students and instructor proceeded to the debrief room while the SP had a maximum of 15 minutes to come out of character, review the guidelines for debrief, and collect his relevant thoughts for verbal feedback. Meanwhile, the instructor began to facilitate the debrief session with all 8 students based on the instructor's debrief guide. When ready the SP joined the nursing students and faculty in debrief and engaged in guided reflection on the encounter by giving positive and meaningful feedback to the students. At the end of the debrief session nursing students completed a web-based evaluation of the SP encounter.
