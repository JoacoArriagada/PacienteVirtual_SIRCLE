SP SIMULATION

PTSD CASE SCENARIO "DAVID"

<u>**Scenario**</u>

David is a 30-year-old war veteran who presents today in the emergency department guarded and hyper-vigilant with complaints of increased insomnia and flashback.

PRESENTING SITUATION

David

David is in the ER today due to having flashbacks of the war with visions of active combat. He states, "I can't take this anymore; I need help before I take matters into my own hands." (David was brought to the ER by his wife due to recurrence of acute PTSD symptoms.)

Vital signs: N/A

When the student enters the room, David is sitting up on a gurney wearing his regular clothes. He is looking straight ahead with a blank stare and sweating.
