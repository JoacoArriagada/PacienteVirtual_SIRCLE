**PTSD Case**

**Guidelines for Debrief**

**“The feedback I give you is from the perspective of the character I just played.”**

Standardized Patient, use these trigger questions to inform your feedback prior to entering the debrief session.

* How did the learners make you feel? Did they diffuse your impending attack? If not, what specific actions or mannerisms made you uncomfortable?
* Did the learners address your emotions sensitively?
* Did you feel judged or stigmatized by having your behavior and psychology assessed? If so, what specific words or actions made you feel this way?
* Did you feel safe during the encounter? If not, what specific actions made you feel at risk of harm? Were there specific things the learners did that made you feel safer?
* Did you feel the learners understood your perspective and preferences? Did you feel your views were respected and incorporated into the plan of care?
* Did you feel cared for and nurtured by the learners? What specific actions led to this feeling?
* Did you feel you could trust the learners? Why?
