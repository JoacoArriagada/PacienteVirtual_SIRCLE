# Crush Injury Simulation: Set-up and Preparation

1) Title: “Crush Injury”

4) Environment

a) Lab Set Up: “collapsed building”; this will take come creativity by the staff to re-create a collapsed building scene using various props   
b) Mannequin Set Up: on floor, with beam, box or crush injury item over lower extremity i) possible makeup for bruising/blood adds to the realism of the situation   
c) Props: AED, basic airway, temporary splint, backboard   
d) Distractors: speakers for high winds/tornado sounds, flashing or dim lights, smoke machine

5) Actors: groups of 5 medical students

a) Roles: team leader, one student for airway, one for medication administration, two for possible chest compressions/to remove beam, on-scene commander and finally the patient

c) Action role

i) Team leader – final say in all decisions   
ii) Patient – relays information   
iii) On scene commander – instructs students about building stability, which objects may be removed, proper time to remove the beam from the patient

# Crush Injury Simulation Narrative

# 1) Background Information and Triage Note (read to team by on scene commander)

A severe tornado has swept through (your city) and the 160 mph winds have destroyed homes and buildings. A local Wal-Mart shopping center collapsed, trapping and injuring over 200 people. The building has been evacuated and the Search and Rescue teams are looking for survivors.

One Search and Rescue teams has found a 45 year old male seeking shelter in a supply room when the building collapsed. He has been isolated for approximately 8 hours and his legs are trapped under a 500lb beam and the team is unable to move him. Construction workers are clearing the area so they can remove the beam but the estimated time will be 45 to 60 minutes. Currently, the patient is short of breath, scared and nervous. Rescue teams have requested a medical team move in and help the patient.

Patient History (read to team by on scene commander):

Patient Name: Bill Smith APVU: Alert and oriented x4, scared CC: SOB, leg pain/unable to move legs PMHx: Asthma, Hypertension Medications: Albuterol inhaler Allergies: none

2) Initial Scenario Conditions: Upon entering the building, students will find the patient trapped under boards/beams covering the lower half of patient's body (they should be instructed as to what beams/objects they may and may not move). He is in obvious distress.
