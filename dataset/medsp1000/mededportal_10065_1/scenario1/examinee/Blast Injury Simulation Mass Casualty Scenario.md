Multiple Casualty Scenario from Bomb/Blast Injury

# **Title: A Bomb Went off at the Bus Station**

# **II. Target Audience**: EM residents

## Environment

1. Lab Set Up –Simulation center ED, trauma bay. Could be carried out in any large open space or in the ED.

## Actors

*Number of people required for each role in parentheses.*

* RN (4)
  + Preferably played by ED nurse, alternatively a well trained actor can play this role
  + Located on the floor, taking direction from the providers managing the case
  + Help providers locate equipment and carry out desired tasks
* EMS (1-2)
  + Preferably played by EMS personnel, alternatively a well trained actor can play this role
  + Bringing in new patients
  + Using standard equipment (e.g. backboards, c-collars, etc.)
* Parent (1)
  + Can be played by any actor
  + Accompanies Sim youth with asthma

1. **Case Narrative** (describes what the learner will experience)
2. Scenario Background Given to Participants
3. Participants are divided into 3 groups and are told that they are doing simulation stations and may have to wait their turn. No further information is provided. A group of 4 is chosen to start the scenario.
4. At the start of the encounter, you are among the initial physicians in the simulation-center ED/trauma bay caring for a child with an asthma exacerbation while nurses are present at bedside.
5. RN or EMS notifies the team that they have heard a bomb went off at the bus station nearby. For each patient dropped off by paramedics, they will only inform the learners of the patient’s vital signs en route and any major complaints. No other information will be given. If other information is asked for the paramedics will state “it was a short transport time, we weren’t able to get any other information and we have to get back to the scene”.
