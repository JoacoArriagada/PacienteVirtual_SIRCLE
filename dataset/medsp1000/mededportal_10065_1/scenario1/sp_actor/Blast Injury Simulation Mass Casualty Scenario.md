Multiple Casualty Scenario from Bomb/Blast Injury

# **Title: A Bomb Went off at the Bus Station**

The scenario begins with participants caring for a severe asthmatic patient when a Mass Casualty Incident (MCI) occurs nearby and patients start arriving at the hospital. There are a total of 11 trauma patients from this MCI who arrive over approximately a 15 minute time period.

## Actors

* Parent (1)
  + Can be played by any actor
  + Accompanies Sim youth with asthma
* Live Victims (5)
  + Can be played by any well trained actor, residents, other students
  + Roles as described in grid

1. **Case Narrative** (describes what the learner will experience)
2. Scenario Background Given to Participants
3. Case starts with child with asthma exacerbation – see grid for details
4. RN or EMS notifies team that they have heard a bomb went off at the bus station nearby.
