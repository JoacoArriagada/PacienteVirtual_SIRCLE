| **Brief narrative description of case** | Frank Marshall is a 68-year-old gentleman who underwent a total thyroidectomy two hours ago and has now been transferred to the inpatient floor. His nurse states that he is feeling more dizzy and lightheaded. On further questioning, the nurse and family member state that his neck appears more swollen compared to an hour ago. Frank is initially speaking but becomes more obtunded as the scenario progresses. His oxygen saturation continues to decrease until the hematoma pocket is incised and evacuated at the bedside and definitive plans made to return to the operating room. |
