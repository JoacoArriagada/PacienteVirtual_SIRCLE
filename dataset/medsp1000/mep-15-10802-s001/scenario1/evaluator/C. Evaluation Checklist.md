Appendix C. Evaluation Checklist

Evaluation Guideline

* Does not meet expectations: The actions were not performed by participants, or significantly delayed, increasing risk of harm for the patient.
* Borderline: The actions were partially performed by participants, or delayed, or required reminder from the confederates.
* Meets expectations: The actions were appropriately performed by participants.
* Exceeds expectations: The actions were accurately and efficiently performed, in a manner expected in senior resident level.
