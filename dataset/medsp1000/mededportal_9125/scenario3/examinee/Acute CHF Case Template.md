I. Title: Acute Congestive Heart Failure (CHF)

IV. Environment

A. Lab Set Up – Emergency Department

V. Actors

A. Roles – facilitator doubles as bedside nurse to carry out physicians’ orders

VI. Case Narrative (describes what the learner will experience)

A. Scenario Background Given to Participants at initiation 1. Chief complaint: Shortness of breath

Triage note: This is a 69 year old female, brought in by daughter with complaint of shortness of breath. It started last night at a birthday party, and worsened throughout the night. Patient unable to sleep lying down. Patient can only speak in short phrases due to shortness of breath.

Past medical history: Significant for hypertension and high cholesterol Meds and allergies: Taking “Procardia” and lovastatin, no known drug allergies Family/social history: non contributory

B. Scenario conditions initially

1 History patient gives: unable to provide further history due to language barrier 2 Patients initial exam: BP 160/90, HR 110 and regular (normal sinus rhythm), RR 35, temperature: afebrile, $S a0 2 8 9 \%$ on room air, lung sounds crackles at moderate volume, 3 Patients physiology: mild to moderate respiratory distress
