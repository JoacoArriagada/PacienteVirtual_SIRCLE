**Peer Observer Checklist**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aspect of performance** | **Sub optimal performance** | **Acceptable performance** | **Performance exceeds expectations** |
| **Establishes a positive atmosphere at the beginning of the interaction** | halting voice  looking down  appears uncomfortable | makes eye contact  shakes the patient's hand  brief, light conversation | confident eye contact, warm smile  friendly voice inflection  puts the patient at ease |
| **Uses non-verbal means to enhance communication** | seating arrangement is awkward  demeanor is too reserved  appears apprehensive | comfortable seating arrangement  comfortable eye contact  leaning slightly forward | adjusted seating arrangement  attentive facial expression  demeanor is reassuring and steady |
| **Identifies the problem that the patient wishes to address** | forgot to ask patient what they would like to discuss | asked the patient what they would like to discuss | asked what problem needs addressing  asked if there are any other problems |
| **Starts with an open-ended question** | begins with directed questioning | asks an open-ended question and then pauses to listen | asks an open-ended question  conducts the interview like a conversation  touched on all key points |
| **Checked for three “red flag” conditions** | forgot to check for serious red flag conditions | checked for one or two serious red flag conditions | checked for all three red flag conditions |
| **Obtains a sexual history in a supportive, nonjudgmental manner** | forgot to take a sexual history | Obtained the sexual history  showed some embarrassment | Obtained the sexual history in a calm, nonjudgmental manner |
| **Washed hands prior to exam** | forgot to wash hands or use hand sanitizer | remembered to wash hands or use hand sanitizer | washed hands (used sanitizer) so that the patient could see them sanitizing |
| **Examined the throat using correct technique** | unable to visualize the throat  caused patient discomfort | did see the throat well  slight patient discomfort | throat well seen  negligible discomfort to the patient  explained findings during exam |
| **Palpated the neck using correct technique** | forgot to palpate the neck  palpated incorrectly | correctly palpated the neck in the correct area | correct technique to palpate the neck  explained findings during exam |
| **Provides closure effectively** | forgot to review findings  forgot to review next steps | reviewed findings  reviewed options for next steps | reviewed findings  discussed options for next steps with patient and formulated a plan |
