**APPENDIX E.3**

**MRI of BRAIN WITH AND WITHOUT CONTRAST AND MRV OF HEAD WITH CONTRAST**

**MRI BRAIN**

An area of high T2 and FLAIR signal at left inferior midcerebellar hemisphere also exhibits blooming susceptibility artifacts and patchy postcontrast enhancement and likely represents a hemorrhagic, likely venous infarct. There is mild interval worsening with increased size of left cerebellar hemisphere causing mild distortion of fourth ventricle which is patent. Low T2 signal at left transverse sinus also exhibits patchy contrast enhancement, along with occlusion of the flow compatible with thromboses of left dural venous sinus.

The ventricular system is normal without shift of midline structures. No evidence for extra-axial hemorrhage of abnormal fluid collection is seen. The craniovertebral junction is unremarkable.

**MRV HEAD**

There is presence of a filling defect at torculum extending into both the left and the right dural venous sinus with mild expansion of right transverse sinus and total occlusion of left transverse sinus. The filling defect also extends into the occipital sinus. The majority of the superior sagittal sinus, inferior sagittal, and straight sinuses are well visualized and appear to be normal in

caliber.

**IMPRESSION:**

1. Thrombosis of torculum, occipital sinus and both the transverse sinuses with complete occlusion of left transverse dural venous sinus and mild expansion of right is worse dural venous sinus as detailed above.

2. Hemorrhagic venous infarct of left inferior mid cerebellar hemisphere.
