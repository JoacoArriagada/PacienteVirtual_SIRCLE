4. Case Narrative (describes what the learner will experience)
   1. Scenario background given to participants (specify if given freely or must be asked for)

      2. Past medical history: Endoscopy for coin in his esophagus (Freely)
      3. Meds and allergies: None (Freely)
      4. Family/social history: Leukemia and lymphoma in family members (Freely)
   2. Scenario conditions initially
      1. History patient gives: For the last 2 weeks he has not been himself and for the last day, he has been worse. (Freely)
      2. Patient’s initial exam: Pale looking toddler, laying still on the gurney with his eyes open. Vitals: Temp: 36.6C, HR: 129, Respiratory rate: 32, blood pressure 95/49, pulse ox 99% RA. Weight 12.7 kg. Sleepy, hard to engage, fussy and not easily consolable. Awakes when poked for IV then right back to sleep

Cardiovascular: 2/6 systolic murmur heard best at left sternal border

Neurologic: When not stimulated falls back asleep. Cranial nerves II-XII are grossly intact. Patella reflexes are 2+ bilaterally. Kicks and moves arms symmetrically. Reaches for things symmetrically. No truncal ataxia when sitting up. Muscular bulk and tone are normal

* + 1. Patient’s physiology: Patient’s consumption of milk is excessive, causing iron deficiency which in turn causes microcytic anemia and thrombocytosis. Anemia and thrombocytosis along with dehydration from an illness caused a cerebral sinus thrombosis.
