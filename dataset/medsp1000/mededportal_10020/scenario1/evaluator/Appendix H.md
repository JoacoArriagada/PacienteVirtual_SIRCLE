**APPENDIX H**

* Listens carefully to the nurse/tech
* Asks for child to be placed on a monitor
* Quickly assesses ABC
  + What does the child look like? **Pale and sleepy**
  + Do I hear noises coming from the airway? **No stridor or wheezing**
  + Do I hear breath sounds? **Good breath sounds bilaterally**
  + Does the child wake up to voice or my exam? **Yes, he tells you to go away and fights** **with decreased but symmetric strength**
  + Gag reflex? **Yes**
  + Do I feel good pulses? **Yes**
  + Capillary refill? **3 seconds**
