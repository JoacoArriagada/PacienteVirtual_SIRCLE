**Case B: Disability and the Patient Experience**

Mrs. Jones, a 30-year-old female patient, has spina bifida (SB), a congenital malformation of the spine, which can result in lower extremity paralysis and autonomic dysfunction. She is a wheelchair user and requires bladder management. Mrs. Jones missed her menstrual period and thinks that she may be pregnant. She presents to her OB/GYN physician for evaluation. A pregnancy test is performed, which is positive.
