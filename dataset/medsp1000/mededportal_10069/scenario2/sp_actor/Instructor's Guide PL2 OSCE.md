# #5. Apparent life threatening event (ALTE)

A. Subject: Identification of ALTE and its possible etiologies

B. Question: A one-week old infant (born at 35 weeks gestation) became cyanotic while in mother’s arms. Mother rushed him to your office.

C. Set-up: The resident will ask a series of questions to an interviewer (i.e. the parent of the child in question). The following answers will be supplied if these questions are specifically asked by the resident: apnea, duration [apneic for 20 seconds] seizure activity or tone [hypotonic] need for intervention [mouth-to-mouth, shaking] relation to feed [two hours after] family history of SIDS [none]
