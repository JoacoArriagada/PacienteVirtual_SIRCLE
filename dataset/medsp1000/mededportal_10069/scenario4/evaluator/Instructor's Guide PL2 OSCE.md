# #17 Prenatal visit

A. Subject: Anticipatory guidance during the prenatal visit.

B. Question: You are being interviewed by expecting parents who are looking for a new pediatrician. They are expecting their child in three weeks. They ask what they should know to prepare for the baby. List and discuss at least four anticipatory guidance issues that you should discuss in the prenatal visit. Do not discuss issues that are more pertinent for the first post-natal hospital visit, e.g. jaundice, bathing, gastroesophageal reflux, etc.

C. Set-up: Interviewer acts as parent.

D. Correct Answer:

- prepare parents for hospital procedures   
- discuss feeding methods   
- discuss sleep location   
- prepare for baby care: 1. furniture - bassinet vs. crib 2. clothing - flame-retardant clothes. 3. crib safety (crib slats no more than $2 3 / 8$ inches apart; no cutouts in headboard or footboard; remove corner posts; avoid lead based paint; snug mattress; place mobiles high; avoid placing crib near window)   
- review needs of siblings   
- discuss effects of medicines on pregnancy and lactation   
- review pros and cons of circumcision   
- suggest prenatal classes   
- make arrangements for the return home   
- home safety   
- car safety restraints

E. Scoring:

1-1/4 point for each correct answer (must discuss the topic briefly).   
1/2 point if only lists topic but doesn’t discuss issues related to topic.
