# #16. Henoch-Schonlein Purpura

A. Subject: Explaining a diagnosis to parents.

B. Question: A five year-old boy named John comes into your office with complaints of rash, abdominal pain, and joint pain. On exam he has normal vital signs, mild abdominal pain, mild arthritis in his knees, and purpura along his lower extremities and buttocks. You think he has Henoch-Schonlein Purpura (HSP) and can be managed as an outpatient. You must now explain this diagnosis to his parents.

C. Set-up: Interviewer acts as parent. Interviewer reads the question initially and may prompt (e.g. what is the possible treatment) if the resident forgets to discuss any issues.
