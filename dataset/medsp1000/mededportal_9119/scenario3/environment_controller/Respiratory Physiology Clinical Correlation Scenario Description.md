# Respiratory Physiology Clinical Correlation

Environment: Basic simulation lab with full-scale human patient simulator (HPS in our case).

# Case 3: Pulmonary Edema

Scenario

Congestive heart failure in a patient who failed to take his digoxin for several days.

# Case Narrative

A. Background: Students are informed by the instructor: An Emergency Department patient is complaining of shortness of breath.   
B. Initial Conditions: Rapid and shallow respiratory pattern, rales, mild hypotension with tachycardia, eyes are open.   
C. Scenario Progression: Improvement in saturation after intubation.

# Instructors Notes

Auscultation reveals rales and an S3 gallop. Pulse oximetry records saturation around $90 \%$. Nasal cannula provides little improvement, and a non-rebreathing mask provides only moderate improvement in saturation. ABG analysis confirms the large P(A-a)O2 and decreased PaCO2. The patient has poorly compliant lungs before diuretic treatment and improved compliance after diuretic treatment and a time warp, with outflow of \`\`urine'' to a Foley catheter bag. Chest radiograph reveals dramatic pulmonary edema.
