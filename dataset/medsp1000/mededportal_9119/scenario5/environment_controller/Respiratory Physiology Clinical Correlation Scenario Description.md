# Respiratory Physiology Clinical Correlation

Environment: Basic simulation lab with full-scale human patient simulator (HPS in our case).

# Case 5: Dead Space

Scenario

Intubated patient with improperly assembled ventilator tubing (interposition of an extended section between the Y-piece and endotracheal tube). This creates a large section of deadspace.

# Case Narrative

A. Background: Students are informed by the instructor: A post-surgical patient is being weaned from the ventilator. He becomes agitated and the nurse requests an order for sedation.   
B. Initial Conditions: Rapid, deep respiratory pattern, hypertension and tachycardia.   
C. Scenario Progression (no sedation administered): Continually elevating CO2, both inspired and expired.

# Instructors Notes

Hyperventilation is ruled out after ABG analysis or assessment of the capnogram (CO2 is high, not low). Auscultation and additional studies are not helpful. The capnogram shows an elevated baseline to the CO2 tracing. Removal of the additional tubing causes immediate improvement and normalization of the respiratory pattern and capnogram.
