# Respiratory Physiology Clinical Correlation

Environment: Basic simulation lab with full-scale human patient simulator.

# Case Narrative

A. Background: A patient on the medical service has become uncommunicative over the last few minutes; he is drooling and is having difficulty opening his eyes. He begins to look “blue.” You are cross-covering on the patient and know little about him.   
B. Initial Conditions: Rapid and shallow breathing, eyes are closed, tachycardia and hypertension.
