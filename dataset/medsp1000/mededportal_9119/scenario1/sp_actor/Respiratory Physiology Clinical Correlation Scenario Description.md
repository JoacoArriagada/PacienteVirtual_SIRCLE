# Respiratory Physiology Clinical Correlation

Environment: Basic simulation lab with full-scale human patient simulator.

# Case 1: Respiratory Muscle Paralysis

# Case Narrative

A. Background: A patient on the medical service has become uncommunicative over the last few minutes; he is drooling and is having difficulty opening his eyes. He begins to look “blue.”   
B. Initial Conditions: Rapid and shallow breathing, eyes are closed, tachycardia and hypertension.   
C. Scenario Progression: Total paralysis occurs over several minutes with a goal of clear desaturation within 2-3 minutes.

# Instructors Notes

Though the patient only moans and grunts to verbal stimulation and shaking, he ``passes'' his basic assessment of Airway, Breathing and Circulation (the ABC's).
