# Respiratory Physiology Clinical Correlation

Environment: Basic simulation lab with full-scale human patient simulator (HPS in our case).

# Case 1: Respiratory Muscle Paralysis

Scenario

Progressive paralysis of respiratory muscles due to inadvertent administration of vecuronium instead of vancomycin.

# Case Narrative

A. Background: Students are informed by the instructor: A patient on the medical service has become uncommunicative over the last few minutes; he is drooling and is having difficulty opening his eyes. He begins to look “blue.” You are cross-covering on the patient and know little about him.   
B. Initial Conditions: Rapid and shallow breathing, eyes are closed, tachycardia and hypertension.   
C. Scenario Progression: Total paralysis occurs over several minutes with a goal of clear desaturation within 2-3 minutes.

# Instructors Notes

Though the patient only moans and grunts to verbal stimulation and shaking, he \`\`passes'' his basic assessment of Airway, Breathing and Circulation (the ABC's). Over several minutes, the oxyhemoglobin saturation (SpO2) begins to fall.

The patient eventually stops breathing and requires mask ventilation. If the patient is intubated and mechanically ventilated, arterial blood gases can be monitored to titrate minute ventilation.
