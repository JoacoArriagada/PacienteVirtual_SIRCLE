# Respiratory Physiology Clinical Correlation

Environment: Basic simulation lab with full-scale human patient simulator.

# Case 2: Hypoventilation

# Case Narrative

A. Background: One day after a knee operation this otherwise healthy patient has become confused and sleepy.   
B. Initial Conditions: Slow and deep respiratory pattern, eyes are closed, pupils pinpoint.   
C. Scenario Progression: None.

# Instructors Notes

The patient is barely arousable to verbal stimulation, but he also ``passes'' his ABC's. If mask ventilation or laryngoscopy is attempted, he fights against the effort.
