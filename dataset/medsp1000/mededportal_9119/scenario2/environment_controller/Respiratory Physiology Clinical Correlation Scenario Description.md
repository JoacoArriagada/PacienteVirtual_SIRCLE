# Respiratory Physiology Clinical Correlation

Environment: Basic simulation lab with full-scale human patient simulator (HPS in our case).

# Case 2: Hypoventilation

Scenario

Opioid-induced hypoventilation

# Case Narrative

A. Background: Students are informed by the instructor: One day after a knee operation this otherwise healthy patient has become confused and sleepy.   
B. Initial Conditions: Slow and deep respiratory pattern, eyes are closed, pupils pinpoint.   
C. Scenario Progression: None.

# Instructors Notes

The patient is barely arousable to verbal stimulation, but he also \`\`passes'' his ABC's. If mask ventilation or laryngoscopy is attempted, the patient fights against the effort. Supplemental oxygen produces improvement in oxygenation over several minutes. ABG reveals elevated PaCO2. Naloxone produces improvement.
