# Pediatrics Scenario 1 – Asthma/Status Asthmaticus

Objectives:

1. Recognize status asthmaticus.   
2. Manage respiratory failure associated with asthma, including intubation and ventilation of patient.

Scenario: 5 year old male asthmatic presents with a 2 day history of URI symptoms that worsened abruptly 6 hours ago with onset of wheeze. Mom gave him 2 albuterol nebulizer doses and then ran out of medications. Child is in marked distress.

Further history if asked: Chronic asthma, 2 PICU admissions without need for intubation. Last admission was last year. He was on fluticasone propionate (Floven $\textsuperscript { \textregistered }$ ) but mom stopped it last month because he was “doing better”.

Past medical history is unremarkable. Meds: Albuterol MDI   
No allergies.

State the following: “The child had increased work of breathing and cannot finish a sentence. He is moderately agitated.”

Physical Exam: This information is for the instructor(s) – most of the exam will be obtained from examination of the mannequin. The mannequin will be started in a sitting position. Note to the trainee that the patient is not cooperative if approached.

T 37.5 P 100 RR 40 BP 105/70 $\mathbf { S } _ { \mathrm { a } } \mathbf { O } _ { 2 }$ : $9 1 \%$ on room air (Information available from mannequin   
via exam or monitor)   
Wt: estimated $2 5 \mathrm { k g }$ (only if asked) Airway: Patent   
Breathing: Flaring with intracostal retractions. Poor air movement on auscultation. Circulation: tachycardic
