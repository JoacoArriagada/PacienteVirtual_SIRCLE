# Case History:

Mr. Stevens is a 73-year-old male who presents to the emergency department after “passing out.” Earlier today, while sitting on a bus on his way into town, he felt weak, dizzy, and was noted by his wife to collapse to the floor. According to his wife, he appeared to be unconscious for less than 1 minute. She states that when he was arousable, he seemed confused for just a few moments. No seizure activity or urinary incontinence was noted during this episode.

Upon arrival at the hospital, the patient is awake, answering questions, but stills complains of feeling weak and lightheaded.

Past Medical History: HTN, hypercholesterolemia

Medications: Metoprolol, diltiazem, pravachol

Allergies: None

Pertinent Review of Symptoms: Denies chest pain, shortness of breath, nausea or vomiting

# General Appearance

He is awake, alert and able to answer your questions.

Skin – Cool and clammy

# Examples:
