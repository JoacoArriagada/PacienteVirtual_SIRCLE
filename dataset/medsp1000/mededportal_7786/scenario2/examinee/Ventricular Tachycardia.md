# Student Instructions

This problem based learning exercise is designed to be interactive and will be presented as an unknown case.

At the beginning of each session, 3 students will be chosen to examine Sim Man while the other students observe the encounter in the simulation conference room. After the initial portion of the case, all students and the clinical and basic science preceptors will reconvene in the conference room for review of the key aspects of this case.

# Keys to enhancing the simulation exercise:

When examining Sim Man – interact with the patient as if you were caring for a real patient.

For this case, 3 students will be asked to examine the Sim Man. Together, you should perform an abbreviated medical interview focusing on key aspects of the history of present illness and obtain any additional relevant data that you feel is necessary.

Other aspects of the physical examination that can not be simulated can be determined simply by asking.
