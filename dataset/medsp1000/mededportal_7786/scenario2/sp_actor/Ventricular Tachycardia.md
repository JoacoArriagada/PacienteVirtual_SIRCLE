# Case History:

A 73-year-old patient presents to the emergency department with a complaint of chest pain and dizziness after passing out. These complaints started about 30 minutes before arrival while the patient was waiting in line at the bank.

The patient recalls feeling warm and flushed and was noted by a bystander at the bank to have a brief fainting episode.

Upon arrival at the hospital, the patient is awake, answering questions, but complains of feeling weak, chest heaviness and shortness of breath.

Past Medical History: HTN, diabetes

Medications: Clonidine, diltiazem, metformin

Allergies: None

Pertinent Review of Symptoms: Denies headache, nausea, vomiting, or abdominal pain

# General Appearance

The patient is awake, alert and able to answer your questions.

Skin – Cool and clammy

# Possible challenge statements from the patient

# Examples:

Doctor, what do you think is wrong?

Why did I pass out?

What did my ECG show?

Do you think I will be able to go home today?
