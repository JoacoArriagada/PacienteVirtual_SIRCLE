# Otitis Media Case: Assume you are in the first months of your internship in pediatrics, emergency medicine, or family medicine.

Yesterday afternoon you saw Johnny, a 25 month old boy who came in with 1 day of runny nose, low grade fever and pulling on his left ear. He was otherwise healthy and fully immunized. Your exam, confirmed by your attending, was consistent with a left ear infection, and he improved after symptomatic treatment in clinic.

You discussed a non-antibiotic treatment plan with Johnny's mother, and she left the clinic seemingly satisfied by this approach.

This morning, you receive this email:

Dear Dr. Smith and Dr.

I am extremely disappointed in you and your clinic by your failure to treat my son's ear infection. He is still sick this morning. My older children always got antibiotics for their infections. You can call in a prescription to the Downtown Pharmacy at 555- 7654. If you don't give me the antibiotics we will take him to a doctor who will. I will check my email later this morning for your response.

John Sr.

Immediately afterward, your continuity clinic advisor (Dr. Smith) sends you an email

Dear

Please respond to this father about the recommended treatment of his son.

Dr. Smith

Please respond to this parent in a way you think would be most appropriate in box 1.

In box 2, communicate with the continuity clinic advisor.
