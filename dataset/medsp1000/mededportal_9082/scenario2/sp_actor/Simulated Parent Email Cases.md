# Strep Throat Case

Two days ago, Daisy, a 7 year old girl, came in with three days of sore throat, fever and headache. A rapid strep screen was negative. Her father was told that Daisy most likely has viral pharyngitis and that no antibiotics are necessary at this time, but that a second swab was being sent for culture.

Today, Daisy’s throat culture comes back positive for Group A Strep. Antibiotic treatment is started.

Dear Dr. Smith and Dr.

My husband just told me that Daisy has strep throat. I don’t understand why this was missed during her clinic visit two days ago. I am very upset because I just read on www.website.com that strep throat can cause kidney disease and permanent heart disease. You should have treated her two days ago when you saw her if you thought there was a chance she had strep throat. If you don’t know how to treat strep throat, maybe I should find a doctor who can.

Jane Doe
