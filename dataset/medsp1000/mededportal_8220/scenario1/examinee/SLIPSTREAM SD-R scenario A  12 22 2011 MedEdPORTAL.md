A. Lab Set Up – ED Critical Care Room / Procedure / Sedation Room in situ

IX. Case Narrative (describes what the learner will experience)

A. Scenario narrative: The patient is a healthy adult who has sustained an isolated recurrent left shoulder dislocation from raising his arm up while reaching for a box. There is no other trauma / secondary injury and no significant past medical history. Due to his prior experiences with shoulder reductions, he insists on procedural sedation prior to reduction attempts.

As the orthopedic consultant is ready at the bedside and the simulation sets the scenario in a busy period for the ED, the study subject has been recruited to conduct an EDPS to help reduce the shoulder. For the purposes of the study, there are no additional providers available to provide assistance for this sedation session.

“You have been asked to sedate Mr. George Anderson, who is a 32 year-old man with a recurrent left shoulder dislocation sustained while raising his arm. The patient is requesting sedation due to prior reduction experiences. You walk in to the Critical Care Room, where the orthopedic consultant and an ED RN are present for the shoulder reduction. Please begin your assessment and

management of the patient.”
