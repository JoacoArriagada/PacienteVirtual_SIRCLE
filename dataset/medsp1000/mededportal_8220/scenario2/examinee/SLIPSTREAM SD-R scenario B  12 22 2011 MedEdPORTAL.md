II. Case Narrative (describes what the learner will experience)

A. Scenario narrative: The patient is a healthy adult who has sustained an isolated recurrent left shoulder dislocation from raising her arms up while stretching. There is no other trauma / secondary injury; she has moderate asthma requiring inhalers and occasional steroids, but has never been mechanically ventilated. Due to her prior experiences with shoulder reductions, she insists on procedural sedation prior to reduction attempts.

As the orthopedic consultant is ready at the bedside and the simulation sets the scenario in a busy period for the ED, the study subject has been recruited to conduct an EDPS to help reduce the shoulder. For the purposes of the study, there are no additional providers available to provide assistance for this sedation session.
