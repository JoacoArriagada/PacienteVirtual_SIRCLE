Notes on flowchart:

1. \*Consults Surgeon\*: The surgeon “on the phone” should inquire about anticoagulation/ thrombolysis/vasodilation, NGT and antibiotics and whether they were completed. The surgeon agrees to see the patient either way. The participant may call general or vascular surgery.

2. ^Encourage participant to disposition patient^: Any call made to a non-surgeon (including interventional radiology) should be answered with “What did the surgeon say?”.

3. #Treatment#: The participant may vocalize the need for heparin, vasodilator, intra-arterial thombolysis or antibiotics.

4. Participants may obtain portable EKGs, CXR or Abdominal XR without a bad outcome at any point in the flowchart

5. Participants may give a fluid bolus but this does not change the vitals, the complaint or the physical exam.

6. All physical exam reassessments yield the same “New Physical Exam” found above Patient should be intubated if they code.

7. If the surgeon is called prior to the CT, the surgeon should respond with “What did the CT show?” or “I’m not taking an unstable patient to the OR. Stabilize the patient, get a CT and call me back.”

8. Participant may initiate fluids, NGT, antibiotics at any point in the algorithm.
