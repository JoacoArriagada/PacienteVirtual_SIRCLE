**Case #1**

*Cases represent amalgamations of real-world patient care scenarios and do not necessarily reflect individual patient narratives.*

Armani was delivered at term after an unremarkable pregnancy. Jada had normal prenatal care including ultrasounds and lab work. During Armani’s second day of life, she develops jitteriness and hypertonia. She is hypoglycemic but her symptoms do not resolve with dextrose gel and normalization of her blood glucose. She is transferred to the neonatal intensive care unit for further evaluation and treatment. Jada denies opioid or other substance use during pregnancy.
