**Case #4**

*Cases represent amalgamations of real-world patient care scenarios and do not necessarily reflect individual patient narratives.*

Li is delivered at term after a pregnancy notable for an absence of prenatal care. Li’s mother, Xiang (pronounced SHYAHNG), reports that she unsuccessfully attempted to make appointments. Given this history, your colleagues order urine toxicology testing on Xiang at time of delivery. Her test is positive for cocaine, and she states that it is a “mystery” to her how this could be possible. Social work reports this to Child Protective Services (CPS) and the CPS supervisor requests testing on Li.
