**Standardized Patient Training Summary**

**Opening Statement:** “I feel just awful. Please give me something to make me feel better.”

**First Position:** Lying in hospital bed in emergency department

**Affect/Physical Symptoms:** You have a sweaty brow and occasional shaking chills. During the physical exam, you may feel a little dizzy and your head hurts when you sit up. If your back is pressed or percussed, you have mild pain. You also have full range of motion in your neck.

**Current Condition**:

You are 28-year-old K. Fletcher. You went to Nicaragua one month ago for your college roommate's wedding and returned ten days later, two and a half weeks ago. You were staying at a hotel by the beach in San Juan del Sur. Seven days after your arrival, you ate seafood at the hotel restaurant. You have not eaten any food from street vendors but ate at the hotel including fish and paella (shellfish). Later that same day, you started to feel sick. You developed fever, chills, and severe body aches that felt like they were in your bones. You have had a persistent high fever. The fevers are not cyclical every few days. The fevers only break when you take ibuprofen or acetaminophen. You have taken your temperature over the past few days and it has been consistently between 101-102 degrees Fahrenheit. If asked, report your pain as a 10 on a ten-point pain scale. You have also felt nauseous and had a generalized, mild headache. The patient also reports night sweats. The patient denies diarrhea or abdominal pain. You have not had much of an appetite and have not wanted to drink much. You feel dizzy and off balance when you walk as if you are going to fall down. You noticed a bumpy, itchy rash on the back of your left arm three days ago, but it has now gone away and you haven't had any other rashes. If asked, you have not been swimming in the ocean or any lakes or rivers; you only swam in the hotel pool.

You report getting mosquito bites while in Nicaragua but deny any tick bites. You did not use bug spray or bed nets while in Nicaragua. You did not have any additional travel immunizations prior to going to Nicaragua and did not take malaria prophylaxis.

When you returned home two and a half weeks ago, you went to a different hospital for these symptoms. You were given IV fluids and had a CT scan of your abdomen and pelvis and a chest x-ray that were reportedly normal. You were then discharged home. You were not treated with any antibiotics.

Other than your recent illness, you have been very healthy and have never been sick like this before. No one else that went to Nicaragua with you has been sick. You have not traveled any place else recently. You did have intercourse with your fiancee while in Nicaragua but not with any other partners. You have never had an HIV test.

**Past Medical History:** None

**Allergies:** None

**Medications:** None

**Vaccinations:** Receives annual flu immunizations, had routine immunizations growing up

**Social History:**

* Occupation: graphic artist for Powell Creative advertising company.
* Marital status: Engaged. In a relationship for six years with a fiancee who is the opposite sex from the standardized patient. Met significant other in college. The significant other went on the trip but is not sick.
* Sexual Status: Sexually active, monogamous, does not use condoms
* Sexual History: 3 prior partners; no history of sexually transmitted infections, never been tested for HIV
* Children: None
* Residence: Neighborhood in Nashville, TN. Moved to Nashville for fiancee to pursue career in music industry.
* Education: BA in graphic design from Lehigh University (Bethlehem, PA). The fiancee's degree is in music composition from the same institution.
* Pets: two dogs, a Boxer (Duke) and a Chinese Crested (Bree) adopted four and three years ago respectively.
* Diet: Does not eat red meat but does eat chicken and fish.
* Caffeine: Two cups of coffee in the morning.
* Smoking: Denies
* Alcohol use: Sometimes has a glass of wine or a beer with dinner
* Illegal drug use: No
* Exercise: Jogs or walks on the treadmill 2-3 times a week for 30-60 minutes.
* Travel: Traveled to Europe at age 22 after graduation from college (England, Spain, France, Italy, Portugal). Took camping trip in the southwest US (Arches National Park) three years ago. Traveled to New York City last winter with fiancee for a long weekend of Broadway shows.

**Family History:**

* Mother: 60, in good health
* Father: 62, has multiple sclerosis but is doing well currently. He was diagnosed when the patient was in high school. He walks with a quad cane.
* Brother: four years younger than standardized patient, has asthma but otherwise in good health.
* Paternal grandparents: late 80’s, living in an assisted living community in Florida
* Maternal grandfather: 82, lives in Columbus, Ohio
* Maternal grandmother: deceased, died 3 years ago at age 75 from complications from pneumonia

**Note: Do not disclose Nicaragua trip unless asked about travel or any recent**

**“unusual” activities.**
