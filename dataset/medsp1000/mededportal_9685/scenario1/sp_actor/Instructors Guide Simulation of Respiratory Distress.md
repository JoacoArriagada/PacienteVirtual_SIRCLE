Simulation Portion

3. Case Scenario: You are seeing a patient who was just admitted to the floors. She came in through the ER and your intern got sign out but is now busy with another patient.

4. The case prompt should be enough to get the students started. Only give the included information that is asked for by the students.

1. History: This is a 21 month old girl with no past medical history who has been having a runny nose and cough for the past day. This morning she began wheezing and her father thought she was having trouble breathing. She had a fever at home to 101 and she was not eating or drinking as much as usual. She has never been sick before, she was born full term, has all of her vaccines and has no allergies. In the ER, she got some “medicine” but dad doesn’t know what it was.
