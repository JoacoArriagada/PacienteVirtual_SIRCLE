1. **Environment**
   1. **Lab Set Up:** Standard simulation room with one-way mirror. Directors may participate in multiple roles (i.e. nurse, EMS provider, cardiology consultant). Simulation participants may consult various services via telephone in the simulation room.
   2. **Manikin Set Up:** A standard simulation doll is required for this simulation. Basic programming allowing for control of essential vital signs and cardiac monitoring is essential to the realism of the scenario.
   3. **Props:**
      1. Resuscitation bay with standard resuscitation equipment (i.e. intubation tools, bag-valve-mask, IV equipment).
      2. EKG machine or ability to project and/or hand the participant the necessary EKG
      3. CXR display device (i.e. PACS machine, hard copy of relevant CXR)
      4. Medications
2. **Actors:**
   1. **Roles**
      1. Paramedic: The paramedic should radio ahead stating that he/she is bringing in a patient with pneumonia from a nursing home. The patient is delirious and it is difficult to obtain further information from the notes sent from the nursing home. He relays however that the patient had one episode of blood-tinged sputum.
      2. Nurse (RN): The nurse should do what the participant asks for but constantly remind the participant of the ill appearance of the patient and that the ED is quite busy. He/she should be helpful however will not contribute towards diagnosis.
      3. Patient: Manikin.
      4. MICU Consultant: Will be available via phone. Will not see the patient and/or the CXR or CT-A (if ordered) but will continually defer to the judgment of the provider. Will accept the patient to the ICU if either diagnosis (pneumonia or PE with infarction) is conveyed.
3. **Case Narrative:**
   1. **Scenario Background**
      1. **Paramedic radio call:**
         1. Bringing you an 86-year-old female from the nursing home. She is protecting her airway and grumbles but does not use words appropriately. As per the nursing home we are transporting for pneumonia. The patient has been coughing for 3 days and had an episode of blood tinged sputum.
         2. Past Medical History: high cholesterol, hypertension, dementia, CVA
         3. Past Surgical history: Cholecystectomy, Hernia repair
         4. Family History: None
         5. Social History: Nursing home resident. No toxic habits.
         6. Medications: Aspirin, Aricept, HCTZ, “statin”
      2. **Initial patient encounter:**
         1. No obvious distress. Occasionally coughs. Essentially non-verbal. Responds to questions with moans and/or incoherently.
      3. **Positive Review of Systems (From EMS)**
         1. Cough
         2. Fever
         3. Episode of blood tinged sputum
         4. All else unable to assess due to patient’s mental condition
      4. **Physical examination:**
         1. BP 170/90, HR 110, RR 20, T 37, O2: 89% (will improve to 95% with supplemental O2)
