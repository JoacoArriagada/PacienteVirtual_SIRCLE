# Cancer Screening for Transgender Patients

# Case 2

Mr P is a 42 Y transgender male who underwent prior bilateral mastectomy and has been on testosterone therapy for over 10 years. He has not undergone TAH/BSO. He has not received any pap smears in the past 10 years. His family history is negative for cancer. He is interested in hearing what cancer screening he's due for.

# Case 2 - Asking the questions

- What are your preferences when having sex with your partner? Mr P responds that he has receptive anal and vaginal sex with his male partner.

- Don't pass up the opportunity to ask about protection! Mr P says his partner uses condoms 100% of the time.

Mr P agrees to a pap smear today, which you complete without difficulty.
