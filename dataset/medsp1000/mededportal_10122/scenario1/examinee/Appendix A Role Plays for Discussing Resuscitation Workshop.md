**Role Plays for Teaching Residents to Discuss Resuscitation with Hospitalized Patients**

**Role Play for “Teaching Residents to Discuss Resuscitation”**

The patient is a 74 year-old man who presents to the hospital with symptoms of worsening dyspnea and acute confusion.

The patient has severe underlying COPD and has required continuous oxygen therapy at 2L via nasal cannula.

He has been admitted two other times in the past four months for AECOPD.

He is sleepy but arousable, although when aroused he is confused.

He is admitted to the MICU in an improved but still critical condition.

He has two adult children, a daughter Sara, who he has lived with for the past several years and a son John, who lives out of state.

After each of his last hospitalizations he was released home with his daughter with his clinical condition worse each time.

**The physician’s goal is to establish a clear plan of care including decisions about resuscitation.**
