1. Pediatric trauma
1. Environment
    1. Lab set up – A METI PediaSim will be placed on an ED stretcher. The scenario takes place in a tertiary care trauma pediatric- capable emergency department
    2. Manikin Set up – The PediaSim will be lying on a stretcher with C-Collar in place and fully clothed.
    3. Props - A stethoscope will be needed. A cardiac monitor with leads, blood pressure cuff, pulse oximeter, supplemental oxygen by nasal cannula, supplies for starting an IV and/or intraosseous line, and simulated emergency release blood, will be needed.  In addition, medications for resuscitation, the treatment of pain, and for rapid sequence intubation should be readily available along with a full spectrum of emergency airway equipment.

1. Actors
    1. Roles
        1. One physician leader
        2. One nurse to administer medications
        3. One medical technician to assist
        4. Other participants can help as directed by the leader
    2. Who can play them – The leader should be played by a resident. Other roles can be played by residents, students, or other people as appropriate,
    3. Action Role
        1. Physician leader – The leader must coordinate and direct the team, obtain a focused history, perform a pertinent physical exam, and manage this pediatric trauma patient.

1. Case Narrative
    1. Scenario given to participants
        1. Chief Complaint – MVA
        2. The physician leader will be given a triage note stating an 8 y/o F arriving by EMS after being a unrestrained backseat passenger involved in a MVA. Vital signs: HR 140 BP 100/65 RR 18, Temp 98
        3. History of present illness (Must be requested): Provided by EMS- The patient was unrestrained sitting in the back seat. Her car was hit while turning left.  The other car was going 35mph.  The patient is crying and says her left upper leg and belly hurt.
    2. Scenario initial conditions:
        1. The leader will be given the triage note above. The patient will be lying on the bed in a c-collar, still clothed, crying. Vitals signs per triage note.
