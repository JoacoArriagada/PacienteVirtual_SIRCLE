## **Scenario 3**

You are the intern taking cross-cover calls for multiple teams overnight. It is 7 PM. You have been called by three nurses regarding three different patients:

(1) The nurse is unable to find a palpable pulse or a “doppler-able” pulse on a patient that underwent surgery earlier in the day.

(2) The nurse reports a patient is acutely short of breath with new hypoxia and now requires supplemental oxygen.

(3) The nurse reports the family of an elderly woman awaiting subacute rehab placement has arrived and would like an update on the plan.

You triage the patients appropriately and see patient #3 last. Brief chart review shows that today is hospital day 17; she was admitted for a hip fracture status post open reduction and internal fixation with what appears to be a complicated postoperative course, but you don’t have time to sift through all the records because the bedside RN pages you again, stating the family is becoming impatient. You arrive to bedside 75 minutes later to meet the family of a patient that you have never met before.
