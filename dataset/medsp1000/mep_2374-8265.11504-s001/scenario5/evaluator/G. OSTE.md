**During this OSTE students will...**

*Professionalism:*

1. Introduce themselves and clearly identify their roles.
2. Truthfully and tactfully navigate a care discussion with a family member whose loved one’s diagnosis is uncertain.

*Communication and Interpersonal skills:*

1. Use open-ended questions including components of the NURSE mnemonic to elicit reasons for and de-escalate a patient’s anger.
2. Use nonverbal skills to indicate support (i.e. sit at eye level with the family member, maintain appropriate eye contact throughout the visit, listen without interruption, etc.).
**Chief Complaint:**

Mr./Mrs. Rogers is a middle-aged patient who was diagnosed 6 months ago with gastric adenocarcinoma after several weeks of chronic nausea, bloating, and epigastric pain. Mr./Mrs. Rogers underwent partial gastrectomy 5 months prior to current presentation and underwent chemotherapy & radiation.

The patient’s chief complaint listed by the Emergency Dept board is intractable vomiting.

Lab review shows the following abnormalities:

* Serum creatinine of 2.6 (baseline ~1)
* Hemoglobin = 7.5 g/dL, Hematocrit = 22.5% (consistent with base-line of last 3 months)

You perform a history and physical examination on the patient. You admit the patient to your team, placing orders for IV fluids & anti-emetics.

Several hours later you are called by the emergency department nurse regarding Mr./Mrs. Rogers, the patient you just admitted. A family member of the patient has just arrived and has concerns. Manage this difficult encounter using skills from the NURSE mnemonic.

**Manage a Difficult Encounter**

**OSTE Rubric**

***Instructions: To be completed by SP at the end of encounter after the student leaves the room.***

**General Description of NURSE Rubric Categories:**

**Excellent**: Student applied the communication tool using suitable phrases and incorporating the patient’s specific needs and context into the response. **Adequate**: Student used the communication tool listed but could have tailored it more to the patient’s specific needs or context.
**Needs Improvement:** Student attempted to use the communication tool but fell into a communication pitfall (see specific cases for examples) or used aggressive/escalating phrasing that contradicted the goals of the NURSE mnemonic.
**Did not complete:** Did not use the communication tool.

| 2. **U**nderstand  **Empathize with/legitimize the emotion without conveying you understand what someone is feeling.** | Contextualizes based on patient perspective. Validates emotion. **General Description of Nonverbal Skills Rubric Categories**

Excellent: Uses this skill 90-100% of the time. Performs the skill in a way that noticeably enhances the conversation.

Adequate: Uses this skill 50-90% of the time. Occasional omissions of the skill do not significantly detract from the conversation.

Needs improvement: Completes this skill less than 50% of the time. Omissions of the skill are notable and significantly detract from the conversation, increasing the sensation of friction/anger.
