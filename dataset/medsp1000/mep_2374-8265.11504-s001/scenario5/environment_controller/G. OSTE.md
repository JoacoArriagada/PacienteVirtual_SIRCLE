**Chief Complaint:**

Sam Rogers is a middle-aged patient who was diagnosed 6 months ago with gastric adenocarcinoma after several weeks of chronic nausea, bloating, and epigastric pain. Sam Rogers underwent partial gastrectomy 5 months prior to current presentation and underwent chemotherapy & radiation.

The patient’s chief complaint listed by the Emergency Dept board is intractable vomiting.

Lab review shows the following abnormalities:

* Serum creatinine of 2.6 (baseline ~1)
* Hemoglobin = 7.5 g/dL, Hematocrit = 22.5% (consistent with base-line of last 3 months)

You perform a history and physical examination on the patient. You admit the patient to your team, placing orders for IV fluids & anti-emetics.

Several hours later you are called by the emergency department nurse regarding Sam Rogers, the patient you just admitted. A family member of the patient has just arrived and has concerns.
