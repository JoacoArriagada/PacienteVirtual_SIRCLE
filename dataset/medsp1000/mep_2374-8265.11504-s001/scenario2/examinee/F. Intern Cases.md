## **Scenario 2**

You are the intern caring for a 13-year-old young man admitted for left unilateral hip pain. He underwent further diagnostic workup early in admission and is being treated with antibiotics for possible chronic osteomyelitis. However, you inform the family that the infectious workup is unlikely to represent true infection. On rounds today, your attending mentioned a growing fear of malignancy but these concerns have not been shared with the family. The daily plan of care for the last 72 hours has been to wait until the final diagnostic read.

You receive a page from the nurse that the mother would like an update on the plan of care.
