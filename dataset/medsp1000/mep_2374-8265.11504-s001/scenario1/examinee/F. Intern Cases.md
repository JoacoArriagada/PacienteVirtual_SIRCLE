## **Scenario 1**

You are the intern taking care of a patient who presented with septic shock secondary to *C. difficile* colitis who is now post-op day #3 following total colectomy. The patient’s hemodynamics have markedly improved. Unfortunately, the patient’s renal function has slowly declined from baseline. You consult the nephrologist who declares the patient is in end stage renal disease and recommends initiation of hemodialysis during this admission. The family understands and agrees to these plans.

Today, a new nephrologist is on service who recommends holding hemodialysis for now in hopes of renal recovery. The new nephrologist tells the patient and spouse this plan.

You walk in the room to update the patient/family about your (the primary team’s) daily plan of care.
