**Scenario 2: Cardiac Arrest**

**Introduction for Learners:**

You are pre-rounding on Mr. Hoffman, a 67-year-old man who was admitted yesterday for left lower extremity cellulitis. Before you enter the room, his nurse tells you that she is glad to see you, because she had been planning to call the intern to let her know that Mr. Hoffman had been complaining of some chest pressure.

**The Rest of the Story:**

The patient is having a myocardial infarction. About one minute after the students enter the room, the patient has a ventricular fibrillation arrest.

**Case Progression:**

* Initially the patient is in bed, not attached to any monitors, and has no supplemental oxygen.
* At the start of the scenario, the patient can be portrayed as a patient with chest pressure.
* About one minute after the scenario begins, the patient has a ventricular fibrillation arrest and can no longer respond.
