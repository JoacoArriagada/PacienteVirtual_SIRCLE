**A child with disruptive behavior**

| ***Case Vignette Part 1 -- Presenting Situation:*** |

First Outpatient Clinic Visit:

Mrs. Blake brings her 12 year old son, Tony to the outpatient family practice clinic. You have been seeing Tony since he was 4 years old. Today, you have a 30 minute appointment with Mrs. Blake and Tony for the following concern: Mrs. Blake read about ADHD online and is convinced that Tony has ADHD and is requesting a referral to a child psychiatrist so that Tony can be prescribed Adderall XR.

Mrs. Blake is worried that Tony is falling behind in academic work. Tony used to be an all A student up until this grade. However, since starting grade 7, Tony’s grades have been falling to Bs and Cs. Although Tony is also having behavior problems in school, Mrs. Blake is most worried about his academic decline. The family has limited financial resources since it is just Mrs. Blake, Tony and his 2 younger brothers in the home. Mrs. Blake has always expected that Tony’s academic success would fund his college career.

Before pursuing a referral, you’d like to find out more about what’s been going on with Tony.
