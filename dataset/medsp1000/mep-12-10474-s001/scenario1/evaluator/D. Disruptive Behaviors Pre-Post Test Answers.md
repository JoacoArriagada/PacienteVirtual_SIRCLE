PBL on Disruptive Behavior Disorders and ADHD

1. Give 1 advantage and 1 disadvantage of using a Vanderbilt form in the assessment of ADHD and DBDs.

Advantages:

1. Assesses for ADHD, DBDs, Anxiety and Mood disorders
2. Available in parent and teacher versions
3. Available in evaluation and follow up versions
4. Free access for clinician

Disadvantages:

1. Longer form requires more time for completion
2. Negative predictive power is greater than positive predictive power
3. Give 1 advantage and 1 disadvantage of using a SNAP IV form in the assessment of ADHD and DBDs.

Advantages:

1. Free access for clinician
2. Short (only 12 questions) form requires limited time to complete
3. Parent and Teacher scores available

Disadvantages:

1. Only assesses for ADHD symptoms
2. Negative predictive power is greater than positive predictive power
3. Only assesses for ADHD and not other mental health disorders
