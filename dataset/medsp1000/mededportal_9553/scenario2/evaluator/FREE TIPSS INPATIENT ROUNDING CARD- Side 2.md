**INPATIENT ROUNDING CARD- NEW PATIENTS**

**Acronym: FREEE TIPSS**

**F=Family and team introductions**—presenter introduces supervising physician, senior resident and on-call intern (if present). If a rounding face sheet is available, this is given to the pt/family

**R=Recap**—presenter gives a succinct HPI, including events leading to admission and ER course if the pt came through the ER

**E=Events**—any pertinent events overnight, or since admission

**E=Extra information**—any pertinent medical, surgical, social or family history, home meds and ROS, not previously mentioned

**E=Exam**—any pertinent exam findings, including vitals and I/Os

**T=Tests**—any pertinent labs or x-rays

**I=Impression**—summary statement including assessment of how the patient is doing

**P=Problem-based plan**—state problem and then plan for the day

**S=Staff input**—ask if the nurse, RT or other staff have anything to add

**S=Summary for patients/parents**—summarize the plan for the patient and family; ask how they feel their child is doing, and if there are any questions

**Total time=5-10 minutes for presentation of simple patients, 10-15 minutes for complex pts.**
