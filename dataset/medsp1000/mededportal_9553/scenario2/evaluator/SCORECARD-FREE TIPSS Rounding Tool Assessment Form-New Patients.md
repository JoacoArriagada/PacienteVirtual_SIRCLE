**Scorecard: Oral Presentation of a New Patient**

**Family and team introductions:**

1. Presenter fails to introduce any members of the team
2. Presenter introduces key members of the team

**Recap:**

1. Presenter fails to mention any key features of the history of present illness (HPI)
2. Presenter gives an HPI, but it either lacks important information, or contains too much information
3. Presenter gives a succinct, accurate HPI

**Events:**

1. Presenter fails to mention any events since admission
2. Presenter mentions either too many or too few events since admission
3. Presenter mentions pertinent events since admission

**Extra information:**

1. Presenter fails to mention any extra information
2. Presenter mentions either too much or too little extra information
3. Presenter gives pertinent details of the past medical history, family history, home medications etc.

**Exam:**

1. Presenter fails to mention any part of the physical exam
2. Presenter gives either too much or too little information
3. Presenter mentions pertinent aspects of physical exam, including vital signs and I/Os

**Tests:**

1. Presenter fails to mention any test results
2. Presenter mentions test results, but gives either too much or too little information
3. Presenter gives all pertinent test results

I**mpression:**

1. Presenter fails to give an impression
2. Presenter gives an impression that fails to display critical thinking
3. Presenter gives a well thought-out, succinct impression

**Plan:**

1. Presenter fails to give plans
2. Presenter gives incomplete or inaccurate plans
3. Presenter gives a succinct problem-based plan

**Staff input:**

1. Presenter fails to ask for input from RN or other staff members present
2. Presenter asks for input from RN or other pertinent staff members present

**Summary:**

1. Presenter fails to summarize the plan for the family
2. Presenter summarizes the plan for the family, but the summary was lacking in accuracy, efficiency or clarity
3. Presenter summarizes the plan for the family in an accurate, efficient and easily understood manner
