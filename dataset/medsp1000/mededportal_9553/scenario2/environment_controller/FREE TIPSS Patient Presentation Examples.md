**Patient Presentation Examples Using FREE TIPSS Rounding Tool**

<u>**New Patients**</u>

**Example:**

**Recap:** “Martha is a 3-month-old female who was admitted last night from the ER with presumed pyelonephritis.  She was previously well until 2 days ago when she developed fevers up to 102 at home, and complained of back pain.  She was taken to her pediatrician yesterday, was diagnosed with a viral infection, and sent home.  Unfortunately, her fevers continued, and her appetite dropped off.  Then last night she had 2 episodes of nonbloody, nonbilious emesis, and she was taken to the ED.  In the ED, a UA showed many WBCs, positive leukocyte esterase and positive nitrites.  Urine and blood culture are pending.  A CBC showed a WBC of 16K with a left shift.  BMP was normal.  CRP and ESR were not done.  She was given a dose of ceftriaxone and admitted to the floor

**Events:**  “Overnight, she slept well and had no further episodes of emesis.

**Extra information:**  “She has no history of pyelonephritis, has never been hospitalized or had surgery.  Family history is also negative.

**Exam:**  “She was febrile overnight, with a Tmax of 39, otherwise HR, BP and other vital signs were within normal limits.  On exam, she was alert, nontoxic, but in some distress.  She did have some abdominal and flank tenderness.  Otherwise, a normal exam.

**Tests:**  “No additional tests were done other than those already mentioned.  Blood and Urine cultures are no growth this morning.
