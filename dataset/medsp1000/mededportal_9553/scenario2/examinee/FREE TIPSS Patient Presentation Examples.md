**Patient Presentation Examples Using FREE TIPSS Rounding Tool**

<u>**New Patients**</u>

**Example:**

**Family and Team Introductions:**  “Hello, Mrs. Anderson, we are the general pediatrics team.  This is Dr. Jones, our attending, Dr. Johnson, our senior resident, and the rest of our team.  We are going to discuss Martha’s case and then we’ll summarize for you at the end.  If you have any questions, or would like to add anything, please chime in.

**Recap:** “Martha is a 3-month-old female who was admitted last night from the ER with presumed pyelonephritis.  She was previously well until 2 days ago when she developed fevers up to 102 at home, and complained of back pain.  She was taken to her pediatrician yesterday, was diagnosed with a viral infection, and sent home.  Unfortunately, her fevers continued, and her appetite dropped off.  Then last night she had 2 episodes of nonbloody, nonbilious emesis, and she was taken to the ED.
