# Simulation Interest Group Scenario Template

Title: Mass Casualty Triage and Tagging Scenario in the Pre-hospital Setting

II. Target Audience: Medical students, nurses, paramedics, or residents

IV. Environment

A) Lab Set Up – A mock multiple car trauma scene on a highway   
This can be set up in a large indoor or outdoor area   
Victims of the ‘car crash’ can be set up setting in ‘chairs’ to simulate seats in a vehicle, or lying on the ground next to the vehicle.

VI. Case Narrative (describes what the learner will experience)

The Scenario: You are students doing a ride along with EMS (Emergency Medical Services) when the rig you are in is the first to respond to a 7 car pileup on 81. You are instructed to assist the EMS personnel in the initial assessment of the victims.

The Victims:

In CAR #1 (two people):   
Actor #1: Forehead Gash with bleeding from site (driver)   
a. Is confused   
Actor #2: Broken clavicle (and visual ecchymosis) from seat belt (passenger)   
a. In pain but is more concerned about the driver’s forehead gash   

In CAR #2 (three people):   
Actor #3: Unconscious (driver)   
a. Slumped over the wheel. Eyes open.   
Actor #4: Ejected passenger (from front seat)   
a. Lying on stomach half inside and half on the hood of the car. Does not interact, answer questions, or follows commands. Stares blankly and exhales “irregularly”.   

Actor #5: “The Distractor”

a. Seems fine (a few scrapes). But physically is dragging a student to take care of Actor #4   
b. Won’t let the student examine him/her   

In Car #3 (single person):   
Actor #6: Posterior Hip Dislocation (driver)   
a. Sitting in driver’s seat with knee pushed against dashboard, complaining about right sided hip pain.   
c. Is pinned and cannot move. Is concerned but not hysterical or aggressive.   

In Car #4 (single person):   
Actor #7: Wanderer   
a. Actor is found wondering around, confused, and will not communicate verbally.   

In Car #5 (single person):

Actor #8: Traumatic Amputation of Arm   
a. Actor is found holding their arm in their car. In state of emotional shock.   
c. Is “out of it” at first, but eventually begins to yell “help” softly, then progressively louder until screaming “OMG MY ARM!!!”   

In Car #6 (single person):   
Actor #9: Whiplash   
a. Complains of neck pain   
c. Not hysterical. Wants rescuers to help the other victims.   

In Car #7 (single person):   
Actor #10: Bowel Evisceration   
a. Complains of belly pain. Upset and protective of abdomen.
