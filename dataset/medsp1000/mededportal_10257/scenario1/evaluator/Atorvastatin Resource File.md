# Opening Statement for SP:

"The hospital gave me this prescription but I have not filled it yet. I have some questions I want to ask before I start taking it."

# Challenge Question/Statement for Students/Learners:

When the student tells you to take this daily, ask:

"My spouse is on Zocor and was told to take it at bedtime. Shouldn't I take mine at night too? Aren't these drugs in the same family?"

o Student should respond: "Cholesterol synthesis occurs while we sleep (2:00am is peak time). Simvastatin has a short life in the body so taking simvastatin at bedtime allows it to have a greatest effect to work while you are sleeping. However, atorvastatin stays in the body for a longer period of time; therefore, it can be taken at any time with the same efficacy."

If the student tells you to avoid grapefruit juice, ask:

"Does it matter if I drink orange juice everyone morning? I love my orange juice everyone morning." o Student should respond: "Orange juice is okay. The interaction is only with grapefruit juice."

If the student asks: How often do you take colchicine for gout attacks? It interacts with the new medication.

"I have an attack about every 3 months. The last one was two months ago. What type of interaction is it?" o Student should respond: "Using these together can increase your risk of muscle pain from the atorvastatin."
After explanation, respond: "Okay, next time I go to my primary doctor, I will be sure to tell him I am on this new medication."

# Any Other Questions:

1. When the student starts to close the encounter and asks if you have any questions, say if they have not already addressed the interaction with colchicine:

"I am always very careful to ask about drug interactions, since I am taking a lot of medications now. Do any of them interact with this new medicine?"

If student says "No interactions", say: "Good because I get gout attacks frequently and have to take colchicine and just wanted to make sure." [Do not pass them on this checklist item.]
If student says "Yes to interactions- colchicine", say: "Well, I just took that 2 months ago for a gout attack. I will be sure to tell my doctor the next time I have an attack to get a different medication since there is an interaction with colchicine." [Pass student on this checklist item.]

2. When the student starts to close the encounter and asks if you have any questions, say:

"How long will I have to take this medication?" Student should respond: "For the rest of your life."

How do statins work in CAD? 1

Inhibit HMG-CoA reductase (rate limiting step of cholesterol synthesis) o decrease LDL by up to 60% o decrease TG by up to 40% o increase HDL by up to 10% o Pleiotropic Effects Improved Endothelial Function Plaque Stability Anti-inflammatory Properties Decrease Lipoprotein Oxidation Decrease Platelet Aggregation

How does atorvastatin interact with colchicine? 2,3

Colchicine may increase the serum concentration of Atorvastatin.

o Category D (Consider therapy modification)
o Severity- Major
o Reliability Rating- fair
o Patient Management- Carefully monitor for signs of muscle pain and/or weakness with concomitant therapy.

What is the interaction with grapefruit juice? 2,4

Mechanism: Grapefruit juice decreases the activity of the cytochrome P450 3A4 (CYP3A4) enzymes that are responsible for breaking down many drugs and toxins. Grapefruit contains compounds known as furanocoumarins that block the CYP3A4 enzymes. When grapefruit juice is consumed, the enzyme's ability to break down the drug for elimination is decreased. Blood levels of the drug may rise, resulting in the risk for new or worsened side effects.

o Will taking medications at different times reduce interactions?

o No, The effects of grapefruit juice on certain medications can last for over 24 hours. So, even if the medicine is taken only once per day, grapefruit and grapefruit juice should still be avoided for the entire treatment period.

o Orange Juice have the same interaction?

o Other kinds of fruit juice may rarely interfere with medications, but for most medications, orange juice, or other kinds of juice like apple or grape, can be consumed instead of grapefruit juice.
