**Title:** Using a Standardized Patient to Counsel on a New Prescription for Atorvastatin

**Keywords:** Statin, myocardial infarction, communication, cardiology, counseling, atorvastatin, pharmacology, simulation

**Part A**

**Purpose of materials:** The case was developed to allow students to apply the knowledge learned in a pharmacology course regarding the use of statins after a myocardial infarction. The goal was to create a real-life scenario to counsel on statin indication, mechanism of action, adverse effects and drug/food interactions.

**SMART Objectives:**

• To counsel a patient on statin indication, mechanism of action, adverse effects and drug/food interactions.

• To apply the knowledge learned in a pharmacology course regarding the use of statins after a myocardial infarction.

• To effectively communicate with patient regarding new prescription counseling.

**Part B**

Identifying Case Factors

* Presenting complaint
  + Patient has concerns regarding a new prescription received at discharge after suffering from a myocardial infarction requiring stent placement.
* Length of patient encounter
  + Prep time = 5
  + Encounter = 10
  + Debrief = 20-30 minutes
* Learning objectives/case purpose
  + To apply the knowledge learned in a pharmacology course regarding the use of statins
  + To correctly counsel a patient on new statin therapy
  + To become more familiar with performing cases in the clinical skills center
* Diagnosis - Myocardial Infarction
* Type of case:
  + Teaching
  + Communication

Case Summary/SP Training Notes

* SP demographics: name, gender, age range, ethnicity
  + Alex Jones, gender neutral, 52 year old, ethnicity not specified
* Setting (i.e. clinic, ER, etc.)
  + Outpatient cardiology clinic
* History of present complaint
  + Alex Jones is 52 and recently suffered a heart attack requiring stent placement. He was diagnosed with CAD and was referred to the cardiology clinic for follow-up. You have already performed a full assessment of the patient. Before leaving the room, Jones hands you a prescription he received in the hospital. He has not gotten it filled because he has questions about the medication.
* Past medical history
  + Hypertension, GERD, Cholecystectomy in 2006
* Family medical history
  + Father had a stroke at age 65. Mother diagnosed with hypertension at age 50. Brother diagnosed with hypertension at age 45
* Social history
  + Married with 2 kids. District Attorney with private insurance.
* If identified as a communication case:
  + Information sharing challenges etc.
    - PROMPT: "How long will I have to take this medication?" - Difficult to tell a patient this is a life-long medication.
    - Identifying drug interactions - discussing with the patient the need to change to a different medication to treat gout flare-ups because the medication interacts with the statin
* Patient presentation (affect, appearance, position of patient at opening)
  + Patient can be sitting in a chair
