**Learner Instructions**

You are working at primary care clinic. A new patient, 4-year old, Johnny, has presented for a sick visit, with the chief complaint of “breathing problems.”

Your objectives:

1. Obtain a history from Johnny’s caregiver.
2. Provide appropriate medical management and guidance as time permits.

\* A physical exam is not required to complete the objectives of this simulation case. You can assume that Johnny’s current exam is within normal limits, and will remain within normal limits for the duration of the case.
