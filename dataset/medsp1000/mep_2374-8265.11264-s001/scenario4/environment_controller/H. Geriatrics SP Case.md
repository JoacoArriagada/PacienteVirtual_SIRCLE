| HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS | You are coming in today for an initial visit with a new doctor. You were recently seen in the emergency room after a fall, and they recommended that you follow up with your primary care doctor. The primary care doctor you had been seeing for many years recently retired. You have chosen to schedule an appointment with a geriatrician. Three weeks ago, you were out grabbing a couple of items at your local grocery store. It was late and dark, and you slipped on some ice. Luckily, you were just bumped and bruised on your right hip, right lower ribs, and right arm. You did not hit your head and did not lose consciousness. You were sore for several days and took over a week to feel better. |
| Falls Risk Assessment | You have not had any other falls “since you were a kid.” You do not have any stairs or rugs at home. You wear your glasses and see your optometrist yearly. If asked, you don’t feel like you have had any issues with balance, dizziness, coordination. You’ve never used assistive devices. |
| Medications | Lisinopril 20 mg daily for high blood pressure x 3 years; Atorvastatin 20 mg daily for high cholesterol x 5 years; plus listed OTCs. |
