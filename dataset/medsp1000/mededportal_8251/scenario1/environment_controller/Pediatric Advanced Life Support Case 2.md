I. Title: Pediatric Advanced Life Support Case 2: A 2 year old becomes unresponsive on the Pediatrics ward Topic: Ventricular Fibrillation (VF) Last modified: 12.2.2010

IV. Case Narrative

A. Scenario Background Given to Participant:
1. Chief complaint, triage note, medic report—You are called to a “Code Blue” on the Pediatrics ward. You arrive to find a 2 year old who is pulseless and not breathing. The nurse caring for the patient tells you this patient recently arrived from the ICU, where she had been treated for congestive heart failure. No one directly witnessed this event.
2. Past medical history—myocarditis, congestive heart failure. Immunizations are up to date.
3. Past surgical history—None
4. Meds and allergies-- digoxin, lasix, and high dose electrolyte supplements. No allergies.
5. Family/social history—Noncontributory
6. Physical exam:

Weight: 12 kg T 36C HR: 0 (VF) BP: Unobtainable RR 0

Airway: Unobstructed Lungs: Absent breath sounds Heart: There is no pulse

7. Labs White blood cell count: 14,000 Electrolytes: Potassium 5, BUN 14, Creatinine 0.45, Glucose 122
