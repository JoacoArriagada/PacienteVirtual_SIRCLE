<u>**Case: M-2 Roy Jones/Behavior Change**</u>

Visit 3

You are seeing Roy Jones for a follow-up visit after a previous heart attack.

Roy Jones is a 55-year-old male.

He continues to smoke 1-2 packs per day. He was given a diet sheet at discharge but has not really followed it. He was instructed to walk daily but has not done any walking.

He has thought about quitting smoking but does not think he can quit and is afraid quitting will cause weight gain.
