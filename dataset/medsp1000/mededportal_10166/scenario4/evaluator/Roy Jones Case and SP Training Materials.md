**SCENARIO**

<u>**Case: M-2 Roy Jones/Behavior Change**</u>

Visit 3

***Why the patient is seeing the student doctor***: You are visiting the doctor's office for follow up.

***Brief Summary of Case*** (Limit to one paragraph): Three months ago you experienced chest pain and went to the ER. The doctors told you that you had had a heart attack. The doctors inserted a tube through a vein in your thigh, found blockage and inflated balloons inside your arteries. You stayed in the hospital for three days during which time further test were completed and the doctors were pleased with your recovery. Over the past 3 months, you have had no more chest pain and generally felt good. You were told to stop smoking but you continue to smoke 1-2 packs per day. You were given a diet sheet at discharge but have not really followed it. You have had no formal nutrition counseling by a physician, registered dietitian nutritionist or other provider.

Visit 3 you will have moved to the Contemplation stage as a result of a positive visit with your doctor at visit 2. You have thought about quitting smoking but you really enjoy smoking a cigarette to relax you. You also got very irritable when you tried to quit after your heart attack and had tremendous cravings. You see that the smoking may have a bad effect on the heart but you don't think you can quit. You are afraid if you quit you will gain weight.

## 3.1. Age: 55

## 3.2. Gender: Male

## 3.4. Affect (Mannerisms, Behavior):

**2nd interview** - You now have mixed feelings about quitting smoking. You realize that smoking is bad for your heart and it can cause emphysema. You enjoy smoking and it helps you relax after a hard day of work in the field. It is also a nice way to take a break during the day. If the student doctor seems to be understanding and acknowledges your concerns, lean forward and be more attentive. Be willing to try Zyban, Nicotine nasal spray or the patch if they are suggested. Be concerned about the cost of the medicine. If it feels like the doctor is "lecturing", cross your arms and be less receptive and emphasis the positive things you get from smoking.

If the student doctor asks about your willingness to change your diet, be willing to negotiate a small change. If the student doctor tries to get you to change everything you eat or drink act overwhelmed. Be agreeable to going to a class or have a visit with a registered dietitian nutritionist to help you with weight management and a heart healthy diet.
