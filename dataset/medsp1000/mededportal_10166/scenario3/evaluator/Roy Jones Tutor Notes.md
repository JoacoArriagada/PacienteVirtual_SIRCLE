## **Tutor Notes**

**Roy Jones: Second and Third Visit**

**Objectives**

1. To learn why, when and how patients decide to change their behavior.
2. To practice effectively promoting the patient's decisional process and support behavior change using an evidence-based patient centered framework
3. To strengthen skills in writing a SOAP note.

1. For the first student interviewer will meet Mr. Jones in the Precontemplation Stage of Change. He actually is a bit resistant to change. The priority should be to help the patient identify a behavior that he is willing to change. The priority should be given to tobacco cessation, however the student could work with the patient on diet and/or physical activity. The student should demonstrate using the 5A's Framework (Ask, Advise, Agree/Assess, Assist, Arrange) demonstrated in the lecture.
2. The standardized patient is unlikely to be willing to make a commitment to change at this visit. The student physician should be able to have Mr. Jones agree to come back in a few weeks for another follow-up appointment.
3. At the end of 45 minutes, have the student report how he/she felt that the interview went.
