## **Tutor Notes**

## **Roy Jones: First Visit**

**Scenario:** Mr. Jones is presenting to the office with an acute Myocardial Infarction.

1. If the examinee asks for physical examination information, provide the listed PE from the case notes.
2. If the examinee requests diagnostic study information, provide the EKG that accompanies the case.
3. If appropriate office interventions are performed, the patient achieves relief of pain, dyspnea, and anxiety, and the rescue van arrives.
