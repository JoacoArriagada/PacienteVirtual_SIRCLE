## **Tutor Notes**

## **Roy Jones: First Visit**

**Scenario:** Mr. Jones is presenting to the office with an acute Myocardial Infarction. Mr. Jones will follow up in clinic with the students in about 3 months for counseling on lifestyle change.

**Objectives:**

1. To perform an appropriate focused interview.
2. To learn how to perform focused testing and intervention (team approach) during the interview.
3. To educate the patient and come to common ground on evaluation and therapy (transfer to Emergency Department with acute cardiac intervention)
4. To communicate the condition of a potentially unstable patient to a receiving physician.
5. To perform a history on a patient in the hospital after an important interventional procedure

**First interviewer:**

1. The student should take an initial history on Mr. Jones.
2. When the student discovers that Mr. Jones is in distress related to his Chest Pain (CP), take time out and discuss what might be going on with Mr. Jones.
3. Ask the students what initial Physical Examination information they need at this time and give them the listed PE from the case notes you are given.
4. Ask the students what diagnostic study should be performed at this time (initially an EKG should be done). Give an EKG which is provided to the interviewing student.
5. As a group, decide what interventions to perform: the goal is to choose the simultaneous and urgent interventions and therapies that should occur in the office setting.
6. After the Hx, PE findings, EKG findings are reviewed and discussed within the group, the student should explain to the patient that he is having a myocardial infarction (MI). In general terms the student should tell the patient his plans and come to common ground with the patient about the plans.
7. If done appropriately, the patient achieves relief (pain, dyspnea and anxiety) with above interventions and is pleased with his care. The rescue van arrives.
8. The student should end the encounter by telling Mr. Jones that a colleague will see him in the hospital after his procedure is completed.
9. The student will communicate information regarding Mr. Jones presenting history, pertinent previous history, PE findings, working Dx, and office based treatment to the receiving physician.
