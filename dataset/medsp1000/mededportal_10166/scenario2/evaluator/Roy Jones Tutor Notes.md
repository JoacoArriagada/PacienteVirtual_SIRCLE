## **Tutor Notes**

## **Roy Jones: First Visit**

**Objectives:**

1. To perform an appropriate focused interview.
2. To learn how to perform focused testing and intervention (team approach) during the interview.
3. To educate the patient and come to common ground on evaluation and therapy (transfer to Emergency Department with acute cardiac intervention)
4. To communicate the condition of a potentially unstable patient to a receiving physician.
5. To perform a history on a patient in the hospital after an important interventional procedure

**Second Interviewer:** In the hospital Cardiac Care Unit (CCU)

1. Explain to the patient that his procedure went well, an angioplasty with stenting to a nearly occluded left anterior descending artery (LAD). There were no other lesions noted. The student may want to describe the coronary anatomy to the patient.
2. The student should take a complete admission history, highlighting Mr. Jones' risk factors for CAD, and the class should determine the extent of an appropriate admission PE. Please provide the students with the likely results of the PE done at this time. Discuss the problem list, the updated Diff Dx, and arrive at an appropriate management plan for Mr. Jones at the time of his admission to the CCU.
3. The student will answer the patient's questions about discharge (3-5 days), walking (tomorrow) driving (one week), work (two weeks providing follow-up reveals good short term and long term prognosis) and sexual intercourse (ten days).
4. If enough time is left in the session, please demonstrate how to write admission orders to the CCU. Write general orders; include enzyme and EKG testing, basic Rx including aspirin and metoprolol.
5. The students' assignment for this session is a write up based on the H&P obtained at the time of Mr. Jones's admission to the CCU.
