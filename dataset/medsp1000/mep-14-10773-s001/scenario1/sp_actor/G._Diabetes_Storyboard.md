**Appendix G: Diabetes Storyboard**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time** | **SP Patient Vital Signs** | **Standardized Patient Responses/Cues** |
| **3 - 10 min**  **Initial Patient Interview** | **Vitals:**  On Door Information Form:  Pulse: 94  BP: 145/85  Respirations: 16  SP02: 99% RA  Temp: 98.8 | **Affect:**  Appears tired and somewhat anxious.  **SP Opening Statement:**  Follows after the SP and physician exchange greetings and the physician asks how she is doing or what brought her to the clinic today. |
| **10 - 12 min**  **Focused Physical Exam** | **Vitals:** Unchanged or are what the physician participant assesses them to be. | **Affect:**  More comfortable but still somewhat embarrassed. However, the embarrassment is within her and discussing her symptoms rather than a function of her relationship with the physician. |
| **12 - 15+ min**  **Discusses Management Plan - End** | **Vitals:** Unchanged or are what the physician participant assesses them to be. | **Affect:**  More comfortable and is interested in what the next steps are. For example she may ask,  *“What do you think is going on?”* (Does not need to be verbatim) |
