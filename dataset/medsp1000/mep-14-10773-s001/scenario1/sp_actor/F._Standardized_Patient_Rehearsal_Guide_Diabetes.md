TENS?* [unsurely] *Something about endocrine problems*.”  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“How about siblings?”*  MRS DEMORO  *“I’m an only child.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Okay and do mind sharing me what your typical day is for your diet”?*  MRS DEMORO  ***“****It’s not low fat is that’s what your getting at.
