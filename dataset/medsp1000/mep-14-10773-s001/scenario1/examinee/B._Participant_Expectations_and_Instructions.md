**Appendix B: Participant Expectations and Instructions**

* The scenario is set in the outpatient clinic where you are working as a physician.
* You will have access to many of the typical tools and items you would normally find in the outpatient clinic. We encourage you to use these tools and items if you decide that you need them.
* Please take your cues from the simulated patient and available diagnostic findings. We encourage you to imagine and engage *as if* you were working in the clinical setting.
* If you would like to request laboratory studies you must ask for them to receive the results; however, this scenario will not be sped up so some laboratory studies may not be immediately available.

**When you are ready to start the scenario you may enter the exam room.**
