Implementation Notes: Scenarios were video-recorded with portable video cameras fitted with removable SD cards. Participants re-watched video-recordings on a laptop in a debriefing room assigned by the simulation lab. Think alouds were audio recorded using a digital audio recorder. Instructions and data collection were conducted by research team members which included simulation center educators. Participants did not change their answers to the PEF following their think-aloud reflection. Implementation details are also described in the educational report article (pgs. 7 - 8).
