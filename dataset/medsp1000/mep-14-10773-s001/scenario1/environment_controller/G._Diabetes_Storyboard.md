| **Diabetes Storyboard** | | | |
| **0-3 min**  **Hand -Off and Scenario Start** | 1. After the participants receive their instructions for expectations and indicate they have no further questions (see participant hand off script below), they are given their doorway information. 2. The participants will have time to review this document and then will determine when they are ready to enter the clinic exam room where the SP will be waiting (in street clothes). This is the start of the live scenario. 3. Note - the scenario will run in real time rather than speeding the time up. We reason that running the scenario in actual time will more accurately allow us to assess the participant's clinical reasoning processes as they might be in the actual clinical setting (i.e., at the same speed as that context). 4. Note - participants will only have access to those diagnostic findings that they seek and perform. Diagnostic findings will not be volunteered without prompting because this will allow us to assess their reasoning processes, including what they prioritize early in their assessment, what diagnostic evidence they use to make choices, and the order in which they ask for and use it. | | |
| **Time** | **SP Patient Vital Signs** | **Standardized Patient Responses/Cues** | **Potential Participant Actions** |
| **3 - 10 min**  **Initial Patient Interview** | **Vitals:**  On Door Information Form:  Pulse: 94  BP: 145/85  Respirations: 16  SP02: 99% RA  Temp: 98.8 | **Affect:**  Appears tired and somewhat anxious.  **SP Opening Statement:**  Follows after the SP and physician exchange greetings and the physician asks how she is doing or what brought her to the clinic today. | 1. Participants should conduct an initial patient interview. 2. The interview should give way to a physical exam. |
| **10 - 12 min**  **Focused Physical Exam** | **Vitals:** Unchanged or are what the physician participant assesses them to be. | **Affect:**  More comfortable but still somewhat embarrassed. However, the embarrassment is within her and discussing her symptoms rather than a function of her relationship with the physician. | 1. May reviews or reassess vital signs. 2. May perform a focused exam. 3. Continues communication with the patient (diagnostic questions, education and counseling). |
| **12 - 15+ min**  **Discusses Management Plan - End** | **Vitals:** Unchanged or are what the physician participant assesses them to be. | **Affect:**  More comfortable and is interested in what the next steps are. For example she may ask,  *“What do you think is going on?”* (Does not need to be verbatim) | 1. May discuss their management plan, including diagnostic testing, referrals to other specialists (e.g., opthamologist). 2. May discuss what they think the most likely diagnoses options are. |
