**Appendix O: Standardized patient implementation checklist for angina**

This form is intended to be used after scenarios are complete in combination with viewing a video-recording of the session. We developed this implementation checklist to help us make decisions about standardized patient (SP) performances that may have been substantially skewed from expectations. This was important to us because we initially used these scenarios for research.

Scoring Instructions:

1. Following each performance study team members viewed the video recordings for each performance.
2. Reviewers were instructed to check off each box that the SP addressed correctly. We did not require SP utterances to be verbatim: the use of the quoted statements are intended to make scoring easier for the reviewer.
3. In the event the physician participant didn’t raise a specific issue (e.g., social or family medical history), we left those items blank and noted “not prompted.”
4. Reviewers were encouraged to keep track of their impressions or any significant deviations in the notes section by recording the time index and the action.
