| **Standardized Patient Rehersal Guide - Angina** | |
| Opening Statement | PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“What brings you in today?”*  PAUL STONE  *“I’ve been getting a burning pain right here doc...” [gestures with open hand over center of chest using a circling motion to indicate that the pain is generally felt to the center of the chest]”* |
| Key Statements | PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“How long has this [chest pain/burning] been going on?”*  PAUL STONE  *“About 3 weeks maybe. I don’t know what it is but it seems like I get winded more easily, too. ”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Was there any kind of pattern to it, like when it occurred?”*  PAUL STONE  *“Anytime I am exerting myself - at least a few times a week. It does get worse if I keep going like when I’m working out.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Does anything make it better?”*  PAUL STONE  “Usually if I stop doing whatever it is that i’m doing it will go away in a few minutes.”  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Can you localize the pain for me? Where in the chest does it hurt?”*  PAUL STONE  *“ It is right at the center of the chest – [uses full hand to gesture to center area of chest consistent with angina]”.*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“When you have the pain, on a scale of 1 to 10, what would you say your pain is?*  PAUL STONE  *“It can go up to 4 or 5 when it’s happening.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“And you do have a history of diabetes? When were you first diagnosed?”*  PAUL STONE  *“ Yes, Type 2 Diabetes. I was diagnosed about four years ago.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Do you take any medications for yout chest pain or for anything else?”*  PAUL STONE  *“I’ve been taking Tums and Prilosec.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“And can you tell me about your family history? Mostly your mom, dad and any siblings?”*  PAUL STONE  *“My father has some unspecified [emphasis] heart problems -- he isn’t very forthcoming when it comes to medical issues. My mother has frequent heartburn and diabetes.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Any recent travel?”*  PAUL STONE  ***“****Yes, I recently flew to San Antonio to attend a funeral for a former colleague.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Can you tell me about how much alcohol you drink on average?”*  PAUL STONE  *“I drink occasionally–4-5 drinks per week.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Do you smoke?” “On average, how much did you smoke a day?”*  PAUL STONE  *“Not anymore - quit a few months ago. But I smoked a pack a day, probably for about 20 years.”*  PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“What’s your typical diet like?”*  PAUL STONE  *“I’m pretty busy, so I eat out a lot. And I probably drink 4-5 cups of coffee a day.”*    PHYSICIAN PARTICIPANT  *“Sounds like you’re pretty active usually?”*  PAUL STONE  *“I haven't been active recently because of my workload. And I got off track from my usual gym schedule. But we have a family vacation coming up, so I’m trying to get back into shape.”* |
