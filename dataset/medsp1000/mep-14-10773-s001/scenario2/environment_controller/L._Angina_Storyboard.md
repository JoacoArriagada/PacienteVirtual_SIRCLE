| **0-3 min**  **Hand -Off and Scenario Start** | 1. After the participant receives their instructions and indicates they have no further questions (Appendix B) they will receive details about their patient in the form of doorway information (Appendix I) with patient details, i.e., reason for today’s visit, vitals, past records (6 mos). 2. The participant will have time to review this document and determine when they are ready to enter the clinic exam room where the SP is already waiting (in street clothes). This is the start of the live scenario. 3. The scenario will run in actual time rather than speeding the time lapses up. We reason that by running the scenario in actual time will more accurately allow us to assess the participant's clinical reasoning processes. Speeding time up may set up a situation in which clinical reasoning could be influenced by time move more quickly than typically experienced in the actual clinical setting. 4. Participants will only have access to those diagnostic findings that they seek and perform. Diagnostic findings will not be volunteered without prompting because this will allow us to assess their reasoning processes, including, what they prioritize early in their assessment, what diagnostic evidence they use to make choices, and the order in which they ask for and use it. | | |
