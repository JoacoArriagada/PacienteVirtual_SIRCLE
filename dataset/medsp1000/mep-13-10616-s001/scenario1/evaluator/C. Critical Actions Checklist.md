**Appendix C: Critical Action Checklist**

1. Recognize that the patient is unresponsive due to opioid overdose
2. Appropriately treat the patient with naloxone
3. Recognize the progressive dyspnea after the naloxone administration
4. Reassess the patient and diagnose pulmonary edema
5. Attempt to oxygenate and ventilate the patient using either a non-rebreather oxygen mask or NIPPV.
6. Recognize that the patient’s O2% saturation is not improving by any means, and perform rapid sequence intubation or awake intubation.
7. Establish lung-protective ventilator settings (e.g., tidal volumes 6-8 mL/kg or even as low as 4-6 mL/kg; rate 14-18; FiO2 100% initially; plateau pressure <30 mmH2O; PEEP 5 mmH2O initially)11
8. Recognize that oxygenation is still poor despite maximizing ventilator settings
9. Initiate advanced ICU management (e.g., prone positioning, advanced ventilator modes, neuromuscular blockage), ECMO procedures per local protocol, or consider transfer to a higher level of care8
