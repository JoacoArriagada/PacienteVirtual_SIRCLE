# Scenario 7: Cardiac Arrest, Code Blue Situation

# Scenario

The patient is an 85-year-old man who had a large anterior myocardial infarction four months ago. Since then, he has had two hospital admissions for congestive heart failure, including this one. He had an ejection fraction of $18 \%$ on an echocardiogram performed two weeks ago, and he really hasn’t felt at all well since his myocardial infarction. He has a past medical history of hypertension for 20 years and type II diabetes mellitus for 15 years. He also has a history of prostate cancer treated with radiation five years ago. A “code” has been called because the patient has been found unresponsive and pulseless. The resident is to lead the code.
