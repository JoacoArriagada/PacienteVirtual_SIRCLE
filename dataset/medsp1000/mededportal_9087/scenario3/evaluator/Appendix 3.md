# NOTE:

 After examining the patient in the hospital room, the resident will come to the residents’ lounge to transfer this patient’s care to you.   
The resident should tell you that s/he must leave the hospital to be in clinic this afternoon and provide details to you so you can assume care of the patient.

# INTERACTION:

 You are sitting at a desk in the residents’ lounge prior to going on duty.   
 You are engrossed in reading a medical journal when the resident comes in to give you the report.   
 At first, you continue to look at your journal as the resident gives you the report.

# REACTION:

If the resident faces you, uses good eye contact, and tries to gain your attention, pay attention and carefully listen to the information.

 When s/he asks for “read back” say,

o “I will follow her closely and check her in 1-4 hours. “ o “I’ll watch for the results of the CT scan.”

Then say, “The way we have to cover for each other in these short time frames can be tough. Sometimes I worry about things getting missed.”

BUT:

If the resident does not gain your attention, say

o “I’ll check on her if the nurses call me.”
