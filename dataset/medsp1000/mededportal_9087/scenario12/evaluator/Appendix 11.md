# Case of Patient 2

# Setting:

Resident with patient in out-patient clinic

# Participants:

Patient: Patient 2 (age 52) (played by a standardized patient)

# Scenario:

Patient 2 is a 52-year-old Caucasian woman who presents in an outpatient clinic at 2:00 PM with no physical complaints. She is well-dressed, neatly groomed, and speaks clearly and articulately. Her vital signs are normal. Her blood pressure is 120/80. She is $5 \ ' 6 \ '$ and weighs 150 lbs. When asked the reason for her visit today, she responds that she has back pain and would like a prescription for pain medications.

# Testing Situation:

There is a patient chart with the nurse’s notes on it (above information). The patient is new to the clinic, having made an appointment the week before, taking the first available opening for non-urgent care. The resident may write on the chart deciding how to handle the patient.

# Desired Outcome

The resident should explore the back pain, determine what medications (over-the-counter and prescription) the patient currently takes, and determine the best course of treatment for her back pain. The resident should uncover the addiction, help the patient establish a care plan, identify resources, and make appropriate referrals.
