# Instructor’s Guide for Eating Fish: Maximizing the benefits and minimizing the risks

Also included are materials for three simulated patient cases designed to allow trainees to practice assessing risk and counseling patients on safe fish eating practices. The cases are appropriate for use with simulated patients. We suggest allowing 15 minutes for each cases when used in a simulated patient exercise.. The patients include an adult male, a woman who is pregnant and a mother discussing her pre-teen son’s health at the end of a new patient visit. These cases cover patients with different risk profiles for eating fish. Each case includes patient training material, generalized scoring guide, references for developing a dietary checklist, a door sign and an information handout students could use as a guide for counseling patients and as well as providing with a patient guide to take home for future reference. The cases could be given after completing the lecture for practicing the material covered in realistic patient care situation. They could be used as assessment tools but probably would work best as an opportunity to practice patient counseling and teaching skills. They can also be useful in small group discussions.

The presentation has been given to $3 ^ { \mathsf { r d } }$ year medical students, residents and practicing physicians and as received positive feedback among attendees at all levels of training. We feel the simulated cases are most appropriate for medical students after they have begun their clinical training. Allowing 15 minutes for each case in our experience has sufficient.
