# Checklist for Case #3

# Patient interview questions

O Asked about how often her son consumes fish i.e. number of meals per week or month.   
O Asked what types of fish consumed.   
O Asked what proportion of her fish meals were wild caught versus store purchased.   
O Asked where the wild caught fish were caught.

# Patient Counseling Issues that should be addressed

O States that the recommend guideline for fish consumption is two servings a week.   
O Cautions that fish may contain potentially dangerous contaminants and she should be cautious about how much fish her son eats and the type and location caught.   
O Notes fish preparation can significantly influence the amount of exposure to certain dangerous contaminants.   
O Cautions children are particularly susceptible to the contaminants in fish and there are more stringent recommendations for fish consumption for children.   
O Provides a handout or informs the patient where they can find more specific information on minimizing the risks of eating fish.

Suggested Scoring

One point per item.
