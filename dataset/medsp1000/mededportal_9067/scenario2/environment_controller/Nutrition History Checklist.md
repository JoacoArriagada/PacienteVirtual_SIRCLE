# Nutrition History Checklist

To streamline the case we suggest having the patient hand the examinee a completed nutrition checklist that they filled out in the waiting room. An example can be found in:

Hark L, Deen D Jr.Taking a nutrition history: a practical approach for family physicians.Am Fam Physician.   
1999 Mar 15;59(6):1521-8, 1531-2.

# Key elements to include on nutrition history checklist for Case #2

Eats several meals a week of fish. Mix of store bought fish high that tend to be high in mercury as well as trout from local streams.
