# Checklist for Case #2

# Patient interview questions

O Asked about how often she consumed fish i.e. number of meals per week or month.   
O Asked what types of fish consumed.   
O Asked what proportion of her fish meals were wild caught versus store purchased.   
O Asked where the wild caught fish were caught.   
O Asked if she took fish oil

# Patient Counseling Issues that should be addressed

O States that the recommend guideline for fish consumption is two servings a week.   
O Notes that there is clear evidence consuming fish reduces risk of complications of cardiovascular disease in those with known coronary heart disease   
O Notes that it is evidence that it is likely consuming fish reduces the occurrence of cardiovascular disease in those currently without coronary heart disease.   
O Cautions that fish may contain potentially dangerous contaminants and she should be cautious about how much fish is eaten, the type and location caught.   
O Notes fish preparation can significantly influence the exposure to certain dangerous contaminants   
O Cautions that her pregnancy makes her particularly susceptible to the dangers from contaminants in fish.   
O Provides a handout or informs the patient where they can find more specific information on minimizing the risks of eating fish.

Suggested Scoring

One point per item.
