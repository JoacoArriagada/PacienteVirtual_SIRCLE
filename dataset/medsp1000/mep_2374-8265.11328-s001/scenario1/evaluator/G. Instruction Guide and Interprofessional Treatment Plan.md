Appendix G: Instruction guide and interprofessional treatment plan

# Breakout Session #2

- Final preparation for standardized patient interview.
- Standardized patient interview.
- Create interprofessional treatment plan.
- Discuss treatment plan with standardized patient.

## Treatment plan structure

- Patient’s health priorities and goals.
- Specific non-pharmacologic interventions, if any.
- Specific referrals or consultations, if any.
- Changes to current medications or addition of new medications, if any.
- Monitoring and follow-up plan.
