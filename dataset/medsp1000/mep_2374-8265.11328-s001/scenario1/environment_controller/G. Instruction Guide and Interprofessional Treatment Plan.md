Appendix G: Instruction guide and interprofessional treatment plan

# Runtime flow

- Review the patient case and the objective findings before the patient enters.
- The standardized patient joins the breakout room for the interview.
- Once the patient joins, begin the patient interview. A physical exam will not be completed.
- The team may ask about the opioid medications taken in the last 24 hours to calculate a morphine equivalent dose (MED).
- Once the interview is completed, the patient exits the breakout room.
- The team completes the interprofessional treatment plan.
- When 10 minutes remain in the breakout session, the standardized patient re-joins the breakout room to discuss the treatment plan.
- If time allows, the standardized patient may give verbal feedback on the team’s interaction.
- Once the discussion with the standardized patient is completed, the patient exits the breakout room.
