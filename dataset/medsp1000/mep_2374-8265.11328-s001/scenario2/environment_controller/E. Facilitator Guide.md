**Appendix E: IPE Session Facilitator Guide**

IPE session overview:

This IPE training is designed to guide interprofessional student teams through patient assessment and care decisions of patient Samantha Jones. The patient was in a car accident three months ago and since then has been taking increasing amounts of opioids with no improvement in pain or function. During the IPE session, interprofessional teams discuss the patient case, screening tool results, and Prescription Monitoring Program (PMP) report; interview a standardized patient; and develop an interprofessional treatment plan and discuss it with the standardized patient.

**SP logistics during breakout room #2**

1. After about 3-5 minutes of starting the breakout rooms, the SP joins the assigned breakout room.
2. After about 20 minutes, the SP exits the breakout room. If the SP does not return within 25 minutes, check the group’s progress and encourage them to move to the next part of the activity.
3. Students then have about 15 minutes to develop a problem list and treatment plan.
4. When 10 minutes are remaining in the breakout session, the SP moves back into the breakout room to discuss the treatment plan with the team.
5. Following discussion of the treatment plan, the SP may provide feedback to the team if time allows, then exits the breakout room.

**Breakout #2 timing**

- Breakout rooms open.
- 3 minutes after starting breakout, SP enters breakout room.
- 23 minutes after starting breakout, interviews should be wrapping up and teams should begin treatment plans.
- 30 minutes after starting breakout, teams should be completing treatment plans and the SP will re-join in 10 minutes.
- 38 minutes after starting breakout, teams should be wrapping up the treatment plan and the SP will re-join in 2 minutes.
- 48 minutes after starting breakout, 2 minutes remain in the breakout room and teams should wrap up.
