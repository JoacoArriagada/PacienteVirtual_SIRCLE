# Single Car MVC Clinical Instructor Checklist

# Clinical Pearls

Airway:

Control airway early (failure of oxygenation, GCS)

Use of lidocaine for premedication given potential head injury

. Have back-up airway plans in mind and verbalize them to the team

Breathing:

Insert chest tube in sterile fashion

Circulation

Volume resuscitation

 Switch IVF to PRBCs given hypotension

sposition:  Hypotensive patients with +FAST need to go to the OR (not to CT)
