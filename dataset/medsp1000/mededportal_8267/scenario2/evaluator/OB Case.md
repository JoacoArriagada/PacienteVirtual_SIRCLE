# Obstetrical Trauma CRM Instructor Highlights

This case is designed to introduce the third CRM principle: Support. It builds on the objectives covered in the previous hours: Leadership, Folowership, Communication and Global Assessment.

# CRM topic: Support

Know when and whom to call for help

Contact Obstetrics prior to patient arrival based on EMS report and mechanism Contact social work upon identification of intimate partner violence

Identify the sources of help available

Utilize radiologist in consultation for counseling of patients regarding radiation risks during pregnancy Utilize social work consult to assist with placement in a shelter after discharge

Ascertain the type of help needed: advice, hands on, specialized equipment

Obstetrics: Need obstetrical hands-on expertise and fetal monitoring equipment Radiology: Need advice or hands-on (optional per Team Leader) Social Work: Need hands-on expertise/patient care

# EMT Report on arrival:

“This is a 20 year old, 32 week pregnant, woman who stated that she fell down 13 stairs onto a concrete floor. She hit the railing on the way down, is having some difficulty breathing, and looks like she has some bruising of her left chest. Her right arm looks broken too. Her latest vitals are a BP of 98/580, pulse of 135, O2 sat of $9 5 \%$ on a NRB, and a RR of 30. We were able to get a $2 0 \mathbf { g }$ IV in her left arm and she's gotten 200cc of saline so far. Also, her boyfriend was pretty pissed off at us when we were there and demanded to come with us but we told him he'd have to find his own way here so he's probably on his way. # Further History:

OB history: G1P0, singleton, poor weight gain, poor obstetrical care due to access issues (no ride, no money, no insurance). OB will initiate fetal cardiac monitoring without prompting.

Intimate Partner Violence: If asked specifically, patient will disclosed that she did not fall down the stairs but was instead grabbed by her right arm and pushed to the floor from standing and then kicked on her left chest in left flank. He often grabs her by her arms and punches her in various places. If offered, she is interested in speaking to social work about a shelter and does not want her boyfriend allowed in to see her.

Radiology Evaluation: Shield abdomen during chest and pelvis, but no other delay. Positive FAST should prompt CT since the low BP resolved with decompression of the IVC. Can discuss risks/benefits with radiology or have them come to talk to patient as well.

# Case Progression:

Vital signs: RR 28, HR 129, BP 94/52, O2 sat $9 3 \%$ NRB

The patient remains clinically stable throughout the case but her voice should be played in an anxious, crying fashion. This patient is demanding of pain medications, explanations, and frequently asks the staff is she is safe there, states that she is scared, and wants to know if “the baby is going to be ok”.

Disposition: To CT and then SICU with OB and Social work involvement
