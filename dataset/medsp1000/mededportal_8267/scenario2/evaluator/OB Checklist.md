# Obstetrical Trauma Clinical Instructor Checklist

# Clinical Pearls

Breathing:

Insert chest tube in sterile fashion

Consider diaphragmatic elevation when deciding on location (ultrasound use optional) irculation

Volume resuscitation, increased requirement given pregnancy Switch to packed red blood cells (PRBCs) given continued hypotension

Disposition:

Priority is the mother – Abdominal/Pelvis CT is necessary given $^ +$ FAST with stable vital signs Patient should be monitored overnight in an Intensive Care Unit prior to transfer to the obstetrical service Social services should be involved due to Intimate Partner Violence
