# Case: Shock - Sepsis (Shock Case #1)

# 3. Patient Scenario

a. Baseline patient information

Age: 84 Sex: F LOC: confused

b. Description of Scenario

An 84-year-old female is brought in by family for increased confusion and lethargy. Son notes that she's not been her usual self. Usually fully functional and lives on her own. But last few days increasing confusion, and sleepy at home.

Denies any know fever. No localized pain. Complains of pain "all over me" and states, "I feel tired" and "I think I'm sick".

e. Moulage and props needed (Obvious physical findings on first sight): None

DM: Insulin
