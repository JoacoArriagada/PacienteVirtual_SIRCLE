# Case: Shock - Sepsis (Shock Case #1)

# 3. Patient Scenario

a. Baseline patient information

Age: 84 Sex: F LOC: confused

b. Description of Scenario

An 84-year-old female is brought in by family for increased confusion and lethargy. Son notes that she's not been her usual self. Usually fully functional and lives on her own. But last few days increasing confusion, and sleepy at home.

Denies any know fever. No localized pain. Complains of pain "all over me" and states, "I feel tired" and "I think I'm sick".

c. Triage notes

a. BP 105/64, HR 115, RR 26, T $3 5 . 8 ^ { \circ } \mathrm { C }$ , SpO2 92% RA

d. Prehospital treatment: None.

e. Moulage and props needed (Obvious physical findings on first sight): None

# 4. Early Problem

Chief complaint / HPI: Increased Lethargy and confusion

PMH:

HTN: Metoprolol
DM: Insulin
Other meds: ASA, Calcium

F/SH: denies smoking, drugs or alcohol use

ROS: No diarrhea, no bleeding, no fever, $^ +$ urinating more than usual (only tell if student asks this), no history of fall or hitting head.

Mental status: alert to self, does not know where she is, and thinks it is 1990
Pupils: Equal and reactive
Cardiac Monitor: Sinus Tachycardia
HR: 115 bpm
BP: 105/64 (first), 80/60 (repeat)
RR: 26 bpm
SpO2: $9 2 \%$ RA, $9 6 \%$ on $\mathrm { O } _ { 2 }$
Temp: $3 5 . 8 ^ { \circ } \mathrm { C }$
Lungs: Clear bilaterally
Abdomen: normal
Bowel Sounds: Present
Extremities: Warm & Pale, with decreased blood pressure

5. If the patient improves:

o If give appropriate volume of IVF (NS or LR), BP will improve to 106/70, HR will also improve somewhat

6. If the patient worsens:

o Worsening Blood Pressure (decreased BP to 80/60 and lower if not treated with fluids and/or pressors)
o Worsening Mental Status
