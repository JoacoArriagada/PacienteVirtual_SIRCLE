**SCENARIO 1:**

**Pediatrician:** You are seeing Jon, a patient you know well who has mild autism but has been struggling in school for the last two years. He is currently in the 7th grade. You want to discuss with the parents, who are both at the appointment, about the possibility of obtaining an IEP, how to obtain it and what it will mean for Jon.

**Parent:** You are one of Jon’s parents. Jon has mild autism but has been struggling in school for the last two years. You are frustrated because the medication has not helped his grades and you are not sure what else to do. You are also overwhelmed with three other kids at home and have a busy job. When the pediatrician discusses an IEP, you are apprehensive at first, but then are willing to give it a try, for Jon’s benefit.
