**Making the diagnosis and treating the patient**

Mom is really concerned about his cough and wants to know what you can give for it. She also read on Wikipedia that this is the exact same presentation as a child who died of MRSA pneumonia. That really concerns her too.

PMH: mild eczema, immunizations up to date except for Flu

Mom wants to know what Jack’s diagnosis is and what you are going to do about it.
