**#3 Obstructed Labor**

**Background:**

You are engaged in work related to your doctoral research project in a rural district in Sierra Leone. Using a community-based intervention strategy, you are partnering with a community health worker (CHW) to increase treatment of malaria, and increase polio vaccination rates amongst children in the village. You are facilitating an academic-community partnership; while engaged in clinical work, you hope to establish a trusting relationship with the community in order to provide a basis for long-term community based participatory research.

In this remote rural village, you and the CHW travel together to provide home visits. Your understanding of the context of your environment is that this is a patriarchal society where polygamy is still practiced, and where females undergo genital cutting- often with significant psychological and medical complications.

**Scene:**

While making home visits, you and the CHW enter a home where a young woman is in labor, in obvious distress. She is attended by her mother-in-law.

2 actors:

**Tenneh** -- community health worker (CHW) who also is a Traditional Birth Attendant (TBA)

**Mamakoh** – patient’s mother-in-law (ML)

Student and Tenneh knock on door and introduce themselves.

**Mamakoh** is distressed. She immediately asks for help. She explains that this is her daughter in law’s (mannequin) 2nd day in labor and that it’s not progressing well. She is so fatigued that she is not even able to talk or drink.
