**#1 Diverted Medications**

**Background:**

You are a trainee working on a medical program in rural Liberia, organized by your home academic medical center. Your institution has been partnering at this site for several years and your colleagues have a good, ongoing relationship with the Liberians who are also working on the project. The primary goal of the project is to help the district level hospital maintain a sufficient inpatient pharmacy formulary. Your institution has been providing funding to obtain the most important medications. Your academic mentor has returned to the US after accompanying you to the site. During the second week of your work helping with the pharmacy inventory, you notice several inconsistencies in the pharmacy records. One of your main duties is to help prevent stock-outs of crucial medications. You discover that the stock of Ceftriaxone IV is considerably less than at the same time last month, and will likely be stocked out in a day or two.

**Scene:**

As you’re checking the inventory and noticing further inconsistencies, you are approached by one of your colleagues, Dr. Alphia, who is a hospital doctor from Monrovia, Liberia’s capital. You are later joined by Dr. Beverly, the medical director of the hospital, who is originally from the rural area where you’re working.

2 actors:

**Dr. Alphia** (hospital doctor from Monrovia – Liberia’s capital),

**Dr. Beverly** (medical director of the hospital who is originally from the area).

Both wearing white coats.

Other characters: supervising pharmacist (Mr. Momolu)
