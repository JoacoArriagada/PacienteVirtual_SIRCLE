**#2 HIV Treatment**

**Background:**

You are assisting an HIV outreach team in rural Malawi. The team’s goal is to visit the most remote villages to do rapid HIV tests on pregnant women. It is known that the majority of pregnant women in these areas do not receive prenatal care or HIV treatment. You bring along rapid HIV tests that can be done in 30 minutes. If positive would be repeated for confirmation. Those who test positive a second time are offered treatment for free, but the patient must go to the nearest clinic monthly to get check ups and their HIV medications. The nearest clinic is a 5-hour walk from home or 30 minutes on motorcycle taxi, which costs $2 dollars each way. The patients are assured that their test results will be kept confidential, as will any other treatment they receive.

**Scene:**

You’re visiting a pregnant woman at her home (Figure). Her name is Blessings. She is in her third trimester. The result of her first HIV test was positive, as was your confirmatory test.
