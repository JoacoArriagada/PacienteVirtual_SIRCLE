**Environment:**

1. Set- Up: An emergency room medical resuscitation bay. The learners may be notified that the patient will be arriving soon, and then the EMS providers arrive with the patient. The learners should identify that there is evidence of foreign body obstruction and instruct the nurse and other participants of what they need, including IV access, radiographs, labs and airway equipment.
2. Mannequin Set-up: High fidelity pediatric simulation mannequin with placement of piece of a pacifier (we used the green hospital pacifiers and cut it to fit into the airway) lodged just below the epiglottitis
3. Props: Laryngoscope, chest radiographs, laboratory results including a blood gas if requested, equipment necessary for placement of a transtracheal jet ventilation, magill foreceps.

Actors:

1. Child’s parent can be played by a case instructor, this person answers the participant’s questions about the history
2. ED nurse: typically played by one of our own peds ED nurses, may also be played by a case instructor. The nurse functions in a typical nursing role,
3. Any consults: the team may place a consult to any subspecialist; pediatric surgery, pediatric otolaryngology, pediatric anesthesiology. To place a consult they may call on the phone in the room, or say out loud to whom they want to consult. We have a phone in the simulation room that they can call a consult on. Typically the consulted team is not available forcing this team to perform the necessary procedures.
