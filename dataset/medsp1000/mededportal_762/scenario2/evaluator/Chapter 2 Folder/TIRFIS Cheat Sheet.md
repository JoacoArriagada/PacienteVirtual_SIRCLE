C.	INSTIGATING BEHAVIOR CHANGE

<table><tr><td><p>Item 7 -- Stages of Change*</p><p>	(5)</p><p>Key Message:  Assesses willingness to change and provides counseling specific to stage of change.</p><p>Key Language:  ARE YOU WILLING TO QUIT?</p><p>Ex:</p><p>Pt states “I’m not sure about quitting.”</p><p>Dr. proceeds appropriately to tell pt. about the risks and benefits of quitting.</p><p>Ex:</p><p>Pt states “I’m ready to quit.”</p><p>Dr. proceeds appropriately by talking about strategies and quit plan.</p><p>Item 8 -- Nicotine Dependence*</p><p>	(5)</p><p>Key Message:  Fully Assesses Nicotine Dependence</p><p>Key Language: </p><p>Doctor asks <strong>3 out 3</strong> of the nicotine dependence questions:</p><ul><li>“When do you smoke the first cigarette of the day?”</li><li>“How many cigarettes do you smoke per day?”</li><li>“Do you have withdrawal symptoms when you cannot smoke?”</li></ul><p>Item 9 -- Behavior Change Technique</p><p>	(5)</p><p>Key Message:  Change Techniques and Quit Date</p><p>Key Language: <strong>OFFERS STRATEGIES TO QUIT and </strong>SETS QUIT DATE</p><p>Doctor employs at least two of the following behavior change techniques and sets a timely quit date and follow-up date.</p><p>Self Monitoring:  Ex. Tells patient to keep track of when s/he smokes and needs a cigarette the most.</p><p>Contingency Contracting/Self Reinforcement:  Ex.   Tells patient to replace cigarettes with carrot sticks.  Tells patient to use the money s/he saves not smoking to buy a new outfit.  </p><p>Stimulus Control:  Tells patient to get rid of cigarettes and lighter, empty ash trays, wash linens, etc.</p></td><td><p>	(4)</p><p>	(4)</p><p> </p><p>(4)</p></td><td><p>	(3)</p><p>Key Language:</p><p>Ex:  </p><p>Pt states “I’m not sure about quitting”</p><p>Dr. inappropriately proceeds with strategies and quit plan without discussing risks and benefits of quitting.</p><p> </p><p>(3)</p><p>Key Message:  Partially Assesses Nicotine Dependence</p><p>Doctor asks <strong>1 out of 3</strong> of the nicotine dependence questions.</p><p>	(3)</p><p>Doctor employs <strong>no behavior change techniques</strong> but may provide brochures and referrals and sets an <strong>inappropriate quit date and follow-up date</strong>.</p></td><td><p>	(2)</p><p>	(2)</p><p> </p><p>(2)</p></td><td><p>	(1)</p><p>There is no determination of readiness to change.</p><p> </p><p>	(1)</p><p>Key Message:  Does not Assess Nicotine Dependence</p><p>Doctor does not ask any of the nicotine dependence questions.</p><p>(1)</p><p>Doctor employs <strong>no behavior change techniques</strong> and sets <strong>no quit date</strong>.</p></td></tr></table>
