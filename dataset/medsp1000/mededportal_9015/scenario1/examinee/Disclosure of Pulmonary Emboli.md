# Disclosure of a Complication: Pulmonary Emboli

RESIDENT CASE NOTES

# THE PATIENT

Henry Bass is a 75-year male with colon cancer. Ten days ago he was admitted to the SICU with respiratory failure, intra-abdominal sepsis, and failure to thrive after Hartman’s procedure for a perforated colon cancer. He has been in the ICU for 10 days, and you have been taking care of him for the last three days. You were able to extubate Mr. Bass three days ago, but he still requires breathing support for thick secretions and a poor cough. He has also had a low grade fever for which you have not found a cause. Last night, in addition to the fever, Mr. Bass became acutely hypoxic and hypotensive and required intubation and ventilator support. You later learned that he has blood clots in both lungs. You also discovered that although he has had compression devices on his legs, a blood-thinning medication had not been prescribed. His hypotension is resolved but he will likely spend several days on the ventilator.

# MS. ARLENE COHEN (SISTER)

Ms. Arlene Cohen and Joanne Bass (Henry’s daughter) are the patient’s only surviving family members. Henry has been a widower for many years and Joanne lives on the west coast; she only recently came into town to see her Dad. Ms. Cohen is the patient’s main caretaker. She has been visiting Henry every day in the hospital and obviously feels quite responsible for him. You have been talking to her for the last three days. It was Ms. Cohen whom you called last night to tell about the blood clots and reintubation.

# MS. JOANNE BASS (DAUGHTER)

You’ve seen the daughter once or twice, but have never spoken with her.
