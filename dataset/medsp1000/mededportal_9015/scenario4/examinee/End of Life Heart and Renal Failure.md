**End of Life: Heart and Renal Failure**

**RESIDENT CASE NOTES**

**THE PATIENT**

Mr. Andrew Smith is a 72-year-old male from St. Paul with diabetes and severe congestive heart failure who underwent a difficult redo CABG. Post-operatively, Mr. Smith is 48 hours out and remains critically ill on the ventilator. He is in acute renal failure and dialysis will be started today. Neurologically, he has not awakened from the operation. Evaluation this morning showed severe neurologic injury, and neurology has indicated there is minimal hope for meaningful recovery.

**MRS. MARY SMITH (WIFE)**

You’ve noticed that the patient’s wife, Mary, has been at the hospital nearly every day. She seems fairly familiar with the medical environment, having cared for her husband during his heart surgery two years ago. You’ve heard from the nurses that she has been asking a lot of questions and is upset about the amount of equipment used to support her husband’s life. She is also worried that he is in pain, but cannot express this. You haven’t had any significant personal interaction with her.

**MR. BILL SMITH (BROTHER)**

The patient’s brother, Bill, has just arrived from Duluth, having been notified by Mary that Andrew was “going downhill.”
