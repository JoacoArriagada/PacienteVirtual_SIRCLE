IX.Debriefing materials

A.Discuss Indications and Contraindications for administration of ivTPA

1.Indications a)Diagnosis of ischemic stroke causing measurable neurological deficit b)The neurological signs should not be clearing spontaneously c)The neurological signs should not be minor and isolated d)Caution should be exercised in treating a patient with major deficits.

2.Absolute Contraindications

a)History or evidence of intracranial hemorrhage   
b)Clinical presentation suggestive of subarachnoid hemorrhage   
c)Known arteriovenous malformation   
d)Systolic blood pressure exceeding 185 mm Hg or diastolic blood pressure exceeding 110 mm Hg despite repeated measurements and treatment   
e)Seizure with post-ictal residual neurologic impairment   
f) Platelet count below 100,000/µL   
g)Prothrombin time (PT) above 15 or INR above 1.7   
h)Active internal bleeding or acute trauma (fracture)   
i) Head trauma or stroke in the previous 3 months   
j) Arterial puncture at a non-compressible site within 1 week

3.Relative Contraindications a)Suspected acute pericarditis b)Rapidly improving stroke symptoms c)Myocardial infarction (MI) in the previous 3 months

# 1.NINDS (National Institute of Neurologic Disorders and Stroke) II

a)Randomized Controlled Trial, 312 patients given 0.9 mg/kg alteplase, within   
3 hours (median time 89 min) (1)Results (a)As compared with patients given placebo, patients treated with t-PA were at least 30 percent more likely to have minimal or no disability at three months on the assessment scales (b)NNT 7 to get favorable outcome $12 \%$ RRR) (c)Symptomatic intracerebral hemorrhage within 36 hours after the onset of stroke occurred in 6.4 percent of patients given t-PA but only 0.6 percent of patients given placebo $( \mathsf { P } { < } 0 . 0 0 1$ ) (d)Mortality at three months was 17 percent in the t-PA group and 21 percent in the placebo group $( \mathsf { P } = 0 . 3 0 )$ )

# 2. European Cooperative Acute Stroke Study III (ECASS III)

a)Randomized Controlled Trial - 821 patients, alteplase given up to 4.5 hours (median time 3 hrs 49 minutes) (1)Results (a)More patients had a favorable outcome (0 or 1 on modified Rainkin scale) with alteplase than with placebo $( 5 2 . 4 \%$ vs. $4 5 . 2 \%$ ; odds ratio, 1.34; $9 5 \%$ confidence interval [CI], 1.02 to 1.76; $\mathsf { P } { = } 0 . 0 4$ ) (b)In the global analysis, the outcome was also improved with alteplase as compared with placebo (odds ratio, 1.28; $9 5 \%$ CI, 1.00 to 1.65; $\mathsf { P } { < } 0 . 0 5 )$ (c)Incidence of intracranial hemorrhage was higher with alteplase than with placebo (for any intracranial hemorrhage, $2 7 . 0 \%$ vs. $1 7 . 6 \%$ ; $_ { \mathsf { P } = 0 . 0 0 1 }$ ; for symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage, $2 . 4 \%$ vs. $0 . 2 \%$ ; $_ { \mathsf { P } = 0 . 0 0 8 }$ ) (d)Mortality did not differ significantly between the alteplase and placebo groups $( 7 . 7 \%$ and $8 . 4 \%$ , respectively; $\mathsf { P = } 0 . 6 8$ ). (e)No significant difference in the rate of other serious adverse events.

(f)AHA/ASA guidelines changed and the time window for administrations of rt-PA extended from 3 – 4.5 hours i) other eligibility criteria similar with the addition of additional exclusion criteria (1)Patients older than 80 years (2)All patients taking oral anticoagulants are excluded regardless of the international normalized ratio (INR) (3)Patients with baseline NIHSS greater than 25 (4)Patients with a history of stroke and diabetes

G.Optimal Management Path

1.Obtains focused history and physical exam, particularly focusing on stroke mimics, indications/contraindications for ivTPA, and the neurologic exam   
2.Recognizes likely diagnosis of CVA and obtains STAT HCT   
3.Orders other appropriate diagnostic and laboratory evaluation including EKG, CBC, basic metabolic panel, INR, aptt   
4.On return from CT scanner, recognizes and treats significant hypertension   
5.Obtains NIHSS   
6.Recognizes that ivTPA is indicated   
7.Discusses the risks and benefits of ivTPA and other treatment options with the patient, who agrees to ivTPA   
8.Administers ivTPA   
9.Discusses case with neurology consultant   
10.Admits to ICU setting
