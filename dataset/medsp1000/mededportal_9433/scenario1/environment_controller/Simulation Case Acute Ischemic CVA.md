C. Scenario branch points:

1.After initial brief evaluation, brief neurologic exam and history focusing on indications/contraindications for ivTPA the patient should be taken quickly to the CT scanner and appropriate labs ordered (especially INR, platelets). Team may complete NIH stroke scale (NIHSS) at this time or after patient returns from CT. Goal time to CT is approximately 5 minutes.

2.After return to the room after CT scan, the team should perform a full NIHSS stroke scale (see Instructor Guide for link to printable PDF) if not performed prior to CT and discuss the risks and benefits of ivTPA with the patient

3. The patient is still very hypertensive on arrival back from CT scan. If that is not addressed, the patient should continue to become more hypertensive (may serve as a prompt to the team to administer antihypertensives prior to ivTPA administration). Goal time is approximately 3 minutes after return from CT to recognize and treat hypertension.

4.When antihypertensives are given, the BP improves

D. Administration of ivTPA and disposition

1.The team should discuss the administration of ivTPA with the patient including potential side effects and risk of bleeding

2.The team should obtain informed consent

3.Neurology should be consulted and the patient admitted to the ICU
4.Case ends

E. Anticipated mistakes

1. Incomplete history/failure to review indications/contraindications to ivTPA

2. Failure to emergently obtain appropriate imaging

3. Failure to recognize uncontrolled hypertension as a contraindication to ivTPA and failure to administer antihypertensives

4. Failure to discuss the risks and benefits of ivTPA with the patient and obtain informed consent

5.Case flow details a)Incomplete history/failure to review indications/contraindications to ivTPA (1) Cueing stimulus: Have the paramedics/nursing staff ask the team if they need any more specific information, or have the nurse comment on how quickly the patient got to the Emergency Room after onset of symptoms.

b) Failure to emergently obtain appropriate imaging (1) Cueing stimulus: Have the nurse ask the team if we should hold the CT scanner in preparation for the patient

c) Failure to recognize uncontrolled hypertension as a contraindication to ivTPA and failure to administer antihypertensives (1)Cueing stimulus: Have the nurse ask the team about the patient’s blood pressure

(2)Change in patient’s condition: the patient becomes more progressively hypertensive d) Failure to discuss the risks and benefits of ivTPA with the patient and obtain informed consent (1)Cueing stimulus: Have the patient ask the team if the medication is safe and what the potential complications are.
