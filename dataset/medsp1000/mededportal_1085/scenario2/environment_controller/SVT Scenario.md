**Pediatrics Scenario – Supraventricular Tachycardia**

Scenario: 6 year old has fallen from his bike and has an obvious left leg deformity. He has no other injuries.

Setting: ED patient room or trauma bay. Patient leg is in splint via EMS. EMS could not get an IV in the field.

Further history if asked: Patient was wearing his helmet. No loss of consciousness

Past medical history is unremarkable.

Meds: None.

No allergies.

Wt: estimated 25 kg (only if asked)

**Trauma Survey** – not injuries, abrasions or contusions noted. His left leg should be described as slight deformed with medial angulation at the mid-portion of the tibia. The skin is intact and the neurovascular exam in the left foot is intact. (Alternatively, the leg can be moulaged to appear injured – a contusion may be easier to demonstrated than deformity).
