**Evaluation Checklist – Supraventricular Tachycardia**

The sessions are about 10-15 minutes in length. Behaviors or skills not demonstrated or incorrectly demonstrated should be left unchecked. Please avoid giving a trainee the “benefit of the doubt” – maintenance of objectivity improves the validity of this rating instrument.

If a resident fails to recognize a critical event, he/she or the instructor may call a “time out” – actions specific to the time out should be considered omitted. (Example – a resident fails to intubate in a timely fashion; after the instructor prompts the trainee, even if he/she intubates, do not give credit for this action.)
