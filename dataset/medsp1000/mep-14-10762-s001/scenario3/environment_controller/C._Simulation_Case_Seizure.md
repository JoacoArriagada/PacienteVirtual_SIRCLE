| **PATIENT NAME:** Julia  **PATIENT AGE:** 12 months old  **PATIENT WEIGHT:** 10 kg  **CHIEF COMPLAINT:** Seizure | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Your nurse calls you to care for a seizing child. The patient was just being roomed when she began to seize. Anticipated interventions include primary assessment, calling for help, initiation of anti-epileptic drugs (AEDs). Medics will be called. Following administration of the AED, the patient develops slowed, irregular breathing. Anticipated interventions include supporting ABC’s until Medics arrive. If available at clinic site, can check POC glucose. |
