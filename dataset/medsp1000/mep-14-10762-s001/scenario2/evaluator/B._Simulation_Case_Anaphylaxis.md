**Ideal Scenario Flow**

*The learners enter the room after the patient receives a dose of IM ceftriaxone (if no ceftriaxone in clinic, adjust to say the patient received another antibiotic available in your clinic) to discover the patient has developed an acute onset of respiratory distress, irritability, and hives. The team immediately assigns team roles (number of roles will depend on number of participants) including but not limited to: team leader, airway, survey, medication administrator, person who calls 911 and communicates with EMS. Place the patient on SpO2 monitor and expose the patient. They do a primary survey, and note that the patient is tachypneic, is in respiratory distress and is developing an urticarial rash. They recognize anaphylaxis and respiratory distress and call EMS immediately. They apply oxygen and administer epinephrine 0.01 mg/kg (1:1000) (max 0.3mg) IM or Epi Pen Jr (as patient is <25kg). Initially the stridor resolves, however 5 minutes after first dose of IM epi is administered stridor recurs. The team administers a second dose of IM epi. Reassess ABCs. After the 2nd dose of IM epi, the stridor resolves and the patient is more calm, with only faint wheezing bilaterally. The team considers giving adjunctive anaphylaxis medications while waiting for medics, including steroids: dexamethasone 0.6mg/kg PO/IM (max 16 mg) or prednisone 2mg/kg PO (max 60 mg), diphenhydramine 1-1.5mg/kg IV/PO (max 50 mg), ranitidine 1mg/kg (max 50mg), and/or albuterol, if available. The medics arrive and the team signs out the patient. This scenario assumes no advanced monitoring or IV and accessory laboratory and imaging capacity at the clinic site.*

**Anticipated Management Mistakes**

***Failure to perform the following critical actions:***

***Crucial:***

1. Call medics (911) immediately upon recognizing decompensating patient.
2. Recognize respiratory distress and anaphylaxis.
3. Monitor airway.
4. Administer oxygen.
5. Administer IM epinephrine immediately.
6. Recognize respiratory decompensation after 1st epi dose, repeat IM epinephrine every 5-15 minutes as needed.

***Second line:***

1. Place patient on SpO2 monitor.
2. Administer adjunct anaphylaxis medications: steroids, diphenhydramine, ranitidine, albuterol.
