# Role-play: Intervention

You are the intern just rotating on to a new service and are about to see this patient who was admitted with a painful sickle cell crisis. Your patient is a 28-year-old person with a long history of sickle cell anemia and painful crises. The patient is admitted at least five times a year in pain, with severe pain in the bones, joints, abdomen, and back. When the patient was admitted to the hospital, the patient negotiated with the intern to get the usual pain-medication dose for the first day or two and agreed that on the third day the dose could be scaled back. This is the third day of the hospitalization, and as agreed you are going to cut back the patient's current pain-medication dose. You suspect that this patient has a problem with substance dependence. Because of the history of painful crises, the admitting team started the patient on narcotics. You have been thinking about getting a psychiatric consult to evaluate the possibility of substance dependence.
