**Appendix A**: Logistics

1. Location of activity
   1. Simulation Center
2. Setting for the encounter:
   1. Primary care physician office
3. Materials required:
   1. The SP can provide a medication list to the student. This list includes the patient’s home medications: Diltiazem 360 mg daily for blood pressure, Advair diskus 500/50 use twice daily for asthma; Albuterol inhaler 2 puffs four times daily and whenever needed for asthma, Proscar 5 mg daily for prostate and Terazosin 10 mg daily for prostate.
   2. Prescriptions should also be written for the medications the SP received from the Veterans Affairs Medical Center (see **Fake Rxs**). The SP brings these prescriptions to his primary care physician because he is unsure whether they are the proper medications for him. These prescriptions include: lisinopril 5mg daily, carvedilol 3.125mg BID, and furosemide 20mg daily; there are no refills on any of the prescriptions.
   3. There should also be 3x5 cards prepared which describe ‘results’ from the physical examination. When students attempt to assess neck, heart, lungs, abdomen, and edema, the SP should hand them the corresponding 3x5 card which lists a description of the finding.
4. Number of encounters completed by each learner:
   1. There is one 10 minute visit with the SP, followed by 10 minutes of plan preparation, then an additional 10 minute SP-student encounter.
5. Length of each encounter
   1. The first SP-student encounter is 10 minutes. During the subsequent 10 minutes, the student develops a plan for the SP. After re-entering the SP room, students spend 10 additional minutes communicating their plan to the SP.
   2. To complete the case, the SP was asked to give feedback to the student for approximately 3 minutes following the second interaction. The SP was asked to provide general feedback based on the student’s communication skills. The SP was asked to not critique specific details from the checklist.
