**Missing Clinic Appointments**

Presentation to Student:

A 50 year old postal carrier with past medical history of poorly controlled diabetes and chronic diabetic foot ulcer who you are seeing in primary care clinic for a follow up visit. They have been referred to the Limb Preservation Clinic, however have no-showed two appointments with them. Their office has called to tell you that if they no-show another appointment, they will be let go as a patient. You are concerned that without proper wound care, they might ultimately require an amputation.
