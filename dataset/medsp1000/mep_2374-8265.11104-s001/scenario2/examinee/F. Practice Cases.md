**Not wearing seat belt**

Presentation to Student:

A 45 year old patient who you have been seeing in clinic for years. They are otherwise healthy, but on a new health questionnaire you have started using in your clinic, they indicated that they does not wear a seatbelt. You are concerned about this health risk. The patient is in a hurry to finish the appointment because it’s their nephew’s 8th birthday (who patient is like a parent to), and they plans on taking him to his favorite restaurant in town. The nephew is excited about his birthday, since he is now old enough to not need a car seat.
