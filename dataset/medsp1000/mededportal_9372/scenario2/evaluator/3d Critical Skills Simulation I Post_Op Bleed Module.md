**Critical Skills for the Senior Medical Student Entering Surgery**

**Post-Op Bleed Module**

**Learning Objectives**

* Identify hypovolemic shock
* Review differential diagnosis for hypovolemic shock
* Initiate appropriate resuscitation
* Obtain appropriate workup
* Diagnose postoperative hemorrhage and transfer / escalate care appropriately

**Patient Information and History**

* 44 year-old female with PMH significant for depression, hypertension, elevated lipids, ex-smoker
* POD 0 s/p sigmoid colectomy for complicated diverticulosis
* Called by nurse because patient has been on the floor for 2 hours since PACU, has had borderline urine output and tachycardia

**Setting the Scene**

* Pt complains of abdominal bloating, incisional pain
* 2 14-guage IV’s in place from OR
* Maintenance IVF is LR running at 120 cc/hr
* Urine output 38 cc since arrival from PACU 2 hours ago
* Baseline VS: 96.3, 109 regular rhythm, BP 88/56, RR 18, SPO2 98%
* Initial Labs: Hgb 9.1, INR 1.2, PTT 19;
