**VERSION C: Sedation for Retrobulbar Block - Resultant retrobulbar hemorrhage and elevated intraocular pressure**

Phase I: Baseline
Patient awake, arrives in OR with monitors on (EKG, NIBP, SpO2) and PIV in place. HR 65, NSR. BP 127/83. RR 16. SpO2 99% on room air.

Phase II: Sedation
HR 65, NSR. BP 130/87. RR 16. SpO2 100% on 2L NC.
After administration of sedation, patient closes eyes, vitals remain stable.

Phase III: Retrobulbar block
HR 65, NSR. BP 115/75. RR 8. SpO2 93% on 2L NC.
Shortly after the block, the patient complains of pain in the right orbit and the surgeon notes significant proptosis and suspects elevated intraocular pressure.

Phase IV: Retrobulbar hemorrhage
HR 75, NSR. BP 150/95. RR 20. SpO2 100 on 2L NC.

Phase V: Induction of general anesthesia or sedation
