**VERSION B: Sedation for Retrobulbar Block - Resultant intravascular injection of local by surgeon and seizure**

Phase I: Baseline
Patient awake, arrives in OR with monitors on (EKG, NIBP, SpO2) and PIV in place. HR 65, NSR. BP 127/83. RR 16. SpO2 99% on room air.
Operator: Begin scenario.
Trigger: Learner proceeds with sedation.

Phase II: Sedation
HR 65, NSR. BP 130/87. RR 16. SpO2 100% on 2L NC.
After administration of sedation, patient closes eyes, vitals remain stable.
Trigger: Learner informs surgeon that block should be performed.

Phase III: Retrobulbar block
HR 65, NSR. BP 115/75. RR 8. SpO2 93% on 2L NC.
Trigger: Shortly after the block, the patient remains somnolent and begins to convulse in a general, tonic-clonic manner.

Phase IV: Intravascular local anesthetic injection and seizure
HR 80, sinus bradycardia. BP 150/90. RR 10. SpO2 93% on 2L NC.
Trigger: If learner correctly identifies intravascular injection and seizure, and provides appropriate seizure treatment and manages the airway appropriately, end for debrief. If after 3 minutes the learner fails to treat the seizure and manage the airway appropriately, proceed to Phase V.

Phase V: Cardiopulmonary arrest
HR 32, PEA. BP 48/32. SpO2 60%.
Trigger: If learner correctly calls for help and institutes ACLS, end for debrief.
