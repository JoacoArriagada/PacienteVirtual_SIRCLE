# #15. Breastfeeding anticipatory guidance

A. Subject: Breastfeeding (oral question)

B. Question: A first time breastfeeding mother is in with her full term infant for his initial visit 48 hours post discharge. The baby is well appearing and not jaundiced. Mother has many questions, including: does her infant need any vitamin supplementation? If so, which one(s)? (if resident does not ask about mom’s diet, prompt him/her by saying “I am vegan”) She has been noticing persistent pain while the baby nipples and wonders if this is normal and what she can do to alleviate the pain. (Resident should state that he/she would like to evaluate the latch, and should be able to describe components of a good latch. Should be prompted if fails to initiate latch eval). Finally, she thinks her “milk came in” this morning: her breasts were engorged and painful and the baby had a difficult time latching on. She would like some advice for this as well.

C. Set up: Oral question with Chief Resident or Program Director playing the part of the parent
