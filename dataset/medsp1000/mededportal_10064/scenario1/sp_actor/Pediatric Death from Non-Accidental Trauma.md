**A Pediatric Death from Child Abuse**

*Alternatively known as the Kobayashi Maru*

1. Actors
   1. Grandmother
   2. Mother

1. Case Narrative:
   1. The patient is Eric, an 8 year old boy who was found unresponsive in bed by grandmother who lives across the street. The patient was left in the care of his step-father while his mother was working late all week. The grandmother came over to check on her grandchild, and found him in bed obtunded and called the emergency medical services (EMS). The patient's step-father was nowhere to be found, and his car was gone. Upon EMS arrival, the patient was found to have a GCS of 3. The patient was intubated at the scene and brought to the ED.
   2. Patient Background (per Grandmother):
      1. ROS - unable to obtain
      2. Past medical history - None
      3. Surgical history - None
      4. Medications - None
      5. Allergies - None
      6. Social History - Divorced parents, Mother recently re-married. Father lives upstate.
      7. Family History - Older sibling (different father) who is in college.
   3. Initial Presentation: The patient is wheeled in by EMS on a stretcher, with an endotracheal tube in place. EMS was called by the Grandmother when she could not arouse patient. EMS intubated the patient out of concern for the airway given the patient's GCS of 3. The patient was placed in a cervical collar and transported to the hospital on a backboard. The patient arrives GCS 3-T after etomidate, succinylcholine, and vecuronium. Fingerstick per EMS is 97mg/dL. En route, EMS noted tachycardia and an initial blood pressure (BP) of 85/40, obtained a 16guage IV in the LEFT antecubital area, and administered a normal saline bolus. The patient's grandmother has called the patient's mother who is now on her way to the hospital. The patient's mother will arrive about 5-10 minutes into the simulation and will be extremely emotional and concerned about her son on arrival.
   4. Final stage - At this time Mom will become distraught, and the simulation team will need to address the emotional distress of the mother and grandmother.

1. Instructors Notes
   1. Social Case Flow Details
      1. The simulation participants will have to make a choice early on as to whether to include the mother and grandmother in the resuscitation room.
      2. If the family is not invited into the resuscitation by the time the patient passes away, when the outcome is divulged, Mom will suffer an acute grief reaction and pass out on floor.
