**Case Instructor Guide: A Pediatric Death from Non-Accidental Trauma**

**Title:**  A Pediatric Death from Non-Accidental Trauma

(*Alternatively known as Kobayashi Maru)*

Participants in this case are given the opportunity to manage the emotionally charged parent reactions to the death of their child.

**Deployment:** This case was run several times as part of our simulation education training during our resident conferences.

Prior to implementing the case, the faculty, academic chief residents, and technician rehearsed the case several times. The day of the case, we recruited two medical students to play the mother and grandmother. They were coached in terms of their grief reactions.
