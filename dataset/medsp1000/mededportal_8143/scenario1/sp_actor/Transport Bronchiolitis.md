Title Acute Respiratory Failure in an Infant

Case Narrative

A. Scenario Background

4 month old male who has had 2 days of congestion and rhinorrhea and 1 day of increased work of breathing, prompting mother to bring infant in to hospital. This information is offered at the scenario start.

If asked:

- Child is a full term infant, normal spontaneous vaginal delivery
- No other medical problems
- Never hospitalized
- No surgeries
- Child has been growing and developing normally
- Child has had tactile fever for the past 2 days
- Child has been drinking but less than usual and noted to be having fewer wet diapers today
- Child was noted to be less active today
- Child has no siblings but there is a sick contact in the home.

B. Scenario Conditions

Initial presentation: vital signs – pulse 168, respirations 72, blood pressure 83/60 pulse oximetry 94% in room air. On physical exam infant has retractions, grunting and wheezing.

If asked:

- Capillary refill is about 3 seconds
- Infant is just lying there not active
- All other physical findings are normal.
