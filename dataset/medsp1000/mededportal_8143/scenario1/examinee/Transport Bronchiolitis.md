Title Acute Respiratory Failure in an Infant

Actors

A. Roles – at a minimum one health care provider and an assistant. They serve as team leader and airway assistant in scenario.

Case Narrative

A. Scenario Background

4 month old male who has had 2 days of congestion and rhinorrhea and 1 day of increased work of breathing, prompting mother to bring infant in to hospital. This information is offered at the scenario start.

B. Scenario Conditions

Initial presentation: vital signs – pulse 168, respirations 72, blood pressure 83/60 pulse oximetry 94% in room air. On physical exam infant has retractions, grunting and wheezing.
