* 1. **Props:**

1. **Actors:** Surgeon
   1. **Who may play them:** other residents, other students, or simulation technician
   2. **Action Role:** The surgeon will dissuade the learner from using anesthesia by stating “the patient won’t feel anything, it’s not necessary, etc.” If the learner still desires to induce the patient, or if the learner is uncertain of the surgeon’s suggestion to continue without anesthesia, the surgeon will state “if it will make you feel better, I can give them a block.” The block, however, will prove insufficient for prophylaxis against autonomic hyperreflexia.

1. **Case Narrative (VERSION B)**

An orthopedic surgeon calls to add on a case for a patient that needs urgent surgery.
