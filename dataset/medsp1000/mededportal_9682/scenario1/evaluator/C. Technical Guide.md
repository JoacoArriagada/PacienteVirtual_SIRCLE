| Phase I:  Baseline | Patient awake, in L&D OR suite with monitors on (EKG, NIBP, SpO2).  HR 75, NSR  BP 110/62  SpO2 98% on room air | **Learner Actions:**  Learner may assess patient, including brief assessment of airway, cardiovascular, pulmonary, and neurologic systems. The most effective prevention of autonomic hyperreflexia in an at-risk patient (besides avoiding precipitating factors) is spinal or GA (not just MAC). Epidural blockade is less reliable. A paracervical block in this case would not be a complete block for the procedure being done; that is, it may block the noxious stimulus of cervical dilation, but not the stimulus of curettage. | **Operator:**  Begin scenario.  **Trigger:**  Learner communicates decision to OB/GYN regarding whether or not he/she will assume anesthetic care for the patient.
