**Examiner Instructions CHALLENGING PELVIC EXAM/PAP TEST**

| **General** | 1. How to begin: Do not introduce yourself— allow trainees to begin as if you were not there. 2. Stay on time: Please keep to the station’s time schedule, use the stopwatch, and make sure to end on time. 3. Being ahead of schedule: If the trainee finishes any element of the exercise in less time than is allotted, move ahead, leaving more time for your feedback at the end. 4. Completing checklists: The standardized patient (SP) will complete the communication skills checklist. Your checklist therefore covers other components of the exercise. 5. When to give feedback: At the end of the station, the SP will give verbal feedback to the trainee on communication skills. Afterwards, you provide feedback on content, skills, and also communication. 6. Double-checking your work: Before the next trainee begins (if performing multiple OSCEs sequentially), make sure all of the items on your checklist are filled in (either DID or DID NOT DO). |

| **Part I** | **Interview**  Observe the trainee’s interview and fill in the checklist. After the trainee obtains permission to proceed with the pelvic examination, say, “PLEASE GET EVERYTHING READY THAT YOU’LL NEED TO PERFORM THE EXAMINATION.” | **2 minutes** |
| **Part II** | **Preparation for the Exam**  The examiner should say, “YOU MAY NOW LEAVE THE ROOM SO THE PATIENT CAN PREPARE FOR THE EXAMINATION.”  When the patient expresses a desire for a support person or chaperone, the examiner should add, “WHEN YOU RETURN TO PERFORM THE EXAMINATION, I WILL SERVE AS THE PATIENT’S SUPPORT PERSON.”  After the trainee leaves the room, the patient will sit on the table. The examiner should place the model in the lithotomy position at the end of the table, draping the model so that it appears connected with the patient.  After the set-up is complete, inform the trainee outside the room by stating, “THE PATIENT IS READY FOR THE EXAMINATION.” | **2 minutes** |
| **Part III** | **Exam**  Please instruct the trainee, “YOU MAY GO AHEAD AND PERFORM THE EXAMINATION NOW. AS YOU ARE PERFORMING THE EXAM, PLEASE TALK TO THE PATIENT AND ALSO TELL ME WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING FOR.”  The examiner may provide assistance by handing materials to the trainee. | **10 minutes** |
| **Part IV** | **Concluding the visit**  If the trainee is not done at 10 minutes, say, “I KNOW YOU ARE NOT DONE EXAMINING THE PATIENT BUT WE NEED TO MOVE ON. AT THIS POINT STEP OUT OF THE ROOM TO LET THE PATIENT GET DRESSED, RETURN TO CONCLUDE THE VISIT AND LET THE PATIENT KNOW WHEN YOU WOULD LIKE TO SEE THEM NEXT.” | **2 minutes** |
