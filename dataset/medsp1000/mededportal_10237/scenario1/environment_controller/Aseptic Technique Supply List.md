**Supplies for learners:**

1. Skin prep swabs (Betadine and Chloraprep): 1 per learner, ideally use the prep commonly issued at your institution
2. Unsterile and sterile gloves (various sizes to accommodate all learners)
3. Sterile sheet with opening (fenestrated): 1 per learner
4. Protective pad for under the arm (disposable underpad (e.g. Chux), which is not sterile): 1 per learner
5. Gauze sponges for dressing: 2-4 per learner (sterile) 4X4
6. Protective eyewear: 5-6 pairs total, these can be reused
7. Tape to secure the dressing: 1 box
8. Alcohol swab pads can be used to clean eye wear and spills

The items above can probably be ordered from hospital central supply.

**Supplies to create the abscess:**

* Tegaderm sheets: 10 cm x 12 cm (cut in half): 1 per abscess. Placed on the skin to begin the abscess. This prevents skin irritation
* Cosmetic wedges: Used to apply stage makeup
* Non sterile cotton tip applicators: Used to apply the petroleum jelly
* Tongue depressors: Used to apply and smooth nose & scar wax
* Petroleum Jelly: For pus in middle in abscess
* Nose & scar wax (Professional modeling putty): In a variety of skin tones as appropriate to the standardized patient.\*\*: To create the height and depth of the abscess.
* Stage makeup coloring in reds, yellows, browns to simulate blister\*\*
* Matte makeup seal\*\*: Spray on abscess after complete
* Have cortisone cream (over-the-counter) on hand for any evidence of skin irritation

\*\*Items can be purchased from Ben Nye at [www.stagemakeuponline.com](http://www.stagemakeuponline.com) or other costume stores
