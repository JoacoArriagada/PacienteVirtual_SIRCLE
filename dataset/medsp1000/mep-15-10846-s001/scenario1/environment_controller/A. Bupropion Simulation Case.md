| **Appendix A: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Scenario**  SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Pediatric Emergency Medicine Simulation Curriculum: Bupropion Ingestion  AUTHORS: **Emily Hartford, MD, MPH, Anita Thomas, MD, MPH, Ashley Keilman, MD, Neil Uspal, MD, Kimberly Stone, MD, Jennifer Reid, MD, Rebekah Burns, MD.** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: John**  **PATIENT AGE: 13 years**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Altered mental status** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Patient is a 13 year old male with no prior medical history who presents with altered mental status / agitation. Symptoms are secondary to an intentional ingestion of bupropion. Patient brought by EMS to the ED agitated and hallucinating. He presents with widening of his QRS interval on cardiac monitoring, then develops a generalized tonic-clonic seizure that resolves with appropriate treatment. Patient subsequently develops pulseless ventricular tachycardia (VT). |
