**Appendix D: Practice Case 3**

**Opening scenario**

A 59-year old male, presents to the emergency department complaining of fatigue.

**Patient information**

HPI: 59-year old male with fatigue for 2 weeks. *“I’m just all out of energy, doc.”* Progressive symptoms – associated with malaise, dyspnea, and weight gain of 15 lbs. Fatigue and dyspnea worse with exertion. Diet is poor: many frozen dinners, fried foods, and fast food meals; poor fruit and vegetable intake.

PMH: Hypertension, hypothyroidism. No surgeries. No recent hospitalizations.

MEDS: *Out of all meds for 3 months.* Hydrochlorothiazide 25mg daily, amlodipine 10mg daily, clonidine 0.2mg BID, levothyroxine 125 mcg daily.

ALL: none

SH: Past history of cocaine use, regular marijuana use. Denies tobacco and alcohol. Single, lives with bother. No current/recent sexual partners. Unemployed for 5 months (construction laborer).

FH: none

ROS: Malaise, dyspnea, 15-lb weight gain, and occasional nausea. Denies fever, chills, URI symptoms, weakness, numbness, headache, dizziness, syncope, chest pain, cough, orthopnea, PND, peripheral edema, leg pain, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, abdominal pain, dysuria, back pain, rash, weight loss, change in appetite, myalgia, and arthritis.
