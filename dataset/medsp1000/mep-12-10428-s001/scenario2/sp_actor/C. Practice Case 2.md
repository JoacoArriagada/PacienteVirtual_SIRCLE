**Appendix C: Practice Case 2**

**Opening scenario**

A 29-year old female, calls the clinic concerned about pelvic pain.

**Patient information**

HPI: 29-year female presents with pelvic painfor one week. Constant, dull, and throbbing. 6/10 intensity. Located in bilateral lower abdomen with some radiation to lower back. Worse with walking. No alleviating factors. Associated with vaginal discharge, dysuria, and irregular menses (missed period).

While her menses is typically very regular, she is two weeks late for her menses to begin, and her last menstrual period was 6 weeks ago. Admittedly, she forgets to take her contraceptive pill some mornings, but her partner *usually* wears a condom every time.

Potentially unprotected intercourse with a new partner has her concerned that these symptoms may represent a sexually transmitted infection.

PMH: Normal Pap smear 9 months ago. Denies history of sexually transmitted infections. G0P0.

MEDS: Ethinyl/estradiol/norgestimate daily.

ALL: none

SH: Denies tobacco, alcohol, and drug use. Lives with a roommate. Recent sexual intercourse with a new partner (4 lifetime partners). Works as a waitress.

FH: none

ROS: Positive for vaginal discharge (white, thin, scant), irregular menses, and dysuria. Denies fever, chills, eye symptoms, rash, arthralgia, numbness, chest pain, dyspnea, cough, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and constipation.
