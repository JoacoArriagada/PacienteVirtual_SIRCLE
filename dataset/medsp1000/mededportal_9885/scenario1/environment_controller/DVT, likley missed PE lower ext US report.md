Comparison: left lower extremity ultrasound 7/13/2011

Procedure: Sonographic evaluation of the lower extremity venous vasculature was performed utilizing gray scale and color-flow Doppler imaging.

Findings:

The left common femoral, superficial femoral, and popliteal veins demonstrate normal compressibility and venous blood flow with no intraluminal filling defect to suggest deep venous thrombosis.
