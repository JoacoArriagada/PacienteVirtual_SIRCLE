Application of the GetINBurnOUT method in the clinical learning environment through practice cases

* Case 2 explores an experience with a resident who is performing well but openly appears exhausted, has given up social activities, and regularly stays late at the hospital and works past the end of his shift.
  + This case represents a trainee at high risk of burnout.
  + The resident seems to be taking on tasks that should be delegated to other team members.
  + The educator knows that this trainee values socializing with colleagues.
