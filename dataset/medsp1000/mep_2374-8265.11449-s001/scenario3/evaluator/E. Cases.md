*What signs or behaviors of burnout is the learner exhibiting?*

*Is learner performance being affected by his/her burnout?*

*How can you use the GetINBurnOUT method to approach this learner? How will you incorporate honest and accurate feedback?*

* You are working with a late year intern. His presentations are brief and superficial. He struggles to answer families’ questions and or explain his thought processes and relies on his upper level to fill in.
* He nods when you give him on-the-fly feedback, but little progress is made. His upper level reveals to you that the intern is working hard, but requires a lot of support. You suspect he has an insufficient medical knowledge base.
* During feedback, he reports that he is feeling “burned out” after several inpatient months in a row and apologizes for seeming “tired.” He blames his “tough month” on burnout saying “I just need a vacation.”
