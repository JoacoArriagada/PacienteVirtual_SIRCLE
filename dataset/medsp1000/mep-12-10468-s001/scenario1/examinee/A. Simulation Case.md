| **Appendix A: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Case Template**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Fever and Seizure in a Young Infant: A Simulation Case**  **AUTHORS: Molly Rideout, MD and William Raszka, MD** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: Timmy South**  **PATIENT AGE: 5 weeks**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Fever and lethargy** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | A 5 week old has fever and lethargy. You are the health care provider evaluating and managing a young infant in the emergency room with the support of a parent at bedside and a nurse in the room. |
| **Learner Preparation**  *What information should the learners be given prior to initiation of the case?* | The location of the scenario is the emergency room. A parent and a nurse are in the room. Students should assume they are the health care provider. Students may call for help, although it may not be available. Students may call for interventions, although they may not be available. |

| **Overall Appearance**  *What do learners see when they first enter the room?* | Baby is lying in warmer or on bed with parent bedside. He is pale and not responsive, with decreased tone. The baby is not yet on the monitor. The nurse looks worried and the parent is distraught. | | |
| **Actors and roles in the room at case start**  *Who is present at the beginning and what is their role? Who may play them?* | -Mother: gives history, answers questions  -Nurse: assists in carrying out orders | | |
