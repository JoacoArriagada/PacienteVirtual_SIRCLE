| **Appendix A: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Case Template**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Fever and Seizure in a Young Infant: A Simulation Case**  **AUTHORS: Molly Rideout, MD and William Raszka, MD** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: Timmy South**  **PATIENT AGE: 5 weeks**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Fever and lethargy** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | A 5 week old has fever and lethargy. He develops a seizure with cyanosis requiring respiratory support. Learners will consider the differential diagnosis and management for a young infant with fever. They will then manage acute respiratory depression and seizure. |
| **Learner Preparation**  *What information should the learners be given prior to initiation of the case?* | The location of the scenario is the emergency room. A parent and a nurse are in the room. Students should assume they are the health care provider. Students may call for help, although it may not be available. Students may call for interventions, although they may not be available. |

| Baby has generalized tonic clonic seizure with cyanosis | New vitals during seizure:  Pulse 170, **RR 8**, BP 102/62, Temp 38.2, **O2 Sat 82%**  Change in exam:  Dusky, unresponsive, lips bluish, tonic clonic movements of all extremities | If students are setting up the LP (possibly with a different mannequin), they may not be aware of the seizure. | Student administers oxygen and starts giving breaths via bag and mask ventilation at approximately 24 breaths per minute | Seizure continues.
