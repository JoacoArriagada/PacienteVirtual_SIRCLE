Provide respiratory support via effective bag-mask ventilation with appropriately-sized mask

Ask team members for input

**Optimal management path:**

ABC’s and vital signs 🡪 Focused history and physical exam 🡪 IV access🡪 fluid bolus🡪 blood and urine samples🡪 antibiotics ordered 🡪 consider LP🡪 seizure develops with respiratory depression🡪 Ativan IV 🡪 Oxygen and Bag Mask Ventilation support until seizure stops🡪 antibiotics

**Patients physiology**

A 5 week old has meningitis, presenting with lethargy and fever. He slept all day and is moderately dehydrated. He develops a seizure with cyanosis requiring respiratory support.

**Potential complications path/s**

-Parent could provide various communication challenges (challenge the need for an LP, ask many interfering questions)

-If students concerned about abuse, CT should be done, but antibiotics should be given first to avoid delay

-If students proceed to intubation, facilitator could suggest calling anesthesia to help

-If students fail to recognize need for anti-seizure medication in infant with seizure and cyanosis, nurse could prompt

-Participants should be oriented to fact that students will be performing procedures beyond their level of training and that this would not happen in real-life scenarios but is acceptable in the simulation exercise
