**Case One:**

**5 week old infant with lethargy and fever**

ABC’s

**Part I**

Monitors/vitals

Bedside glucose

Focused history and physical exam

IV Access, Draw Labs, including blood culture

Consider fluid bolus

Order antibiotics

Urine catheterization for culture

Acetaminophen

Consider Lumbar Puncture

**Part II**

Generalized tonic clonic seizure with cyanosis

Call for help

Oxygen,

Respiratory support

Seizure does not stop

Lorazepam

Seizure stops

Repeat dose lorazepam

Decision made to postpone LP until infant more stable

Lumbar Puncture

Antibiotics

Decision to admit

Consider additional workup based on labs
