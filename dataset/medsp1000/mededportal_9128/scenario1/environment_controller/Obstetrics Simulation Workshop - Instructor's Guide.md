# An Introduction to the Management of Labour and Delivery - A Simulation-Based Obstetrics Workshop for Medical Students

# Teaching Methods

Noelle will be preloaded with a baby and dilated to $2 { \cdot } 3 \mathsf { c m }$ . The case will then be presented to the students:

“A 25 year old woman presents to the OAU stating that she thinks she is in labour. Take a focused history from this patient.”

The students will ask questions in a round-table format, and will be answered by the manikin.

Patient History (to be provided as asked by the students)

o She is gravida 1, para 0, 40 weeks plus 3 days   
o No medical problems, no medications, and no allergies   
o Uneventful pregnancy, no GDM, no HTN, normal ultrasounds   
o No bleeding, no ROM, fetal movement present   
o Contracting q 4-5 minutes x 30 seconds over last 2 hours   
o Lives 15 minutes from the hospital   
o She was examined in Dr. Posner’s office yesterday and was 1 cm dilated; he was unable to strip the membranes

# Physical Exam

o 2 cm dilated o FHR reactive/normal tracing

Patient is sent walking and returns for examination in two hours.

Students are given a second opportunity to examine Noelle, who is now 3-4 cm and contracting q 3-4 minutes x 45-60 seconds.

Noelle’s nurse comments that her BP is 130/90. Is this pre-eclampsia or anxiety?

Students should be given the opportunity to ask Noelle questions relevant to the diagnosis of pre-eclampsia.

# History Findings

o No headache   
o No scotoma or other changes in vision   
o No epigastric or RUQ pain   
o Normal swelling

# Physical Exam

o Normal swelling o Normal reflexes

# Laboratory Investigations

o Urine dip normal o Blood work is normal

Noelle asks the students, “What can you give me for the pain?!?!?!?!”

Noelle opts to have an epidural, but it is then noted that her labour progress has slowed.

Noelle is fully dilated.

After two hours of pushing, you are called to the delivery.
