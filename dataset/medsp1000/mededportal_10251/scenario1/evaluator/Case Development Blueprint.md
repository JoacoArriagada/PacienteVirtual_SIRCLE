# Case Development Blueprint

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Presenting Complaint:  (with setting/vitals) | Patient is being admitted from emergency room for altered mental status and leg swelling. |
| Gender and Age Range: | Male Age 80 (Standardized patient could be 60+, could use a man or woman if you make the change in the case to adjust for gender) |
| Name: | Mr. John Merrill |
| Daughter | Daughter (Mary) who is his power of attorney for healthcare (Standardized patient could be 40+, could use a man or woman if you adjust the case for gender) |
| Opening Statement: | Patient - "My legs are swollen”  Daughter – my father is confused since the past 3 days and we have also noticed leg swelling over the past few days. |
| Brief Summary: | Mr. Merrill has history of diabetes mellitus, high blood pressure, chronic kidney disease and osteoarthritis. He developed cellulitis ~ 2 weeks ago and was started on IV antibiotics that time. |
| Case Objective Domains: | 1. Communication 2. Patient Care 3. Medical Knowledge 4. System Based Practice (Team) |
| Care Objectives & Key Challenge(s) of Case: | To have the fellow (when called as a consultant) be able to quickly:   1. Identify that leg swelling could be due to acute on chronic kidney injury 2. Identify signs of drug overdose / toxicity in an elderly patient 3. Formulate a management plan 4. Communicate the management plan to the patient / family |
| Differential Diagnosis (actual diagnosis): | 1. Acute kidney injury secondary to antibiotic toxicity causing volume retention and leg swelling 2. Bacterial resistance to antibiotic and the patient is becoming septic (worsening infection) leading to acute kidney injury and changes in mental status 3. A new central nervous system infection 4. Other possible causes of change in thinking including delirium or dementia |
| Exam Room Needs: | Emergency room or clinic room |
| Follow-up Station Needs: | Wheelchair, medication list |
| Activities & Time Req: | 20 Minutes |
| Speaker/Announcements: | Warning at 3 minutes\* (Over the speaker system) |
| Data Collection Methods: | SP Rated Checklists |
| Course, Student Level: | Nephrology Fellow |
| Pre-Reading: | None |
