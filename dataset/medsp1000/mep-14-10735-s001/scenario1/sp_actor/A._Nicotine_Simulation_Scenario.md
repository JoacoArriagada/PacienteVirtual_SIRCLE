| **Overall Appearance** | A 23-month-old boy, who was in his usual state of health until half an hour prior to presentation when mom noticed he was acting drowsy. After this he became unresponsive (on the way to the hospital). He was brought to the emergency department by car, during which time he had a two-minute episode where he became unresponsive, had stiff arms, and was deviating his gaze to the right. He seemed to improve slightly, then had another such episode while being roomed. When the providers walk in to the room at the request of nursing, the child is having some rhythmic stiff movements in his upper extremities, has gaze deviation to the right, and is unresponsive. He is drooling. He has some residual emesis on his shirt. His pants are wet with an odd smelling substance. Mom is at bedside, and very concerned. | | |
| **Actors and roles in the room at case start** | Mom is at bedside and available to answer questions. | | |
| **HPI** | Kevin is a 23-month-old boy with a history of mild reactive airway disease who was in his usual state of health until half an hour prior to presentation when mom noticed he was acting drowsy. He was brought to the emergency department by car, during which time he had a two-minute episode where he became unresponsive, had stiff arms, and was deviating his gaze to the right. He seemed to improve slightly, and then had another such episode while being roomed. He takes no medications. He has no seizure history. He is fully immunized/up-to-date. Has not had any recent fevers, URI symptoms, color changes, diarrhea, or rashes. Healthy since birth other than two episodes of “wheezing” when he had URIs. Does not require an albuterol inhaler generally. No known sick contacts. Today, he vomited twice prior to arriving to the hospital. Key history: about 20 minutes prior to mom noticing his drowsiness, she walked into the kitchen to find he had “knocked over” a bottle of liquid nicotine. Some of the liquid got onto his pants. Mom did not initially think he drank any, but she “just can’t be sure”. Lives with mom and dad. He is an only child. Mom and dad smoke e-cigarettes. They switched over from normal cigarettes when Kevin was born because they “didn’t want him to be around cigarette smoke all the time”. | | |
