| **PATIENT NAME: Kevin**  **PATIENT AGE: 23 months**  **Weight: 12 kg**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Altered mental status** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | The triage nurse informs you that she is bringing back a child with altered mental status. The patient’s mother reports that he became sleepy and minimally responsive and has been this way for about half an hour. She noticed at one point, on the way to the hospital, that he was looking off to the right and had stiff arms. He has also been drooling, and he had one episode of non-bloody and non-projectile emesis. The child is brought to a room where he is noted to be having another “stiff arm” episode and gaze deviation. |

| **Overall Appearance** | A 23-month-old boy, who was in his usual state of health until half an hour prior to presentation when mom noticed he was acting drowsy. After this he became unresponsive (on the way to the hospital). He was brought to the emergency department by car, during which time he had a two-minute episode where he became unresponsive, had stiff arms, and was deviating his gaze to the right. He seemed to improve slightly, then had another such episode while being roomed. When the providers walk in to the room at the request of nursing, the child is having some rhythmic stiff movements in his upper extremities, has gaze deviation to the right, and is unresponsive. He is drooling. He has some residual emesis on his shirt. His pants are wet with an odd smelling substance. Mom is at bedside, and very concerned. | | |
