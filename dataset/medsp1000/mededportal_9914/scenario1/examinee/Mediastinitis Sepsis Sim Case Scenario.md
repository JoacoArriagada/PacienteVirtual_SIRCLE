# **Title** : Sepsis secondary to mediastinitis

## Target Audience: EM residents, medical students

## Environment

1. Lab Set Up

* ED or simulation lab

## Actors

* Paramedic
  + Provides history about scene and transport to the hospital
* Nurse
  + Administers medications ordered by physician
* Patient’s wife
  + Not present immediately in the ED

1. **Case Narrative** (describes what the learner will experience)
2. Scenario Background Given to Participants

Chief complaint: 41 y.o. male with chest pain

Medic report: “We were called to his house by his wife. He has had chest pain for the past 2 days. He thinks he’s had a fever. The wife said that he’s been really lethargic and just not looking well. She called today because he’s been sleeping most of the day and said he’s having trouble breathing. She should be here in a few minutes.”

1. Patient’s initial exam:

* VS: T 99.8 (took acetaminophen 2 hours prior to arrival) HR 118 RR 22 BP 100/60 O2 96% on room air - 99% on 2L nasal cannula
* General: Tired-appearing, ill-appearing
* Neuro: Moving all extremities, answers questions appropriately but slowly
* Skin: Damp
