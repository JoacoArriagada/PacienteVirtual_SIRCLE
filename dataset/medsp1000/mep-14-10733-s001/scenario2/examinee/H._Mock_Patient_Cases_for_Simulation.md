**Resident: Family-Centered Rounds Simulation Case Scenario #2**

Welcome to the Family-Centered Rounds simulation. You will be participating in a simulated rounding experience where you will be acting as the intern on the team. You will give a brief patient presentation (SOAP style, as if patient has already been staffed) about the patient described below. There will be a “parent” of the patient present as well as some other members of the care team/observers. Keep in mind, this family has been in the hospital for the past few days and have been present for rounds.

Below is the pertinent information about the patient that you gathered through pre-rounding. Choose how you would like to present this information on family centered rounds. The focus of this experience is on communication with families and NOT on medical knowledge. In the course of the simulation if you are asked question that you don’t know that the answer to, use that as an opportunity to practice your communication skills in expressing the limits of your knowledge which is a normal experience in your medical training.

**Patient Identifier:** Amy Smith is a 4-year-old, otherwise healthy girl admitted four days ago for dehydration secondary to viral gastroenteritis.

**Subjective/Interval Events**:

* Amy did well yesterday
* Tolerated PO intake and fluids stopped yesterday evening
* She ate a small meal for dinner and breakfast this morning.
* No emesis since yesterday morning.
* Few small volume, loose stools over the course of the evening and into the morning.
* This morning, Mom asked about discharge plans.
* Not vomiting

**Objective:**

*Vital signs*: Afebrile, HR 80s-90s, other vital signs within normal limits

*Intake/Output:*

Intake: 1.7 L (1000 mL IV, 700 mL PO)

Output: Only one diapers charted overnight, stool x4

**Assessment:** Amy is a healthy 4-year-old girl admitted for dehydration in the setting of emesis and diarrhea due to viral gastroenteritis. She is now euvolemic after IV fluid rehydration, which was discontinued last night, but she has had decreased urinary output.
