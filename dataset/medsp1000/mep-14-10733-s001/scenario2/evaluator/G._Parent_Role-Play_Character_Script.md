**Parent: Family-Centered Rounds Simulation Case Scenario #2**

Welcome to the FCR simulation. You will be participating in a simulated rounding experience where you will be acting as the parent of a child in the hospital. The goals of this simulation are centered on improving residents communication with families during family centered rounds (objectives on attached sheet – Appendix E). We would like you to act like a parent of the child in the case below, however, the most valuable part of this simulation is your feedback to the resident regarding their communication and management of family centered rounds in a constructive and non-threatening manner.

The simulation will last for about 10 minutes where the resident and team will come to your hospital room for “rounds”. After the simulation the resident will be asked to tell the group what they thought went well and areas for improvement. During this time, we would like you to provide feedback in both of these domains. There will also be attending physicians, nurses, and psychologists who will also help provide feedback. We will then ask the resident what they would like to practice again and give them another opportunity to repeat part of the scenario.

**Case Presentation:**

Your daughter, Amy is four years old and has been admitted to the hospital for the past four days due to viral gastroenteritis (lots of vomiting and diarrhea). When she first came to the hospital she needed IV fluids and was not eating and drinking but this has slowly improved. The team turned off the IV fluids yesterday evening and Amy has been eating and drinking better overnight. You have been present for rounds every day and know the members of the medical team.

**Characteristics of this parent role:**

* 38-year-old married female
* Employed as a lawyer in a local firm
* High socioeconomic status
* Three other children at home
* “Type A” personality
* Prefers to speak only with attending physicians
* Ready to go home, provide this information to the team during rounds
* Worried that this will happen again
* Wondering if she is not vomiting because of the anti-nausea medication vs. illness improving

**Overnight in the hospital:**

* Amy started to eat yesterday and had a small dinner and breakfast
* She is acting much more like herself today compared to other days
* She is still taking the anti-nausea medication every 6-8 hours
* She only had one wet diaper overnight

**Types of questions you might as the resident:**

* When can we go home?
* What can I do to prevent this from happening again?
* Does she need to keep taking the anti-nausea medication?
* Can I continue to monitor her and hope she continues to get better at home? If so, what do I watch for?
* When will her diarrhea stop?
* Is she still contagious?
