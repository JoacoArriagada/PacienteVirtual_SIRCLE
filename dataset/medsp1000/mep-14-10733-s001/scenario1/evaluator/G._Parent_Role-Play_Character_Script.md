**Parent: Family-Centered Rounds Simulation Case Scenario #1**

Welcome to the FCR simulation. You will be participating in a simulated rounding experience where you will be acting as the parent of a child in the hospital. The goals of this simulation are centered on improving residents communication with families during family centered rounds (objectives on attached sheet- Appendix E). We would like you to act like a parent of the child in the case below, however, the most valuable part of this simulation is your feedback to the resident regarding their communication and management of family centered rounds in a constructive and non-threatening manner.

The simulation will last for about 10 minutes where the resident and team will come to your hospital room for “rounds”. After the simulation the resident will be asked to tell the group what they thought went well and areas for improvement. During this time we would like you to provide feedback in both of these domains. There will also be attending physicians, nurses, and psychologists who will also help provide feedback. We will then ask the resident what they would like to practice again and give them another opportunity to repeat part of the scenario. T

**Case Presentation:**

Your son, John Smith, is a 2-month-old infant who was admitted to the hospital yesterday evening for RSV Bronchiolitis (viral respiratory infection which causes wheezing/coughing/increased work of breathing). Your baby has had a runny nose and cough for the past four days. Yesterday you noticed that he seemed to be working harder to breathe so you brought him to the ER in the evening. An IV was placed in the ER and he was put on oxygen via a nasal cannula.

This is the first time for you to meet the entire medical team (a different team admitted your child). You met the intern (first year resident) earlier this morning when they came to check in and see how you and your baby were doing. You and your child have never been hospitalized before aside from when he was delivered. He has otherwise been a very healthy and happy baby and this is the first time he has gotten sick.

**Characteristics of this parent role:**

* 22 year old single mother who stays at home with her son
* Went to community college and have training as an EMT
* From low socioeconomic status
* First time in the health system (unfamiliar surroundings, unfamiliar with rounds)
* Worried (RSV causes increased work of breathing making it seem like your child can’t catch their breath)
* Had a friend who had a kid with a similar illness who was recently in the hospital

**Overnight in the hospital:**

* You were awake all night because your baby was coughing and having a hard time breathing
* The night team doctors and overnight nurse came in multiple times to check on your baby
* There was an episode where the monitor kept beeping and the nurse and doctor came in and decided to increase the oxygen. After this your baby seemed more comfortable.
* Your baby continued to breastfeed every three hours overnight but at times it was difficult because of the coughing episodes

**Types of questions you might as the resident:**

* How long will he be sick?
* How long will we be in the hospital?
* What can I do to help him?
* Does this mean there is something permanently wrong with his lungs?
* How can we prevent this from happening again?
* Is he contagious?
* Did we do something wrong?
* Anything you feel families would have been confused by during the presentation
