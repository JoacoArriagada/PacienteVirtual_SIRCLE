| **Communication Element:** Communication with families on the first day of an admission |  |
| **Learning Objectives**   1. Demonstrating effective greeting and Introduction of rounds by:    1. Asking permission to enter room    2. Greeting family members and requesting preference for participation    3. Explaining purpose of patient and family centered rounds    4. Introducing of all team members    5. Discussing FCR brochure 2. Inviting family members and/or patient to participate by:    1. Inviting family members to ask questions and share information    2. Addressing questions if they are asked and/or making a plan to address questions that could not be answered during rounds 3. Creating inviting physical space by:    1. Including family members in rounding circle    2. Minimizing distractions in the room if possible    3. Making eye contact with family    4. Consistently engaging with patient/family | **Debriefing/Take Home Learning Points** |
