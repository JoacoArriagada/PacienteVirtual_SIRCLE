**Resident: Family-Centered Rounds Simulation Case Scenario #1**

Welcome to the Family-Centered Rounds simulation. You will be participating in a simulated rounding experience where you will be acting as the intern on the team. You will give a brief patient presentation (SOAP style, as if patient has already been staffed) about the patient described below. There will be a “parent” of the patient present as well as some other members of the care team/observers. Keep in mind, this family has never been admitted to the hospital before and this will be their first time experiencing “rounds.”

Below is the pertinent information about the patient that you gathered through pre-rounding. Choose how you would like to present this information on family centered rounds. The focus of this experience is on communication with families and NOT on medical knowledge.
