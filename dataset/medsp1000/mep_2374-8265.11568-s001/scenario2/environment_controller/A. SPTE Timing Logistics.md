**Timing of Discharge Scenario**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time** | **Location and Participants** | **Description** |
| **Gather and Introductions**  **10 min** | Students meet in assigned team room | Once students all arrive, Facilitators and Students introduce themselves  Facilitators explain flow of experience, answer questions, then sit at observation table |
| **Team Discharge Planning**  **20 min** | Students in team room  Facilitators observe | Students share highlights of their professions’ discharge plan with team  Students collectively determine additional information to gather from SP and the groupings of students to meet with the SP |
| **SP Encounter 1**  10 min | Student Group 1 in SP room; All others observe | Student Group 1 introduce themselves/roles, ask SP questions  Team may wish to regroup in team room for discussion before Group 2 speaks to SP |
| **SP Encounter 2**  **10 min** | Student Group 2 in SP room; All others observe | Student Group 2 introduce themselves/roles, speak with SP |
| **Discharge Plan**  **15 min** | Students in team room | Team regroups to finalize the discharge plan, then goes to SP room to share plan with SP and answer final questions |
| **Group Feedback/Reflection**  **20 min**  **(Start by 1:35, 3:05, or 4:35)** | Facilitators join students in SP room | Facilitators join students and SP in room, lead debrief of team experience.  Students reflect on what worked well and identify opportunities for improvement. |
| **Wrap-up**  **5 min** | Assigned rooms | **Please remember to take post-experience survey!** |
|  |  |  |
