**Standardized Patient Team Experience (SPTE) Facilitator Guide – Discharge**

Students will work with the team to develop a discharge plan for a patient that reflects the contributions of each team member.

***Standardized Patient Case Description****:*

Middle-aged patient with history of high blood pressure developed slurred speech and right-sided weakness at home four days ago. EMS brought patient to hospital, where they received IV tenecteplase (TNK, a thrombolytic medication) with some improvement in weakness. Team will be interacting with patient on hospital day 4 to form a discharge plan. Patient chart provided to students before and during the case has additional information.
