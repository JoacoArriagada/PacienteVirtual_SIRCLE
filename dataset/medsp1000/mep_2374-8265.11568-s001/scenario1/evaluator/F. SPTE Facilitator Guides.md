**Standardized Patient Team Experience (SPTE) Facilitator Guide – Admission**

***Overview for faculty:***

This experience is designed to be an engaging session for Medicine, Nursing +/- Pharmacy students to learn and reflect on the ways they interact with patients and other team members.   Standardized Patients (SPs) are actors who have been trained to realistically portray patients with many different medical conditions. Students will work with the team to develop an admission plan for a patient that reflects the contributions of each team member. Students will receive feedback as a team on ways to promote positive working relationships in healthcare teams.

***Participants:***

Each team will have approximately 3 - 4 students total from Medicine, Nursing +/- Pharmacy

Each team will have two facilitators for this session, acting as “guides on the side.”

1. Lead facilitator
   1. Guides students through the session, keeping note of time
   2. Makes notes as time allows on SPTE Feedback and Reflection Form
   3. BY TUES AFTER SESSION: Emails forms to students and uploads to Box folder
2. Co-facilitator
   1. Observes the encounter and takes notes on SPTE Feedback and Reflection form
   2. Leads the debrief
   3. BY MON AFTER SESSION: Emails forms to lead facilitator for finalization

**For those who are new to SPTE**, please refer to the General IPE Facilitator Training Guide (Appendix D).

***Objectives of the session:***

1. Employ effective communication skills to ensure common understanding of interprofessional team goals and plans.
2. Utilize the appropriate interprofessional team members in shared decision making or problem solving.
3. Assess team strengths and areas of improvement.
4. Demonstrate respect and professionalism in an interprofessional team.
5. Establish an environment of collaboration, trust, and appreciation for diversity and differing opinions in an interprofessional team.

***Standardized Patient Case Description****:*

Middle-aged patient with history of high blood pressure suddenly became weak and started to fall to the right at home earlier today. Spouse called 911. Spouse reported patient could answer questions, but speech sounded slurred. EMS brought patient to hospital where code stroke protocol was activated and IV thrombolytic therapy (tenecteplase) was administered.  Weakness started improving after thrombolytic administration.  Team will be interacting with patient on arrival to step-down unit to develop an admission plan. Patient chart (Appendix J) provided to students has more information.

***Session Logistics:***

Students participate in one 90-min session. You will facilitate one team of students in each of three rounds (12:30 – 2 pm, 2 pm – 3:30 pm, 3:30 – 5 pm). **Refer to detailed schedule provided on day of session for additional information about timing (Appendix A).**

**SPTE Experience Introduction (10 min)**

**Lead Facilitator starts**

1. Each facilitator introduces self (name, profession, co- or lead)
2. Students introduce selves (name, profession, organization/school). Use an icebreaker if desired.
3. Remind students to write down names AND EMAILS on sign-in sheet – will use for survey sent by email
4. Remind students of goal:
   1. Not evaluating whether the team got the “right” admission plan or “correct” stroke management
   2. We are observing how the team communicates with each other and with SP to create a plan that respects everyone’s roles and expertise

* **Orient students to schedule projected on screen:**

1. Emphasize the schedule is self-paced. Some teams will take more or less time on certain sections.
2. Lead facilitator may guide team if time seems to be running short

* **Remind students about “rules” for interacting with SP:**

1. When students talk to SP in groups, all other people in room should stay silent and observe.
2. Everyone should talk to SP at some point during entire encounter; no more than 1 student per profession at a time, no more than 3 professions at a time
3. Acknowledge they may be feeling a little awkward working with people they just met, or talking to SP – just lean in and make each other look good.
4. Any facilitator or student can call for Time out and Reset if they feel conversation is stuck or off-track.

**Team Prep (5 minutes) and SP Encounter 1 (20 minutes):**

Students determine how they will perform the NIH Stroke Scale (NIHSS) and gather info from SP, then move to SP room to perform their assessment, gather medication history, and address any SP concerns

**Admission Planning (20 minutes) and SP Encounter 2 (10 minutes):**

In workroom, students review information obtained from SP and finalize admission orders, then return to SP room to review admission plan and address SP concerns

**Group Feedback/Reflection (20 min; start at least 25 min before end of 90-min session)**

**Co-facilitator starts by asking team to reflect on what went well.**

* 1. Say: “Ok, now let’s take a few minutes to reflect on your team performance and share suggestions for enhancing your teamwork in the future. What went well?”
  2. Suggested order:
     1. Students share their thoughts first, and co-facilitator elaborates based on SPTE form.
     2. Facilitators and SPs can share their thoughts next, as discussed in advance.

**Next Co-facilitator asks students**: “What could have made the team’s performance even better?”

**Co-facilitator should end feedback on a positive note**

* 1. Reiterate things the students did well that led to good interaction with each other and with the SP.
  2. Ask students to reflect on what they will take from this experience that they can carry forward to the work they do in real life.
  3. Ask students what they think they’d like to learn more about from other professions.

**Wrap Up (5 min)**
 Thank students for participating and remind them to write down names/emails for post-session survey

***Post Session Information:***

1. By 5 pm Monday after the session:

Co-facilitator will email the three finalized SPTE Feedback & Reflection forms (one per student team) to the lead facilitator for review

1. By 5 pm Tuesday after the session:

Lead facilitator will review and then email the completed SPTE Feedback & Reflection forms to the students
