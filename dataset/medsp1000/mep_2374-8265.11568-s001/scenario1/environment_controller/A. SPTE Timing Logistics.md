**Timing of Admission Scenario**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time** | **Location and Participants** | **Description** |
| **Gather and Introductions**  **10 min** | Students meet in assigned team room | Once students all arrive, Facilitators and Students introduce themselves  Facilitators explain flow of experience, answer questions, then leave for observation room |
| **Team Prep**  **5 min** | Students in team room  Facilitators observe | Students briefly decide how they will gather information needed for admission planning  Need to perform the National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (NIHSS), gather medication list, and any other history or assessment they deem relevant |
| **SP Encounter 1**  **20 min** | Students in SP room | Students go to SP room, introduce themselves/roles, perform NIHSS and get med list |
| **Admission order planning**  **20 min** | Students in team room | Students create collaborative admission plan using template as a guide |
| **SP Encounter 2**  **10 min** | Students in SP room | Students return to SP room, discuss their plan with SP, address any concerns |
| **Group Feedback/Reflection**  **20 min**  **(Start by 1:35, 3:05, or 4:35)** | Facilitators join students in SP room | Facilitators join students and SP in room, lead debrief of team experience.  Students reflect on what worked well and identify opportunities for improvement |
| **Wrap-up**  **5 min** | Assigned rooms | **Please remember to take post-experience survey!** |
