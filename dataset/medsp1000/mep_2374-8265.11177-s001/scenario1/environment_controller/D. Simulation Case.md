**Runtime Prompting**

1. If participants do not begin discussion of blood pressure before t-PA, the embedded nurse may express concern about the patient’s blood pressure.
2. If point-of-care glucose is not obtained prior to departure for the CT suite, the embedded nurse or simulated family member may remind learners of the patient’s history of diabetes.
3. If learners do not continue neurological assessments after t-PA administration, a simulated family member may ask for an update on how the patient is doing after receiving medication.
