Ideal Scenario

Case started by parent role: “Help, my baby Julia needs help! She’s coughing and can’t breathe!”

First Person: Enters the room, checks for responsiveness, sees difficulty breathing, cyanosis, and hypoxia.

[Baby should be making noise and grunting]

“Patient is in distress. I need help!” calls for help and then get gloves

Second responder enters and gets gloves as the First Person conducts rapid assessment of perfusion, respiratory examination including airway, breath sounds, and pulse oximeter reading.

“She has a pulse, has a clear airway and is breathing adequately.”

Both responders should help get oxygen applied to the patient, ideally nasal cannula and then ask the parent for more details, as the remainder of team arrives.

Team leader assigns roles: “My name is Dr. Xavier, and I will be in charge, can you [Airway] continue watching her respiratory status and prepare the BVM and suction, in case we need it, and can you [1st responder] continue a head to toe examination, and can you [3rd person] get the remainder of the monitors applied while I talk with the parent.”

When asked for details, and while monitors are applied, parent can state:

“She was admitted for hypoxia and bronchiolitis, she was taken off oxygen this morning and has had more trouble since. She has been coughing a lot.”

Team Leader: Shares Mental Model (single problem: hypoxia)

Stop scenario if all objectives met, praise team for specific points, rotate roles, and restart with next round.
