Ideal Scenario

Case started by parent role: “Help, my baby needs help! She can’t breathe!”

First Person: Enters the room, checks for responsiveness, sees difficulty breathing, cyanosis, and hypoxia.”

[Baby should be quiet]

“Patient is in distress. I need help!” pushes code button and then gets gloves

Expected Actions:
Second responder enters and gets gloves as the First Person conducts rapid assessment of perfusion, respiratory examination including pulse, airway, and breath sounds while obtaining pulse oximeter reading.

Instructor reports that there is a great deal of secretions in the airway.

“She has a pulse, has some secretions in her airway, and is not breathing adequately! I’m opening the airway and beginning ventilations.”

Prepare suctioning while bagging

After suctioning and oxygen, team leader should share partial mental model recognizing tachycardia and compensated shock.

Administer bolus 20ml/kg of NS or LR via push-pull method

Team Leader shares mental model Five problems: airway obstruction (now cleared), apnea (continuing) hypoxia (now improving), compensated shock (now improving), and need to transfer to intensive care

Instructor may need to encourage interns to proceed with intubation as if adequate supervision were present.

Intubation:

Instructor provides RSI medications as instructor
(as if you were the team leader of a responding ICU team)

Team should follow choreography of intubation

*During intubation, drop oxygen saturation slowly over 20-30 seconds into 70s until airway established, then turn oxygen back up to 94%.*
