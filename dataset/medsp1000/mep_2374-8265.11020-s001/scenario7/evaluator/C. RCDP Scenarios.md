Ideal Scenario

Case started by parent role: “Help, my baby needs help! She collapsed!”

First Responder: Enters the room, checks for responsiveness, sees no response. Shakes and shouts to make sure.

[Baby should be quiet]

“Patient is unresponsive. I’m calling for help!” pushes code button and then gets gloves

Expected Actions:
Second responder enters and gets gloves as the First Responder conducts rapid assessment of perfusion, respiratory examination including airway, breath sounds and pulse oximeter reading.

* First responder feels for carotid pulse 5-10 seconds
* Pulses should not be found, says “There is no pulse, I’m starting compressions” and starts compressions
* Second responder becomes *airway*, gets BVM, hooks up O2, and starts coordinated CPR, remainder of team arrives

Initial Priorities: See Choreography

Place stool for compressor, backboard, defibrillation pad placement, length-based tape, set defibrillator, and charge it.

First Pulse-Rhythm Check See Choreography sheet

* Lead directs bedside provider into position, and for compressor to hold compressions as soon as initial priorities are finished.
* Lead: “At end of cycle of 15, [bedside provider] feel for a pulse, [compressor] hold compressions and step down, I will analyze rhythm, [airway] please be ready to start 2-minute timer”

*For positions labeled in [] encourage leader to use the name of each team member.*

* Bedside provider feels for 5-10 seconds and reports, “There is a pulse”

*Most common error at this point is simply not knowing a way to do this as a team. One way to handle this learning point is to time how long it takes to get to pulse-rhythm analysis, then provide this data as feedback, show them the choreography, and repeat until actions are smooth. Another common error seen at this time is for team to want to do more steps before pulse and rhythm check, such as place ECG monitors, give medications, obtain a BP.*

* “We have ROSC, hold compressions, assess airway and breathing, get BP, temperature, draw labs and get an EKG”
* Share Mental Model and continue rescue breathing.

STOP and round up: Praise success with data, i.e. it took you 180 seconds to get to first rhythm check
