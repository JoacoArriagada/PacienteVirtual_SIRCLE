**Minnie Pauls**

SESSION I: Part 1

**PATIENT PRESENTATION**

Ms. Pauls is a 48 year old female from Masontown, WV who presents to your office for a check-up to establish care as a new patient after not seeing a doctor in 15 years because she did not have insurance. She states that she was “mostly healthy” during that time and didn’t think that there was any reason for her to get a check-up. She comes in today with no pressing concerns, but she states that she hasn’t been feeling “herself”. Ever since her 46th birthday, two years ago, she has been having trouble sleeping and frequently feels like she is hot and flushed during the day and sweats at night. Her menstrual bleeding has become very irregular. She has noticed bleeding between her periods and sometimes her periods skip a month. She also seems to get grumpy with her husband and children at the smallest things.

On further questioning of her bleeding patterns, you find she has spotting almost continuously and has to wear a pad most days. The bleeding increases following sexual intercourse, and she has mild pain following sexual intercourse. However, she only has heavy bleeding for about four days every one to two months. This abnormal pattern of bleeding between her periods has been going on for several months. She has no pelvic or abdominal pain.

As Ms. Pauls is your new patient and has not been followed for some time, you decide you need to go through a full history and physical to learn more about her and establish a baseline for her future care as well as further investigate this problem of abnormal uterine bleeding.

One of the most important topics that you want to discuss with her at this new patient visit is health maintenance.
