**Minnie Pauls**

SESSION I: Part 1

**PATIENT PRESENTATION**

Ms. Pauls is a 48 year old female from Masontown, WV who presents to your office for a check-up to establish care as a new patient after not seeing a doctor in 15 years because she did not have insurance. She states that she was “mostly healthy” during that time and didn’t think that there was any reason for her to get a check-up. She comes in today with no pressing concerns, but she states that she hasn’t been feeling “herself”. Ever since her 46th birthday, two years ago, she has been having trouble sleeping and frequently feels like she is hot and flushed during the day and sweats at night. Her menstrual bleeding has become very irregular. She has noticed bleeding between her periods and sometimes her periods skip a month. She also seems to get grumpy with her husband and children at the smallest things.

You reassure Ms. Pauls that many of these changes are typical in the period leading up the “change of life”, or menopause. You explain to her that for months or years leading up to menopause, during a period called perimenopause, women can expect irregular periods, vaginal dryness, loss of libido, hot flashes, night sweats, sleep problems, mood changes, weight gain and changes in metabolism, thinning of hair, and drying of skin. She is glad now that she knows this process won’t go on forever. She says that although this is bothersome, it isn’t making her life “terrible”, so she would prefer to forgo any treatment as she “does not like to take medicines”.

On further questioning of her bleeding patterns, you find she has spotting almost continuously and has to wear a pad most days. The bleeding increases following sexual intercourse, and she has mild pain following sexual intercourse. However, she only has heavy bleeding for about four days every one to two months. This abnormal pattern of bleeding between her periods has been going on for several months. She has no pelvic or abdominal pain.

As Ms. Pauls is your new patient and has not been followed for some time, you decide you need to go through a full history and physical to learn more about her and establish a baseline for her future care as well as further investigate this problem of abnormal uterine bleeding.

One of the most important topics that you want to discuss with her at this new patient visit is health maintenance such as the need for periodic mammograms every year to two years, tetanus shots every 10 years, and pap smears periodically based on guidelines.

### Previous Medical History

Her blood pressure was a little high when she was younger, and she had “low thyroid”. She had genital warts when she was in her 20’s but doesn’t know of any other sexual transmitted diseases that she has had.

### Previous Surgical History

She had an appendectomy as a kid and had C-Sections when both of her boys were born. After her second C-Section the doctor “talked her into getting fixed”.

### Current Medications

She is not taking any medications currently. She says that she used to be on something for her thyroid when she was young, but since she hasn’t had insurance she quit following up to get her medicine refilled.

### Social History

She has been smoking for 20 years and currently smokes 2 packs per day. “Two packs for two boys”. She drinks alcohol on occasion and does not use any illegal drugs. She works in the dry cleaning business part-time. She lives with her two boys who are 20 and 15. She divorced her husband 12 years ago and has dated a number of men since. She is currently sexually active with one partner and does not use contraception since she “got her tubes tied”.

### Family History

Her father had a melanoma and died of a heart attack. Her mother is still alive and has hypertension. Her sons are both healthy. She doesn’t know much about the rest of her family.

### Allergies

She states that her “eyes get itchy in the spring time” and she “sneezes like a tornado”.

### Transfusion History

She has no history of transfusions.

### Immunization history

She knows that she had all of her immunizations as a kid, but the last time she had a shot was when she stepped on a rusty nail before her sons were born.

### OBstetric

She is a G3P2012. She had a miscarriage when she was “young”. Both of her sons were delivered via C-Section because they “wouldn’t come down”. She hasn’t had a Pap smear since her youngest son was born. She remembers them having to use a microscope after her Pap one time but doesn’t remember why. She has never had a mammogram. For the last two years, she has had irregular periods and sometimes skips a month. Her periods have been heavy for years.
