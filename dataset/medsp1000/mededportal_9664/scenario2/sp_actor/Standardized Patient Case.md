**Standardized Patient Case**

Oral-systemic manifestations of type-2-diabetes

**Standardized Patient:**

Patient Demographics:

Age: 82 years of age

Sex: Male

Race: Hispanic American

Height: 5 ft. 0 inches

Weight: 185 lb.

History of present complaint:

“My gums are swollen and bleed when I touch them. They do not hurt, however Mr. Dove states, “I have postponed my appointment with the dentist because I do not have dental insurance I can only afford to have my teeth cleaned every couple of years.

Scenario Background:

Mr. Dove’s posterior teeth have some sharp edges, his tongue feels sore, and occasionally bleeds, he has had bad taste in his mouth for the past 3 months he uses over the counter mouth rinse to remedy the symptoms. He wears a partial removable dental prosthesis however, during chewing function the prosthesis lacerates his gums and underlying mucosa, he uses copious amounts of denture adhesive to reduce the irritation and improve the retention and fit. Mr. Dove has difficulty eating leafy vegetables, fruits, grains, meat, and unable to maintain his diabetic diet and resorts to foods that are high in carbohydrates and refined sugars. Food consumption options are limited if he does not wear his prosthesis. There have been occasions that his blood glucose has dropped below 70mg/dL and he becomes weak, has blurred vision, with episodes of confusion. Mr. Dove is able to manage his diabetes however there have been occasions that he has forgotten to take his medications. His brother Larry (79 years of age) as his sole caregiver has helped Mr. Dove get back on track.

*Other significant symptoms*: The past few weeks Mr. Dove has experienced feeling of dizziness and has stumbled while in the bathroom. Mr. Dove is also challenged with hearing impairment and wears a left ear hearing aid. He had a left total hip replacement 20 months ago and currently has independent ambulation.

Chief Complaint*:* Bleeding gums

Past medical history**:** hypertension, edema of the lower extremities, diabetes, left total hip replacement, degenerative joint disease, benign paroxysmal positional vertigo, depression, hyperlipemia, urinary incontinence (occasional), peripheral neuropathy

Medications and Allergies: Verapamil SR 240mg once a day, Furosemide 40 mg daily prn, Humalog insulin 6U with each meal and sliding scale, Wellbutrin 100mg three times a day, Lipitor 40mg once a day, and over the counter Tylenol as needed. Herbal Supplements: Vit D 1000IUdaily, Vit A, Centrum Silver multivitamin with zinc, ginkgo biloba. Allergic to PENICILLIN

Family/social history

Mr. Dove has completed 6th grade education, has lived in Las Vegas for the last 10 years, and suffered personal tragedy when he lost his wife about 4 years ago. *Occupation:* Mr. Dove worked at General Motors as an assembly line autoworker for over 40 years in Chicago then 5years prior to retirement, he was a construction worker in Las Vegas. Mr. Dove has two children Michael and Katie. They are both married with their own children. Michael and his family live in Florida and Katie and her family live in Manhattan. They love their father (Mr. Dove) very much; however, they cannot provide any care giving.

*Financial/living arrangements:* Mr. Dove owns his home lives with his brother Larry and has small retirement pension and social security being the only source of income.

*Activities of Daily Living/Instrumental Activities of Daily Living:* During the day he watches TV, gambles at the local casino one to two times a week, has lunch and plays bingo at the senior center once a month. He follows-up with his regular doctor visits, goes grocery shopping, and attends church at least once a month. Whenever he visits the casino he smokes a number of cigarettes, has a couple of drinks (i.e. gin and tonic or coke and rum) and takes advantage of their elaborate buffet.

Oral assessment:

Lips: dry chapped white/red/ulcerated patches bleeding at corners of mouth

Tongue: patch that is red and or white with ulceration on the lateral border, and swollen, metallic taste, sensation burning and numbness

Gums and tissues: swollen, bleeding around remaining posterior teeth, generalized redness, there is draining sinus on the lower right posterior area

Saliva: saliva is thick and ropey, and very little is present

Natural teeth: over 5 teeth are broken and decayed on the upper posterior arch. Over 3 teeth are worn down on the lower posterior arch, and anterior teeth have large tooth colored restorations.

Dentures: patient has one broken metal area in the partial denture, rarely wears it because it hurts his gums; he needs to use copious amounts of denture adhesive to make the partial denture functional.

Oral hygiene: Food particles (soft and hard) debris in most areas of the mouth especially on lower anterior area, and the upper right and left posterior area. Pt also expressed that he has been told that he has halitosis. His mouth feels dry with burning and numbing sensation.
