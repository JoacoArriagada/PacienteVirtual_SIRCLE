ORAL SYSTEMIC MANIFESTATIONS OF TYPE-2-DIABETES

INSTRUCTOR GUIDE:

**Purpose of module**:

The purpose of this interprofessional patient centered module is to train diverse (discipline examples: physicians, nurses, dentists/oral health professionals, allied health care professionals, pharmacists, nutritionist, psychologist, and sociologist) health professional learners to collaboratively address the bidirectional oral-systemic manifestations of type 2 diabetes and comorbidities in older adults. Identify at-risk older adults, recognize the oral-systemic pathophysiologic relationship, and incorporate the oral status into physical assessment and care planning. Interprofessional team building skills that integrate oral-systemic management through effective communication, collaboration, and coordination of care delineates the role of each team learner as a member of the team as well as a discipline expert.

**Conceptual background**:

This interprofessional comprehensive patient centered concept module will teach the interprofessional learners to collaboratively assess, recognize, and incorporate oral status into routine physical assessment of older adults. The interprofessional learners should receive the didactic content of the two power point presentations prior to patient (standardized/simulation) encounters.

The diabetes mellitus oral-systemic manifestations power point provides a comprehensive biopsychosocial overview and links oral disease with the systemic disease type-2-diabetes in older adults. This power point should serve as background information in the joint learning environment to enhance and facilitate the active learning activity.

The interprofessional team-building power point highlights teambuilding and an approach to manage the oral-systemic manifestations of type 2 diabetes in older adults. Included are team-building skills, coordination of oral-systemic healthcare, and development of an interprofessional treatment plan. For educator convenience, this power point can be used in its entirety or tailored to meet the specific needs of the interprofessional academic environment.

**Objectives**:

1. Discuss the prevalence of oral disease in older adults with type 2 diabetes. (slide # 3-6 DM)
2. Recognize the bidirectional impact of oral disease and systemic disease. (slide # 7-18 and 19-24 DM)
3. Identify the biopsychosocial aspects of at risk patient for oral disease. (slide # 7-12, 25-36 DM and #1-13 IPE)
4. Describe the role of health care professional(s) in integration of oral health promotion and disease prevention within an interprofessional environment.(slide # 14-27 IPE and 35,36 DM)
5. Discuss shared responsibilities and coordination of care to manage the oral-systemic health care needs of older adults with type-2-diabetes (slide #24-40 IPE)
6. Develop interprofessional care plan(s) (slide #29-40 IPE)

This module is designed to be an interprofessional classroom power point presentations followed by active-learning encounter. The power point(s) include speaker notes as well as references for additional in-depth information for the content presented. It is recommended that the learners successfully complete the evaluation prior to active-learning encounter(s).

**Classroom-Presentation**: Didactic power point(s) presentation followed by evaluation.

**Active-Learning Encounter(s)**:

Following the classroom presentations, interprofessional teams are structured. The composition of each interprofessional health care team includes a representative learner from each health care discipline. Each discipline member of the team receives a brief patient history. Each discipline team member will introduce him/her self as well as indentify the discipline they represent to the patient. Patient assessment is in accordance to each discipline regulatory code, guidelines, and standards. Each discipline team member will interview, examine the patient (standardized and or simulation) identify at-risk patients, assess oral status, and systemic condition, gather data, establish oral-systemic treatment goals, outcomes benchmarks, communicate/discuss shared responsibility, coordination of care to formulate an oral-systemic treatment plan. Each healthcare professional learner will be both a discipline expert and a member of the team.

Educator(s) facilitated post-encounter debriefing session enables the learners to self- assess, reflect on the interprofessional experience, bridge oral-systemic teaching concept, peer discussion, strengthen teambuilding skills, to develop an oral-systemic interprofessional treatment plan. The treatment plan identifies each member of the team as a discipline expert as well as a member of the team to coordinate the oral-systemic health care needs of older adults with type-2-diabetes.
