# History of Present Illness:

Pat Martin is a 37 year-old patient. Today Pat is here for complaints of progressive low back pain. Pat reports a gradually increasing back pain that is worse with prolonged sitting or with heavy physical activity. This has been present for the last 6 months or so. The pain can go from mild to moderate or severe depending on the day and activity level. Pain does not radiate down legs. Pat denies fevers, chills, and bowel or bladder incontinence. Pat works as packaging clerk and is worried about being able to work. Pat finds it difficult to make it through a whole day without taking several pills (usually ibuprofen, 2 or 3 pills every 4 hours). Pat has tried to see about getting the back pain seen as a workrelated injury, but because there was no direct injury (insidious onset) was told the pain it was likely because of Pat’s weight and not related to work. Pat admits being reluctant to go to the doctor, and doesn’t have any insurance, but finds the pain is just getting to be too much.
