**Case Scenario**:

A 6 month old baby girl was admitted to the hospital yesterday because of a cough and poor PO intake. The mom comes to find you to tell you that she is looking worse and has started to make a strange noise every time she breathes in that she has never heard before.

**HPI:**

This is a 6 month old girl with no past medical history who has been having a cough and poor PO intake for the past 3 days. Her parents brought her to the hospital because they were concerned that she could have pneumonia because of all the coughing. Her chest x-ray was normal but she was admitted for monitoring and IVF hydration. She has never been sick before, she was born full term and she received her 2 and 4 month vaccines but has still not received her 6 month vaccines.
