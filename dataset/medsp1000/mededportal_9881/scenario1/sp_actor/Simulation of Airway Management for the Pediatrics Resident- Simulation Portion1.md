**Case Scenario**:

A 21 month old baby boy was admitted to the hospital for dehydration in the setting of an illness with vomiting and diarrhea. The mother runs into the hallway and yells “help, something is wrong with my baby!” As you are the only person standing in the hallway you decide to see if you can help. You walk in and see the baby’s arms and legs jerking in a rhythmic fashion. The mom says he was totally fine then he just started making these odd movements

**HPI:**

This is a 21 month old boy with no past medical history who has been having vomiting and diarrhea for the past 3 days. He hasn’t been able to take anything PO other than water. His parents brought him in to the hospital because he hadn’t made a wet diaper in 1 day. He has no past medical history, he has no allergies, he was born full term, has all of his vaccines and has no allergies. In the ED he received 2 IVF boluses.
