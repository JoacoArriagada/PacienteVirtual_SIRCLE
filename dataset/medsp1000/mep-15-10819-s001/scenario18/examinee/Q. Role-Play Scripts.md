Module Six Role Play Scenario Two

**Facilitator:** Mary has mentioned to you that her son, Tom, has been asking questions recently, and she doesn’t know how to handle them. Tom is 17, a high school junior, and has a history of depression which developed in the context of his parent’s divorce. With ongoing therapy and medication, he has been doing well emotionally and is making good grades in school.

Even though she is in remission, back to work and activities, Mary perceives Tom to be more anxious. She’s especially worried about how to handle with Tom the issue of the cancer possibly relapsing. She asks if it’s ok if she brings him to an appointment so he can meet you and talk about his concerns.
