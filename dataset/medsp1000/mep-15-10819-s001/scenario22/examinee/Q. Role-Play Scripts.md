Module Seven Role Play Scenario Two

**Facilitator** (to learner): Mary participated in the trial; she did not respond but fortunately had no side effects. She is grateful to you for having given her the chance to try a new treatment. Mary’s progression continues, and she is becoming more symptomatic. She has developed incomplete bowel obstructions, necessitating hospitalization, and has also developed an enterovaginal fistula, which periodically drains a foul-smelling discharge. It causes her great distress. She is hospitalized and after appropriate palliative medical therapy, she is more comfortable, but isn’t eating much, and is losing weight. Her previous ascites has stabilized. Her pain level is satisfactory after receiving a superior hypogastric plexus block. She comes to your office for a follow up visit. Your focus is on explaining DNR in the context of Mary’s religious faith.
