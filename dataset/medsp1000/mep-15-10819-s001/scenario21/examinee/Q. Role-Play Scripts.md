Module Seven Role Play Scenario One

Facilitator (to learner): Mary has had 4 regimens for her relapsed disease. Each regimen has been associated with a shorter duration of remission than the previous one. On office visits prior to changes in therapy, she has stated “I’m still hoping you’ll get this in remission”.

She had side effects of diarrhea and nausea from her last regimen but persevered. She has told you repeatedly “I’m going to fight.” The most recent CT after 12 weeks of the regimen shows disease progression.

She has become weaker over time. She has stopped working and is on disability. At home, she can ambulate but with effort. She can take care of activities of daily living. She has an aide assist her during the day while Peter is at work. Peter has curtailed his working hours to be with her.

She now comes with Peter to discuss her situation. In anticipation of this meeting you have thought about your agenda and found a phase 1 trial in a nearby cancer center for which Mary is eligible. You have contacted the P.I. and he has agreed to evaluate her for the trial. He tells you that of ten patients accrued so far, there has been one response lasting 4 months. However, you think palliative care would be a better option for Mary. The primary task is to begin the discussion on palliative care. (Th facilitator or another faculty member should step in as Peter for this scenario.)
