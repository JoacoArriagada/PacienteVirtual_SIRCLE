Module Seven Role Play Scenario Three

**Facilitator** (to learner): Mary is starting to rapidly decline. Although lucid from time to time, she has been at times in excruciating pain at home which was causing the family much grief. Many phone calls from hospice ensued and finally, in collaboration with Palliative Care and Hospice care, you feel she needs in-patient management of her pain. She is admitted to the Palliative Care Unit. Her son Tommy is a constant presence at her bedside. Peter is also there most of the time, though he has to leave from time to time because of work obligations. Laura and Paul are there almost every evening. Richard also has stayed in town to help with the bedside vigil. Her priest has also visited her. You go to see her as she has requested you visit her. The task here is to attend to a dying patient and explore the dynamics of a bedside vigil.
