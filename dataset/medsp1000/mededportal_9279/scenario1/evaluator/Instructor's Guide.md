# Background:

This case was created from a patient that was seen in the Emergency Department. A similar simulation case has not yet been published. Our intent is to use this simulation to prepare medical students and residents to recognize and diagnose pyloric stenosis. This case also allows the medical student and/or resident to assess a patient that is desaturating after aspiration and how to handle fluid resuscitation in a young patient that is difficult to obtain intravenous access.

# Implementation:

This can be given as simulation case in an Emergency Department, Simulation Lab or Trauma bay with a high-fidelity simulator manikin, monitor, peripheral IV and IO, airway equipment with suction, ultrasound machine and code blue cart. The roles would be played by an ED resident, airway resident or respiratory therapist and a nurse. Set up for simulation should take less than 5 minutes. The entire case should take approximately 10-20 minutes, depending on the experience of the examinee and discussion with

debriefing is approximately 5-10 minutes.

# Previous usage of case:

This case was used as a simulation in a Simulation Laboratory for emergency residents and medical students and was successful. There were no pitfalls to report.

Limitations of resources: None.
