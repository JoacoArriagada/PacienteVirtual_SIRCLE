# OSTE Precepting Case: Toddler with Vomiting and Diarrhea Standardized Learner Instructions

Standardized Learner Cheat Sheet to keep in Exam Rooms

# Daniella Ortiz

Chief Complaint: 18 month old with her mother for vomiting, fever and diarrhea.

HPI (“History of present illness): Daniella is an 18 month old here with her mother for a sick visit for vomiting, fever and diarrhea.

Her mom is really worried about her Daniella.

She has been really sick for the past 3 days – vomiting about 6 times a day initially, 3 times the next day and once so far today. No blood or bile.

She’s had diarrhea about 10 times the first day, then 3 times the next day and once so far today, also with no blood.

She had a fever to 101.3 on the first day of illness. She was given Tylenol, which resolved the fever. It has not recurred.

Mom says Daniella has not urinated for the past 18 hours – since 7PM last night. She insists the diaper was dry this morning.

No travel, no sick contacts, not in daycare

I’m worried that she might be really sick.

# Pause and wait for attending to ask for more

If asks about drinking:

She has been drinking well, but “vomits everything.” She hasn’t been eating normally, however. She just prefers to drink –just eating a bite or two of food about twice a day since her symptoms started (you did not ask what or exactly how much she has been drinking).

If asks for more history/ information

Her birth history was normal – full term, vaginal delivery, no complications She has no significant past medical history

Social history: Daniella lives with her two parents, her father works driving a taxicab. Her mother takes odd jobs cleaning houses. The grandmother watches children when mom working. Daniella has one older sibling, a 5 yo brother . All are healthy. No one smokes in the home.

If asked specifically about family history   
Be a little flustered if asked about Family History. You had meant to mention this when you did your initial presentation, but you just forgot about it.

There is a distant cousin with a rare genetic disorder that involves vomiting called citrullinemia. The cousin had severe vomiting starting around 8 months of age and ultimately died from the disorder at age 12 months. I am worried that maybe Daniella could have this disorder. (You have never heard of this disorder.)

# If asked about vital signs:

You did not take vitals signs on this patient. The nurse did not either. You are embarrassed, but promise to check them – especially the heart rate – when you return to the room. You know that the heart rate would be increased (tachycardic) if dehydrated.

# If asked for a summary:

In summary this is an 18 month old female with no significant past medical history who presents with 3 day history of fever, vomiting and diarrhea. The vomiting and diarrhea is starting to get better and she has been drinking but has not voided in 18 hours. Her physical exam is really unremarkable.

# If asked for your assessment

You are most worried about citrullinemia because of the family history. Other diagnoses you are considering

• Gastroenteritis, most likely viral but could be bacterial (fever suggests there is an infection GE reflux (goes along with vomiting but wouldn’t expect fever) • Milk protein allergy (goes along with diarrhea but wouldn’t expect fever)

# If asked about your plan:

Discuss that even though patient has not urinated in a long time, you aren’t too worried about dehydration as she looks so good. Suggest send her home and “we can just watch this.” (Don’t offer a plan to educate the family about the signs and symptoms of dehydration unless prompted in some way.)
