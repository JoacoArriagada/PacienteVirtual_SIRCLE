INFORMATION FOR PHYSICIAN

# MEDICAL HISTORY

You are on duty one early morning when you get a trauma alert with a 20 y/o female with chest pain, hypotension, lethargy and difficulty breathing and she arrives in the emergency room. You resuscitate the patient along with the trauma team and her vital signs stabilize. She is now heading over to radiology with the trauma team for imaging. There is suspicion of head injury due to initial mental status. The nurse tells you that the mother is anxiously waiting in the ED family room and does not know about the patient condition.

**SETTING**

The patients’ mother is sitting is alone in a waiting room in the ED, sitting in a chair.

**TASK**

You are the ED physician--tell the mother the status of her child.
