# FAMILY MEMBER PROFILE

# MEDICAL HISTORY

Your daughter, Ms. MVA, is a 20 y/o college student with no history of any medical problems. This morning at 3 am you received a call from the police that she was involved in a major car crash on Highway 420 and taken by ambulance to the state level-1 trauma center. You have driven for 3 hours to get to the trauma center and have been waiting in the waiting room and you have not met the doctor. You did see a nurse who said "your daughter is having some problems. The doctors are with her and will be out to talk with you soon".

# SOCIAL HISTORY

You have three children, ages 25, 20, and 17. Your parents are deceased; you have no siblings, and are divorced. You work as an administrative assistant.

**SETTING**

You are alone, in a quiet waiting room, located in the ED, sitting on a chair.

**TASK**

Your partner, in the role of the ED physician, will break the bad news to you. Some questions/comments you may wish to pose (or any you think are appropriate to the situation) include:

* How can this be? She was fine when I saw her last week!
* Are you sure it is my daughter?
* She’s never been sick a day in her life!
* What should I tell the children?
* Should I call my ex-husband?
* Will she live?
* How serious are the injuries?
* I want to see her right now!

**ROLE-PLAY CASE #2**

INFORMATION FOR PHYSICIAN

# MEDICAL HISTORY

You are on duty one early morning when you get a trauma alert with a 20 y/o female with chest pain, hypotension, lethargy and difficulty breathing and she arrives in the emergency room. You resuscitate the patient along with the trauma team and her vital signs stabilize after chest tube placement, intubation and respiratory support, splinting of an open lower limb fracture and 3 litres of crystalloid infusion. She is now heading over to radiology with the trauma team for a full body CT scan imaging. There is suspicion of undetermined head injury due to initial mental status. The nurse tells you that the mother is anxiously waiting in the ED family room and does not know about the patient condition.

**SETTING**

The patients’ mother is sitting is alone in a waiting room in the ED, sitting in a chair.

**TASK**

You are the ED physician--tell the mother the status of her child.
