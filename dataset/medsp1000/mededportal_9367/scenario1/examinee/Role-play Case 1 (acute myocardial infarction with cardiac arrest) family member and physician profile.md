INFORMATION FOR PHYSICIAN

# MEDICAL HISTORY

You are on duty one early morning when a 54 y/o with chest pain is brought to the emergency room. While you are seeing another patient the ED nurse informs you about the patient's arrival, and tells you that he "looks stable and has normal vital signs". You tell her you'll be over shortly. Five minutes later the nurse runs in to say the patient is ‘coding’. You supervise an attempt at cardiopulmonary resuscitation that fails and you pronounce the patient dead. The nurse tells you that the spouse is in the waiting room and does not know about the cardiac arrest.

**SETTING**

The patients’ spouse is sitting is alone in a waiting room in the ED, sitting in a chair.

**TASK**

You are the Emergency Department physician--tell the husband/wife that their spouse has died.
