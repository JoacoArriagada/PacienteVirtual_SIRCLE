**Faculty Guidelines for Debriefing (post resuscitation death)**

(simulated or real patient case scenario)

The following is a model for a quick debriefing following a patient’s death (similar guidelines/format may be followed for post-resuscitation debriefings):

* **Review** the medical events that led up to the death.
* **Discuss the experience of patient** during final stage of dying process and of the **family** after death notification.
* **Address unanswered concerns** about the death.
  + *What concerns or questions about care did this patient’s death raise for you?*
  + *What did you learn from this patient’s dying process and death?*
* **Explore student’s emotional reaction** to the death.
  + *What was this patient’s death like for you? How are you dealing with it?*
  + *How did you feel about the care you were able to provide the patient? The family?*
  + *Are there aspects of the death that feel difficult or upsetting as you reflect back on it?*
* **Validate the student’s emotional response.**
  + *Your identification with the family and affection for the patient probably allowed you to be especially responsive to their needs.*
  + *Even though it feels terrible now, your warm feelings about this patient/family are a testament to your fine doctoring.*
  + *It was really tough taking care of this patient because he was so angry at everyone, but you hung in there with him.…*
* Consider **sharing your own response** to the death.
  + *Avoid saying “I know how you feel”.*
* **Encourage further discussion** if additional thoughts/feelings arise.
* **Review plans for bereavement** follow-up.
