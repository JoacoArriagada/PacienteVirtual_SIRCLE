**SCENE 4: Change in Patient’s Status/Movement of Pt to New Location**

Purpose: To inform patient’s family of a change in status and location. To address the importance of calling family as soon as a patient is stabilized, to avoid a surprised family member.

**Opening quote: “Where is my husband?”…….”Why is he in the ICU”**

**Action 1:** Camilla is angry, demanding and frightened, looking for answers.

***Positive Response:*** Start by asking what the patient knows because you haven’t talked to her before. Take time to LISTEN. Name the anger that the patient is demonstrating, being empathic while doing so (e.g “I know, this must be very shocking for you”). Understand what the patient/family is going through. Respect how often the family has been with the patient (They were only gone a small time when this all happened)

**Negative Response:** Student will be defensive, asks loaded questions, defensive, or deflective, e.g., “I am just an intern.”

***Action 2:  Camilla is frightened and guilty*** (because she left to go to hotel & has some burgeoning awareness that she pressed the morphine button maybe too many times).

***Positive Response****:* Student will be there for Camilla, listening to her concerns, redirecting her away from her feelings of guilt. Asks about family support. (Ask what the patient knows, takes time to listen.) Names Camilla’s emotions. Explores why she feels guilty. Demonstrates respect towards her and how much time she has spent at the bedside.

***Negative Response:*** Student unaware of the concerns driving Camilla’s reactions. Student overdramatizes or downplays respiratory arrest, unaware of her immediate needs.
