**SCENE 7: Discuss DNAR and potential transfer to Skilled Nursing Facility**

 Purpose:  To initiate discussions about DNR/DNI—again the transition in care from cure to comfort, to avoid needless suffering.

Opening quote: "Is there any possibility he will wake up?" “Is there any hope?”

**Action 1:** Camilla is numb, helpless and confused, looking for hope and reassurance; she feels family members blame her and that they have deserted her; but she also feels it is somehow her fault.

**Action 2:** Camilla is appalled at the suggestion of DNR. She is still expecting a miracle.
