**SCENE 6: Discuss Need for Tracheostomy/Percutaneous Gastrostomy Endoscopic Tube**

Purpose:  To discuss the need for tracheostomy and PEG tube placement, with focus that these procedures are not curative, they only make care easier. Avoid false hope.

**Opening quote: "What is the plan for Alonzo today? What are you going to do to help him now?”**

**Action 1:**  Camilla is digging in her heels and ready to get to the next progressive step in Alonzo’s returning to “normal.”

***Positive Response:***  Explore what Camilla knows about the patient’s future, and why he is on the ventilator now. Name her fear and her uncertainty. Use an I wish statement. Ask if you can talk about a procedure that would make the patient more comfortable (it will not cure him, change his neurologic prognosis, etc).

***Negative Response:*** Use jargon to describe the procedure. Assume it is a done deal that this procedure is happening with or without Camilla understanding that it is only about Alonzo’s care, not making an improvement in his condition. Student is very matter-of-fact.
