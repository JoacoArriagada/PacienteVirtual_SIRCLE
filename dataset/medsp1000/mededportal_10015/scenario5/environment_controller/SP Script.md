**SCENE 5b: Reprioritize care goals with a family that is difficult (action 2 is about family who says care is bad due to patient’s race)**

Purpose: To discuss the consequences of care when a patient has this type of brain injury---the prioritization of care when an event like this one has happened.

**Opening quote: “When is my husband going to have his leg fixed?”  When is the surgery scheduled?”**

**Action 1:** Camilla is completely in denial, angry and empowered with “family physician’s” opinion that her husband will be fine but needs immediate orthopedic surgery.

**Action 2:** Camilla believes that the surgery is not taking place because of racial disparities and says, “You say that you aren’t doing surgery because of Alonzo’s new injury, but I suspect that if his name was Mr. Smith, you would be doing that surgery.”
