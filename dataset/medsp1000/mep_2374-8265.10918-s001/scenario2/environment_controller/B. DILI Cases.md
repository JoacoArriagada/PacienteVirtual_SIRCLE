# Case 2

**HPI:** A 25-year-old bodybuilder presents to your outpatient clinic with jaundice. This has been gradually progressive over the last several weeks. He denies any recent viral illness symptoms. Denies abdominal pain. In addition, He complains that he has lost 10 lbs in the past three months.

**PMH:** none - He has been in good health most of his life.

**PSHx:** none

**Social history:** No new sexual contacts. He works out six days a week at a small neighborhood gym. He does not drink alcohol, smoke cigarettes or use recreational drugs.

**Medications:** He takes no medications regularly. Denies acetaminophen. He has been using a steroid sold to him by a friend from his gym for the past six months.

**Dietary:** He drinks two protein shakes daily, prepared with a commercial powder he purchases from a major nutritional supplement store.

**Exam:**

General - Appears healthy but jaundiced.

Vitals - Temp 36.9 C, HR 65, RR 16, BP 130/80.

His heart and lung exam are normal.

Abdomen - soft, nondistended, good bowel sounds, nontender. Liver appears to be of normal size and contour. Spleen is not palpable.

Laboratory testing reveals a normal complete blood count and basic metabolic panel. His AST and ALT are normal, but bilirubin is 25 mg/dL. His INR is 1.1.
