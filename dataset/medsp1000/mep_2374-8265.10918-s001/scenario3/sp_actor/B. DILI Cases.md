# Case 3

**HPI:** A 65-year-old man was recently hospitalized after his first generalized tonic-clonic seizure. He presents to the ER 2 weeks after discharge with fever, jaundice and a rash. He reports that he has been taking all of his medications as prescribed. He has not been around any sick contacts. He denies abdominal pain.

**PMHx:** New diagnosis of seizure disorder, otherwise none

**PSHx:** none

**Family history:** noncontributory

**Social history:** No alcohol, tobacco, or illicit drugs

**Medications:** He was discharged on several new medications, including carvedilol, simvastatin, phenytoin, a multivitamin, and levothyroxine.

**Exam:**

HEENT - His sclerae are icteric, mucous membranes are moist.

Skin - His skin is faintly jaundiced with an erythematous macular rash.
