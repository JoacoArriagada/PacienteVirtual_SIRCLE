| PATIENT NAME: Lucia Martinez  PATIENT AGE: 31 years old, female  CHIEF COMPLAINT: Seizures | |
| Learner Preparation | Case Stem (provided prior to entering room):  *You are working in a community hospital emergency department. All consultants are available by phone, and a medical interpreter is available if needed. Your next patient is Lucia Martinez, who is a 31 year old female. EMS will give you report when you enter the room.*  EMS Report (given verbally, by sim operator once patient enters room)  *This is a 31 year old female whose family member called 911 for seizures. She was seizing when we arrived and was given 5mg alprazolam intramuscularly. The seizure stopped and she is post-ictal now. The family member was there with their children, and he said she has no history of seizures. Vital signs en route include: HR 109, BP 166/88, RR 20, Sat 95% on room air, T37.1C.* |
