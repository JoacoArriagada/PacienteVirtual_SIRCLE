# Case Summary

Ms. Nidia Diaz is a 29 year old woman who has come to the doctor’s office for a postoperative visit. She had a suction curettage (D&C) for a miscarriage in the emergency room last week. She works as a pre-school aide, and is recently married. She and her husband recently moved here from New York to be closer to her family. She loves her husband, and they live with her parents. There has been no emotional or physical abuse in the relationship.

# Presentation/Emotional Tone

Ms. Diaz is a pleasant woman. She is relieved that the doctor who has just examined her has found everything to be normal, and to be told that one miscarriage will not change her chances of successful pregnancy in the future. She is relieved that this pregnancy ended spontaneously, as the timing was very difficult and she was considering termination up until the miscarriage occurred.
