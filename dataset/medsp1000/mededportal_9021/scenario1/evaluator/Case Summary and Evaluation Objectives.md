# CASE SUMMARY, EVALUATION OBJECTIVES and REQUIRED PROPS

# REASON FOR VISIT: Post operative visit (after procedure for miscarriage)

# Profile:

Ms. Nidia Diaz is a 29 year old female who underwent suction curettage for an incomplete abortion 5 days earlier in the emergency room. She is an aide in a preschool, and a newlywed. She and her husband recently moved here to be closer to her family. She has not yet located primary care in this area. She was not planning to become pregnant at this time. She experienced relief at hearing that the pregnancy was a miscarriage, but is worried about future fertility. The first doctor was reassuring that one miscarriage should not change her chances of successful future pregnancies.

# Evaluation Objectives:

 Determine whether the incomplete abortion was a desired pregnancy Determine her baseline experience, knowledge and preferences regarding contraception   
 Determine her risk for sexually transmitted diseases   
 Determine desire for and timing of future pregnancies   
 Determine desire for/tolerance of amenorrhea and/or irregular bleeding   
 Determine ability to pay for and obtain desired method   
 Explain efficacy and clinically important risks of methods most appropriate Explain common side effects of intended method   
 Explain how to start the method   
 Correctly apply the WHO Medical Eligibility Criteria   
 Close interview with plan for follow-up

The OSCE was conducted in actual examination rooms with the patient sitting in a chair. The examiner pointed out to students upon entering the room the props available which included industry models of the LngIUS, the CopperT, the etonorgestrel implant, a package of birth control pills, a vaginal contraceptive ring, and the contraceptive patch.
