1. **TITLE OF CASE:** North American Pit Viper Envenomation
4. **ENVIRONMENT:**

**A. Lab set up** – Pediatric Emergency Department bed in simulation center or true emergency department.

**B. Manikin set up** – The manikin will be a clothed adult size manikin. At the beginning of the simulation, there are no monitor leads on the patient, no IV access has been obtained, and the patient is not on oxygen.

**C. Props** – Available for use will be a heart monitor with leads, blood pressure cuff, and pulse oximeter. Supplemental oxygen by nasal cannula and face mask will be available. There will be a medicine cart or tray with a full complement of vasoactive agents, PALS medications, medicines necessary for sedation, rapid sequence intubation, and analgesia. In addition, a fully stocked code cart with defibrillator will be available for use, along with a selection of direct laryngoscopes and intubation supplies.

1. **ACTORS:**
   1. Actions for the roles will be as follows:
      1. Primary physician: The main scenario participant will act as the primary physician and do the primary evaluation of the patient to include obtaining a history, conducting a physical exam, and ordering any necessary medications or other interventions. The primary physician can perform any needed procedures, or can delegate these to other providers if multiple participants are present.

3. **Scenario Background**
   1. Chief Complaint: Bite to right middle finger

   3. Vital Signs: BP 95/45, HR 135, RR 18, O2 sat 97%, T 99.8 F oral
   4. Past Medical History: None
   5. Medications: None
   6. Allergies: No known allergies
4. **Initial Scenario Conditions**
   1. **Initial Exam**
      1. **General**: Patient is in moderate distress secondary to pain.
      2. **Extremities/Skin:** Right middle finger with puncture wounds on the dorsal aspect of the middle phalanx. Significant swelling of the entire finger extending to the dorsum of the hand. Ecchymosis and bullous lesions noted on the middle finger.
