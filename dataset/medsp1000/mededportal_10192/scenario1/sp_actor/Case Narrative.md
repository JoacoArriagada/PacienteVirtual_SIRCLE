1. **TITLE OF CASE:** North American Pit Viper Envenomation
2. **CASE NARRATIVE:**
3. **Scenario Background**
   1. Chief Complaint: Bite to right middle finger
   2. Triage Nursing Note: 14 year old female patient complains of intense right middle finger pain with bruising, generalized weakness and vomiting
   3. Past Medical History: None
   4. Medications: None
   5. Allergies: No known allergies
   6. Family and Social History: Lives on farm with father, mother and 6 year old sister. Maternal grandmother with cardiovascular disease.
4. **Initial Scenario Conditions**
   1. **History given by patient and parents:** Parents reports that the patient was outside feeding the horses on the farm when she dropped her cell phone into the grassy field. She began searching for it and felt something abruptly bite her right hand about 1 hour ago. She states she couldn’t see the animal in the grass but thinks it was a snake. She returned home and gently cleaned the wound. However, the wound has developed progressive swelling, pain and bruising. They decided to come to the local ED. While on the way to the emergency department, the patient started to feel tired, weak and vomited twice.
   2. **Circumstances at symptom onset:** (Other clues to be provided if elicited). Previously, the family has seen rattlesnakes on the farm, but no one has been bitten in the past. The patient heard a faint rattle sound just prior to today’s bite. The patient noticed paired puncture wounds at the bite site (dorsal surface of finger).
   3. **Associated symptoms (given by system as review of system is completed):** Patient has pain and swelling that initially started on the tip of her right middle finger. It has now progressed to the whole finger with mild swelling on the dorsum of the hand. Within 30 minutes, the finger developed bruising and blisters. She is lightheaded and feels generally weak. She vomited twice that is non-bloody and non-bilious. No SOB, wheezing, or coughing. No fevers. The remaining review of systems is negative.
   4. **Initial Exam**
      1. **General**: Patient is in moderate distress secondary to pain.
      2. **Extremities/Skin:** Right middle finger with puncture wounds on the dorsal aspect of the middle phalanx. Significant swelling of the entire finger extending to the dorsum of the hand. The edema is soft, but extremely tender to touch and increases with passive motion. Ecchymosis and bullous lesions noted on the middle finger.
