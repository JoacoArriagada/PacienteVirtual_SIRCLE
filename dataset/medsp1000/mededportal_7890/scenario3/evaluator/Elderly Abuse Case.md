Elder Abuse Case

Objectives:

- The student will be able to recognize the psychological symptoms and physical signs of vulnerable older adults
- The students will be able to demonstrate skills in screening for elder mistreatment
- The students will be able to demonstrate empathetic responses to patients revealing personal mistreatment histories
- The students will be able to demonstrate appropriate documentation of mistreatment findings for medical record keeping
- The students will be able to know the legal requirements for reporting elder mistreatment
- The students will become aware of treatment, counseling, and community resources for victims of mistreatment and caregivers at risk for inciting mistreatment

Setting:  Primary Care Office; regular check-up for Ellen Baker (mother)

Presenting Health Issue:

Elder:  lack of appetite and energy, depression

Caregiver:  stress elderly parent is putting on self, family, and job

Demographics:

Ellen Baker is a 79-year old female who lives with her daughter (only child) for the last 10-months

Barbara Allen is Ms. Baker's only child. Barbara Allen is 54-year old, married with two grown children and 4 grandchildren. Ms. Allen moved her mother Ms. Baker into their home when Ms. Baker was unable to care for herself in her own home. At her mother's request, Ms. Allen did not put her in an assisted-living facility. Didn't realize how this would impact her relationship with her mother and family, as well as the time commitments that would take her away from her job. Mother is no longer feeding herself and is extremely hard to spoon feed.

Patient Concerns:

- Patient is withdrawn and quiet seems to jump when daughter touches her
- Does not feel like eating but daughter makes her
- Needs to hurry this appointment so daughter can get back to work, as she acts angry when she has appointments
- Daughter means well and has given up soo much for me

Caregiver Concerns:

- Caregiver is on the edge of her seat for most of the visit and glances at her watch often
- She answers questions directed to mother in hopes of speeding up the visit
- Would like for the doctor to place mom in an assisted living facility, so exaggerates mother's symptoms

History of Domestic Violence:

The last few months the mother has been unable to feed herself and just wishes she could die so her daughter wouldn't have to force food into her mouth: sometime it hurts but I know it is just because she wants me to get my strength up. The days are so long and lonely in my room as I can no longer get to the other room by myself.
