**Pediatric Transport Bag Skills Session**

**Case #1**

Scenario: It’s 4:45PM and you are the ED resident preparing to leave for the day. You have one patient left in the ED, a 5-year-old with a known seizure disorder who presented in status epilepticus, requiring 2 doses of lorazepam and a levetiracetam load which stopped his seizures. Due to his prolonged post-ictal period and continued altered mental status, you decide to admit him to the PICU.

His nurse tells you that his bed is ready in the PICU and asks you if you are ready to travel with them.

Another seizure occurs in the elevator while transport is delayed. It is generalized tonic-clonic and goes on for 5 minutes.

After 1 dose of AED, the seizure stops.

Oxygen saturation decreases to 85%.

Oxygen saturation decreases to 83%, and the respiratory rate is about 4.

Sats improve to 98% with bagging and NPA in place.
