**Patient History**

* Obtained a pertinent HPI.
  + Inquired about the onset/duration/timing of the chief complaint.
  + Inquired about the character of the patient’s pain.
  + Inquired about associated symptomatology two weeks prior to presentation.
    - Inquired about the patient’s occupational history.
* Obtained a ROS.
* Obtained a pertinent PMH.
* Obtained a pertinent social history (i.e., occupation)
* Inquired about medications.
  + Inquired about allergies.

**Physical Examination**

* Interpreted vital signs.
  + Verbalized abnormal vital signs.
* Performed a pertinent physical exam.
  + Assessed the patient's skin.
  + Assessed the patient’s cardiovascular function (i.e., heart, peripheral pulses).\*
  + Assessed the patient’s lungs.\*
  + Assessed the patient’s abdomen.\*

**Labs**

* Obtained a CBC.
* Obtained a BMP.
* Obtained coagulation tests (PT, INR, PTT).
* Obtained a serum lactic acid level.\*
* Obtained a urinalysis.\*
* Ordered blood cultures.\*

**Differential Diagnosis**

* Considered sepsis.\*
* Recognized the exposure to ionizing radiation.
  + WITH prompting.
  + WITHOUT prompting.
* Established a diagnosis of Acute Radiation Syndrome.

**Treatment**

* Administered fluids (i.e., lactated Ringer’s or normal saline).
* Administered antipyretic/analgesic (i.e., acetaminophen).
* Administered broad-spectrum antibiotics (i.e., vancomycin + cefepime).
* Considered the need for decontamination.
* Consulted appropriate disciplines/resources (e.g., hematology, toxicology, internal medicine, Radiation Emergency Assistance Center/Training Site).
* Admitted the patient.
