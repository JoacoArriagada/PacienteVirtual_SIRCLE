**Appendix I: Autonomy and Entrustment Facilitator’s Guide/Talking Points**

10 minutes: Case 2 (Appendix F) presents a second case to encourage discussion about the appropriate amount of faculty involvement in the resident’s patient care. This case suggests that the resident involved may have experience and may have demonstrated skills suggesting that a fairly high level of entrustment would be merited. On the other hand, because the resident is noted to be “very confident”, the case also highlights learner characteristics that could be an issue with allowing autonomy based on false impressions of learner knowledge. This should encourage discussion about how faculty could react with too much or too little autonomy and what cues the resident might provide to impact this.
