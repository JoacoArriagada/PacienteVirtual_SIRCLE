STANDARDIZED PATIENT (SP) SCRIPT

*This script is to be used by a SP playing the mother who just brought in her 4wk old baby into your Emergency Room (ER) due to lethargy and bruising.*

CASE BACKGROUND INFORMATION

*The case is a 4-week-old, ex-full term, baby girl brought in by her mother to the pediatric ER due to increasing fussiness, lethargy, intermittent episodes of tremulousness and bruising that has been getting worse over the past 5 days. The baby delivered at home without notable complications. Parents refused vitamin K, erythromycin and Hepatitis B at birth. She has been seen by her Naturopath pediatrician at a 2 week visit, at which point she had regained her birthweight. She is exclusively breastfeeding. Her newborn screen returned normal. Upon arrival to the ER, babe is irritable and inconsolable. As soon as the patient is placed on the exam table in the resuscitation bay, she becomes lethargic, quiet, and no longer opens her eyes. At this point, a full code is called and the mother is attended to by a physician assigned to talk with her. The patient starts seizing.*

STANDARDIZED PATIENT INFORMATION

*The mother is emotional about not being able to hold her baby and is frightened by the austere and unfamiliar ER environment. She should make it known that she is very upset and wants to be with her baby, but is calmed by the provider who is attending to her as to not distract the participants from the neonatal resuscitation.*

PATIENT INFORMATION (Please remember not to offer any of this information, but when asked please respond while remaining in character.)

CHIEF COMPLAINT: *Lethargic with scattered bruising.*

NAME: *Hallie*

AGE: *4 weeks old*

ADDITIONAL HISTORY: *Born at home, did not receive erythromycin eye drops, hepatitis B vaccine or vitamin K prophylaxis.* 

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: *None. At one week checkup did notice some oozing at umbilical stump, but primary care physician said there was nothing to worry about.*

SOCIAL HISTORY: *Lives with Mom, Dad at home. Grandparents watch baby at times, nobody else. No concerns for inflicted or accidental trauma. Followed by a Naturopath.*

FAMILY HISTORY: *No known history of coagulation or bleeding/hematologic disorders.*

PAST SURGICAL HISTORY: *None.*

MEDICATIONS:  *None.*

ALLERGIES: *None.*

IMMUNIZATIONS:  *None.*

PRENATAL HISTORY: *Normal, no maternal meds.*

POTENTIAL DIALOGUE:

REMEMBER, IT IS IMPORTANT THAT YOU DO NOT OFFER INFORMATION. PLEASE ALLOW THE LEARNERS TO ASK QUESTIONS. **DO NOT OFFER INFORMATION UNLESS THEY ASK YOU**.

<u>THINGS YOU COULD SAY WITHOUT BEING ASKED:</u>

*How is my baby? I want to hold my baby. Is my baby seizing? Is that bruising? Is my baby bleeding? Can someone tell me what’s going on?* 

<u>THINGS YOU MIGHT SAY TRIGGERED BY EVENTS IN THE SCENARIO.</u>

*Are those medications you’re giving my baby necessary?*

*If asked about who is with the baby and if you’re concerned about trauma, say only you and your husband and the grandparents have been with her and you have no concerns.*

*When prompted, reiterate that no shots were given at the home birth and that you don’t like toxic medications.*
