| Event | Runtime response |
| --- | --- |
| Initial state | Eyes open, but not conversant. If spoken to, the simulated patient does not answer. BP 145/60, HR 70, SpO2 96% (on room air), RR 6, EtCO2 42 mmHg. |
| Uncal herniation. Start of "Cushing's triad," heralded by anesthesia technician stating that the patient is sleepier than when he came into the room | Eyes close, loses consciousness. The left pupil is widely dilated and does not react to light. BP increases to 240/120, HR decreases to 40, RR to zero, SpO2 to 80% over 30 seconds. |
| Hyperventilation with face-mask | Over 2 minutes BP decreases to 170/90, HR increases to 80. SpO2 increases, depending on the use of supplemental oxygen. |
| Raise the head of the bed | Over 2 minutes the BP decreases by 5 mmHg. |
| Propofol administered | ≤50 mg decreases BP by 10%, HR increases 5% (per dose); 51-100 mg decreases BP by 15% and increases HR by 10% (per dose); 101-150 mg decreases BP by 20% and increases HR by 15% (per dose); ≥151 mg decreases BP by 25% and increases HR by 20% (per dose). |
| Administer muscle relaxant (either succinylcholine or rocuronium) | No effect on the mannequin. |
| Administer mannitol | BP decreases by 10 mmHg, HR increases 5 beats-per-minute over 2 minutes. |
| Administer fentanyl | For doses of 1-3 cc's: BP decreases by 5 mmHg, no effect on HR; doses of 3-5 cc's BP decreases 8 mmHg, and HR decreases by 3 bpm. |
| Administer hydralizine, sodium nitroprusside, nitroglycerin | Reduces BP by 20 mmHg, increases HR by 10 bpm. |
| Administer labetalol | Reduces BP by 20 mmHg, reduces HR by 5 bpm. |
| Administer atropine, epinephrine or ephedrine | Increases HR (depending on dose), no change in BP. |
| Administer nicardipine | Decreases BP by 20 mmHg, slight increase in HR. |
| Direct laryngoscopy (DL) | If the BP is ≤140 mmHg, BP increases by 5 mmHg and HR by only a few beats/minute. BP 141-170 mmHg produces a greater stress response. BP ≥171 produces a large response with a 30 mmHg rise in BP and 40 bpm increase in HR. |
| Call for help | Help arrives and can be utilized. |
| Call neurosurgeons | The neurosurgeon arrives agitated and urging rapid action. |
