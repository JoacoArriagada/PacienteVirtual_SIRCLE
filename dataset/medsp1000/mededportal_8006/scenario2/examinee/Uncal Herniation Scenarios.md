**Two human-patient simulations to illustrate acute uncal herniation**

**UNCAL HERNIATION IN THE PACU**

**Background and scenario overview**

This scenario occurs in the Post Anesthesia Care Unit (PACU). The patient is a 42 year-old previously healthy man who is two-hours status-post a craniotomy for tumor. A PACU nurse reports that although the patient has never been fully responsive in the unit, he is now completely unresponsive.

**Case Narrative**

1. **Scenario Background**:
   1. **Chief complaint**-"Can you see this patient in the PACU, please? This is 42 year-old, previously healthy man who had a craniotomy for tumor. He came out of the operating room about 2-hours ago. He has always been a bit groggy and difficult to arouse, but he seems much sleepier to me in the last 20 minutes."
   2. **Past Medical History**: benign and non-contributory to the scenario.
   3. **Meds and allergies**: The patient does not have medication allergies. Medications have been described.
   4. **Family/social history**: Non-contributory.
2. **Scenario conditions initially**
   1. **History patient gives**-none. The patient is somnolent.
   2. **Patients' physiology**-somnolent, bradypneic (RR of 6), SpO2 of 96% on 6 lpm of oxygen, BP 145/60, HR of 70 in sinus rhythm
