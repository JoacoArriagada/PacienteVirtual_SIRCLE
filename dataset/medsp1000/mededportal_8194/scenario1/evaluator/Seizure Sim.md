# Title:

# Target Audience:

Anesthesia Residents, Anesthesia Fellows, Anesthesia Faculty

# Learning Objectives:

# Primary:

1. Recognition and management of infant seizures   
2. Institution of basic life support (BLS)   
3. Institution of appropriate pediatric advanced life support (PALS)   
algorithm for cardiac arrythmias   
4. Institution of appropriate treatment of local anesthetic toxicity   
5. Plan and institute necessary post-seizure/arrest care and transfer to the   
intensive care unit (ICU)

# Secondary: Technical Goals

Basic and advanced airway management with bag mask and oral   
intubation   
Specific cardiac arrhythmia management – bradycardia and ventricular   
tachycardia (VT) induced by resuscitation epinephrine bolus   
Pharmacological seizure management   
Pharmacological local anesthetic toxicity management

# Behavioral Goals

Directed, closed-loop communication Communication directed at specific individuals Individuals communicate when action is complete Individuals in assessment roles continuously communicate with team leader   
There is evidence of good teamwork among the members, e.g., it is clear who is in charge but all team members contribute their ideas tasks are delegated efficiently and clearly. members share with team what they are thinking and doing members announce what tasks they have done   
Recognition that initially there is minimal information available for patient   
management   
Deal with the difficult and obstructive nurse/colleague who is initially   
caring for the patient

# Didactic points

Differential diagnosis of failure to wake up after anesthesia e.g.   
hypoglycemia, neurological insult etc.   
Differential diagnosis of seizure activity (e.g. hypocalcaemia, electrolyte imbalance)   
Differential diagnosis of VT (e.g. epinephrine bolus)   
‘Lateral thinking’ to determine the actual cause of the seizures (local anesthesia toxicity)

# Critical Action Checklist/Optimal order of events

Time 0: Institution of basic airway management in an unconscious patient – Oxygen delivery, facemask, ensure adequate and patent airway. Placate the anxious and demanding nurse managing the patient, elicit what minimal history is available.

Time 3 min: Recognition of seizure activity and consider various management options

Time 4 min: Before time is available to institute management of seizures recognition of inadequate ventilation with progressive desaturation

Time 5 min: Recognition of hypoxia induced bradycardia and institution of external chest compressions

Time 6 mins: Institute PALS based management of bradycardia including epinephrine bolus which precipitates VT, recognition of VT following epinephrine bolus and subsequent management of pulseless VT including cardioversion

Time 12 mins: After 2 cycles of cardioversion, recognition of restoration of sinus rhythum.

Time 14 mins: Recognize recurrent seizure activity, ensure bezodiazepines used in management.

Time 16 mins: Receive information from nurse via external source that patient received a caudal at the end of the anesthetic procedure and hence institute treatment for local anesthetic toxicity

Time 18 mins: Arrange transfer to ICU
