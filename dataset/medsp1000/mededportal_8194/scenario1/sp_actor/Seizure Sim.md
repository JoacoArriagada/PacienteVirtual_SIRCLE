# Environment:

Set Up: In the operating room post anesthesia care unit

# Props:

Distracters: The anesthesiologist responsible for the case is not available so very little clinical information on the case is available at the beginning of the scenario.

# Actors:

Role PACU nurse Actor Ideally a facilitator from the SIM group or an actual PACU nurse Action Endless talking, minimal clinical information, intermittently obstructive

# Background:

The candidate is called to see a 6 month old $7 \mathrm { K g }$ male infant who has just arrived in the PACU following unremarkable anesthesia for inguinal hernia repair. The otherwise healthy term infant was anesthetized by one of your colleagues who is currently placing a challenging epidural in another case and unable to attend. The child was extubated while breathing spontaneously under deep inhalational anesthesia and transferred from the OR uneventfully. The PACU nurse is concerned the child does not seem to be breathing adequately and is making abnormal twitching movements; she calls the candidate to review.
