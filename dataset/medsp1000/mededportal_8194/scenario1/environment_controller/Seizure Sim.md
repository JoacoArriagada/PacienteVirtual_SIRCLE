# Background:

The candidate is called to see a 6 month old $7 \mathrm { K g }$ male infant who has just arrived in the PACU following unremarkable anesthesia for inguinal hernia repair. The otherwise healthy term infant was anesthetized by one of your colleagues who is currently placing a challenging epidural in another case and unable to attend. The child was extubated while breathing spontaneously under deep inhalational anesthesia and transferred from the OR uneventfully. The PACU nurse is concerned the child does not seem to be breathing adequately and is making abnormal twitching movements; she calls the candidate to review.

# Initial conditions (time 0):

# The baby is unconscious lying on its back with a partially obstructed airway, inspiratory noise, tracheal tugging and expiratory gurgling

HR: 90, ECG: Sinus Rhythm, SpO2: 93, NIBP: 85/45 (cycle time 15 mins), RR: 8, Temp: 37.3.   
The baby is showing signs of partial airway obstruction

# Progression 1 (time 3 mins):

The facilitator starts to shake the baby simulating a prolonged seizure

HR: 140, ECG: Sinus Tachycardia, SpO2: 95, NIBP: 85/45, RR: 0, Temp: 37.4. The baby has clenched teeth

# Progression 2 (time 4 mins)

The infant is apneic, continues to seize and starts to desaturate before the Lorazepam has arrived.

HR: 100, ECG: Sinus Rhythm, SpO2: 85, NIBP: 85/45, RR: 0, Temp: 37.5. No discernable air entry is achieved and no airway is achievable due to masseter spasm

# Progression 3 (time 5 mins):

The infant has hypoxia induced bradycardia.

HR: 55, ECG: Bradycardia, $S p O _ { 2 }$ : unrecordable, NIBP: 85/45, RR: 0, Temp: 37.5. The baby is cyanotic

# Progression 4 (time 6 mins):

The infant converts into VT (pulseless) following administration of epinephrine.

HR: 200, ECG: VT, SpO2: unrecordable, NIBP: 85/45, RR: 0, Temp: 37.6. The baby is cyanosed

# Progression 5 (time 12 mins):

Restoration of sinus rhythum.

HR: 130, ECG: Sinus Tachycardia, SpO2: 99, NIBP: 120/70, RR: Bag Ventilation, Temp: 37.8.   
The baby is pink

A brief period of stability, unless the team has changed the set up there has only been one blood pressure measurement due to the 15 min cycle time. The patients temperature is slowly rising.

# Progression 6 (time 14 mins):

Recurrant sustained seizure

HR: 140, ECG: Sinus Tachycardia, SpO2: 99, NIBP: 120/70, RR: Bag Ventilation, Temp: 38.0.   
The baby is pink

# Progression 7 (time 16 mins):

Information passed on from the operating room where the primary anesthesiologist was working that the patient received a single shot caudal anesthetic at the end of the procedure with 1ml/kg of Bupivicaine, 2 passes were made with the caudal cannula – the first was a bloody tap.

HR: 140, ECG: Sinus Tachycardia, SpO2: 99, NIBP: 120/70, RR: Bag Ventilation, Temp: 38.2.   
The baby is pink

# Progression 8 (time 18 mins):

The patient is stabilized but unconscious, intubated and ventilated

HR: 110, ECG: Sinus Rhythm, $\cdot$ : 99, NIBP: 105/60, RR: Bag Ventilation, Temp: 38.2.   
The baby is pink
