# Management plan:

The student should recognize the following features:

A syndrome of resting tremor/bradykinesia/ rigidity/postural instability.   
A localization involving extrapyramidal system (basal ganglia or substantia nigra).

The student’s management plan should include the following:

Consideration of brain imaging with MRI or CT scanning. Plan to include trial of an anti-parkinsonian medicine (L-DOPA or dopamine agonist or MAO-B inhibitor). Plan to re-assess examination after medications started.  Plan to include referral for physical/occupational therapy.
