# Problem-Specific Physical Examination Steps

15. Student tested for eyelid droop with sustained upgaze.

If asked to sustain an upgaze for at least 30 seconds, your eyelids will droop halfway (ptosis). This ptosis will improve over a period of minutes during the remainder of the examination.

16. Student tested for fatigability of muscles by sustained muscle contraction.

The student may test you for this by a prolonged ( $^ { 3 0 + }$ seconds) deltoid test.

The student may perform the hip flexor test with prolonged resistance $^ { \prime 3 0 + }$ seconds).

For both of these tests, the student should then stop and test again. At that point, you show slightly more weakness (4-/5).

The student may ask you to do 10 deep knee bends. In this case, show weakness at $6 ^ { t h }$ rep. Weakness is bilateral always.

17. Student evaluated reflexes after sustained muscle contraction.

This is most often performed w/ extension of the lower extremity against the examiner’s resistance. After $^ { 3 0 + }$ seconds of this, the student then tests the patellar reflexes. You exhibit NO CHANGE IN REFLEXES.

18. Student evaluated neck flexion/extension.

You should exhibit the same mild weakness $( 4 + / 5 )$ if you are asked to move you your neck/head against resistance.
