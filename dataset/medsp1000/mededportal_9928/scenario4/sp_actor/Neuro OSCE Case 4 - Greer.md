# Case Summary & SP Training Notes

# SP Demographics

Name: Michelle Greer
Gender: female
Age: 25

# Ethnicity: Any Educational Background: College-educated

Setting: Neurology clinic

Case overview: The generalized weakness and fatigue has been present for about 3 months. It is not constant, but instead is more noticeable at some times than others. You primarily feel it in your legs and arms. If questioned, you notice it in your legs when climbing stairs or walking long distances, or in your arms when carrying objects, or holding them up. For instance, your arms get ‘tired’ when you’re putting things up on a shelf. No particular problems with grip strength – you deny problems opening a jar, turning the key in the lock, typing/ writing. You have not had any falls. You were referred by your primary care physician.

# Opening statement:

# History of present complaint:

Over the past week you’ve noticed some difficulty swallowing.

Occasionally it feels as if things “go down the wrong tube”. If questioned, this occurs most frequently with liquids. If questioned, you have not really had this problem with solid pieces of food.
This has been present for only a short time. You have NOT experienced any weight loss. You have NOT had any significant change in appetite.

Over the past 2 weeks, you feel that you’ve been slurring your words.

Over the past few days your coworkers have occasionally noted this as well and have commented on it. Some have even joked that you sound “drunk”.
You comprehend everything they are saying and they seem to understand the content of everything which you are trying to express.
You have no difficulty with understanding written language (reading) and have no difficulty with writing. Your work performance has not been impaired.

You’ve had some double vision (diplopia) over the past month. It is not constant and seems to come and go.

If asked, the double vision disappears when covering up one eye (you see only single objects when one eye is covered).
If asked how the two images are displaced, it appears they are mostly (but not always) side by side.
The double vision is not clearly worse when looking in specific directions.
The double vision appears to be most pronounced after a tiring day at work.
When you first wake up in the morning it is not typically present. There is no headache associated with the double vision. You have NOT noticed any decrease in visual acuity. You have NOT noticed any change in the brightness of colors.
When you look in the mirror before going to bed on some days it appears as if both of your eyelids are sagging. You have attributed this to feeling worn out by work.

# Details of the recent illness/episode:

You have not had similar symptoms in the past.
You have not had any recent illness.
Your symptoms seem worse at the end of the day compared to the beginning.
You do not regularly exercise, so you’re not sure how that would affect your symptoms.
You have had no changes in the color of your urine. You do not have muscle pain or discomfort. You do not have joint pain. You do not have numbness or tingling. You have not had difficulty with your bladder or bowels. You have not had shortness of breath. You have not noticed any rash or skin changes. Your symptoms are not worsened by ambient heat (hot bath, etc.) You have not had any fevers. You have not had any diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, or constipation. You have not had any exposure to toxins/chemicals (including organophosphates). You do not work on a farm. You have not eaten any canned goods or home jarred honey. You have NOT had any recent vaccinations. You have NOT had any recent travel. You have NOT had any recent insect/animal bites. You have NOT recently gone camping or drunk unfiltered water.

# Past medical history:

No medical problems.

# Family medical history:

No one in your family has had symptoms like these.

Double vision. Relatively mild symmetric weakness (4+/5)

Mental Status Exam: Within normal limits.

Diagnosis: Myasthenia Gravis

Patient is wearing a gown over bra and panties and is seated on the exam table. You’ve noticed some weakness, but you are unaware of the extent or how serious it is.
