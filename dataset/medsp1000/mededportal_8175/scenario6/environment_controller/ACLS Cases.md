**CASE 6**

You are called by a nurse at 3:00 AM to evaluate a 49 year old woman admitted to another intern’s team. Her sign-out to you was that the patient had pneumonia. The woman complains that her heart is beating rapidly. The nurse tells you that the patient is alert and oriented and has only right pleuritic chest pain but no dyspnea. The patient’s heart rate is 135 beats per minute, her blood pressure is 150/90 and her lungs have minimal right base crackles. The nurse is obtaining a 12 ECG but requests that you come evaluate the patient immediately.
