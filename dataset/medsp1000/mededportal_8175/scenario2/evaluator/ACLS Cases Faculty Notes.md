**Case 2 faculty notes:**

1. Same initial issues of assessment and getting help.
   1. Variation: patient is still talking to you from the bed meaning strip # 1 is artifact. Stress to “assess and treat the patient not the monitor”.
   2. Again review the adult cardiac arrest algorithm (repetition is good) with a twist here that the arrest was witnessed so you can try a precordial thump. The 2010 AHA Guidelines state: “The precordial thump may be considered for patients with witnessed, monitored, unstable VT (including pulseless VT) if a defibrillator is not immediately ready for use, but it should not delay CPR)
   3. Realistic variation: patient goes asystolic after thump: is this true asystole or a lead loosened from the thump?
2. Strip #8: stress to treat pulseless VT as VF
