Intern:

You are caring for Jade, a 10-day-old full term baby girl with a skeletal dysplasia. As part of her condition, she has a small thorax with abnormal ribs. She is now on the ventilator and clinically stable, though you know her hospitalization is likely to be prolonged and perhaps complicated. You have come to know her family over the past few days and after providing medical updates, wish to discuss code status.
