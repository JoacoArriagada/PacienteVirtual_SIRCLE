**OB/GYN Scenario #1: Discussing Code Status on Admission**

Intern

Intern: You are admitting Ms. Reed, a nulliparous 63-year-old presenting with abnormal uterine bleeding. She is being admitted for overnight observation after receiving 1 unit packed red blood cell transfusion for a hemoglobin of 6.8. She has a known history of iron deficiency anemia with a baseline hemoglobin of 7.5, and she has been hemodynamically stable since arrival to the ER today. Your upper level is placing her orders and you are tasked with discussing her code status.
