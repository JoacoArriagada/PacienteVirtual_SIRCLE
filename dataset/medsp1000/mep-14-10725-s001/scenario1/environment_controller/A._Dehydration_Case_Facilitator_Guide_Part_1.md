**PART 1**

Sam Stevens, a 28-year-old male, arrives at your office. He is planning to run a marathon in a few weeks and wants to make sure he is properly prepared for it. He asks for a complete physical and blood work to make sure he is healthy enough for the race. He has had no significant medical problems in the past and is on no medications; he has no family history of sudden death. During his physical evaluation, you and Sam start chatting about the importance of drinking water during the race.
