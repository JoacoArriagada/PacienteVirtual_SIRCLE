**Instructor Guide**

**Title: Interprofessional Geriatric Assessment Elective for Health Professional Students: A Standardized Patient Case Study and Patient Script**

**Resource Files:**

Case Study 3: Standardized Patient Script

Case Study 3: Hospital Discharge Summary

Case Study 3: Instructions to Students

Case Study 3: Patient Medication List

Case Study 3: Physical Exam, Laboratory Results, and Additional Chart Information

**Use of Resource:**

This case study is designed to be used with a team of any mix of interprofessional healthcare students and 2 standardized patients (1 patient and 1 adult child/caregiver) as a standalone activity or as a part of a larger interprofessional course. The standardized patient encounter utilizes five documents. The Case Study 3: Standardized Patient Script is presented to the standardized patient and caregiver as part of pre-encounter training. The script is reviewed with faculty to clarify any questions related to their roles. Immediately before the encounter, students receive two documents, Case Study 3: Hospital Discharge Summary and Instructions to Students; the only documents available to guide the student team assessments. It is up to the student groups to decide how to go about doing team interviews (how to divide up the time, what to cover, how to handle logistics of a team interview etc.) Fifty minutes is allotted for the team assessment. During the encounter, the standardized patient’s family member presents the medication list to the team if asked. And finally, following the SP encounter, teams receive Case Study 3: Physical Exam, Laboratory Results, and Additional Chart Information. This document provides additional information about the patient e.g., laboratory results that would be reasonable for teams to have requested as part of the assessment. Developing a team- oriented care plan is based on the five documents and the 50-minute assessment.

This case could also be used in a variety of less resource intensive formats. For example, the case could be used in a paper-based format for small or large interprofessional or single profession group discussions to illustrate various geriatric patient care issues. These groups could be self-directed or supported by a faculty facilitator.

**Implementation advice:**

On the day of the simulation, student teams are given 10 minutes to review the door chart and hospital discharge note. They are then given 50 minutes to interview and examine the standardized patient in the skilled nursing facility room with the family member/caregiver (another standardized patient) present. Encounter props include a bed, side chair, wheelchair and walker. In addition, the student teams should have access to standard physical assessment equipment.
