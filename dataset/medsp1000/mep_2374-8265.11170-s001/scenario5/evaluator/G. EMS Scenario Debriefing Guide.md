***Scenario 5***

Key Debriefing Points:

1. Identification of hypoglycemia
2. Discuss whether a patient can refuse medical care after receiving treatment (i.e. IV, D50, finger stick).
3. Identify social concerns that may impact patient (do they have someone to stay with them, do they have food in the kitchen, etc)
4. Discuss how EMS crews have cooked food for patients who were refusing transport to ensure they didn’t become hypoglycemic again.
5. Discuss the role of medical control in patient’s refusing transport. How does medical control determine if a patient has capacity to make the decision?
