| **PATIENT NAME: Dave Jentro**  **PATIENT AGE: 32**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: First responder extricated from a building after rescuing occupants**  **PHYSICAL SETTING: sidewalk of a fire scene** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | *EMS responds to a fire scene to “stand-by” as per protocol. Upon arrival, they find firefighters treating a first responder who entered the building without equipment to notify occupants to evacuate. The patient is lying on the ground with black soot under his nose and around his mouth. They are responsive to verbal commands, but have an altered mental status and need to have their airway maintained.* |
| **Primary Learning Objectives** | *Demonstrate intubation in an austere environment (on the floor with ambient noise)*  *Identify the need for administration of* Hydroxycobalamin (Cyanokit)*.*  *Demonstrate the proper administration of* Hydroxycobalamin (Cyanokit)*.*  *Discuss transport options to burn center locations.* |
| **Critical Actions** | *Ensure scene safety*  *Assess patient’s airway, breathing, and circulation*  *Identify airway emergency*  *Administer oxygen*  *Apply a carbon monoxide monitor (if available)*  *Intubate the patient to maintain the airway*  *Obtain vascular access*  *Implement a 500 ml normal saline bolus*  Obtain a 12-lead ECG  Administer Hydroxycobalamin (Cyanokit) |
| **Learner Preparation or Prework** | *Review EMS protocols* |
