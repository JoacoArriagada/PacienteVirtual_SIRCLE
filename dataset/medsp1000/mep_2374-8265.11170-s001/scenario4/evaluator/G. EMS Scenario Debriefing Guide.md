**Scenario 4**

Key Debriefing Points:

1. Identification of burns (rule of 9’s, superficial 🡪 full thickness)
2. Intubation versus immediate transport (with a potential airway burn the importance of securing an airway).
3. Discuss hydroxycobalamin administration. Hydroxycobalamin is precursor to Vitamin B12. It combines with cyanide (from burning plastics) to be renally excreted.
4. Review how to obtain and administer hydroxycobalamin. Which EMS units carry hydroxycobalamin? All? Just Supervisors? When reconstituting the medication, it needs to be inverted NOT shaken.
5. Identification of local burn centers and location.
6. Discuss the number of burn centers and beds available in the state and region. We discuss large scale incidents which necessitated transferring patients throughout the region, such as the Station Nightclub Fire in Rhode Island. This can be made more relevant by using local examples.
