| **Appendix B: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Case Template**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Abdominal Pain with Aggressive Partner** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: Monica Mercado**  **PATIENT AGE: 22**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Abdominal Pain**  **PHYSICAL SETTING: In apartment, sitting at table/on couch** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | *EMS responds to a patient complaining of abdominal pain. Upon arrival, they find a patient sitting in a chair or on a couch. On a table and the floor are empty beer cans, plates, red plastic cups, and slices of cake. Loud music is playing in the background and arguing can be heard from an adjacent room. EMS arrives to find the patient sitting learning forward complaining of abdominal pain. As they begin to assess the patient an intoxicated individual from the adjacent room enters. They begin to yell incoherently and try to get EMS to leave. They state the patient doesn’t want to go with them. EMS will likely ask the intoxicated friend to go back to the adjacent room while they continue managing the patient. The intoxicated friend initially complies and then becomes increasingly hostile. Eventually, they return holding a broken bottle or hammer. The EMS providers must decide whether to continue to engage with this individual, request assistance (Police), evacuate the area, leave the patient, or bring the patient with them.* |
