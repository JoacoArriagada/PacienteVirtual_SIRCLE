| **PATIENT NAME: Monica Mercado**  **PATIENT AGE: 22**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Abdominal Pain**  **PHYSICAL SETTING: In apartment, sitting at table/on couch** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | *EMS responds to a patient complaining of abdominal pain. Upon arrival, they find a patient sitting in a chair or on a couch. On a table and the floor are empty beer cans, plates, red plastic cups, and slices of cake. Loud music is playing in the background and arguing can be heard from an adjacent room. EMS arrives to find the patient sitting learning forward complaining of abdominal pain. As they begin to assess the patient an intoxicated individual from the adjacent room enters. They begin to yell incoherently and try to get EMS to leave. They state the patient doesn’t want to go with them. EMS will likely ask the intoxicated friend to go back to the adjacent room while they continue managing the patient. The intoxicated friend initially complies and then becomes increasingly hostile. Eventually, they return holding a broken bottle or hammer. The EMS providers must decide whether to continue to engage with this individual, request assistance (Police), evacuate the area, leave the patient, or bring the patient with them.* |
| **Primary Learning Objectives** | *Determine whether the scene is safe*  *Identify potential hazards in the environment*  *Review proper positioning in a patient environment*  *Discuss the procedure for de-escalating a hostile situation*  *Defend their course of action* |
| **Critical Actions** | *Ensure scene safety*  *Identify potential hazards*  *Position themselves with an escape route (if possible)*  *De-escalate the hostile individual (if possible)*  *Safely escort the patient out of the environment*  *Request assistance from police* |

**Ideal Scenario Flow**

*The learners enter the scene to find a patient in sitting on a chair complaining of abdominal pain. Loud music and arguing can be heard from an adjacent room. One learner should investigate what is occurring in the adjacent room while one begins attending to the patient. A history and physical should be started. A decision on whether the patient can be moved to a more secure area or needs to be treated in place should be made. EMS should determine if the patient is intoxicated and whether they have decision making capacity. Assistance from Police should be considered. EMS will need to de-escalate the aggressive friend, request Police assistance, and safely extricate themselves and the patient.*
