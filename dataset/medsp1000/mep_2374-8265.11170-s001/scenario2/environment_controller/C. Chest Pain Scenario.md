| **Brief narrative description of case** | *In this simulation 2 learners, representing an EMS team/crew, will evaluate and manage a patient with chest pain, who is suffering from a ST segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI). The learners have to implement appropriate treatment and determine where best to transport the patient.* |
