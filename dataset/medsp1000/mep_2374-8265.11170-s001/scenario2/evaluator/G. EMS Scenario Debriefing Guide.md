**Scenario 2**

Key Debriefing Points:

1. How to utilize the monitor to perform a 3-lead and 12-lead EKG.
2. EMS protocols surrounding transmitting 12-lead EKG to medical control and treatment of STEMI in the field.
3. Identification of what medications can be given via protocol (aspirin, nitroglycerin, morphine, oxygen).
4. Knowledge of STEMI Centers in the local area. For our scenario, which took place in an urban environment, dispatch advised the patient of their location. Learners could ask for the closest hospital and dispatch would advise of the 2 closest facilities by name (not capability). The closest hospital was approximately 4 minutes away but is NOT a STEMI receiving hospital. The other hospital is the residents home hospital which is a STEMI Center but is approximately 12 minutes. We did this to allow us to discuss the residents familiarity with the other hospitals in the region.
