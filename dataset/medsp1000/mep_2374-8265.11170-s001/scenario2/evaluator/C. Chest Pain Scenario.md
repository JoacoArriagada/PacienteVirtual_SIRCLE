**Ideal Scenario Flow**

*The learners enter the room to find the patient in moderate distress, sitting with their hand clutching their chest, and breathing fast. They immediately place the patient on the monitor and recognize the patient is hypoxic. Supplemental oxygen is provided. The patient’s respiratory status improves. Learners should establish IV access. After completing the history and physical exam an ECG should be obtained. The ECG shows Sinus Rhythm with ST elevations in V2-V4 with reciprocal depressions. The patient is provided aspirin and nitroglycerin which improves the patient’s pain. The learners call dispatch and activate a STEMI. Dispatch informs the “crew” that Hospital x is 5 minutes away but is not a STEMI center, and Hospital Y is 15 minutes away but is a STEMI center, and ask where they are taking the patient. The learners agree to take the patient to the STEMI center/cath lab.*

**Anticipated Management Mistakes**

*Provide a list of management errors or difficulties that are commonly encountered when using this simulation case.*

*For example:*

1. *Difficulty with EKG: Learners may have difficulty analyzing the EKG, especially if more novice individuals are taking part in the simulation. They may also have difficulty properly applying the leads to obtain an EKG as this is not a skill most EM residents typically do.*
2. *Failure to administer appropriate medications: Some learners may only administer aspirin and not give the patient anything for their chest pain*
3. *Failure to transport to a STEMI center: some learners may elect to transport the patient to the closest ED and not a hospital that can manage the patient’s condition.*
