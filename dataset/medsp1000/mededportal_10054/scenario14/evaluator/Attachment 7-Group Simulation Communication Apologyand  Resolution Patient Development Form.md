# SIMULATION CENTER

Goldberg Surgical Skills Laboratory An American College of Surgeons Accredited Education Institute Patient Development Form

Lesson Title: CARe (Communication, Apology and Resolution)

Clinical Service and Learner: Anesthesia/Group Simulation: CA-1 through CA-3 residents. CA-3 residents will team lead the disclosure.

# Goals:

To reinforce principles of apology and disclosure following adverse patient outcomes

# Objectives and Competencies:

 Recognize the need for risk management and possible medical consultation before the disclosure to optimize team preparedness and patient outcome (Patient Care, Medical Knowledge, Practice Based Learning and Improvement, Systems Based Practice, Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Professionalism)
 Demonstrate appropriate (know what to say, what not to say) and compassionate patient consultative skills when a medical error or critical event transpires (Patient Care, Medical Knowledge, Practice Based Learning and Improvement, Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Professionalism)
. Articulate the need for essential follow up care after an intraoperative error or critical event transpires (Patient Care, Medical Knowledge, Practice Based Learning and Improvement, Systems Based Practice, Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Professionalism) Demonstrate an understanding of SRS forms, the QI process and Safety Improvement initiatives to reduce adverse outcome recurrence rates and optimize patient safety (Patient Care, Medical Knowledge, Practice Based Learning and Improvement, Systems Based Practice)

Environment and Scenarios: Two clinical scenarios will be presented in either a patient care or simulation consultation room, during which time two CA-3 participants (primary anesthesiologist and either the surgeon or circulating nurse) will be made aware of a perioperative error or complication. They will be instructed to inform the patient about what happened and provide consultative advice. Participants are expected to work through principles of communication, apology and resolution, develop a patient care plan and initiate appropriate follow up therapy in a timely manner.

# Scenario 2: Anoxic Brain Injury

A 35yo woman for breast augmentation under general anesthesia has an unexpectedly difficult induction. Difficult mask ventilation, unable to intubate The patient is significantly hypoxemic for 10 minutes before a surgical airway is secured, despite multiple attempts to ventilate and intubate the patient Surgery is aborted and the patient fails to wake up Emergency head radiologic studies are consistent with anoxic brain injury The CA-3 participant is instructed to consult with the patient's husband regarding perioperative events and provide follow up recommendations The patient's husband questions the following: What happened? Why wasn't I told about this as a complication before surgery? Will she recover? Will there be financial compensation and how much? How can this be prevented in the future?

# Patient Care:

o Describe possible consequences of the event, including immediate or long term effects
o Provide information regarding any tests, procedures, therapies that may be necessary as a result of the event
o Provide information regarding changes in level of monitoring or care that may be necessary as a result of the event
o Make a plan for managing the patient's condition and comfort

# Medical Knowledge

o Explain the nature of an unanticipated outcome
o Give a factual explanation of what is known to have contributed to an unanticipated outcome

Practice Based Learning and Improvement

o Communicate that the factors that resulted in the event will be investigated o Describe the steps that will be taken to evaluate contributing factors o Describe the steps that will be taken to prevent recurrences

# Systems Based Practice

o Offer to obtain support from appropriate health care workers (social workers, clergy, consultants)
o Notify the correct people in hospital administration that an error has occurred
o Provide adequate documentation in the patient's medical chart regarding the event
o Provide appropriate documentation in the patient's medical chart regarding your discussion

Interpersonal Skills and Communication o Communicate a statement that an unexpected event occurred o Provide an expression of regret/empathy to the patient and/or family o Answer questions posed by patient and/or family about the medical error o Maintain eye contact at comfortable intervals throughout the interview

# Professionalism

o Avoid medical jargon
o Respond to patient/family concerns and expectations
o Expresses willingness to be helpful to the patient/family in addressing their concerns
o Identify who will communicate with the patient and/or their family on an ongoing basis
