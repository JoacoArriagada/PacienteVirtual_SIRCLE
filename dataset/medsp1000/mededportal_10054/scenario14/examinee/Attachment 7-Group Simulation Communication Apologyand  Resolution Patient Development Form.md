# SIMULATION CENTER

Goldberg Surgical Skills Laboratory An American College of Surgeons Accredited Education Institute Patient Development Form

Lesson Title: CARe (Communication, Apology and Resolution)

Clinical Service and Learner: Anesthesia/Group Simulation: CA-1 through CA-3 residents. CA-3 residents will team lead the disclosure.

Environment and Scenarios: Two clinical scenarios will be presented in either a patient care or simulation consultation room, during which time two CA-3 participants (primary anesthesiologist and either the surgeon or circulating nurse) will be made aware of a perioperative error or complication. They will be instructed to inform the patient about what happened and provide consultative advice.

# Scenario 2: Anoxic Brain Injury

A 35yo woman for breast augmentation under general anesthesia has an unexpectedly difficult induction. Difficult mask ventilation, unable to intubate The patient is significantly hypoxemic for 10 minutes before a surgical airway is secured, despite multiple attempts to ventilate and intubate the patient Surgery is aborted and the patient fails to wake up The CA-3 participant is instructed to consult with the patient's husband regarding perioperative events and provide follow up recommendations
