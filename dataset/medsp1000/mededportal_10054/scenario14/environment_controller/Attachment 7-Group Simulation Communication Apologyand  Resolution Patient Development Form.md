# SIMULATION CENTER

Goldberg Surgical Skills Laboratory An American College of Surgeons Accredited Education Institute Patient Development Form

Lesson Title: CARe (Communication, Apology and Resolution)

Environment and Scenarios: Two clinical scenarios will be presented in either a patient care or simulation consultation room, during which time two CA-3 participants (primary anesthesiologist and either the surgeon or circulating nurse) will be made aware of a perioperative error or complication. They will be instructed to inform the patient about what happened and provide consultative advice. Participants are expected to work through principles of communication, apology and resolution, develop a patient care plan and initiate appropriate follow up therapy in a timely manner.

# Scenario 2: Anoxic Brain Injury

A 35yo woman for breast augmentation under general anesthesia has an unexpectedly difficult induction. Difficult mask ventilation, unable to intubate The patient is significantly hypoxemic for 10 minutes before a surgical airway is secured, despite multiple attempts to ventilate and intubate the patient Surgery is aborted and the patient fails to wake up Emergency head radiologic studies are consistent with anoxic brain injury The CA-3 participant is instructed to consult with the patient's husband regarding perioperative events and provide follow up recommendations The patient's husband questions the following: What happened? Why wasn't I told about this as a complication before surgery? Will she recover? Will there be financial compensation and how much? How can this be prevented in the future?
