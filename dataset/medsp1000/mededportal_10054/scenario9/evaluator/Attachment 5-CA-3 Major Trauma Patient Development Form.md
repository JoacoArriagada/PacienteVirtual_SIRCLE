# SIMULATION CENTER

Goldberg Surgical Skills Laboratory An American College of Surgeons Accredited Education Institute Patient Development Form

Lesson Title: Major Trauma

Clinical Service and Learner: CA-3 Anesthesia Residents

# Goals:

Gather essential patient data and mobilize appropriate resources to manage a major trauma patient in a timely, efficient manner, maximizing patient safety and care quality

# Objectives and Competencies:

Demonstrate ability to gather essential patient data quickly and methodically in an effort to provide a seamless handover (history, labs, physical exam findings, SBAR) (Patient Care, Medical Knowledge, Practice Based Learning and Improvement, Systems Based Practice, Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Professionalism)
Demonstrate ability to identify a team leader and back up assistants (Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Professionalism)
 Initiate the process of crew and crisis resource management (Systems Based Practice, Practice Based Learning and Improvement, Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Professionalism) o Mobilize staff to accommodate patient needs (secretary, orderly, nurse, anesthesia, tech, surgeon) and give specific instructions to each employing closed loop communication o Articulate specific anesthesia workstation setup instructions o Order appropriate blood products and surgical airway devices (if indicated) o Systems based awareness of need for back up anesthesia and respiratory support with mechanical ventilation and intensive care consultation for postoperative care

# Environment and Scenario:

One scenario will be presented at a live OR desk wherein participants will be notified of an impending major trauma patient requiring an emergency exploratory laparotomy. Participants will be expected to gather essential patient data quickly and methodically, identify a team leader and back up assistants, and implement principles of both crew and crisis resource management expeditiously. All interventions will be hands on.

# Part 1:

CA-3 participants receive a phone call from the trauma surgeon notifying them that a patient will be brought to the OR in exactly10 minutes for an emergency exploratory laparotomy. The patient is currently in the CT scanner. The trauma surgeon is very busy triaging other ED patient problems on the phone and is precluded from communicating additional information. No additional information will be provided unless requested by the participants. Residents will be expected to inquire about the following:

o HPI
o PMHx
o Medications
o Allergies
o Vital Signs
o Height and Weight
o Airway Status
o IV Access / Invasive Lines
o Cervical stability
o Labs
o T&S / Blood availability
o Foley catheter

Part 2: Participants will be expected to identify a team leader and designate backup assistants.
