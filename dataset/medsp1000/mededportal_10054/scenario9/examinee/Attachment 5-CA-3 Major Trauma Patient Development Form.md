# SIMULATION CENTER

Goldberg Surgical Skills Laboratory An American College of Surgeons Accredited Education Institute Patient Development Form

Lesson Title: Major Trauma

Clinical Service and Learner: CA-3 Anesthesia Residents

# Environment and Scenario:

One scenario will be presented at a live OR desk wherein participants will be notified of an impending major trauma patient requiring an emergency exploratory laparotomy. All interventions will be hands on.

# Part 1:

CA-3 participants receive a phone call from the trauma surgeon notifying them that a patient will be brought to the OR in exactly10 minutes for an emergency exploratory laparotomy. The patient is currently in the CT scanner. The trauma surgeon is very busy triaging other ED patient problems on the phone and is precluded from communicating additional information. No additional information will be provided unless requested by the participants.
