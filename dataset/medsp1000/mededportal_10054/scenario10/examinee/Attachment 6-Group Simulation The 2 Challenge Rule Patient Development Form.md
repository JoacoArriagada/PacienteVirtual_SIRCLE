# Scenario 1: Incomplete Medical Workup / Difficult Intubation

A surgeon, running significantly behind schedule, requests expeditious case turnover for an elective right mastectomy on a 78 year old female. General anesthesia consent has been obtained, although the anesthesiologist requested to speak with the patient's PCP to obtain more detailed medical information. The PCP was busy evaluating a patient and will return the phone call in approximately 10 minutes. An anesthesia tech inform the anesthesiologist that there will be a 10 minute delay obtaining an advanced airway cart, secondary to equipment cleaning. The surgeon and circulating nurse both enter the room rushed and begin to wheel the patient back to the OR, in an effort make up for lost time. They reassure the patient that they will take great care of her during the procedure in a cheerful demeanor.
