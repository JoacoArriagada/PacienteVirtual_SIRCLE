# Scenario 1: Incomplete Medical Workup / Difficult Intubation

A surgeon, running significantly behind schedule, requests expeditious case turnover for an elective right mastectomy on a 78 year old female with the below noted medical problems:

o Right breast CA, o Symptomatic Reflux,

o HTN,
o CAD,
o Valvular Heart Disease o COPD,
o Longstanding IDDM, and o Renal Insufficiency.

The patient is a poor historian and states that she had some "heart tests" in the recent past because of a PCP concern. She does not know the test results. The patient did not bring in her medication list, admits being allergic to some antibiotic ("swelling"). The only labs charted are a Hct of 42, Plt of 320K, and an EKG with SR at 90, NSSTT changes, T wave inversion inferior leads. More labs were drawn in her PCP's office although the results are not available. On physical exam the woman has a receding mandible and is a presumed difficult intubation. She does not remember being told about previous anesthesia-related problems. There are no old medical records available for review. The patient requests general anesthesia and does not want to know anything about the procedure. General anesthesia consent has been obtained.
