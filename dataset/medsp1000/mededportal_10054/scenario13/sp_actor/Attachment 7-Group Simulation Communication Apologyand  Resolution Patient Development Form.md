Lesson Title: CARe (Communication, Apology and Resolution)

# Scenario 1: Awareness Under Anesthesia

A 36 year old woman $\mathsf { s } / \mathsf { p }$ emergency cesarean section under general anesthesia for fetal distress secondary to placental abruption and maternal hypotension is reported to have awareness under anesthesia by the PACU nurse
The patient participant reports: "Feeling a cold tube go down my throat", "Trying to scream that I felt pain but I couldn't move" "Heard everything that was said in the OR"
The patient participant questions the following: What happened? Did I imagine this? Why wasn't I told about this as a complication before surgery? Who is to blame? Couldn't this have been prevented with a BIS monitor? Will there be financial compensation and how much? How can I be sure that this won't happen again?
