# SIMULATION CENTER

Goldberg Surgical Skills Laboratory An American College of Surgeons Accredited Education Institute Patient Development Form

Lesson Title: CARe (Communication, Apology and Resolution)

Environment and Scenarios: Two clinical scenarios will be presented in either a patient care or simulation consultation room, during which time two CA-3 participants (primary anesthesiologist and either the surgeon or circulating nurse) will be made aware of a perioperative error or complication. They will be instructed to inform the patient about what happened and provide consultative advice. Participants are expected to work through principles of communication, apology and resolution, develop a patient care plan and initiate appropriate follow up therapy in a timely manner.

# Scenario 1: Awareness Under Anesthesia

A 36 year old woman $\mathsf { s } / \mathsf { p }$ emergency cesarean section under general anesthesia for fetal distress secondary to placental abruption and maternal hypotension is reported to have awareness under anesthesia by the PACU nurse
The patient participant reports: "Feeling a cold tube go down my throat", "Trying to scream that I felt pain but I couldn't move" "Heard everything that was said in the OR"
The CA-3 participant is instructed to consult with the patient regarding awareness under anesthesia and provide follow up recommendations
The patient participant questions the following: What happened? Did I imagine this? Why wasn't I told about this as a complication before surgery? Who is to blame? Couldn't this have been prevented with a BIS monitor? Will there be financial compensation and how much? How can I be sure that this won't happen again?
