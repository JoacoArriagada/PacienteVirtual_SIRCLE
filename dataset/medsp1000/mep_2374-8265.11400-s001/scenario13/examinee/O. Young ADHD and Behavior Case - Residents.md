**Case 5**

**Initial Visit**

CC: behavior and school concerns

Miles is 4-year-old African American boy who presents with his mother due to behavioral concerns. He was just kicked out of his second preschool, and mom is very concerned because her husband is deployed and she works full time, so she needs him to be in preschool or some other childcare during the day. They just moved to Ohio from Florida, where he was also recently kicked out of a daycare for disruptive behavior. She thinks Miles has always been an “on-the-go” and strong-willed child, but his behaviors seem to be escalating recently. She is looking for advice on what to do next and how to handle his behaviors.
