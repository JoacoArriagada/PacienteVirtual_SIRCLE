**Case 4: Depression**

**Follow-up Visit #2 (Clinic Visit)**

Recap: Chris is a 15yoM with MDD who was started on Zoloft and increased from 25mg to 50mg PO daily and switched to bedtime dosing at a phone check-in 4wks ago.

History with parents in room:

Chris is reportedly adherent with current dose of Zoloft 50mg po daily with some mild improvements in mood. His parents claim that he has a better attitude towards them and seems to make a better effort at trying to improve his grades. Since switching to evening dosing, Chris denies any medication side effects. He is still not sure if it is helping him much. He recently had an intake appointment for individual psychotherapy that he says went “OK.”

When interviewed alone, Chris admits to having frequent thoughts of wishing he would not wake up or that he might be better off dead, occurring several days per week and lasting at least an hour before bed, though these thoughts have been decreasing in frequency since starting mental health treatment. He has never tried to kill himself, but he has had fleeting thoughts about shooting himself in the past. He denies any intent to act on them and denies access to a gun at home.

Chris also reports his primary coping mechanism for dealing with these feelings has been smoking marijuana. He admits to smoking marijuana a couple times per week after school with a friend. He has a caffeinated soda 1-2 times per day and vapes nicotine a few times per month. He denies other substance use, denies negative consequences to substance use, and does not want you to tell his parents about his substance use.

He is more open and engaged in interview and less irritable, though his affect remains dysphoric and constricted.
