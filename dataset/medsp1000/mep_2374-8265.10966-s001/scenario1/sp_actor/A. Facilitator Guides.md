# **COMMON CALLS: CHEST PAIN**

**CASE # 1 (12-15 minutes):**

You are on a night float rotation and are called at 10 pm about a 52-year-old woman admitted to general surgery 2 days ago with cholecystitis, s/p cholecystectomy yesterday. She developed chest pain earlier tonight. You go to the bedside.

She has 6/10 pressure in the middle and left side of her chest. Has nausea but no SOB. Has diabetic neuropathy but no past cardiac problems or workup.
