**Appendix F. Medicine Role Plays**

**Medicine Role Play #2**

**Scenario:**

HW is a 70-year-old long-standing patient at your clinic. HW has a PMHx of embolic stroke from atrial fibrillation that occurred 5 years ago and has no residual deficits. HW is here for an annual visit. When prompted about COVID vaccination, HW reports hearing on the news about clots due to the COVID vaccine and now refuses to get it.

***Patient:*** You have a strong relationship with your physician and trust their advice. You appreciate how helpful they have been towards you, especially in your care after the stroke. You’ve heard at the community center and on the news about people with strokes from the vaccine, and if you are truly at high risk because of your history, then you don’t want to get vaccinated.
