**Appendix G. Pediatrics Role Plays**

**Pediatrics Role Play #1**

**Scenario:**

DF is a 34-year-old parent who brings Tracy, a 12-year old adolescent daughter, to the clinic for a well child visit. When prompted about COVID vaccination, DF says they are not interested. Tracy has received all other pediatric vaccines.

***Physician:*** Employ ARTS and MI strategies to understand DF’s stage of readiness around COVID vaccination and how to best respect the patient’s autonomy.

***Patient:*** You are concerned that COVID vaccination will affect your daughter’s menstrual cycle and later fertility.
