**Appendix F. Medicine Role Plays**

**Medicine Role Play #1**

**Scenario:**

AK is 45 years old with a PMHx of HTN, HLD, and COPD, who presents to the clinic to establish care. AK just moved to LI with family, and is seeking a new physician. When prompted about flu and COVID vaccination, AK reports getting the flu shot every year but has not been vaccinated for COVID.

***Physician:*** Employ ARTS and MI strategies to understand AK’s stage of readiness around COVID vaccination and how to best respect the patient’s autonomy.

***Patient:*** You believe the COVID vaccine was rushed and also have a close family member who felt sick and passed out after getting vaccinated, needing to go to the hospital. In late 2020, you had COVID and were sick for a week with fever and cough, but recovered. You do not want to get vaccinated for these reasons.
