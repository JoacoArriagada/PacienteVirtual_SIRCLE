**Appendix G. Pediatrics Role Plays**

**Pediatric Role Play #2**

**Scenario:**

BN is a 40-year-old parent who brings in Dylan, a 10 year old child, to the clinic for an annual well visit. The child has a history of asthma and atopic dermatitis (well controlled with as needed inhaler) who has met all developmental milestones and growth is as expected. All childhood vaccines are UTD. When prompted about COVID vaccination, BN says they have personally been vaccinated, but doesn't think Dylan needs it.
