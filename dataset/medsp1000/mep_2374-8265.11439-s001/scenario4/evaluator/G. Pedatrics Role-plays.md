**Appendix G. Pediatrics Role Plays**

**Pediatric Role Play #2**

**Scenario:**

BN is a 40-year-old parent who brings in Dylan, a 10 year old child, to the clinic for an annual well visit. The child has a history of asthma and atopic dermatitis (well controlled with as needed inhaler) who has met all developmental milestones and growth is as expected. All childhood vaccines are UTD. When prompted about COVID vaccination, BN says they have personally been vaccinated, but doesn't think Dylan needs it.

***Physician:*** Employ ARTS and MI strategies to understand BN’s stage of readiness around COVID vaccination and best support the parent in deciding about their child’s health.

***Patient:*** You believe your child’s immune system is very strong because you ensure they only eat organic foods and rarely eat out. Also, you keep up with the news on a frequent basis and know that infection rates for COVID are currently very low, so you feel it is not a worry anymore.

Your child’s physician addresses your concerns around vaccination and you would like to have your child vaccinated, but you are now worried because your partner is not in agreement.
