**ACGME Competencies: Systems Based Practice, Patient Care, Communication**

Writer: Foley (October 2010)

Mr. L. is an 89 year old man you have cared for with advanced Alzheimer’s dementia who resides in the memory care section of the Acme Nursing Home. He has exhibited episodic delusional thinking that has interfered with his personal care. The nursing home staff has been able to care for him through behavioral interventions and until recently, he has not required treatment with a psychoactive medication. Three months ago, he exhibited uncontrollable “agitation” related to a fixed delusion (he needed to join the Army) that did not respond to staff attempts at distraction, redirection, and reassurance. He required admission to a geropsychiatry unit and was treated with quetiapine. He was discharged on a scheduled dose of quetiapine, 50 mg in the morning and 100 mg at night. He has tolerated the medication well without emergence of tardive dyskinesia. Mr. L.’s care is due to be discussed at the next behavioral team meeting. CNA’s have reported that some personal care (bathing) cannot be completed (1-2 days per week) due to his delusional thinking. They continue to try non-behavioral interventions with limited success. Mr. L. is receiving donepezil and memantine for his dementia and no additional prescription medications other than quetiapine. Mr. L.’s daughter (his guardian) will attend the team meeting. During a recent telephone conversation with you, she requested that the quetiapine not be discontinued as Mr. L. had a “difficult time” in the geropsychiatry unit and she would “not like that situation to happen again”.

**Premise:** Issues to be discussed at the meeting include a review of Mr. L’s behaviors, the interventions used to relieve his distress, the care team’s perspective of his behaviors, and the daughter’s concerns. Attendees will include the daughter, the primary CNA for Mr. L., and an LPN who knows Mr. L. well.

**Ideally, the fellow should ask for [and evaluate]:** 1) Behavior log for Mr. L., 2) Description of the specific interventions used by facility staff to lessen behaviors, 3) Events that occurred during the geropsychiatry admission.

**Case Objectives:**

**PRIMARY**

1. Fellow will be able to lead a team meeting and demonstrate the ability to gather information from attendees in a collaborative and respectful manner.

2. Fellow will be able to provide effective non-pharmacologic behavior management advice.

**SECONDARY**

3. Fellow will demonstrate the ability to create a care plan taking into account the feedback provided by multiple parties.

**Case specific needs:** Elderly male SP; and 3 women SPs to participate in the meeting as the CNA, LPN, and daughter.
