You are feeling very weak with very little energy. You have accepted the fact that you have a terminal illness and feel that dying would be preferred to lying in bed all day. You always have pain, even though you have been getting some pain medication. Your son is important to you and is your power of attorney for health care. You have talked to him about your wishes.
