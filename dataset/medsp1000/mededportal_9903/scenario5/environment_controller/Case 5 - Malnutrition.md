**(Geriatric Content: Geriatric Syndromes)**

**ACGME Competencies: Pt. Care; Medical Knowledge; Communication**

Writer: Noel (Sept. 2010)

Mrs. H is a 75 year woman who has been a widow for 10 years. She was admitted to the long term care (LTC) section of the nursing facility about 1 year ago after a routine hospitalization. Up to that point she had lived in her home of 50 years, a large two story home in an established upper income community. Her family had always lived in the community with her parents’ home across the street from the one she and her husband built. Her parents were both faculty members at a large university and her husband was a prominent business man in the community. She had a college degree before many women in her community. She had 2 children, a son and daughter who are both in their 50s now and live across the country (one east coast, the other in a city about 500 miles away). The son has 2 children – one who lived with Mrs. H while she was in college and still lives nearby; the daughter is single with an estranged relationship with her mother. Students have rented out rooms in Mrs. H’s large home and provided services to Mrs. H such as grocery shopping up until the time that Mrs. H entered the LTC. After the birth of her children she had child birth fever with seizures which at the time was thought to be due to her teeth, so many of her teeth were pulled. No replacements (such as partial dentures or bridges) were made. Over the last ten years as a widow, her only visits to her home are the graduate students who live with her and her children once a year when she is admitted to the hospital (usually in July). This last admission a year ago resulted in the decision that she is unable to continue to live alone since all of the students she has had living with her are moving out, and no replacements can easily be found.
