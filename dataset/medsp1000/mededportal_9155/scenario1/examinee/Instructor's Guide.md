# INSTRUCTOR’S GUIDE FOR TOTAL ANOMALOUS PULMONARY VENOUSCONNECTION SIMULATION SCENARIO

XI. Case Narrative

a. Scenario background given to participants. Mode of arrival is by ambulance. An EMS run sheet is provided that lists the chief complaint, history of present illness and review of systems, past medical history, medication information and allergies.

Pertinent details (such as the particular extremity on which the blood pressure was obtained) are not specified for both purposes of realism and to provide learners with an opportunity for discovery during their own assessment. In the critical care room are the patient, parent (mother or father who can provide greater historical detail when asked by the participants), and a nurse.

# i. EMS management in transport:

1. No pre-hospital intravenous access is obtained. Vital signs obtained include: heart rate, blood pressure (extremity not specified), respiration rate, temperature (forehead), oxygen saturation.
2. By report, supplemental oxygen by face mask was employed briefly in transit, but this has come off during transfer to the emergency department
