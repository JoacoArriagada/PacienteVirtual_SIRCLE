# Scripted Role Play 4

Student Role playing the Adolescent

You are John Carlson, an 18-year-old male adolescent presenting for your annual physical. You have dropped out of Colorado HS and are now working at McDonalds. You are attracted to boys and girls and have had about 15 sexual partners. Your last sexual activity was yesterday morning. You do not have a girlfriend currently. You have tested positive for Chlamydia in the past and took some medicines for it. You do not use condoms since you don’t like how they feel. You have had both receptive and penetrative anal sex. When you had gone to visit your uncle during the summer of your $7 ^ { \mathrm { t h } }$ grade, your uncle touched you in your genital area 3 consecutive nights and forced you to have oral sex with him. You have not shared this information with anyone. Sometimes you do not like how you feel and you continue to get into fights at home and at work. You have not had suicidal or homicidal thoughts. You have irregular eating habits and eat your meals either at the McDonalds or at other fast food places.
