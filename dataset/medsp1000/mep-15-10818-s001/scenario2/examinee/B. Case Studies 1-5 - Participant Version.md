**Case Study 2**:

ZH presents to the health department for their third STD/STI scare of 2018. At those appointments, the patient received HIV testing, and all were negative. A rapid HIV test was performed at the current visit, and is also negative. ZH, who identifies as gender non-binary, appears nervous in the exam room. ZH has IlliniCare health insurance (a managed Medicaid product).
