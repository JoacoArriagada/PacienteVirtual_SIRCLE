# Report: CT neck soft tissue without contrast: Soft tissue

# Procedure/Findings:

Noncontrast axial 2.5 mm images. There is diffuse soft tissue stranding in the subcutaneous fat around the mandible anteriorly and inferiorly extending down into the submandibular/submental regions. There also appears to be some skin thickening. These findings are consistent with cellulitis and appears somewhat worse on the right side. Anterior to the mandible on images 40-43, there is some low density raising the possibility of phlegmon or small abscess formation.

# Report: CT neck soft tissue without contrast: Bone Window

Procedure/Findings:

Bone window images are at 5 mm slice thickness and show some nonspecific heterogeneous sclerotic change in the region of the genu of the mandible along with some irregular curvilinear lucencies just below the alveolar ridge and some smal areas with loss of lingual cortex.
