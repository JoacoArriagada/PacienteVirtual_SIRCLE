**Case Scenario**

Rebecca Ryan is a 29 yo G2P1001 who was diagnosed yesterday at 18w3d GA with an IUFD. She has been talking with her husband and her obstetrician about her delivery options including expectant management, induction of labor, and D&E. She is obese with a h/o mild asthma. She has had an SVD without complications 2 years ago. This morning she presented to the ED with a temperature of 101.3°F oral, a WBC of 19.6, normal coagulation studies, and uterine cramping. Her cramping has resulted in 2 cm of cervical dilation. Due to the evidence of uterine infection, you and your ward attending have decided to proceed with IV antibiotics and an urgent D&E in the labor and delivery operative suite.
