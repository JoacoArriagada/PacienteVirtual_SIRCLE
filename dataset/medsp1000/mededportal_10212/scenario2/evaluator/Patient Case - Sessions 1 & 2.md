You met our patient, Roger Crosby, last week and spoke with some of his family members.

To remind you, he is a 75 year old man with past history of severe COPD who presented with respiratory distress, sepsis with new infiltrate on CXR. He has now been intubated for 10 days.

Essentially, everything reversible has reversed:

* his blood pressure has normalized
* he defervesced and completed his course of antibiotics
* he had mild ARF but now his renal function has returned to normal

Unfortunately, he remains ventilator dependent.

He failed an extubation attempt 3 days ago-he required reintubation within 30 minutes for tachypnea, agitation. He remains sedated because he becomes agitated with attempts to lighten sedation for daily spontaneous breathing trials.

This information that he has persistent respiratory failure with dependence on the mechanical ventilator is new information to the family.

Eventually a decision must be made whether to proceed with trach/PEG or transition to palliative/comfort care.
