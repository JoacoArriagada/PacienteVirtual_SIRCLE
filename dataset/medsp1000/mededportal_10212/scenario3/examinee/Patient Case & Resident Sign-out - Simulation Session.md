Simulated Patient Case: Roger Crosby GI Bleed

**Resident sign-out**

You are MICU residents, admitting a new patient who has just arrived in MICU from ED. You have a nurse in the room (RN). You have a chart which includes the ED H&P. You may ask for whichever studies or data you think are appropriate.

The patient is a 76 year old man with severe COPD who had a prolonged intubation last year for COPD exacerbation and pneumonia. He underwent trach and PEG and finally recovered after six weeks in a long term vent facility rehabilitation. He has been admitted from the ED after presenting with GI bleed, shock, afib with RVR, hypoxemia.
