This survey is part of our efforts to improve how doctors in the ICU communicate with our patients’ families. How families feel about the quality of communication they have with doctors is the most important measure of success in learning about what we do well and what needs improving.

The questions in this survey ask about your satisfaction with communication you had with the doctor leading the simulated family meeting. They should take no more than 5 minutes to complete. If there are questions that you are not comfortable answering, please skip them. Your honest opinions are appreciated.

Information collected in this study will not be shared directly with the trainee who led the meeting in simulation. It will however inform our efforts at improving the course curriculum.

If you change your mind about participating in this study, you do not have to complete the survey.

Thank you for your time in this. Your feedback is much appreciated!
