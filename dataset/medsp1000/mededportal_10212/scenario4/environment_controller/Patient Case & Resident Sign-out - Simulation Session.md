Simulated Patient Case: Roger Crosby GI Bleed

Clinical update: now ICU day 4

Pt was intubated for EGD, which was done shortly after arrival. EGD revealed multiple large ulcers in duodenum: 2 in bulb and 2 in D1. None had signs of active bleeding. Two had signs of recent bleed but no areas to cauterize or clip.

Pt had 2 days of clinical stability after his initial resuscitation, during which he received an additional 6U PRBC. His hct remained relatively stable. He remained hemodynamically stable. He converted to sinus rhythm after his initial resuscitation.

Yesterday he began to have several large bloody BM. Unfortunately he bled rapidly and had a period of hypotension, with BP approximately 80-90/50 for two hours, despite massive transfusion. He was intubated and received 6 additional units PRBC. It appears that he may have aspirated since his CXR now shows bilateral lower lung field infiltrates. Labs indicate AKI and urine output is poor. Rhythm reverted to atrial fibrillation with RVR.

CT angiogram was repeated, with no extravasation of blood.

GI did another EGD which again showed duodenal ulcers with signs of recent bleeding but nothing amenable to endoscopic intervention.

ACS was consulted and they have stated that although he is high risk they recommend taking him to the OR for exploratory laparotomy and duodenotomy to control bleeding.

Summary of clinical status:

* Rapid upper GI bleed, likely from duodenal ulcers, massive transfusion
* Afib with RVR
* Hypotensive
* Intubated
* Aspiration
* AKI
* h/o severe COPD
