| **PATIENT NAME: Eleanor**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: headaches and blurry vision** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Pregnant patient presents with headaches and blurry vision and has elevated blood pressures. She is diagnosed with preeclampsia with severe features. She then has an eclamptic seizure. |
| **Learner Preparation** | Learners will be working as a team  Orient the learners to the mannequin  Orient the learners to the monitor and other equipment  Describe that the location of the scenario is a Labor and Delivery room.  Explain that all learners will evaluate the patient at the same time  Explain that facilitator will be the voice of the patient behind the mirror and will communicate via an intercom. The facilitator will communicate any pertinent information the learners need on request. |

| **Actors and roles in the room at case start** | Prior to the start of the simulation, the facilitator and simulation technician will orient the learners to the mannequin, monitor and other equipment. They will orient the learners to the structure of the room.  Then the facilitator will go to a different room with a one-way mirror so as to be able to observe the learners.  Learners will evaluate the patient together on initial presentation.  The facilitator will be the voice of the patient and will provide history (on inquiry) and physical exam, laboratory and sonographic findings and will assist learners in finding equipment if needed. Information that is requested from the learners will be given by the facilitator.  The facilitator will guide learners through timepoints (i.e. when ten minutes have elapsed from time of antihypertensive administration). The simulation technician will then change vital signs and fetal heart rate and contraction patterns over the course of the scenario accordingly. | | |

**Case Branch Points**

- After 15 minutes have passed: Maternal vital signs are BP 170/90, P 77, RR 14, Oxygen saturation 100%, Temp 98.9. Fetal heart rate tracing shows baseline 140 beats per minute, moderate variability, accelerations present, no decelerations. Tocodynamometer shows contractions every 8 minutes, duration 30 seconds, mild intensity.
- If blood pressure is not treated or treatment is delayed, the case goes on to the next time point.
- After 10 or 20 minutes have passed, depending on antihypertensive choice if antihypertensive is given: Maternal vital signs are BP 180/100, P 87, RR 14, Oxygen saturation 100%, Temp 98.9. Fetal heart rate tracing shows baseline 140 beats per minute, moderate variability, accelerations present, no decelerations. Tocodynamometer shows contractions every 8 minutes, duration 30 seconds, mild intensity.
- If blood pressure is not treated or treatment is delayed, the case goes on to the next time point.
- Seizure occurs regardless of magnesium sulfate administration. Vital signs are BP 180/110, P 67, RR 16, Oxygen saturation 92%. Fetal heart rate tracing shows baseline 140 beats per minute, moderate variability, no accelerations, bradycardia. Tocodynamometer shows contractions every 8 minutes, duration 30 seconds, mild intensity.
- If the seizure is not treated, it continues for a total of five minutes and the case ends.
- After seizure stops: Vital signs are BP 150/80, P 74, RR 16, Oxygen saturation 96%. Fetal heart rate tracing shows baseline 140 beats per minute, moderate variability, accelerations present, no decelerations. Tocodynamometer shows contractions every 8 minutes, duration 30 seconds, mild intensity.
