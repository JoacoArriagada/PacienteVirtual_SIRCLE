**Appendix D: Critical Actions Checklist for Management of Preeclampsia and Eclampsia**

1. **Initial Evaluation and Management of Preeclampsia and Emergent Hypertension**
   * Obtains history of present illness, past medical history, past surgical history, medication history, history of allergies, family history and past obstetric/gynecologic history
   * Assesses vital signs
   * Performs physical examination that must include (at a minimum) evaluation of chest, lungs, abdomen, extremities and pelvis
   * Orders the following laboratory tests: complete blood count, comprehensive metabolic panel, uric acid, urine protein/creatinine ratio
   * Appropriately assesses fetal heart rate tracing and contraction pattern
   * Optional: Performs transabdominal sonogram, requests findings verbally
   * Asks for repeat blood pressure 15 minutes after initial one was obtained
   * Administers anti-hypertensive if repeat blood pressure remains in severe range (intravenous labetalol, intravenous hydralazine or oral nifedipine)
   * Asks for repeat blood pressure in 10 or 20 minutes depending on antihypertensive administered
   * Starts magnesium sulfate infusion for seizure prophylaxis
   * Administers anti-hypertensive if repeat blood pressure remains in severe range
2. **Seizure Management**
   * Calls for help (anesthesia team, additionally labor and delivery staff, pediatricians)
   * Protects airway
   * Obtains pulse oximetry
   * Administers oxygen via facemask
   * Places patient in left lateral decubitus
   * Ensures bedrails are up and pads bedrails
   * Establishes suction at bedside
   * Continues magnesium infusion
   * Monitors fetus continuously
   * Administers anticonvulsants
   * Determines ultimate delivery plan
3. **Interpersonal Skills**
   * Explains clinical findings and plan to patient and addresses patient concerns
   * Demonstrates professionalism and appropriate communication
   * Checks back with fellow learners regarding medication administration
