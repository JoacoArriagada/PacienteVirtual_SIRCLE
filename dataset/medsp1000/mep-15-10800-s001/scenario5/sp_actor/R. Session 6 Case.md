**Chief Complaint:** “shortness of breath”

**History of Present Illness:**

Ms. D is a 37 year old woman with no prior medical history who presented to the hospital 2 weeks ago with the worst headache of her life. She was diagnosed with subarachnoid hemorrhage secondary to basilar tip aneurysm. The aneurysm was coiled and thrombectomy was performed.

1 week later, the patient developed right sided weakness and status epilepticus. The patient was intubated. Angioplasty was performed to the L ICA and L MCA.

Post procedure the patient developed bloody output from her orogastric tube associated with tachycardia and hypotension. She received 4 units of packed red blood cells with stabilization of the bleeding. The bleeding resolved and the patient remained hemodynamically stable.

Course was relatively unremarkable over the next week. She was extubated with no further respiratory issues. She was tolerating a diet and about to be discharged when she developed shortness of breath…

**Physical Exam:**

General: mild distress secondary to tachypnea

Lungs: tachypnea, lungs are clear to auscultation
