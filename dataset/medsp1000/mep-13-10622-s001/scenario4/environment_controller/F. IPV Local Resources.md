# 1. DayOne RI

1-800-494-8100

Available 24 hours a day. A counselor or advocate is available to provide confidential support, information, and advocacy on the phone. They are also available to provide accompaniment for victims at emergency departments or police departments. (www.dayoneri.org)

# 2. Domestic Violence Program of Crossroads 401-861-2760

Available 24 hours a day. Offers a hotline with access to trained volunteers who can offer advice on staying safe or creating an escape plan. Also provides emergency shelter, case management, life skills counseling, advocacy, referrals and support to help clients access education, employment, health and legal services, protective orders and safe housing. (http://www.crossroadsri.org/domesticviolence)

# 3. National Domestic Violence Hotline

1-800-799-SAFE (7233)

Available 24 hours a day. Over 170 languages available including video phone access for deaf callers. Provide resources such as a path to safety, help for friends and family, help for survivors, and help for abusive partners.   
(www.thehotline.org)

# 4. RI Coalition against Domestic Violence

Up-to-date list of direct services available including shelters. (www.ricadv.org)
