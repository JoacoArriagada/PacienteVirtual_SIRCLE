# Sexual History Communication Session Facilitator Guide

This session is designed to teach students to create a welcoming environment and approaches to taking a basic and comprehensive sexual history.

# The cases:

NOTE: For all cases, students should ask a comprehensive sexual history. We have asked the patients to answer questions honestly and openly if posed in a non-threatening and nonjudgmental manner. They will only answer the questions asked and not offer additional information. In addition, if they feel the interviewer is unsupportive or hostile, we have asked them to shut down. This is to mimic what would happen in reality. They are not intentionally withholding information.

1. Daphny Chick is a 45 year old woman who comes in for her annual exam and would like to discuss some “exciting things” that are going on with her and her husband. She has recently started experimenting with bondage, discipline, and sadomasochism (BDSM) with her husband. They have also started “swinging” with other couples where they will have sexual intercourse with other couples.

 If asked about “the exciting things happening in her life,” Ms. Chick will describe the new activities she is doing.
 She is concerned about doing things “safely.” She and her husband are using condoms for penile-vaginal intercourse, but not always for oral or anal intercourse.
 The goal is for students to gain exposure to alternate means of sexual expression and to do this in a non-judgmental way. We encourage the students to ask questions if they do not understand the terminology Ms. Chick uses.
