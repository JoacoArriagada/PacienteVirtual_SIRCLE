# Sexual History Communication Session Facilitator Guide

This session is designed to teach students to create a welcoming environment and approaches to taking a basic and comprehensive sexual history.

# The cases:

NOTE: For all cases, students should ask a comprehensive sexual history. We have asked the patients to answer questions honestly and openly if posed in a non-threatening and nonjudgmental manner. They will only answer the questions asked and not offer additional information. In addition, if they feel the interviewer is unsupportive or hostile, we have asked them to shut down. This is to mimic what would happen in reality. They are not intentionally withholding information.

4. Danni Allen is 24-year old woman who presents for annual check up. She has no medical problems. She is currently monogamous with her boyfriend of 3 months. She is on oral contraceptives and does not use condoms. She has had 15-20 partners in her lifetime…all in serially monogamous relationships.

Students should counsel Danni on her high-risk sexual behavior in a non-judgmental manner.
