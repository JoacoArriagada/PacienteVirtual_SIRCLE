# Sexual History Communication Session Facilitator Guide

This session is designed to teach students to create a welcoming environment and approaches to taking a basic and comprehensive sexual history.

# The cases:

NOTE: For all cases, students should ask a comprehensive sexual history. We have asked the patients to answer questions honestly and openly if posed in a non-threatening and nonjudgmental manner. They will only answer the questions asked and not offer additional information. In addition, if they feel the interviewer is unsupportive or hostile, we have asked them to shut down. This is to mimic what would happen in reality. They are not intentionally withholding information.

3. Gerald (Walt) Moore is a 72-year old widower who comes in with some burning when he urinates and some penile discharge. His wife passed away 5 years ago. He self-identifies as a heterosexual man, but has been having anonymous sex with men for the past $2 \%$ years. In that time, he has sex with over 150 men—mostly anal insertive and oral (insertive and receptive) intercourse. He does not use condoms.

 This might be challenging to students because of the age difference and number of partners.
 Students should counsel Mr. Moore on the risk of multiple partners and unprotected intercourse in a non-judgmental manner.
 Even though he is a man having sex with men, he does not self-identify as gay or bisexual.
