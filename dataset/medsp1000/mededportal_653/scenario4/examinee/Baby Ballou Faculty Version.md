**Subgroup 4**

Miki is a 2 week old infant born to Greek immigrant parents who had an abnormal screening result. She has been growing and gaining weight well, and has had no unusual problems. Her dad knows that he is a carrier for sickle cell trait, and her mom was treated for iron deficiency anemia during her prenatal care. Miki has a brother of toddler age who is taking iron drops for iron deficiency anemia but is otherwise healthy. Her exam is normal.

You will explain the diagnosis and its significance to the parent of this child.
