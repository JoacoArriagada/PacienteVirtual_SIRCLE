**Scenario # 5**

You are working in the Emergency Department in December. You are called to see a 14 month-old female in mild respiratory distress, brought in by her mother. PE is remarkable for a fever of 101, Pulse 130, RR 54, P02 94% on RA, BP 80/50. Lung exam is significant for subcostal retractions, fair air entry, scattered wheezes and crackles. Remainder of PE is WNL and she appears well hydrated. On reviewing the intake form you notice that she had been brought in a month ago with similar symptoms and that both parents are smokers.
