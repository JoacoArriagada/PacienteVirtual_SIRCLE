**Scenario # 2**

Emily is a 15 year-old patient you have known since she was a toddler. She is brought in today by her parents for a yearly physical. You are surprised that both parents are with her, since usually only one parent brings her. You suspect they have something they are concerned about. Indeed, Emily’s mother pulls you aside as the nurse is obtaining her vitals, saying, “Doc, we are very concerned about Emily’s behavior. We think she has been vaping and maybe also using marijuana, but we’re just not sure. We are hoping you can talk to her. Emily’s HEADDSS exam reveals that she feels safe at home, is doing well in school ninth grade and loves History, has lots of friends, but that she has started vaping a few times a week with her friends – and she has on occasion tried marijuana too.
