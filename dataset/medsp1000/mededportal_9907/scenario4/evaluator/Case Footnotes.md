**Case Footnotes**

**Low Risk Case 2**

This station has been created to help the learners develop the skills needed to help low risk patients effectively manage their pain. While most people with arm fractures have minimal pain 15 months post fracture, elbow fractures in particular can be problematic. The patient has one aberrant drug behavior and is, requesting a more frequent dose of MS Contin (currently BID and would like to increase to TID). She appears at low risk for prescription drug abuse. Challenges with this scenario include the patient’s reaction to a drug screen request, how to handle request for increase dose, adherence to a pain treatment contract and need for more frequent PT and supervised rehabilitation.
