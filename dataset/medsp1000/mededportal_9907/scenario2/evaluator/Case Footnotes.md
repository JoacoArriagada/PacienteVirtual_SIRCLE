**Screening Case 2**

The station is designed to help learners develop the ability to identify patients at risk for prescription drug abuse. The patient has had chronic pain for 5 years secondary to a bicycle accident. He is currently prescribed 6 Vicodin/day and is requesting a refill from a new physician. The learner should inquire about the frequency of aberrant drug behaviors, past and current history of prescribed medication for pain, past and current substance use and past and current treatment for a mental health disorders. Research has found that patients at greatest risk are those who have been hospitalized for a mental health disorder, positive toxicology test for illicit drugs, absence of prescribed medication in the toxicology test and four or more aberrant drug behaviors.
