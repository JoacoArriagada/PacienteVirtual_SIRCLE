**High Risk Case 1**

The goal of this scenario is to assess the ability of the learner to manage a patient with chronic pain at high risk for prescription drug abuse. The urgency of providing a medication refill (withdrawal if opioids not prescribed at this visit), unclear pain diagnosis and other factors makes this is a difficult scenario for most physicians. One immediately becomes sympathetic with the patient due to her past childhood history. While this patient is at high risk she has a number of positive factors including employment, apparent recovery from heroin addiction and stable mental health issues. It is unclear how best to proceed. The physician has a number of options including trying to better define the source of the pain, complementary medicine therapies such as yoga, meditation, etc., detoxification, other non-opioid medications, or a single long acting opioid. The learner may want to explore some of these options with the SP.
