**Case Footnotes**

**Screening Case 1**

The station is designed to improve the skills of physicians to identify patients at risk for prescription drug abuse. The patient is a young man with chronic knee pain due to previous sports injuries. He has been taking 6 Percocets per day and will run out of medication in 3 days. The learner should inquire about the frequency of aberrant drug behaviors, past and current history of prescribed medication for pain, past and current substance use and past and current treatment for mental health disorders.
