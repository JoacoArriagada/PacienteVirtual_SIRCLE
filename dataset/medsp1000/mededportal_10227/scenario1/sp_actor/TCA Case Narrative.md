1. **TITLE OF CASE:** Toxic Ingestion/Acute Tricyclic Antidepressant (TCA) Ingestion
2. **CASE NARRATIVE:**
3. **Scenario Background**
   1. Chief Complaint: 16 year old female presents after a suicide attempt
   2. Triage Nursing Note: Patient took approximately three handfuls of nortriptyline approximately 30 minutes prior to arrival
   3. Past Medical History: ADHD
   4. Medications: methylphenidate (ER) 40mg po daily
   5. Allergies: No known allergies
   6. Family and Social History: Lives with mother and maternal aunt (who is prescribed nortriptyline 25 mg tablets). Denies ethanol, tobacco, or drug use.
4. **Initial Scenario Conditions**
   1. **History given by patient:** Patient reports she took 3 handfuls of her aunt's nortriptyline (25 mg tablets) in an attempt to kill herself. Her mother walked in and saw the pill bottle on the floor. The only pills she could find at the house were her aunt’s. She denies any report of other medication ingestion today or other daily medications.
   2. **Circumstances at symptom onset:** Patient reports she has been in a strained relationship with her mother, and they have been arguing more frequently. She has a history of ADHD and is followed by a counselor and psychiatrist. She reports approximately one month of increasing depressive symptoms for which she has an upcoming appointment with her psychiatrist.
   3. **Associated symptoms (given by system as review of system is completed):** Review of systems positive for dizziness, suicidal ideas, depression, and anxiousness.
   4. **Initial Exam**
      1. **General**: Patient is a well-nourished, normally developed teenage female. She is alert and oriented to person, place, and time.
      2. **Psychiatric**: Her speech is normal and behavior is normal. Her mood appears anxious. Thought content is not delusional. She expresses impulsivity. She expresses suicidal ideation. She expresses no homicidal ideation. She expresses suicidal plans.
