<u>**Overview of Case:**</u>

Paul Harris is a 58 year old mechanic who comes to your clinic for the first time. He previously received his healthcare from a community physician, Dr. Jones, but he was unhappy with his care, reporting that Dr. Jones didn’t listen to him. Mr. Harris has hypertension, hyperlipidemia, paroxysmal atrial fibrillation, bleeding gums~~,~~ and significant low back pain. He is also a 1 pack a day smoker for 30 years. He is frustrated by his “piles of pills”, and he reports that his old provider gave him pills for everything, but didn’t really address his “main problem” – his back pain. Recently he has been missing work because of the pain, and he is fearful of losing his job. He admits to having significant difficulty keeping up with his drug regimen. He was hospitalized two weeks ago for what was thought to be a transient ischemic attack (TIA). The experience frightened him, and now he wants to “get in control” of his health.  The goal of the visit from the provider’s perspective is to become acquainted with the patient, to acquire relevant information about his chronic health conditions, and to inquire about his adherence to his drug regimen and other health related behaviors. The patient’s agenda is to get help for his for his back pain, and to get a better handle on his drug regimen.
