# Student instructions: STD case

Ms. Taylor is in the clinic to learn the results of the evaluation you ordered 2 days ago. On that date, she came to your office complaining of burning with urination, a greenishyellow, creamy vaginal discharge, and lower abdominal discomfort which had been bothering her for two days. She thinks she may have a yeast infection. Your evaluation led you to believe that she probably has gonorrhea and the results confirm this. Your task is to inform Mrs. Taylor of her diagnosis and be prepared to respond to her questions and concerns. Ms. Taylor has been married for 12 years and has two children ages 8 and 6. Ms. Taylor has been monogamous in her married relationship.
