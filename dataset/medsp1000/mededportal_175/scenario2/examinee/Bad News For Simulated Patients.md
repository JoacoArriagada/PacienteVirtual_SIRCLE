# Case 2

# Student Instructions: Mother Wants Hospice

Amy Johnson is a 45 y.o. year old woman with an aggressive and non-differentiated astrocytoma. Following a biopsy, Amy was received standard chemotherapy and radiation therapy. In spite of this, the tumor had not receded significantly and the symptoms returned. She agreed to undergo an additional and more aggressive chemotherapeutic regimen which was very difficult for her. This was not effective and the tumor increased in size. She has been offered an experimental therapy which has only a small chance of success and she has declined. Amy understands the prognosis and has requested that you enroll her in hospice because her life expectancy is less than 2 months Her daughter has made an appointment to speak with you about Amy’s condition. Amy’s daughter has flown in from Texas because she is concerned that her mother is forgoing life-saving treatment. Amy has asked you to discuss the prognosis and their decision with her daughter.
