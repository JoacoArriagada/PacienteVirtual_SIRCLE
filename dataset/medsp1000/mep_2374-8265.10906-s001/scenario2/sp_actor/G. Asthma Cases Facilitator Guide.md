## SCENARIO 2 – Inpatient Management and Disposition

Scenario - Eli has been admitted to the General Medicine in-patient service for further treatment and evaluation. The resident team is finishing admitting him, placing orders, and preparing to sign out to the overnight team.

On the floor his vitals are: HR 140, RR 45, Temp 38.1, BP 130/70, O2 Sat 94% on 3L NC, weight 16kg. He is alert and cooperative, somewhat tired appearing but able to answer questions. He has subcostal, intercostal and suprasternal retractions as well as mild nasal flaring. On auscultation, his lungs are diminished bilaterally with some slightly better aeration at the apices. He has a prolonged expiratory phase, and he has an end expiatory wheeze on forced expiration.

After a fairly uneventful night, the day team assesses the child in the morning and it seems like Eli has reached his discharge criteria.
