## SCENARIO 3 – Outpatient PCP follow-up visit (first time asthma exacerbation)

Goals

1. Practice eliciting an asthma history including possible environmental triggers
2. Practice ways to discuss mitigation of asthma triggers
3. Develop strategies for introducing different therapeutic options for asthma management including potential start of long acting inhaled steroids
4. Discuss reasonable outpatient follow up

Scenario - Eli returns to his primary care provider 3 days after being discharged from the hospital. He has been out of preschool since being discharged and he continues to have a runny nose, cough, no fevers. Parents report that he continues on the medications that he was given in the hospital and seems to be breathing easier and his energy level has picked up. In discussion with the family, it seems that Eli has been coughing more at nights than usual prior to his admission. His teachers noted that he had been taking breaks while playing outside because of his breathing in the month leading up to his admission. The parents recall that his pediatrician had mentioned wheezes once while they were being evaluated for a cold a few months ago. The family has 2 cats and a dog.
