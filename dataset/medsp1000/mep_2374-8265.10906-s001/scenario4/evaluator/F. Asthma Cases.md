## Scenario 4 – Outpatient – Chronic Management

Goals:

1. Practice obtaining a history of medication adherence and symptom severity in patients with asthma
2. Practice discussing use of long term inhaled steroids
3. Discuss expectations for Eli’s participation in activities and how asthma will affect his future
4. Discuss ways to decrease risk of asthma exacerbations

Scenario - It is now late September, 9-months after Eli’s hospitalization. Eli is 3 years old and has overall been healthy. He has been into Urgent Care 3 times in the past 3 months for acute exacerbations (once while at a birthday party with cats in the house, once in the setting of a URI and the last time when he was out on a camping trip). Each time he has required a short course of steroids. He continues on his albuterol inhaler as needed, usually around 2 times per week and sometime takes Zyrtec when he seems to be getting itchy. Parents report continued coughing at night. He is in clinic today before for the start of school to discuss his health and asthma in particular.
