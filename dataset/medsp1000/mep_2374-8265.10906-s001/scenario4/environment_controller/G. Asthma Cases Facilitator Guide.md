## Scenario 4 – Outpatient – Chronic Management

Scenario - It is now late September, 9-months after Eli’s hospitalization. Eli is 3 years old and has overall been healthy. He has been into Urgent Care 3 times in the past 3 months for acute exacerbations (once while at a birthday party with cats in the house, once in the setting of a URI and the last time when he was out on a camping trip). Each time he has required a short course of steroids. He continues on his albuterol inhaler as needed, usually around 2 times per week and sometime takes Zyrtec when he seems to be getting itchy. His parents report continued coughing at night. He is in clinic today before for the start of school to discuss his health and asthma in particular.

1. Clinic Visit (30 min) – in pairs, with one student taking on the role of the parent and the other as the physician, discuss the following topics and role play eliciting the following the information:
   1. How often is Eli using his inhaler?
   2. Is he able to participate in activities of his choosing? Is he, his parents or teachers noticing any limitations to his activities?
   3. Have parents identified any new triggers?
   4. Review urgent care visits.
