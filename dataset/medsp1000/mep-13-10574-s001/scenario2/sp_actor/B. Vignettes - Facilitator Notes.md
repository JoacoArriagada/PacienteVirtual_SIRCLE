**CASE 2**

This case is also based on a real patient. The untold back story is that this patient had a history of chronic pancreatitis and a lot of incidental pancreatic lesions over the years. He had been subjected to false alarms of pancreatic cancer from previous abdominal imaging, and he did not handle this anxiety very well at all. He turned to drinking even more heavily whenever he was faced with the prospect of bad news.
