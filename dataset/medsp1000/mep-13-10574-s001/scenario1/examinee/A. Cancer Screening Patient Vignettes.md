**CASE 1**

Mr. Anderson is a 68-y/o Caucasian man presents to establish care with you. He has no major complaints today. He is accompanied by his wife.

His past medical history is most notable for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation with a CHADS2 score of 1 for age. He takes a full dose aspirin for his atrial fibrillation. He also has advanced carpal tunnel syndrome, and he is eager to have this problem surgically repaired.

He is still sexually active with his wife. He is a retired banker and avid cross-country skier, often skiing competitively.

All of his health screenings and immunizations are up to date. His last doctor was checking his PSA annually. He asks today about getting a PSA as part of his “annual bloodwork.”

***KEY QUESTION:*** Should you screen Mr. Anderson for prostate cancer?
