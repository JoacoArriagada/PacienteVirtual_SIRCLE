Alcohol Withdrawal Scenario Critical Actions Checklist

* Requests additional history (e.g., prior hospital course and exam).
* Requests fingerstick glucose.
* Identifies alcohol withdrawal.
* Requests treatment with benzodiazepines.
* Discusses alternative pharmacologic agents for refractory seizures.
* Applies supplemental oxygen initially via non-rebreather or bag-valve-mask.
* Decides to intubate patient.
