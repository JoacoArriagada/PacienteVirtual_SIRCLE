The patient (confederate):  Portrayed by a simulation staff member.  ICU intern (confederate):  A faculty or simulation staff member should perform the role of intern. ICU respiratory therapist (confederate):  A respiratory therapist should perform this role and assist with respiratory care needs.    Observer:  A faculty member who will observe in the room and end the scenario. | | |
