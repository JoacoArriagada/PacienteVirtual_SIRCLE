Brain Herniation Scenario Critical Actions Checklist

* Requests additional history (e.g., prior Emergency Department course and exam, results of head imaging, and therapies administered prior to arrival).
* Performs or delegates focused neurologic exam (mental status, cranial nerve exam, gross motor function of extremities, and reflexes).
* Identifies acute coma and signs of impending herniation.
* Requests emergent neurosurgery consultation.
* Proceeds with intubation for airway control.
* Performs interventions to acutely decrease intracranial pressure (e.g. head of bed elevation and hyperosmolar therapy).
