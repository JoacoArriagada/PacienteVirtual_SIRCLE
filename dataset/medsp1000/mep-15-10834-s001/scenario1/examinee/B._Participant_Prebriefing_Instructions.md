**Appendix B: Participant Pre-Briefing Instructions**

* This scenario is set in the inpatient medical/surgical ward where you are working as a physician.
* During this scenario you will have access to most of the typical tools and equipment you would normally find in the inpatient setting; note that they will be available in actual time, meaning we will not speed up their performance or arrival. We encourage you to use these items if you decide that you need them. If you would like a specific piece of equipment or diagnostic test, you may ask the primary nurse.
* If you require or would like to request certain laboratory or imaging studies you must ask for them before you will receive the results. These will also be made available in actual time and will not be sped up.
* You may ask for the assistance of any healthcare providers or teams that you would normally have access to in the hospital setting.
* Please take your cues from the simulated patient and other diagnostic findings. We encourage you to imagine and engage *as if* you were working in the inpatient setting. If you would like to perform any physical exam maneuvers you may do so on the simulated patient. If a simulation mannequin is needed, the study team will direct you to use it.
* If you choose to assess the patient’s heart or lung sounds, please use the simulated stethoscope we have provided. We will demonstrate its use before the scenario begins.
* While engaging in the scenario, please engage at a level that is consistent with your practice abilities and perform only those procedures that you are familiar with or are comfortable performing independently.
