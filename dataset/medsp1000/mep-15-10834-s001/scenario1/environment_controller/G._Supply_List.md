Appendix G: Supplies List

Supplies for the inpatient room:

1. Inpatient bed with sheets, blanket, and pillow
2. Patient gown (worn by SP)
3. Auris Stethoscope or other simulation stethoscope
4. Vital signs machine
5. Blood pressure cuff
6. Pulse oximeter
7. Clock on wall (helps the simulated patient and nurse gauge the scenario length)
8. Chest model that can be used for needle or tube thoracostomy
9. Crash cart with continuous cardiac monitoring device
10. Chest tube kit
11. Several 14 gauge 5.1 or 6.4cm angiocaths (for needle decompression)
12. Additional angiocaths of differing sizes (e.g., 18g, 16g) for additional peripheral line access
13. Nasal cannula
14. Oxygen mask
15. IV start kit
16. Two 1L bags of normal saline (1 hanging at the bedside at the start of the scenario, 1 available for additional fluid administration if requested)
17. Empty or nearly empty saline bag containing 1500mg Vancomycin hanging at the start of scenario

Personal Protective Equipment/Supplies

1. Working sink
2. Soap
3. Paper towels
4. Gloves of various sizes
5. Alcohol gel for hands
6. Alcohol prep pads
7. Alcohol or chlorhexidine wipes (for cleaning between scenarios)

Diagnostic Artifacts

1. Normal 12 Lead ECG showing sinus tachycardia (available and provided upon request)
2. Vital signs flash cards designed to match the typical vitals machine readout with different vitals for various points in the scenario or simulated monitor if available
3. ED medical record print out (Appendix M)
