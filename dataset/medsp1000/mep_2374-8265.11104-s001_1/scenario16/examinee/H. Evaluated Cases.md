**Pregnant with Noncompliance of Prenatal Care**

Information for the student:

Brenda Smithson is a 34-year-old pregnant female who presents to you, the family practitioner, for scheduled well-child check for her healthy 2-year-old daughter, Ellie. During this visit, she mentions to you that she is pregnant again. During conversation, she also mentions that she has not been as good at going to prenatal care OB appointments or taking her prenatal vitamins, since this is her second pregnancy, she gets busy with her toddler, and she finds herself running out of time and energy in the day.
