**Missing Clinic Appointments**

Presentation to Student:

The patient is a 50 year old postal carrier with past medical history of poorly controlled diabetes and chronic diabetic foot ulcer who you are seeing in primary care clinic for a follow up visit. The patient has been referred to the Limb Preservation Clinic, however has no-showed two appointments with them. Their office has called to tell you that if the patient no-shows another appointment, the patient will be let go as a patient. You are concerned that without proper wound care, the patient might ultimately require an amputation.
