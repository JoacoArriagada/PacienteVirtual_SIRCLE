**Not wearing seat belt**

Presentation to Student:

The patient is a 45 year old patient who you have been seeing in clinic for years. The patient is otherwise healthy, but on a new health questionnaire you have started using in your clinic, the patient indicated that the patient does not wear a seatbelt. You are concerned about this health risk. The patient is in a hurry to finish the appointment because it’s the patient's nephew’s 8th birthday (who patient is like a parent to), and the patient plans on taking him to his favorite restaurant in town. The nephew is excited about his birthday, since he is now old enough to not need a car seat.
