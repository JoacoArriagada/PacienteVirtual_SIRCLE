**Appendix G: Standardized Patient Recruiting Criteria and Training**
2. SP Training Methods
   1. 17 individuals were trained as SPs for the event. All SPs were provided copies of the cases and participated in a virtual, one-hour training prior to the event. During the training, participating faculty provided a thorough overview of each case and answered all questions from the participants. Each case was presented and included the purpose of the case, the specific dialogue needed to convey the microaggression, and how to redirect if the learner missed the microaggression. The SPs were instructed to convey the microaggression either 5 minutes into the case or when the resident was about to start the physical exam (whichever came first). SPs were also educated on when to end the case (when the resident addressed the microaggression).
