**Background:** You are the upper level resident on the general medicine service evaluating a new patient, Nelson, who was admitted to the hospital overnight for a COPD exacerbation after experiencing 2 days of shortness of breath and worsening cough. Your intern is Dr. Harris, who identifies as a Black woman. She has already pre-rounded on the patient, but you are checking on the patient prior to morning rounds since the patient has a new oxygen requirement.

You look at the chart before entering the room and see that the patient has a history of hyperlipidemia and osteoarthritis. Respiratory virus panel including COVID-19 and flu are negative. CXR showed hyperinflation with no consolidation.

*Medications*: ipratropium-albuterol nebulizer treatment q4H, prednisone 40 mg daily, simvastatin 40 mg daily, azithromycin 500 mg daily, naproxen 500 mg BID PRN, subcutaneous enoxaparin 40 mg daily

*Vital signs*: P 99, RR 20, BP 145/78, SpO2 94% on 2 L NC

Labs are significant for a leukocytosis (11K) with neutrophilic predominance, but otherwise unremarkable.

**Your tasks:**

-Collect an interval history and perform a physical exam.
