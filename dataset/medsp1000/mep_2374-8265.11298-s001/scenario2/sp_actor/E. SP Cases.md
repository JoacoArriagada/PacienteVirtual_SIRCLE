The emotional context should be able to ask how are you doing with this, how does this make you feel, how has this affected you emotionally? IMPACT: How has this affected your life? | Physical Exam-  *The learner will not do a physical exam in this encounter. I’m not so sure if I feel comfortable with him taking care of me.”  *If the learner responds or if none of the learners acknowledge the comments about the attending physicians, the standardized patient should say the following:*  “I mean, all these dang foreigners are coming into this country to take American jobs, including doctor jobs like yours!
