**Case 2: CHF Exacerbation**

**“Patient is still only net negative 0.75 L for the day.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: -2 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: +2 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: 0 points

Defer to primary team: -2 points

Rationale: This patient has not met her diuresis goal for the day. The correct course of action is to order additional diuretic after looking at the patient's electrolytes to ensure she is replete. Taking no action, or deferring to the primary team the next day, would needlessly delay effective care for this patient's heart failure exacerbation.
