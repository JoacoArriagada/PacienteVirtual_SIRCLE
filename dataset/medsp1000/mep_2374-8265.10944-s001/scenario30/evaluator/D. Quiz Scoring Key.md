**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 2: CHF Exacerbation**

**“Morning labs show potassium is 2.2 mEq/L.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: -2 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: +2 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: 0 points

Defer to primary team: -2 points

Rationale: This is a critically low potassium value and puts the patient at high risk for arrhythmia. The patient needs urgent potassium supplementation, as well as an EKG to evaluate for changes such as QRS prolongation, U waves, or ST-segment and T-wave depression. Taking no action or deferring to the primary team is inappropriate, as it leaves the patient untreated with a critical electrolyte abnormality. (Similar to discussion points for mock page 17; a critical electrolyte abnormality which requires prompt action.)
