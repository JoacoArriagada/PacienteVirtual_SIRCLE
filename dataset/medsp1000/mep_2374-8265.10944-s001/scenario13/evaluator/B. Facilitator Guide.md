**Cross-Cover Curriculum Facilitator's Guide**

1. **Page 13: Something doesn’t seem right with Porter—seems like BPs have been drifting down in last hour. Can you come see him?**

Acuity: High

Cross-cover to see patient: YesHere you are being paged with a nursing intuition that ‘something isn’t right.’ It is important to listen to our nursing colleagues, as they spend much more time with each patient than we do, and have a better sense of what is 'normal' for the patient. If something is out of the normal range for that patient, it may be the mark of something more serious, particularly if the nurse is concerned enough to ask you to see the patient. BPs could be drifting down for any number of reasons, from the benign (sleeping) to the more serious (sepsis/septic shock). However, when a nurse is concerned enough to ask you to see the patient, you should go see them (even when you otherwise probably wouldn’t).
