**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 2: CHF Exacerbation**

**“Patient would like her son to be called to update him on her clinical course.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: 0 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: 0 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: +1 points

Defer to primary team: +2 points

Rationale: Updating family members on the care plan is an important role for the inpatient medical team. However, this patient is currently stable and there is no need for an urgent update overnight. This should be deferred to the day team. Any discussion with the patient at bedside should be centered around why the conversation is being deferred and ensuring that any specific concerns the patient has are passed on to the day team. (Similar to discussion points for mock page 7; complex, non-acute issues are not well served with overnight discussion.)
