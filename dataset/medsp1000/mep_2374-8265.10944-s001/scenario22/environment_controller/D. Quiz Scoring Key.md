**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient has questions about potentially starting infliximab.”**

Rationale: Infliximab is a long-term medication to be started for ulcerative colitis therefore there the issue does not need to be addressed urgently overnight by cross-cover. Rather, it is one that the patient (+/- their family) and attending physician should discuss as a group. Having this discussion during cross-cover is not realistic and it may actually be harmful if not all the information is appropriately conveyed. (Similar to discussion points for mock page 7; complex, non-acute issues are not well served with overnight discussion.)
