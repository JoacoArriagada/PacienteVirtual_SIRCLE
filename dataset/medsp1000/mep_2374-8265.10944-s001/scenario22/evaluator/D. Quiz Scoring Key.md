**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient has questions about potentially starting infliximab.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: 0 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: -1 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: +1 points

Defer to primary team: +2 points

Rationale: This is a complex risk/benefit discussion that should not be handled by a night resident. Infliximab is a long-term medication to be started for ulcerative colitis therefore there the issue does not need to be addressed urgently overnight by cross-cover. Rather, it is one that the patient (+/- their family) and attending physician should discuss as a group. Having this discussion during cross-cover is not realistic and it may actually be harmful if not all the information is appropriately conveyed. Hence, the most appropriate thing to do in this case is to defer to the primary team. Any discussion with the patient at bedside should be centered around why the conversation is being deferred to the following day and ensuring that the patient write down their questions so they can be sure that all are addressed. (Similar to discussion points for mock page 7; complex, non-acute issues are not well served with overnight discussion.)
