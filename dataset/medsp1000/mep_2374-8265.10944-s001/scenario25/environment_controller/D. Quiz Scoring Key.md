**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient is complaining of palpitations. Heart rate is 127.”**

Rationale: New tachycardia is concerning in this patient who previously had normal heart rate. This could indicate a number of underlying issues: sepsis, perforation, dehydration (from significant diarrhea), pulmonary embolism (ulcerative colitis is a pro-thrombotic state).
