**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient is complaining of palpitations. Heart rate is 127.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: -2 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: +2 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: +2 points

Defer to primary team: -2 points

Rationale: New tachycardia is concerning in this patient who previously had normal heart rate. This could indicate a number of underlying issues: sepsis, perforation, dehydration (from significant diarrhea), pulmonary embolism (ulcerative colitis is a pro-thrombotic state). The patient should be evaluated immediately. It would be appropriate to first order an EKG prior to immediately evaluating the patient, though regardless the patient should be evaluated at bedside.
