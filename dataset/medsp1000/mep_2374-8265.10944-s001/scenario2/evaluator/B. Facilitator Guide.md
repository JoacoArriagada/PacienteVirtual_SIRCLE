1. **Page 2: Mr. Rivers complaining of some knee pain. Can he get something for that?**

Acuity: Low

Cross-cover to see patient: NoBased on the page, this is a localized pain, not clearly related to the patient’s pathology. We need to be careful in avoiding adverse medication effects in this patient with multiple comorbidities. Avoid NSAIDs (e.g. naproxen, ibuprofen) given recent GI bleed, as these could potentiate ulcers. Avoid opioids if you can. If you are going to give opioids, remember that if the patient has significant renal dysfunction, the safest opioids to use are fentanyl, hydromorphone, and tramadol, which are cleared primarily by the liver. What, then, can we give this patient? Simple measures such as lidocaine patches, heating pads, and ice packs can often provide pain relief and avoid systemic side effects. Acetaminophen may also be appropriate here.
