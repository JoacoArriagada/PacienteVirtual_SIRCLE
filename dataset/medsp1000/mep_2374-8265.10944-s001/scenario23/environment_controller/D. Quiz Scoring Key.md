**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient has increased abdominal pain, would like something stronger for pain.”**

Rationale: Increased abdominal pain in a patient with severe ulcerative colitis flare should be taken very seriously as this could indicate a life-threatening condition such as toxic megacolon or perforation. Deferring this to the primary team or simply entering an order for increased pain medication could lead to significant harm if a serious condition went undiagnosed. (Similar to discussion points for mock pages 1, 8, and 15: a potentially serious situation that requires prompt action.)
