**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient has increased abdominal pain, would like something stronger for pain.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: -2 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: -1 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: +2 points

Defer to primary team: -2 points

Rationale: Increased abdominal pain in a patient with severe ulcerative colitis flare should be taken very seriously as this could indicate a life-threatening condition such as toxic megacolon or perforation. The only appropriate action here is to evaluate the patient at bedside. Deferring this to the primary team or simply entering an order for increased pain medication could lead to significant harm if a serious condition went undiagnosed. (Similar to discussion points for mock pages 1, 8, and 15: a potentially serious situation that requires prompt action.)
