**Case 2: CHF Exacerbation**

**“Patient is reporting left shoulder pain after walking to the bathroom.”**

Rationale: The patient is reporting left shoulder pain on exertion, which could be indicative of angina or, more seriously, acute coronary syndrome, especially given her extensive cardiac history. While other courses of action, such as ordering an EKG, may be appropriate, there is no substitute to prompt bedside evaluation in this situation. (Similar to discussion points for mock pages 8 and 15, where symptoms could be benign or indicative of something more serious.)
