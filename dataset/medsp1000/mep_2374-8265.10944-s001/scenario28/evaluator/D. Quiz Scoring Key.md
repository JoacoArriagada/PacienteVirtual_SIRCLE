**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 2: CHF Exacerbation**

**“Patient is reporting left shoulder pain after walking to the bathroom.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: -2 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: +1 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: +2 points

Defer to primary team: -2 points

Rationale: The patient is reporting left shoulder pain on exertion, which could be indicative of angina or, more seriously, acute coronary syndrome, especially given her extensive cardiac history. The correct course of action is to evaluate the patient at bedside immediately. While other courses of action, such as ordering an EKG, may be appropriate, there is no substitute to prompt bedside evaluation in this situation. Taking no action or deferring to the primary team would be inappropriate given the risk for a cardiac event. (Similar to discussion points for mock pages 8 and 15, where symptoms could be benign or indicative of something more serious.)
