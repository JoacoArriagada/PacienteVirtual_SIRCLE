**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient has itchy rash on legs. Please evaluate.”**

Rationale: This patient should be evaluated for two reasons: (1) they have a new medical problem that occurred during your cross-cover shift, and (2) The nurse asked you to evaluate. If a nurse asks you to see a patient, don’t question them or argue with them. Nurses spend far more face-to-face time with patients and are oftentimes the first to notice a new issue or complaint.
