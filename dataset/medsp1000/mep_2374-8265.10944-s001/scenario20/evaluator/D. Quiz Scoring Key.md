**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“Patient has difficulty sleeping, would like sleep aid.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: 0 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: +2 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: +1 points

Defer to primary team: -1 points

Rationale: Insomnia is very common in the hospital. This page can be managed with an order for a sleep aid (such as melatonin) and does not require discussing with the patient at bedside unless the patient or nurse requests. It would not be in the patient’s best interest to defer to the primary team as this would mean the patient would go the night without this issue being addressed. (Similar to discussion points for mock page 14, which discusses considerations for treatment of insomnia.)
