**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“FYI: Patient has blood sugar of 78. He is asymptomatic.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: +2 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy: -1 points

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: 0 points

Defer to primary team: -1 points

Rationale: This is a normal blood sugar therefore no action needs to be taken. Entering another test or discussing this normal value with the patient at bedside would not be beneficial. (Similar to discussion points for mock page 18; low-acuity, FYI-type page which does not require urgent management or examination of the patient.)
