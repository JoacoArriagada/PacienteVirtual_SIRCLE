1. **Page 6: Mr. Jones complaining of chest pain while getting up and going to the bathroom.**

Mr. Jones is very high risk from a cardiac perspective, and his chest pain started on exertion. As such, we need to rule out acute coronary syndrome. He is on a heparin drip, which is some reassurance, but patient should still be evaluated for ACS. If his exam is concerning for ACS, and he has not received a full dose of aspirin, this should be considered as well. Other life-threatening conditions besides myocardial infarction to think about on your differential for severe chest pain, potentially with hemodynamic instability, would include: pulmonary embolism, aortic dissection, cardiac tamponade, Boerhaave syndrome, and tension pneumothorax.
