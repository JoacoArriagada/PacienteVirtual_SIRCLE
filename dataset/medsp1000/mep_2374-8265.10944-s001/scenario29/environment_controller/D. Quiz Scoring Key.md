**Case 2: CHF Exacerbation**

**“Patient was desaturating to 84% on 2L/min oxygen, so I increased her to 4L/min.”**

Rationale: The patient has worsening hypoxia and was given additional oxygen supplementation; this requires action, and should not be deferred to the primary team. It is important to evaluate the patient at bedside as soon as able. It is also reasonable to order further diagnostics, such as a portable chest X-ray (or another EKG, given the earlier complaints of potential cardiac chest pain), as you prepare to go up and see the patient. (Similar to discussion points for mock pages 4 and 9, which discuss considerations in oxygen supplementation.)
