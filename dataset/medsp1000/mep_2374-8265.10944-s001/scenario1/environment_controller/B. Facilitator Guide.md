This is an “FYI page” that in actuality is anything but. We are told in handoff that the patient is neutropenic from chemotherapy. As such, this is a neutropenic fever, defined as one temperature over 38.3 or two measurements 1 hour apart of 38.0 in a patient with neutropenia (less than 1500 neutrophils/microliter blood. We are particularly concerned as this patient’s ANC (absolute neutrophil count) is below 500. This is an emergency, as the patient is without an immune system and is likely to go into septic shock. In addition, this patient merits broad-spectrum antibiotics and cultures looking for potential source of infection. While in most cases no clear source for the infection is found, prompt antibiotics and supportive care (e.g. fluids) are important to prevent patient from decompensating.
