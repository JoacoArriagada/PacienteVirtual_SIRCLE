**Cross-Cover Curriculum Facilitator's Guide**

1. **Page 8: Rivers had some dizziness while going to bathroom. Just wanted to pass along.**

Acuity: High

Cross-cover to see patient: YesThis is a page that could be benign or could be very serious, and needs some further investigation. Often patients who are inpatient complain of dizziness. However, in a patient at risk for GI bleeding this could be a sign of orthostasis, and potentially, new bleeding. In addition, sometimes dizziness is due to vertigo, which could bring up concern for a stroke or TIA. This deserves an investigation to go and see the patient and see if he has any other signs of new bleeding, or any focal neurological deficits. If you find yourself in a similar situation where a complaint could be benign or more serious, it is always better to assume that it could be the serious thing and see the patient. Ask for help from your senior resident if needed, or if you are unsure.
