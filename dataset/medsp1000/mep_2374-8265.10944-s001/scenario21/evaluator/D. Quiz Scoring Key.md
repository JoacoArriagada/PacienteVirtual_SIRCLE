**Cross Cover Quiz Scoring Key**

**Case 1: Ulcerative Colitis**

**“FYI: Patient refusing methylprednisolone dose tonight.”**

No action, confirm that you received page: -2 points

Enter order for diagnostic test or therapy -1 point

Evaluate and/or discuss with patient at bedside: +2 points

Defer to primary team: -2 points

Rationale: Intravenous methylprednisolone is a steroid which is an important treatment for this patient’s ulcerative colitis flare, hence missing a dose could have negative consequences. Therefore it is important to discuss the risks/benefits of this medication with the patient and also inquire as to *why* they are refusing the dose – do they not understand the justification for giving the medication? Are they having side effects that need to be addressed, such as insomnia (which can be treated, as above)? (Similar to discussion points for mock pages 9 and 16; it is important to follow up on the plan of care from the primary team to ensure that goals are being met and appropriate treatments are being used.)
