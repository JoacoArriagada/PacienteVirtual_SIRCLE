**Cross-Cover Curriculum Facilitator's Guide**

1. **Page 7: Ms. Kent’s family is here, looking for update on plan of care. Can you come see them?**

Acuity: Low

Cross-cover to see patient: No
You are told in the handoff that there was a difficult family meeting concerning this patient earlier in the day, with different family members strongly disagreeing with each other regarding the plan of care. We are not told who is at bedside, only that ‘family’ is. If this is a family member who disagrees with the current plan of care, your involvement could cause more harm than good, as they may anchor on anything that you tell them that they perceive as different from the day team. In these circumstances, it is often better to defer to the day team that has been having these difficult conversations with the family. This avoids the family getting potentially mixed messages from the medical staff; you were not at the meeting, so you don’t know the full details of what was discussed.
