**Neurology Case 8 (Group 4):** A 16 year old girl with history of polycystic ovarian syndrome on an oral contraceptive and episodic headaches is returning for evaluation of worsening headaches via telehealth. Previously occurring 1-2 times per month, now happening constantly over the last 2 weeks. No photophobia, phonophobia, nausea or vomiting. Headaches are worse in the morning. Today, she does report some double vision. She does endorse tinnitus.
