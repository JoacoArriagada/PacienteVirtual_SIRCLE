**Neurology Case 2 (Group 2):** A 3 month old infant presents via telehealth for consultation of newly noted hypotonia. The mother feels he has been weaker and more "floppy" over the past few days. She first noticed severe constipation and him unable to lift his head up well. Now he isn’t moving his arms and legs much. On history, more recently they are also noted to have poor feeding, ptosis, facial weakness, and dry mouth.
