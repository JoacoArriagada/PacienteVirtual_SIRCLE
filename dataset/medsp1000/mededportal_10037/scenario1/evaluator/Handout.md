1. **Group Application Exercise (GAE) I-patient 1: “should I take an antihypertensive drug to prevent a stroke?”**

Mr. A is 80 years old and a retired high school teacher who is living in an apartment in Charlottesville. He is independent. Both he and his wife enjoy community activities. He is very active. He has no medical disease. He has not seen any doctor for decades. However, his wife asks him to see a doctor for regular check-up. He calls your clinic and comes to see you as a new patient. In your clinic, his BP was around 170/70 mmHg during his last two visits. Physical exam was unremarkable. Urinalysis (UA), lipid profile, blood metabolic panel (BMP) and complete blood count (CBC), and EKG were in the normal range. Both the patient and his wife have expressed their concern about hypertension. They are interested in taking antihypertensive drug because they know untreated hypertension might cause a stroke. The patient asks you whether he should take an antihypertensive drug to prevent a stroke, and be active, healthy and live longer.
