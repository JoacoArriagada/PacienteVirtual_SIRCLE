**Waste Management in the EVD Inpatient Unit**

**PURPOSE:** To standardize the process and define the staff’s responsibilities regarding waste management in the EVD Inpatient Unit.

**PROCESS**

1. All waste – including linens, unused medications, and sealed sharps container(s) – will be placed into red biohazard waste containers.
2. Red biohazard waste containers should be lined with 2 red bags.
3. ½ a container of Premisorb should be poured in the bottom of the inner bag.
4. Red biohazard waste containers are considered “full” when they are ¾ full.
5. Apply (spray) enough bleach-based disinfectant into the red bag to sufficiently cover the surface of the waste.
6. Securely tie the first red bag.
7. Securely tie the second red bag.
8. Lift the red bag out of the red biohazard waste container and wipe the exterior surface with bleach-based disinfectant (Dispatch wipes).
9. Place tied red bag into the 32-gallon Brute Barrel just outside of the Patient Care Room door (by the Doffing Room).
10. Transport the full 32-gallon Brute Barrel to the threshold of Room 8/Room 9. ESD staff will process the waste from this point.
11. ESD staff will provide a replacement 32-gallon Brute Barrel.
12. Return to Patient Care Room and re-line the empty red biohazard waste containers with 2 red bags and pour ½ a container of Premisorb into the bottom of the inner bag.

**Clean-Up of a Body Fluid Spill**

**PURPOSE:** To standardize the process and define the staff’s responsibilities regarding clean-up of a body fluid spill.

*Note:* Do NOT step into the contaminated area during clean-up of a body fluid spill.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Number** | **Task** |
| 1 | Notify the Doffing Monitor |
| 2 | Make sure that staff is prepared |
| 3 | Make sure that you are wearing shoe covers and put on a third pair of gloves |
| 4 | Get Premisorb, a trash container, Dispatch wipes, Dispatch spray container, flat mop, and two sets (4 pieces) of yellow laminated sheets |
| 5 | Sprinkle Premisorb to cover the spill |
| 6 | Wait approximately 1 minute for solidification |
| 7 | With one piece in each hand, use the yellow laminated sheets to scrape up the now solidified spill and throw it in the trash container |
| 8 | Repeat until the area is cleared of body fluid |
| 9 | Use a second set of yellow laminated sheets if the first set is not strong enough |
| 10 | Throw the yellow laminated sheets in the trash container – be careful not to step in the contaminated area or extra Premisorb |
| 11 | Extra Premisorb will be left on the floor and will need to be flat mopped |
| 12 | Wet the area and extra Premisorb with Dispatch spray |
| 13 | With the flat mop, wipe the floor in a circular motion to remove the extra Premisorb and to disinfect the area |
| 14 | Let the area dry |
| 15 | Bring flat mop to entrance of Doffing Room |
| 16 | Place flat mop in Brute barrel in Doffing Room |
| 17 | Disinfect gloves with Dispatch wipes |
| 18 | Remove the outermost pair of gloves and discard |
| 19 | If staff cleaning up a spill outside of patient room, remove the shoe covers, disinfect outer gloves with Dispatch wipes, remove, and discard |
| 20 | Staff will discard flat mop head, disinfect mop handle |
| 21 | A new, clean flat mop head and handle will be passed into Patient Room |
