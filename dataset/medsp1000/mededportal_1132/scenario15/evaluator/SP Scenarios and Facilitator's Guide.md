**First open ended question response**:  “Doc, I really feel terrible.  Look at this abdomen.  Something is really wrong with me.”

<u>**Onset**</u>**-** “Well I have not really felt myself for the past four months.  My bowel habits used to be really regular but that all changed.”

Bowel movement daily:  Are you kidding? I have not gone in 7 days.

Pattern over the past four months-much less regular—used to be like clockwork, every morning

Caliber of stools-not really changed, just seems to be less

Blood- “You know, every once in a while I do see some blood.  I figured it was there because of straining.”

No Pain- no, just this really uncomfortable feeling of fullness

**Yes** Nausea – “It started yesterday and I even felt like vomiting.”

How is your appetite? -  “I don’t seem to eat as much as I used to.”

**Yes** Weight loss-“Funny you should ask—you would think I would weigh more with all this stool in me.  I actually weigh 12 pounds less than I did when I weighed myself at a health fair last month.”
