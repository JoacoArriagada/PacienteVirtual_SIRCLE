**Assessing First-Responder and BLS Skills: The Case of Mona Shadid**

**SP Script for Susan/Friend of Mona**

Total encounter time: 5 minutes

When the student enters the room, a mannequin (“Mona Shadid”) is on the floor.

You, Susan (Mona’s friend), are very upset and panicky (though not hysterical), and tell the student:

“Doctor, I don’t know what happened! After you left, Mona said she had pain here (point to chest area) and that she was not feeling well. I stepped out to get her a glass of water but when I returned, she was on the floor.”

- When the student assesses the mannequin for responsiveness, you should say, “She is not responding.” firmly shaking your head from side to side for emphasis.

- If the student asks you to leave the room, politely say, “I will stay calm and out of your way doctor but I insist on staying in the room.”

- When the student asks you to call EMS/911/ambulance, peek your head out of the room and say, “Ethel, call EMS/911/ambulance!”

- If the student says "get help," you should ask, "What do you mean? Who should I get?"

- When the student asks for an AED, peek your head out of the room and ask for an AED. Then, turn to the student and tell them, “Ethel, the secretary, tells me it’s being used in the next room! She will bring it as soon as it is available.”

- **At 2 minutes into the case, there will be an overhead cue: “DING”**

Provide the following prompts to the student if needed, only after this cue.

1. If the student has not called EMS by this time, stick your head out of the room and say, “Ethel, call 911!”

2. If the student has not checked the mannequin’s pulse by this time, say, “Does she have a pulse?”

After stating this prompt, if the student still fails to check for a pulse, wait 5 seconds and then reach down to check for a pulse and say, “She does not have a pulse!” firmly shaking your head side to side for emphasis.

Each time the student assesses the pulse, you should also reach down and feel for the pulse and say, “She does not have a pulse!” firmly shaking your head side to side for emphasis.

- From the time you tell the student Mona does not have a pulse, allow the student 10 seconds to start chest compressions. If the student does not initiate chest compressions by this time, say: “Her heart isn’t beating. Is there anything you can do to help get her heart going?”

- If the student asks you to perform chest compressions or ventilations, you should firmly say: “I’m sorry doctor, but I don’t feel comfortable doing that.”

- If the student initiates chest compressions prior to checking a pulse, immediately ask: “Does she have a pulse?” Follow the steps above regarding informing student of patient’s pulse.

- If the student assesses the mannequin for breathing, firmly say: “She isn’t breathing!”

- If the student does not ventilate the mannequin after 30 chest compressions, say: “She isn’t breathing! Can you do something to help her breathe?”

When the student goes to provide the mannequin ventilations (“mouth to mouth”), hand them the barrier device saying, “Doctor, please use this! I don’t want you to expose my friend to any germs!” Make sure that you hand the student the barrier device only when they are about to initiate mouth-to-mouth and not if they are only doing the airway maneuver.

When there is 1 minute remaining, there will be an overhead announcement: “(Sound of phone ringing)--- This is EMS on the phone. I need you to give me a full report of the situation.” You should hand the student a phone and say, “Here, talk to EMS.” Refuse to speak to EMS yourself. If the student asks for clarification, just state, “EMS wants you to give them a full report of the situation.” Do not give the student any more guidelines for what EMS needs to be told.

The encounter will end at 5 minutes.
