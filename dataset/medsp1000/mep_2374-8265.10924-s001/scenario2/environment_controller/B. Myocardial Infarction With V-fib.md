Brief narrative description of case

60 year old male coming from home with chest pain for 2-3 hours. Learners are expected to recognize STEMI on EKG and perform appropriate diagnostic workup. Also learners are expected to recognize ventricular fibrillation cardiac arrest (Vfib arrest) and lead a resuscitation.

Initial vital signs

HR 80, BP 110/60, RR 14, Temp 37, Room air O2 saturation 99%

Overall Appearance

Patient diaphoretic and dry heaving.

Actors and roles in the room at case start

He says he has never felt like this before. Endorses nausea. If asked about any leg swelling, recent travel, hemoptysis, history of blood clots, patient will deny.
