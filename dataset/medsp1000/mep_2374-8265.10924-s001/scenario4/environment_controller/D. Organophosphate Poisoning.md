Brief narrative description of case                                                                          | 30 year old farmer comes in with vomiting, salivation and respiratory distress that started earlier today after fertilizing his crops. Learners are expected to recognize organophosphate poisoning and recognize the importance of personal protective equipment (PPE), decontamination, and resuscitate the patient.
