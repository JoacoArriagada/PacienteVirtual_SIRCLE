| **Appendix A: Simulation Case Post-tonsillectomy and Adenoidectomy Bleed**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Post-tonsillectomy and Adenoidectomy Bleed** | |
| **Role** | The anesthesia provider is emergently called to the second stage recovery area to evaluate Henry. |
| **PATIENT NAME: Henry Peterson**  **PATIENT AGE: 10 years old**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Oropharyngeal bleeding post-tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy** | |
| **Initial vital signs** | Current vitals in second stage recovery: BP 96/61, HR 123 regular, SpO2 95% on RA |
| **HPI** | Henry is a 10 year old male, 73 kg, Height 140 cm, and a BMI of 37 who is noted to have a tonsillar bleed with nausea and hematemesis. He is 6 hours status post adenotonsillectomy performed for treatment of suspected obstructive sleep apnea. Nursing reports that his discharge to home was imminent, and that his IV had recently been removed. The Otolaryngologists have requested to return to the OR immediately for hemostasis with electrocautery. He is anxious and crying, and his mother requests that he go to sleep by mask induction with IV placement after he is asleep “like he was earlier today, because he is deathly afraid of needles.” On transport to the OR he has a second episode of hematemesis of ~ 150 ml. |
