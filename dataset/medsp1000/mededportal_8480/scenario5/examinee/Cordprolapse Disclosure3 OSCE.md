# Objective Structured Clinical Examination Disclosure after Adverse Event – Iatrogenic Cord Prolapse

Name

You are the attending physician on-call. Ms. Reynolds is a 30 year-old primip who was admitted in active labour at term. After the patient received an epidural at 6cm, the PGY1 asked you to supervise her while she performed an artificial rupture of membranes. This resulted in a cord prolapse and necessitated an emergency cesarean section. The surgery went well, but the baby is in the NICU after a prolonged resuscitation.

Please conduct a disclosure meeting with the patient who was just wheeled to the recovery room.
