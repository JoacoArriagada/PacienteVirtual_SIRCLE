# Objective Structured Clinical Examination Disclosure after Adverse Event – Inadvertent Cystotomy after C/S on demand

Name

You are seeing Mrs. Brown from the last station. She had seen you initially during a routine antenatal visit on behalf of your partner while he was at a conference and had indicated to you that she wanted an elective cesarean section in the absence of a medical indication. You had counseled the patient as to her options.

Your partner has returned from his conference and agreed to perform the elective cesarean section for Mrs. Brown, which was scheduled for this morning, during his oncall shift. An inadvertent cystotomy occurred during the cesarean section, which was recognized. A urologist was consulted intra-operatively, who has repaired the cystotomy and cystoscopy was performed. A Foley is left in place. Your partner has not yet had an opportunity to see this patient, as the caseroom is very busy. The patient is very upset and the recovery room nurse, seeing you on rounds, asks you to see the patient. The baby is doing well and the patient is stable and otherwise well.

Please conduct a disclosure meeting with the patient.
