Checklist for Initial Disclosure Process   
Adapted from Canadian Patient Safety Institute, with modifications from Canadian   
Medical Protective Association

1. Introduce self.   
2. Introduce reasons for the meeting.   
3. Use language and terminology that is appropriate for the patient.   
4. Find out what the patient already knows and is experiencing.   
5. Describe the facts of the adverse event and its outcome known at the time.   
6. Describe the steps that were and will be taken in the care of the patient.   
7. Avoid speculation or blame.   
8. Express regret.   
9. Provide time to express feelings.   
10. Provide time for questions.   
11. Clarify whether the information is understood.   
12. Summarize discussion of the facts.   
13. Offer to arrange subsequent meetings.   
14. Using active listening skills to help understand the patient’s experiences and   
needs.   
15. Adopting an open, forthright and sincere approach.   
16. Conveying this also with body language/eye contact.   
17. Sit at eye level/do not dominate.   
18. Avoid physical barriers.   
19. Speak at a comfortably slow rate.   
20. Touch hand/forearm.
