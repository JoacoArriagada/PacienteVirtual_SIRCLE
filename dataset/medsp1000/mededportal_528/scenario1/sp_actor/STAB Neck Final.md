I. ‘Stab to neck’

VI. Case Narrative

A. Scenario Background

1. Chief complaint: ‘stabbed in neck’. 20 year old male patien   
2. Past medical history: ‘none’   
3. Meds and allergies: ‘no meds, no allergies’   
4. Family/social history: ‘admits to ETOH tonight’

B. Scenario conditions initially

1. History patient gives: ‘I was at a bar with some buddies, when this guy jumped me and stabbed me in the neck. I ran out of there, and my friends dropped me off here’ - complains of pain in the neck/stab area frequently (10/10) - if asked, states he may have been stabbed in the back as well ‘but I was running really fast’ - initially denies trouble breathing/swallowing, and is sitting up in bed (as much as the HPS bellows tolerate (about 30 degrees) - does state his right arm/hand feel ‘funny’

2. Patient’s initial presentation

Patient sitting up in bed with stab wounds to right neck and left back.

C. Scenario branch points

1. If at any point in the case the awake patient has the bed flattened, he notes increasing trouble with breathing—‘Can you sit me back up?’.   
2. As time progresses in the case, patient notes some trouble swallowing and breathing and swelling in his right neck
