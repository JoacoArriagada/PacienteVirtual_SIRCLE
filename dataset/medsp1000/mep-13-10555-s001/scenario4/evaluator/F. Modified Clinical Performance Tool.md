| **Task** | **O points** | **1 point** | **2 points**  *all components present* |

| Assess Breathing | * Not Done | * > 30 seconds * Auscultation only, no recognition of decreased effort | * < 30 seconds, auscultation, tachypnea, and WOB |

| Monitors | * Not Done | * > 60 seconds * Pulse oximetry or cardiac monitors in place | * < 60 seconds * Pulse oximetry and cardiac monitor in place |
| Pulse Check | * Not Done | * > 30 seconds | * < 30 seconds |
| Blood Pressure | * Not Done | * >120 seconds | * <120 seconds |
| IV/IO | * Not Done | * PIV or IO placed in >120 seconds | * PIV or IO placed in < 120 seconds |
| CPR  (Asystole or Respiratory Arrest | * Not Done | * > 30 seconds after pulselessness * Poor technique | * <30 seconds after pulselessness * Good technique |
