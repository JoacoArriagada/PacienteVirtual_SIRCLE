**Instructor’s Guide**

***Novice Health Care Students Learn Intimate Partner Violence Communication Skills Through Standardized Patient Encounters***

**Purpose/goal of the resource:**

This resource will assist educators with giving novice health care learners skills for, and practice with IPV patient communication.

Objectives:

At the end of this session the student should be able to:

1. Explain IPV mandatory reporting requirements.
2. Recognize clinical situations appropriate for IPV screening.
3. Demonstrate technique of framing general question followed by direct question.
4. Demonstrate empathetic and nonjudgmental attitude when interacting with a potential victim of IPV.
5. Assess safety upon disposition planning for patients experiencing current or past IPV.
6. Recognize that a successful interaction is not one that necessarily results in a patient disclosing violence or leaving violent relationship.
7. Demonstrates respect for patients’ autonomy in decision-making.

**Discussion Topics:**

During the SP interview, students will encounter communication barriers unique to each case. These challenges provide opportunity for the group to discuss IPV assessment methods. General questions to initiate discussion include how to:

* Establish rapport with patient
* Show openness to hearing her story with compassion and without judgment
* Conduct medical interview that includes conversation about IPV
