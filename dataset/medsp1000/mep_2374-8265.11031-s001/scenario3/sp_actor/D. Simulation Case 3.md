|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Violet is a 6-year-old female who underwent bone marrow transplant three weeks ago and remains neutropenic. It is February, and there is an outbreak of influenza on the floor. Yesterday, she spiked a fever. Initial testing, including blood cultures and urinalysis, are negative thus far. Violet reports symptoms (cough, subjective fever) concerning for flu. |
