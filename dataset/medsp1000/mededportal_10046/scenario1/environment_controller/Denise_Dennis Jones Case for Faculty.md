**DENISE JONES STANDARDIZED PATIENT CASE**

# **Faculty Materials**

**Identifying Case Factors**

**Presenting complaint:** “I’ve been having trouble sleeping. I haven’t slept in days.”

**Length of patient encounter:** 15 minutes

**Diagnosis:** Bipolar Disorder, current episode manic.

**Case Summary/SP Training Notes:** Denise is a 43 YO woman with three children. The family has just moved to the area. This is the first visit to a psychiatrist in town. Her last treatment was with a family practitioner six months ago.

**SP demographics: name, gender, age range, and ethnicity:** Denise, female, 30-50 years old, any ethnicity.

**Setting (i.e. clinic, ER, etc**.): Doctor’s office.

### **History of present complaint:** 43 years old female who presents to clinic complaining of insomnia. She stays up at night writing a book about her life and moving furniture and may fall asleep from 3-6 AM. She states that she has been unable to sleep for several days. She also states that her family told her that she is talking more than usual, louder than usual, and faster than usual. She states that she is very irritable at times. She actually threw the vacuum cleaner at the kids because they didn’t do their chores. She has gone shopping and maxed out all of her credit cards. She has quit her job, and bought a new car. She has had a similar episode six months ago, with similar symptoms for 3 weeks, followed by 2 weeks of low moods, when she “crashed”. At that time the PCP prescribed Prozac but she stopped it after few weeks because she felt better.

In the past, when “crashing”, Denise stays in bed for days, feels hopeless, like she is not a good mother, thinks of suicide. Never attempted suicide or made plans of suicide because “I don’t want to do this to my children; my mother committed suicide when I was 12”. During this time she loses weight because of not eating, doesn’t take a shower every day and can’t pay attention to things. Everything makes her nervous and she is irritable, people “step on her nerves”.

She has never seen a psychiatrist before.

**Past medical history:** Diabetes, diagnosed five years ago.

**Family medical history:** Father died ten years ago of natural causes. Mother suffered from depression and committed suicide when Denise was a teenager. Mother had diabetes. Brother has bipolar disorder. Children are alive and well.

**Social history:** Denise found a job as an office manager as soon as she moved to town with her husband, but she quit her job a few days ago. She’s been married for twenty years with three children aged 10, 12, and 15. She recently moved to town so that her husband could take a job as an engineer at a paper plant here. She denies drug use, but states that she has been drinking more wine than usual every night to help with sleep**.**

**Physical examination findings (if indicated):**

**MSE:** Dressed in bright colors with lots of eye make-up. She is fidgety and can’t sit still. Her affect is elated and silly and she laughs a lot. Speech is fast and pressured (it is difficult for others to interrupt her).

**Diagnosis:** Bipolar Disorder, current episode manic. Rule out substance induced manic episode.

**Patient presentation (affect, appearance, position of patient at opening (i.e. sitting, lying down, holding abdomen etc.):** Silly elated affect. Lots of laughing. Dressed in bright colors with lots of eye make-up. Fast and pressured speech.

**Special case considerations/props:** None

**Specific body type/physical requirements:** None

**Props (i.e. pregnancy pillow):** None

**Make-up (please include application guidelines if available):** Lots of eye make-up, especially bright eye shadow for Denise.
