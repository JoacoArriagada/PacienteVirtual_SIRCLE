**DENNIS JONES STANDARDIZED PATIENT CASE**

# **Faculty Materials**

**Identifying Case Factors**

**Presenting complaint:** “I’ve been having trouble sleeping. I haven’t slept in days.”

**Length of patient encounter:** 15 minutes

**Diagnosis:** Bipolar Disorder, current episode manic.

**Case Summary/SP Training Notes:** Dennis is a 43 YO man with three children. The family has just moved to the area. This is the first visit to a psychiatrist in town. His last treatment was with a family practitioner six months ago.

**SP demographics: name, gender, age range, and ethnicity:** Dennis, male, 30-50 years old, any ethnicity.

**Setting (i.e. clinic, ER, etc**.): Doctor’s office.

### **History of present complaint:** 43 years old male who presents to clinic complaining of insomnia. He stays up at night writing a book about his life and moving furniture and may fall asleep from 3-6 AM. He states that he has been unable to sleep for several days. He also states that his family told him that he is talking more than usual, louder than usual, and faster than usual. He states that he is very irritable at times. He actually threw the vacuum cleaner at the kids because they didn’t do their chores. He has gone shopping and maxed out all of his credit cards. He has quit his job, and bought a new car. He has had a similar episode six months ago, with similar symptoms for 3 weeks, followed by 2 weeks of low moods, when he “crashed”. At that time the PCP prescribed Prozac but he stopped it after few weeks because he felt better.

In the past, when “crashing”, Dennis stays in bed for days, feels hopeless, like he is not a good father, thinks of suicide. Never attempted suicide or made plans of suicide because “I don’t want to do this to my children; my mother committed suicide when I was 12”. During this time he loses weight because of not eating, doesn’t take a shower every day and can’t pay attention to things. Everything makes him nervous and he is irritable, people “step on his nerves”.

He has never seen a psychiatrist before.

**Past medical history:** Diabetes, diagnosed five years ago.

**Family medical history:** Father died ten years ago of natural causes. Mother suffered from depression and committed suicide when Dennis was a teenager. Mother had diabetes. Brother has bipolar disorder. Children are alive and well.

**Social history:** Dennis found a job as an office manager as soon as he moved to town with his wife, but he quit his job a few days ago. He’s been married for twenty years with three children aged 10, 12, and 15. He recently moved to town so that his wife could take a job as an engineer at a paper plant here. He denies drug use, but states that he has been drinking more wine than usual every night to help with sleep**.**

**Physical examination findings (if indicated):**

**MSE:** Dressed in bright colors with cowboy boots or another distinctive feature for Dennis. He is fidgety and can’t sit still. His affect is elated and silly and he laughs a lot. Speech is fast and pressured (it is difficult for others to interrupt him).

**Diagnosis:** Bipolar Disorder, current episode manic. Rule out substance induced manic episode.

**Patient presentation (affect, appearance, position of patient at opening (i.e. sitting, lying down, holding abdomen etc.):** Silly elated affect. Lots of laughing. Dressed in bright colors with another distinctive feature for Dennis (boots, silly hat, etc.). Fast and pressured speech.

**Special case considerations/props:** None

**Props (i.e. pregnancy pillow):** None

**Make-up (please include application guidelines if available):** Another distinctive feature for Dennis, such as boots or a silly hat.
