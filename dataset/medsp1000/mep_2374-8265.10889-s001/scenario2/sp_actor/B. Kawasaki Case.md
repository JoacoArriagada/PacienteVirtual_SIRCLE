| **PATIENT NAME:** Kendall  **PATIENT AGE:** 4 years old  **CHIEF COMPLAINT:** abdominal pain and vomiting | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Kendall is a 4-year-old girl with a past medical history of recently diagnosed Kawasaki disease who presents to the ED with 1-day history of abdominal pain and vomiting. Six weeks prior she was diagnosed with Kawasaki disease, and her initial echocardiogram was normal. After being treated with 2 rounds of IVIG and methylprednisolone, she was discharged home with aspirin. She had her 6 week follow up with Cardiology 1 day prior, at which time a repeat echocardiogram showed giant aneurysms of both the right coronary artery and the left circumflex artery. She is admitted to the PICU for cardiopulmonary monitoring and management. |
