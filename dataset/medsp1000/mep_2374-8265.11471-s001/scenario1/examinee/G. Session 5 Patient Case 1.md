Patient Case – Colorectal Cancer:

Post operatively, she has had a slow recovery with delayed wound healing and continued weakness/fatigue. She currently finds going out of her home difficult without assistance and she had 1 fall 2 weeks ago because she ‘lost her footing’. Prior to surgery, the patient was more active.
