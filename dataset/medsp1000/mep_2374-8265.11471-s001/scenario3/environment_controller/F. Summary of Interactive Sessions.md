This appendix is used to outline instructions to the interactive activities included within the course presentations.

The approximate time required for each individual activity is listed below the activity description.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Cancer Therapy in Older Adults  (Appendix E) | 1. A clinical case was given to the learners (see appendix I). The test patient within the case was diagnosed with acute myeloid leukemia. There was a brief summation of the case history. The learners would then choose their recommended treatment plan for the selected patient based on the available information. Participants were asked to discuss their selection and reasons for selection. Later in the lecture, learners were given the results of a comprehensive geriatric assessment for the case. Participants were instructed to list patient information into either the CARG or CRASH model to estimate chemotherapy toxicity. Learners then discussed together if their treatment recommendations changed from their initial assessment based on the findings from these tools. Approximate activity time: 15-20 minutes |
