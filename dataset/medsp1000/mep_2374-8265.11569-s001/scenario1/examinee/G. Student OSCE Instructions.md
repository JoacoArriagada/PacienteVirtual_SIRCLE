To be used to introduce students to the standardized patient they’ll encounter immediately prior to the OSCE encounter itself.

**Jacob Montgomery**

(25-year-old male, homeless vet – opioid use)

He is improving after treatment for heat exhaustion and has indicated that he would like to talk with someone about his substance use. Your attending has asked you to speak with the patient about his substance use and counsel him within your role as the medical student caring for him.
