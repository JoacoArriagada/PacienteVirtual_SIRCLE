**Appendix G: Student OSCE Instructions**

To be used to introduce students to the standardized patient they’ll encounter immediately prior to the OSCE encounter itself.

**Olivia Harris**

(32-year-old female, mixed risky alcohol and drug use)

**Student instructions:** Olivia Harris is a 32-year-old woman who presents to the ED complaining of fever and back pain following a week of dysuria and urinary frequency, suspicious for pyelonephritis. During your social history, she mentioned that she uses alcohol frequently with co-workers after work, though she was hesitant to do so. Your attending has asked you to assess this further. Your role is to address her substance use and counsel her as the medical student caring for her.
