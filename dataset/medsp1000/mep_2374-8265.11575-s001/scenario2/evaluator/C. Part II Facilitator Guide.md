***Case Studies (30 minute per case, 3 total cases)***

***Case Study Introduction***

Facilitator Talking Points: "The goal of this portion of the session is to give everyone the chance to work through specific case scenarios using the platform for when you are navigating with real patients. We have divided everyone into break out groups with three specific roles. Please be sure to rotate for each case so that every participant gets an opportunity to be in each role.

The roles are as follows:

* Student Navigator - This student should have the necessary training platform open and go through the call as if in a real scenario.
* Family - This student should follow the patient scenario provided for each case and act as the family member as prompted.

**Case 2**

Facilitator Talking Points: "Let's move on to case 2. If you are playing the role of the family member please open "Case 2 Family Prompt", if you are the student navigator please open your training platform."
