***Case Studies (30 minute per case, 3 total cases)***

***Case Study Introduction***

Facilitator Talking Points: "The goal of this portion of the session is to give everyone the chance to work through specific case scenarios using the platform for when you are navigating with real patients. We have divided everyone into break out groups with three specific roles. Please be sure to rotate for each case so that everyone gets an opportunity to be in each role.

The roles are as follows:

* Student Navigator - This student should have the necessary training platform open and go through the call as if in a real scenario.
* Family - This student should follow the patient scenario provided for each case and act as the family member as prompted.

**Case 3**

Facilitator: "Let's move on to case 3. If you are playing the role of the family member please open "Case 3 Family Prompt", if you are the student navigator please open your training platform."

**Case 3 Family Prompt**

Instructions: You will be asked a series of questions by your peer who is serving as the patient navigator. Please use the information provided in the below answer the questions to the best of your ability.

Not all questions that you will be asked are included - be prepared to form your own answers to additional questions.

**Background Information**

**Name:** Sam Sim

**Age:** 37 year old

**Address:** 111 Michigan Ave NW, Washington DC 20001

**Job**: Student

**Family Information**: Parent of 3 little ones ages 11 months, 3 years, and 5 years. Your 3-year-old has chronic health needs and was recently diagnosed with Autism.

**Call Information**

**Context:** You completed the FLiP Navigator form when you were at the Pediatrician for your 5 year old last week. You are interested in resources related to **nutrition education** for each of your children individually and for the family. You have enough food for the week and are not in need of emergency food resources.

*Unfortunately, when the FLiP Navigator calls you for the initial call you are in the middle of trying to prepare dinner and your children are arguing in the background. You are unable to hear the Navigator and are feeling overwhelmed and frustrated.*

**Specific Instructions:** Please state that this is **not a good time to talk** when asked by Navigator. After follow-up, resume case as if available. Instruct navigator to call again and proceed through intake form. Please use your imagination to make up the responses as you go!
