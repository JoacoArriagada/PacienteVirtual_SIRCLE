# Case 2- Leg Pain

Chief Complaint: Sophia Jones is a 9 month old girl brought to the urgent care clinic by her parent for swelling of her right lower leg.

History of Present Illness: According to her parent she had been in her normal state of health this morning before they left for work, but upon returning home she seemed fussy, and the swelling was noticed. She was with the nanny this morning who does not recall the child falling or being injured in any way. They did go to the park this afternoon where Sophia was placed into a baby swing but she did not seem to be hurt or upset during this time. Her parent does not have an explanation for the swelling. Sophia does spend a 1-2 hours a day in her jumper. Sophia is not yet cruising or walking. She cannot pull herself to a stand and enjoys being held under the arms while she jumps up and down. She has bumped her head against the ground while crawling but has not fallen off of anything that the parents can recollect.

Past Medical History: Born at term. She had bronchiolitis at age 3 months requiring hospitalization for oxygen via nasal cannula for 2 days. Sophia sat at 6 months and began crawling at 8 months. Her parents feel she has been developing normally.

Immunizations: Up to date

Family History: Father with eczema

Social History: She is an only child and lives with her parents. Both parents work full time, and she has a nanny who the family has employed since Sophia was 3 months old. She has never traveled outside the United States. There are no pets at home.

Medications: ranitidine 10mg daily

Allergies: None
