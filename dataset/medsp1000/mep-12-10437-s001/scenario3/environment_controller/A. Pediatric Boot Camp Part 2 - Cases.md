# Case 3- Abdominal Pain

Chief Complaint: Martin Sanchez is a 7 year old boy who had been brought to the emergency department by his mother for emesis and abdominal pain.

History of Present Illness: The pain initially started 5 days ago. One the first day it was diffuse and did not radiate. He was seen in the emergency department 3 days ago. According to his mother they checked a urine test but did not send any blood work or do any imaging. When you look at the medical record you see the doctor was concerned for constipation at the time as the pain was diffuse, and he was afebrile. He developed a fever to $1 0 1 ^ { \circ } \mathsf { F }$ later that day and the pain moved into the right lower quadrant. He has had frequent episodes of non-bloody, non-bilious emesis for 2 days and 2 loose nonbloody stools today. He had a headache this morning but has not had a cough or sore throat. He denies dysuria, constipation or back pain. Although he was initially able to eat normally in the first day of his illness he has only had a few crackers and very little to drink in the past 2 days. He says the pain is a little better today than yesterday, but his mother is worried since he hasn’t been able to drink well. There are some other students at school who have been diagnosed with strep throat. No one at home is sick.

Past Medical History: History of constipation 1 year ago that was treated with diet modification

Immunizations: Up to date

Family History: Paternal grandmother has hypertension. His parents and sister are all healthy.

Social History: He lives with his parents and 4 year old sister. He has never traveled outside the United States. He is in $2 ^ { \mathsf { n d } }$ grade.

Medications: He has been taking Tylenol every 6 hours ago for the fever and stomach pain with minimal relief.

Allergies: None

Review of Systems: Negative other than as reported in HPI
