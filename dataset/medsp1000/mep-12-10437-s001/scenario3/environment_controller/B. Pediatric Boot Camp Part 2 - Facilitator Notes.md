# Discussing Difficult News

# Physician

Martin’s CT scan showed a ruptured appendicitis with a small walled off abscess. Surgery would like to admit him to their service for IV antibiotics. He will have a drain placed into the abscess by interventional radiology in 1-2 days. If he improves with the drain and antibiotics he will have a PICC line placed and will be discharged home on several weeks of antibiotics with an appendectomy planned for 6 weeks later. If the fever and pain do not improve he will be taken to the operating room in the next couple of days. It is likely that he had early appendicitis during the initial emergency department visit. Martin’s parent does not yet know the results of today’s CT or the surgical team’s plan.
