# Discussing Difficult News

# Parent

You are concerned that Sara is going to need dialysis because your co-worker had a kidney problem and had to go 3 times a week to a center for years before getting a kidney transplant. You don’t want Sara to have to have any more needle sticks because she is so afraid. You hope that they can just take a picture of her kidneys without having to do anything invasive but want to know what is going on with Sara.
