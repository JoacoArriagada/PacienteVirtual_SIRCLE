# Physician

The CBC was similar to the previous, and the basic chemistry panel was normal. The liver function panel was significant for an AST of 116 and an ALT of 95. Alkaline phosphatase and bilirubin were normal for age. An ultrasound showed a 6x8cm solid mass arising in or adjacent to the superior pole of the right kidney with extension to the inferior aspect of the liver. The inferior vena cava and left kidney appear normal. You are concerned that this is a neuroblastoma but further work-up needs to be done. You are the emergency department intern, and the plan is to admit to the hematology/oncology team. As recommend by the oncology fellow a CT of the chest, abdomen and pelvis was obtained that shows a tumor that appears to be extending from his right adrenal gland with invasion into the liver. The chest CT was normal. The surgical team will take him to the operating room tomorrow to get a biopsy. Treatment will be dependent on the final pathology and the staging of the tumor, but you anticipate chemotherapy will be initiated in the next 2 days. Sam’s parent knows that a mass was found but the results of the CT but the proposed plan has not yet been discussed.
