# Case 1- Abdominal mass
Chief Complaint: Sam Smith is an 18 month old boy who has been sent to the emergency department by his pediatrician for an abdominal mass.
History of Present Illness: The abdominal mass was discovered during his routine well child check today. After the mass was identified on his exam, his parent mentioned that they thought his belly had looked a little bigger over the past month but had just thought it was normal weight gain. In retrospect they also have noticed a slight decrease in his appetite in the past two weeks and he has seemed more tired. He has not had any recent fevers, emesis or diarrhea. He has had a normal amount of wet diapers, and there has been no blood in the urine. He has been growing normally. His grandmother mentioned he looked pale earlier this week. He has been reaching his developmental milestones on time. He walks and has 4-5 words.
Past Medical History: Born at 39 weeks by normal spontaneous vaginal delivery. No medical issues
Immunizations: Up to date
Family History: 6 year old brother with asthma, maternal grandfather died from lung cancer
Social History: He lives with his parents and 6 year old brother. He has never traveled outside the United States. There are no pets at home.
Medications: Multivitamin
Allergies: None
Review of Systems: Negative other than as reported in HPI
