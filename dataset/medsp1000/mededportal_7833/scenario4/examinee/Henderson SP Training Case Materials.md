# 31. **The Case of** ***Jane Henderson***

## Part IV

# 38. **Presenting Problem:** 

Patient is returning for a regularly scheduled follow-up visit with her primary care provider, Dr. Johnson, who she has now met three times.  Her first visit was on November 13/15th to establish care, the second was on January 15/17th, and the most recent visit was June 10/12th.

Prior to her last visit, Mrs. Henderson had been to the Emergency Department for chest pain and was told that she had experienced a panic attack. She had further follow-up and was reassured that her heart was not the cause of her chest pain. During her last visit to this clinic, she discussed her fear of having more panic attacks and her worries about her health. She has been better about exercising more often and consistent about taking her medications.

This visit is a scheduled visit to address health care maintenance.

**Patient Demographics:**

	**Age:**	61 years old

	**Sex:**	Female

	**Ethnicity:** Open

	**Height:**	Average

	**Weight:**	Average to overweight

**PRESENTING SITUATION**

**Fourth Interview**

**(Information given to students)**

From the clinic preceptor, Dr. Johnson:

Welcome back from your summer off!  I’m looking forward to seeing you again.

Mrs. Henderson has an appointment during your next session with me. As you will remember, she is our 61 year-old woman with history of hypertension, dyslipidemia, coronary artery disease with stent placement, panic attacks, arthritis, and tension headaches.  She is a retired schoolteacher who lives with her husband and their two granddaughters, who have been living with them while Mrs. Henderson’s daughter is incarcerated. Though the first three times we saw Mrs. Henderson were for relatively acute issues, the purpose of this next visit is to be sure we think about her health care maintenance (HCM). With patients with chronic disorders, there is a tendency to forget about standard HCM that we would do in our other patients.
