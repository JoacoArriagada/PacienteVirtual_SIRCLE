**Syllabus Materials - Mrs. Henderson Case Part 2**

**OBJECTIVES** 

- Elicit a patient’s goals for managing his/her chronic illness.
- Elicit barriers to the optimal management of chronic illness and problem-solve with the patient to overcome potential barriers
- Discuss the patient’s culture and how it impacts the experience of illness
- Brainstorm potential community resources for a patient with chronic illness

KEY WORDS

Chronic Illness

**reading assignment**

- Review the posted write-up of Mrs. Henderson’s medical history from her first visit as well as any visit notes and lab studies prior to her follow-up visit (see attached).
- Review the issues generated by your group at the last meeting that you wanted to follow-up on at this visit.

**ORIENTATION TO THE INTERVIEW**

B. Orientations to today’s interview

1. Jane Henderson is coming for a regularly scheduled follow-up visit with your preceptor, Dr. Johnson.  She comes in today to follow up on her blood pressure and cholesterol control.  She had one visit with the nurse since her last visit for a blood pressure check and to review her medications. She was supposed to have lab tests done prior to this visit.
2. Inquire about any new concerns that have arisen since the patient’s last visit and check in regarding her ongoing medical issues.
3. Explore Mrs. Henderson’s culture and how it has impacted her reactions to her chronic illnesses.
4. Possible questions may include:
    1. How confident does she feel about her ability to manage her illnesses?
    2. What are her goals for managing her illnesses?
    3. What changes in her behavior does she want to make to meet these goals?
    4. What barriers, if any, have made it difficult for her to reach these goals?  (note: consider financial barriers, health literacy, lack of time, psychosocial stressors etc)

1. Mrs. Henderson will return for follow-up to the group in June.

**session activities** 

1. Brief group check-in

2. Assign observers of the various parts of the interview as you may have done

in prior weeks.

3. Student interviews Mrs. Henderson for 30 minutes. Assign one student to be

the timekeeper.

4. As in prior weeks, please make sure to debrief with student interviewer (give

opportunities for self-assessment and group feedback):

• Student interviewer should first give his/her perceptions of the interview, including what went well and what to focus on improving.

• The group shares feedback with student interviewer, remembering to offer specific feedback on observed behaviors.

5. A student volunteers to summarize briefly the interim medical history.

6. Discuss what recommendations the group might make for Mrs. Henderson to optimize management of her illnesses.  Keep in mind her goals for managing her chronic illness and some of the barriers that she has faced in reaching her goals.  Brainstorm some possible solutions to these barriers.  Can you imagine any community resources that might address some of these challenges?

7. As a group, collaboratively create one SOAP note recording Mrs. Henderson’s interim medical history.

One student will post this write-up for the group as a continuation of Mrs. Henderson’s chart. You will add to the record once again when she returns for her third visit.
