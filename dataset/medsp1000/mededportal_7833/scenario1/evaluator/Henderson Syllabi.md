**Syllabus Materials - Mrs. Henderson Case Part 1**

**OBJECTIVES**

- Conduct a full medical history, including CC, HPI, PMH, Meds, Allergies, HRBs, FH, SH, ROS.
- Elicit a patient’s explanatory model for chronic illness
- Explore ways in which chronic illness modifies daily routines
- Investigate patient concerns regarding chronic illness
- Explore the role of a patient’s family in managing chronic illness
- Elicit psychosocial stressors, current social supports and coping mechanisms for a patient with chronic illness
- Document the medical history in the standard format

**KEY WORDS**

Chronic illness

**ORIENTATION TO THE INTERVIEW**

**A. Chronology of every interview – in today’s session we will emphasize 1-5**

**and 8.**

1. Open the interview

2. Gather information, 1st phase

3. Build relationship

4. Understand the patient’s perspective

*5*. Gather information, 2nd phase: closed-ended inquiry (PMH, Meds,

Allergies, HRB, FHx, SHx, ROS

*6. Share information*

*7. Reach agreement*

8. Close the interview.

B. Orientation to today’s interview

1. You are working in the Family Practice clinic, where Mrs. Henderson comes in for her initial visit.  Because of a recent change in her insurance plan, she is transferring care to your preceptor, Dr. Johnson. He has asked you to perform a complete history.
2. Mrs. Henderson is living with several chronic illnesses.  Take time to explore her experience of these illnesses.  Possible questions may include:
    1. What is her understanding of her illnesses?
    2. How do her illnesses affect her daily life?
    3. What concerns does she have regarding her illnesses?
    4. How have her illnesses impacted her family and what role does the family play in helping her to manage her chronic illnesses?
    5. What is the patient’s social support system?
    6. Are there any psychosocial stressors in her life that have impacted her ability to manage her illnesses?
    7. What are the patient’s goals for managing her illnesses?
3. Mrs. Henderson will return for follow-up to the group in early January.

**SESSION ACTIVITIES**

1. Brief group check-in

2. Assign observers of the various parts of the interview as you may have done

in prior weeks.

3. Student interviews Mrs. Henderson for 40 minutes. Assign one student to be

the timekeeper.

4. As in prior weeks, please make sure to debrief with student interviewer (give

opportunities for self-assessment and group feedback):

• Student interviewer should first give his/her perceptions of the interview, including:

what went well and what to focus on improving.

• The group shares feedback with student interviewer, remembering to offer specific feedback on observed behaviors.

- Suggested questions for discussion:

• What examples of information did the interviewer obtain concerning the

HPI, PMH, Meds, Allergies, FH, SH, HRB, and ROS?

• What communication strategies did the interviewer use?

• In what ways did the interviewer build rapport with the patient?

• What did the interviewer do well?

• What are suggestions for improvement?

5. A student or SG leader volunteers to summarize briefly the medical history.

6. Discuss what recommendations the group might make for Mrs. Henderson to optimize management of her illnesses.  What issues might you want to explore further with her at the next visit?

7. As a group, collaboratively create one write-up of the patient’s medical history in the standard format. One student will post this write-up for the group as the beginning of the patient’s chart. You will keep this as a group medical record for Mrs. Henderson who will return for 3 additional visits.
