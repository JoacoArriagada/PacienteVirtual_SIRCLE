| SETTING | Pediatric inpatient room. A 15-year-old male patient is admitted to the pediatric general medicine service due to emergency psychiatric reasons. He is awaiting placement to an inpatient level of psychiatric care due to lack of bed availability. On arrival, the patient is angry and borderline combative, but alert and oriented. |
| PATIENT PROFILE | |
| Age | 15 years old |
| Sex | Male |

When addressed, answers are initially short 1-5 words, terse and pressured, which may escalate to yelling. Avoids eye contact unless his name is explicitly said to him. Clothing is hospital-provided scrubs for patients admitted medically while awaiting psychiatric placement. |

| Habits | Typical American diet. 0-2 caffeinated beverages daily. No regular exercise. Occasional marijuana use; several times a week currently, last use yesterday. |

| Typical day | 6:30AM – Woken up by parent for school. 7:15AM – Bus to school. 7:50AM to 2:15PM – School. 2:30 PM to 3:10PM – Bus to home. 3:10PM to 10PM – Video games, listening to music, homework, dinner. 10PM to midnight – Bedtime. |

| THE PATIENT’S STORY | SP offers no additional history. |
| Onset (when; gradual or sudden) & Setting (what was going on or where was patient when symptoms first noticed?) | Onset was sudden within 20-30 min of arrival to inpatient room. |

| Aggravated by what | In the past, symptoms have worsened with intramuscular diphenhydramine (Benadryl shot). |
