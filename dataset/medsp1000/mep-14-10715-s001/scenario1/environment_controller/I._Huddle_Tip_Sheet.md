**Interprofessional Huddle Tip Sheet**

**GO TEAM**

**GOALS of the IP Huddle**:

* **Establish mutual respect** - Attitude
  + All members of the team bring value
  + Shared understanding of team members unique role and skill
* **Establish mutual trust** - Attitude
  + Allows for monitoring of team performance
  + Back-up and support of team members
* **Team decision making** – Cognition
  + Affects team performance and goal attainment
  + Necessary to streamline performance and improve success
  + Team decision making categories
    - Operational- immediate, process driven, combining expertise
    - Tactical- team decision making
    - Strategic- team strategy
* **Information Sharing** – Behavior
  + Unique or new information shared by team members
  + Open sharing and team communication improves performance of the team
  + Build cohesion and support respect and trust in team members

Huddles are great!

**Let’s do this!**

**Huddle #1:** 15 min “team huddle”

* Read Presenting information
* Each team member should discuss their professional role and how they would approach the patient visit
* Discuss the case as a team in order to:
* Determine the student order for the interviews
* Consider the unique skills that each team member brings to the encounter and areas of overlap
* Strategize and divide up tasks
