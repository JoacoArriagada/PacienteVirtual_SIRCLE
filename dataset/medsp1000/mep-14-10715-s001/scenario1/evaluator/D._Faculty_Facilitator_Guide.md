**ISPE Faculty Facilitator Guide**

**Educational Objectives**

By the end of this activity, learners will be able to:

1. Demonstrate the ability to efficiently interview a patient interested in integrative medicine and complementary therapies, showing sensitivity to a patient’s personal needs and resources.
2. Demonstrate the ability to collaborate effectively with learners from other health professions.
3. Develop a comprehensive care plan in collaboration with other health professions learners to meet the patient’s healthcare needs.
4. Describe the role of other healthcare professionals in caring for a patient who wishes to pursue integrative health strategies.
5. Discuss the experience of developing a comprehensive care plan in collaboration with other health professions learners.

**Introduction**

During the ISPE, you will be assigned to observe 2-3 teams during the afternoon as they work together during the exercise. The main goal of the debrief session is to discuss the interprofessional team experience and to reflect on interprofessional collaborative care.

Many students will have already worked with other healthcare professionals in their clinical experiences, but may not have been part of interprofessional collaborative teams.

As an ISPE facilitator, your job is to guide the discussion and have learners reflect on their interprofessional experiences both prior to and during this exercise. Avoid allowing too much time discussing the diagnostics/therapeutics of the case, which will prevent adequate time to be spent on the discussion questions (note: Students will receive a link to a sample team care plan after the exercise that contains information and resources that students can explore should they want to look further into the medical issues in the case). Do not worry about covering *every* question; these are intended to be used as a guide to facilitate active discussion among the students about their experience.

You can share some of your observations of the team’s interactions during the discussion.

# Format for Debrief Session:

1. **Introduction:** Have each student say their name and profession and introduce yourself [2 minutes]. Inform students of basic ground rules for the group:
   * Respect the privacy of patients, staff, and colleagues when discussing examples
   * Keep discourse professional and respectful
   * Everyone is encouraged to participate
2. **Generate a problem list** (please write on large paper or white board, asking for student input) [2 minutes]
3. **Review elements of the team plan developed by the student teams** (1 spokesperson from each of the 2 teams to present 1 problem from their problem list and their assessment and plan for the problem) [~3 minutes per team]

NOTE: Because this case is complicated, presenting the entire team care plan for each team isn’t feasible. Thus, each team should focus on one problem from the list and the other team can comment whether they had anything different for this problem.

1. **Discussion Questions** [20 minutes]
   1. How was your experience working with this team of health care professions students?
      1. What strategies worked well in helping your team work together effectively?
      2. Were there times that you had difficulty understanding the terminology another team member was using?
      3. Did any member become the leader? How did that evolve?

d. What challenges did your team encounter? Were there any disagreements about the team plan? How were they handled?

1. What surprised you about the role of each team member? Their skills? What they contributed?
2. How did your team handle overlapping roles?
3. How did your team deliver the team care plan to the patient? Did you feel that this was an effective strategy?
4. How does this experience compare with previous experiences working with other healthcare professionals? In your previous experiences collaborating with other healthcare professionals, are there things that worked particularly well or things that you found challenging?
5. **Closing:** Have each person go around and say something they appreciated about or learned from another member of their team [5 minutes]

# Evaluations

At the end of the debrief session:

* 1. Remind the students to go to the Simulation Center to complete their feedback for team members and their evaluation of the exercise.
  2. Remind the students to consider reviewing their recording on their own and with a faculty member.
  3. Collect the team plans for each team.
  4. Complete the on-line faculty evaluation of the experience.

# Key Teaching Points

* As health professions students *and as health professionals in the future*, they all will collaborate with other healthcare professionals to care for patients. **(Note: It may help to bring in your own experiences working as part of an interprofessional health care team)**
* For patients with chronic illnesses, use of interprofessional teams is advocated to improve their care.
* The interprofessional team achieves its purpose through the collaborative learning and working and the application of the collective knowledge and skills of all team members.
* A well-functioning interprofessional team involves members who respect and value each other and have an awareness of each other’s shared and unique skills and scopes of practice.
