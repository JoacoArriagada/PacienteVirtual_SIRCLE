| **Appendix A: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Case**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE:**  **Development of a Hybrid Simulated Patient Experience to Practice Care of the Dying Older Adult** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: Barney Smith**  **PATIENT AGE: 70**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Pain and restlessness per wife** | |
| **Learner Preparation** | You are on your ER rotation. You are to see Mr. Smith who was brought in by EMS this AM for pain and restlessness. He is in the exam room with his wife.  PMH: Multiple Myeloma x 8 years, on hospice.  Your task is to:   1. Obtain history 2. Assess and treat symptoms 3. Provide support to wife during encounter.   Mr. Smith is a mannequin today. The mannequin is a SimMan who is breathing, has pulses and heart and lung sounds for auscultation. He has an IV and vital signs are displayed on the monitor at the bedside. Treat the mannequin as you would a patient. You have a nurse in the room and she can respond to medication orders or other requests that you might have. |
