SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Pediatric Disaster Simulation Series: The Five Minute Trauma Assessment for Pediatric Residents (TRAP-5)

PATIENT: Sofia

PATIENT AGE: 5 years

CHIEF COMPLAINT: Left leg injury

PHYSICAL SETTING: ED

Overall Setting and Appearance

Sofia was brought from the bus accident for a left leg injury. She was found by medics lying on the ground next to the overturned school bus, moaning, eyes closed, not moving with bleeding from her left thigh. She is carried from the scene on a stretcher by medics.

At scenario start, Sofia is lying on a backboard with bleeding from her left thigh, her eyes open, and crying. Blood soaked gauze obscures the open fracture with visible deformity. No parents or teachers are present.

HPI

Sofia is a 5-year-old female on a field trip with her Kindergarten class when the bus overturned and she sustained a left leg injury.
