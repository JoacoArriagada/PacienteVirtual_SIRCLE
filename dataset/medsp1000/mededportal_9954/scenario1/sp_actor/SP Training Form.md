Case: Infant Anaphylaxis

Mr/Ms Puffy is the parent of an infant admitted to ACH for pneumonia, who develops respiratory distress after receiving a shot of antibiotics.

# What led up to being here today?

What do you (SP) know:

Your 6 month old infant was admitted last night with diagnosis of pneumonia after being transported from an outside hospital where they were unable to get an IV.

The infant started running a fever 3 days ago and has had a max temperature of 104. You took her to an OSH last night where they did a CXR and diagnosed her with pneumonia. They started her on 1L of oxygen, but were unable to get a PIV there, they stuck her at least 10 times, so she was transferred here for further care.

Upon arrival at ACH she got labs drawn and a urine sample obtained but they still couldn’t get an IV so she received a shot of antibiotics about 30 minutes ago.

# Challenge Question/Statement for Students/Learners:

Is this asthma? (if team mentions wheezing) Do you have to start another IV? (if another PIV attempt is made) What is that you are sticking in her leg? (If start IO without explaining to parent)

# Time Line
