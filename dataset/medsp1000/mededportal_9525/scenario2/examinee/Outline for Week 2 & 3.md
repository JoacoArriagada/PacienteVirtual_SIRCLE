**Week 3 Part 1**

**Follow-up**

Your 2nd year adult preceptorship has been with Dr. Weinstein, and you are pleased to find out that Mrs. Lee’s follow-up visit is scheduled at a time when you will be in the office.

You ask Dr. Weinstein if you can sit in on the visit, so you can see how he gives patients this news. Knowing that Mrs. Lee does not mind seeing medical students, he offers you the opportunity to speak with her first. When you hesitate, he says enthusiastically, “This is an incredible opportunity for you to try out a skill that will be vitally important for the rest of your profession.”
