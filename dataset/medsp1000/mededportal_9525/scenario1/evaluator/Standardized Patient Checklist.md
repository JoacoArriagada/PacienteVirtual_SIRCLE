# COMMUNICATION SKILLS (PPI)

Modified SEGUE

The student:

1. made a personal connection during the visit (e.g., went beyond medical issues at hand, conversed about personal background, interests, job, etc.).

Strongly Agree: The student doctor made a personal connection. (Warm, interested in me as a person, greeted me by name. The connection felt genuine.)
Agree: The student doctor attempted to make a personal connection. (Made some effort to get to know me as a person, but I didn’t feel s/he was genuine.)
Disagree: The student doctor did not attempt to make a personal connection. (Made no effort to know me as a person. Gave me the impression she was only interested in me as a disease or a symptom.)

# 2. gave me an opportunity/time to talk (e.g., didn't interrupt).

○ Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor's effective use of this behavior. (Invited me to speak. Encouraged me to tell my story. Asked open-ended questions. Did not interrupt me.)
$\bigcirc$ Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Gave me an opportunity to speak without interruption.)
○ Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (I just answered the questions asked; student used closed-ended questions only.)

3. listened. Gave me undivided attention (e.g., eye contact, verbal acknowledgment, non-verbal feedback).

$\bigcirc$ Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor's effective use of this behavior. (Used body language that was open and encouraging - appropriate eye contact, body position. Use of silence facilitated communication.)
$\bigcirc$ Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Didn’t rush me. Comfortable eye contact.)
$\bigcirc$ Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (Asked same question several times. Nervousness interfered with ability to focus on me. Long pauses made me feel uncomfortable. Positioned too close or too far away. Used closed body language. Focused solely on clipboard or notes.)

4. checked/clarified information (e.g., recapped, paraphrased, echoed, summarized, asked for clarification when not certain about what described).

Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor's effective use of this behavior. (Followed up on my answers, summarized and allowed me to clarify; I was sure the student understood my story.) Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Followed up or clarified some of my answers.) Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (Never verified what I was saying.)

5. encouraged me to ask questions/checked my understanding of information provided.

$\bigcirc$ Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor's effective use of this behavior. (Asked if I had questions as the encounter progressed.)
$\bigcirc$ Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Asked me if I had any questions only at the end of the encounter.)
Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (Didn’t ask me if I had any questions or if I understood him/her.)

6. adapted to my level of understanding, using appropriate language (e.g., avoided or explained jargon; avoided child-like slang).

Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor's effective use of this behavior. (Spoke clearly in a way I could
understand without talking down to me. All explanations and questions were clear.)
Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Used a little jargon but explained without prompting.)
Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (Some jargon, explained with prompting; explanations were
vague.)

7. expressed empathy (e.g., demonstrated care and concern for me, acknowledged my feelings, expressed understanding of my feelings / respect for my situation / willingness to support me).

Strongly Agree: The student doctor VERBALLY expressed empathy in a genuine manner. (Found out my concerns, offered
comments to validate my feelings and concerns.)
Agree: The student doctor VERBALLY expressed empathy BUT it did NOT feel genuine (said the "right words: without exhibiting
genuine warmth/sincerity). (Heard my concerns, sounded a bit empathetic, didn’t validate my concerns.)
Disagree: The student doctor did not verbally express empathy. (Glossed over symptoms or concerns that should have elicited
empathy.)

8. maintained a respectful tone (e.g., did not belittle me; did not use humor inappropriately, did not talk down to me).

Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor’s effective use of this behavior. (Treated me with respect at all
times. Recognized by discomfort and helped me to feel more comfortable.)
Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Was mostly respectfully with tone of voice. I felt my problems were
taken seriously by the student.)
Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (I felt ignored, or that the student’s agenda was more
important than me, or that my problems were not important.)

9. involved me in deciding upon a plan (e.g., presented me with options [if any], provided rationale for their assessment

$\bigcirc$ Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor’s effective use of this behavior. (Negotiated with me on the feasibility of the plan.)
$\bigcirc$ Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Presented a plan and asked me if it was okay).
$\bigcirc$ Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (Did not involve me in making the plan and did not check with me. Made it clear that I had to do things in the way s/he suggested and my opinion about it did not matter.)

10. elicited and addressed any concerns I have about the plan.

Strongly Agree: I was favorably impressed by the student doctor's effective use of this behavior. (Specifically asked if I had any
concerns about the plan AND addressed them.)
Agree: I observed the student doctor using this behavior. (Asked if I have any concerns about the plan but did not directly
address them.)
Disagree: I did not observe the student doctor using this behavior. (Did not ask if I have any concerns about the plan.)
