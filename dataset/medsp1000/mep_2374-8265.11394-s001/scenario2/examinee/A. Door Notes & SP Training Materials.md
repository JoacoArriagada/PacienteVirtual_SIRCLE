**Case 2- Inappropriate Medicine**

**Medical Student**

**Patient’s Name:** Eddie Cabot

**Medical Profile:** 77-year-old male with a history of high blood pressure, high cholesterol, and coronary artery disease on rivaroxaban who was admitted to the hospitalist service after being diagnosed with a head bleed following a mechanical fall.

**Family Member Present:** Son/Daughter

**Background:** Mr. Cabot came to the emergency department by ambulance after a mechanical fall at home. He was admitted to the ICU for monitoring. You are part of the daytime ICU admitting service and are responsible for the admission orders. Overnight, a treatment-order error contributed to worsening intracranial bleeding, and Mr. Cabot was taken emergently to the operating room. His family member has just returned to the hospital after receiving a call that the surgery is over.

**Patient’s Present Condition:** Mr. Cabot is currently in the post-anesthesia care unit after an emergent craniotomy. The surgery went well, and he is considered stable. He remains intubated and will continue to stay intubated for the foreseeable future. The neurosurgery team has already updated the family about the surgery and believes the patient has a good prognosis.

You need to inform the family member of the error from your order and answer any questions he/she may have.
