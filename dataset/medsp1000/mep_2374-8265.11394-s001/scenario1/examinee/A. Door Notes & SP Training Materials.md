**Case 1- Wrong Patient/Wrong Medicine**

**Update for Medical Student**

**Patient’s Name:** Lana Archer

**Medical Profile:** 45-year-old female with a history of high blood pressure and tobacco use who originally presented to the emergency department for shortness of breath and cough.

**Family Member Present:** Sibling

**Background:** Lana was assessed by your emergency medicine colleague and her care was signed over to you. During treatment in the emergency department, a medication-related error led to a severe reaction. After the patient was stabilized, she was admitted to the ICU. Lana’s family member is now in the emergency department, expecting to pick her up to take her home.

**Patient’s Present Condition:** Lana came to the emergency department with low grade fevers, shortness of breath, and cough for the past four days. Lana is now stable and has been admitted to the ICU.

You need to inform the family member of the error and answer any questions he/she may have.
