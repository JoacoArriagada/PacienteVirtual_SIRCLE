**Case 5- Inaccurate Sign-Out**

**Medical Student**

**Patient’s Name:** Ellie Danan

**Medical Profile:** 50-year-old female with a past medical history of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), coronary artery disease (CAD), and chronic tobacco use who presented to the emergency department with shortness of breath.

**Background:** Ms. Danan was initially seen by your emergency medicine colleague and her care was signed over to you pending repeat evaluation. At the time of sign-out, she had been told she would likely go home. Before discharge, you identified a serious missed finding that required a change in plans, a procedure, and hospital admission.

**Patient’s Present Condition:** Ms. Danan underwent a chest procedure and was admitted to the floor. She remains hemodynamically stable and is not requiring supplemental oxygen.

Your colleague receives sign-out from you the next morning and asks how the shift went.

You need to inform your co-resident of the error and answer any questions he/she may have.
