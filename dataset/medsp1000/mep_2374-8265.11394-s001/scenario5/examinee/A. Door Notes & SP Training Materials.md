**Case 4- Misdiagnosed Sign-Out**

**Medical Student**

**Patient’s Name:** Jennifer Wright

**Medical Profile:** 22-year-old female with no known past medical history who originally presented to the emergency department for palpitations and shortness of breath.

**Colleague:** Co-Resident

**Background:** Ms. Wright was assessed by your emergency medicine colleague for palpitations and shortness of breath. During treatment, she developed a life-threatening arrhythmia but was resuscitated and is now conscious in the ICU on cardiac monitoring. You are rotating off-service as part of the cardiology service that has been asked to consult on her care. On review of the patient’s initial heart tracing, your team identified features showing that the original treatment choice was unsafe for her underlying rhythm problem.

**Patient’s Present Condition:** Ms. Wright is currently hemodynamically stable in the ICU under close cardiac monitoring. Her vital signs are stable. You have been tasked by your cardiology team to communicate with your colleague about this error.

You need to inform your co-resident of the error and answer any questions he/she may have.
