**APPENDIX B: Medical Error Standardized Patient Checklist: Patient and Peer Disclosure**

PATIENT DISCLOSURE: To be completed by the participating standardized patient after Case 1 and Case 3A. Estimated time for checklist completion: 15 minutes.

**HEEAL Competence Instrument-- Patient**

**Directions**: Please indicate whether the physician completed the stated actions, with

**Y= completed (Yes) or N= did not complete (No)**

**The Physician…**

**Honesty**

\_\_ 1. Explicitly stated that an error occurred

**Empathy**

\_\_ 2. Verbalized an empathetic statement during discussion of events

Ex. “I can’t pretend to understand/This must be difficult”

\_\_ 3. Made appropriate eye contact and maintained focus during interaction

\_\_ 4. Exhibited positive, open body language in interaction with patient/family

\_\_ 5. Did not interrupt patient/family during interaction

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Always interrupted patient or family |  | Sometimes interrupted Patient or family  (at least 3- times) |  | Never interrupted patient or family |

\_\_ 6. Inquired about patient/family’s current emotions regarding the event

\_\_ 7. Summarized interaction

\_\_ 8. Used patient’s words/phrasing during summation of conversation

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Used medical jargon |  | Sometimes used patient or family’s words or phrases |  | Always used patient or family’s words |

**Education**

\_\_ 9. Clearly stated why the error occurred

\_\_ 10. Discussed the results/implications of the error

**Apology**

\_\_ 11. Used the phrase “I am sorry” during disclosure

\_\_ 12. Accepted responsibility on behalf of physician/hospital\*

**Lessen Chance for Future Errors**

\_\_ 13. When possible, clearly addressed how recurrences will be prevented\*

\_\_ 14. Discussed details for further reporting/follow-up

\_\_ 15. Provided contact information for follow-up questions

\* Refers to checklist items that may vary by institution. Inclusion of these items in the checklist’s final scoring are up to the discretion of the institution incorporating this curriculum.
