Student Worksheets: Who Are the Underserved

## Case 2, Part 1:

A 45-year-old man comes in for a routine check up of his type 2 diabetes. He feels well but has not been able to check blood sugars because he ran out of test strips and could not afford more. He's been trying to make healthy meals from what he gets from the food bank. His past medical history include diabetes and high blood pressure for 10 years. He's never had a surgery. He quit smoking about 5 years ago and drinks a beer with friends for special occasions. He's single, finds work as a day laborer, and lives in low-income housing. His Dad died from kidney failure related to diabetes, his two sisters also have diabetes. He denies increased thirst or urination.
