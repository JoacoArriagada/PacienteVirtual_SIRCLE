Instructor Guide: Who Are the Underserved

## Objectives:

1. Compare what attributes makes a population underserved as opposed to vulnerable with regards to health and health care.
2. Compare the terms health disparity, health inequality, and health inequity.
3. Describe factors that contribute to a person having poor health outcomes.
4. Examine changing landscape of healthcare and health insurance in the U.S.
5. Evaluate several specific strategies that can be used to decrease health inequities.
6. Describe approaches to personal biases toward specific groups of people or particular situations.

## Why this topic?

This session is intended to get students "on the same page" with regard to understanding which segments of the population are included in the label "underserved", and to give them a common language to use when learning about and working with underserved patients. Information on factors associated with poor access to healthcare services is included, along with some ways that the health of underserved communities can be improved. This session could be offered in the preclinical or clinical curriculum or as an extracurricular session for students desiring more knowledge about this topic.
