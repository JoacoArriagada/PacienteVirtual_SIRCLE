# OSTE Assessment

To assess competency in teaching, all students completed an OSTE applying what they had learned to a novel case (Appendix F) after the workshop. The standardized patient (SP) in the OSTE played the role of a third-year medical student and read a script of a typical case presentation on a new patient admitted with pneumonia. The actual fourth-year student played the role of the intern and taught the SP using the OMP model.
