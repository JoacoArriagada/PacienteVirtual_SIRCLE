# Heart Failure Disease Trajectory

![](images/79ec0fc2d99ca834de4e088f1c55751956c097e7a7d705750f426b14afc70729.jpg)

HF is a chronic disease, managed for months to years, but often terminal

Prognosis is highly variable and uncertain, but illness trajectory is more predictable

Trajectory should guide serious illness discussions

# Poor outcome predictors:

• Recurrent hospitalizations   
• Lower systolic blood pressure   
• Renal dysfunction   
• NYHA class   
• Age   
• Co-morbid conditions   
(1)Initial symptoms develop and treatment begins   
(2)Plateaus of variable length with optimal medical/surgical therapies   
(3)Functional status declines $^ +$ exacerbations that respond to rescue efforts   
(4)Refractory symptoms and limited function   
(5)End of life
