# MATT Team Case 5: Dotty Green SIM Driver Guide

Written for: 3GSim

# Setting:

General medical floor

# Background:

Case of Dotty Green, an 87 year-old admitted 3 days ago with community acquired pneumonia. Has been asymptomatic, but assessment by Bedside Nurse will reveal a new irregular heart rate of 140.

# Floor Nurse script:

Case of Dotty Green, an 87 year-old lady admitted 3 days ago with community acquired pneumonia. Has been improving but likely will need rehab before returning home. She has been asymptomatic today with normal heart rate and blood pressure, and a stable O2 saturation in the low $9 0 ' s$ . It is time for you to perform a routine assessment.

# Needs: (extra equipment necessary for case scenario)

Wig for female patient   
IV access in place without fluids   
BP cuff and pulse oximeter attached to patient   
Bedside chart “Green, MATT 5”   
Prepare to have O2, fluids, cardiac medications, defibrillator for cardioversion

Case: You are Dotty Green, an 87 year-od recovering from pneumonia & awaiting rehab. HX: You feel fine and are not sure why your nurse got so excited and called all these people in. (You are awake, alert, and oriented.)

Your cough has been improving and you are not short of breath.   
No chest pain.

You have not noticed any problems with retaining fluid lately, although in the past your ankles sometimes swelled a little.

No heart failure symptoms.   
On DVT prophylaxis with no prior clot history.   
You take a blood pressure pill, some inhalers, aspirin, plavix, and a thyroid pill.   
No hyperthyroid symptoms.

# In the chart:

PMH: COPD, CAD with stent last year, diastolic heart failure due to HTN, normal systolic function by echo, hypothyroidism.

CURRENT MEDS: Plavix 75 daily, Lopressor 50 BID, synthroid 50mcg daily, asa 81 mg daily, Rocephin/ zithromax, prednisone 40 daily, Duonebs every 6 hours prn, heparin 5000 units Q8 hours.

PE: Vitals: T 98.6 RR 14 HR 140 (irreg) BP 100/50 O2 Sat $9 0 \%$ on room air

LABS: (in chart)

Resolved leukocytosis, stable Hgb, normal chem 8 for 3 days.

Echo: normal LV size with EF $6 1 \%$ , abnormal diastolic function, otherwise unremarkable.

Nurse Educator can provide (if requested) a STAT EKG showing atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response. Labs and ABG will be normal.

# TIPS:

Patient will not deteriorate unless you opt to increase level of difficulty.   
Express anxiety if team members do not explain to patient what is happening. Be prepared to manually raise blood pressure slightly if fluids are given and O2 if oxygen is increased.   
Patient will improve once rate control is given.   
Scenario ends when need to transfer to PCU is identified and Resident reviews plan with Floor Nurse.   
To increase level of difficulty, click “Make Unstable” to begin trend of RVR with hypotension. Cardioversion must be performed to reverse the trend. Complain of CP or SOB to increase sense of urgency.   
You can simulate cardioversion if defib not available by hitting “prepare for cardioversion”
