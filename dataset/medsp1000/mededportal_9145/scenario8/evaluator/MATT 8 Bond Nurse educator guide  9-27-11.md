# MATT Team Case 8: Elizabeth Bond Nurse Educator Guide

Written for: 3GSim

# Learner preparation:

Bedside Nurse will be given script describing the change in patient status requiring an assessment and initiation of a MATT call. The Bedside Nurse will have time to briefly review the chart and be oriented to the mannequin and available equipment. They should expect a Resident Physician, an ICU Nurse, and a Respiratory Therapist to respond to the MATT call.

# Objectives:

work as team (identify self, assign roles, use closed-loop communication, think aloud)   
manage status epilepticus   
transfer to higher level of care

# Intended learner level:

Seasoned MATT team with junior resident leader

# Setting:

General medical floor/ stroke unit

# Background:

61 year-old with history of metastatic renal cell carcinoma and known seizure disorder admitted from ED yesterday with 20-second tonic-clonic seizure at home, here for workup.

# To prep floor nurse:

Prep floor nurse for 30-60 seconds.   
Tell them script below, ask them to assess patient as they would and call MATT Tell them they are to assume a role on team   
Introduce them to simulator, physical findings (pulses, lung and heart sounds, pupils, verbal responses).

Give them bedside chart and the “floor nurse script” page that has data below:

Floor Nurse Script:

Case of Elizabeth Bond, 61 year-old lady admitted yesterday to stroke unit for a seizure. Was pleasant and cooperative according to night nurse, but your assistant thought she was very groggy while getting her vitals a few minutes ago, and has asked you to come assess her.

# CASE TIPS:

Patient will be post-ictal when nurse first evaluates   
SIM Driver will make patient seize during assessment that should provoke a MATT (you will be able to know they are seizing when eyes close, hands will shake (but it is hard to recognize hands). If nurse does not recognize hands, you may prompt them by saying “I think patient may be seizing”.   
Will get very hypoxic without O2; will respond to NRBFM.   
Seizure will get better after resident orders Midazolam.   
They may order other Benzo’s; tell them they need to be summoned from   
pharmacy, and there will be a delay   
Patient will seize again and will not respond to Benzo- should use Fosphenytoin IV. Optional Case Complications:
