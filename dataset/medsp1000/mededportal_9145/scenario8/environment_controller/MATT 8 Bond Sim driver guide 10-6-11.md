# MATT Team Case 8: Elizabeth Bond Sim Driver Guide

Written for: 3GSim

# Setting:

Floor, stroke unit

# Background:

61 year-old with history of metastatic renal cell carcinoma and known seizure disorder admitted from ED yesterday with 20-second tonic-clonic seizure at home, here for workup.

# Floor Nurse Script:

Case of Elizabeth Bond, 61 year-old lady admitted yesterday to stroke unit for a seizure. Was pleasant and cooperative according to night nurse, but your assistant thought she was very groggy while getting her vitals a few minutes ago, and has asked you to come assess her.

# Needs: (extra equipment necessary for case scenario)

No oxygen initally   
Ward chart "Bond MATT 8"   
IV pole, IV pump without any fluids hanging   
Leave off pulse ox monitor and chest leads   
Crash cart to bedside   
Prepare to have nasal cannula, non-rebreather, BiPAP

# Case.

HX: Moans in answer to most questions; can’t give viable history

PMH: Renal Cell CA, metastatic; seizure disorder on Keppra recently MEDS: Benicar 20, Synthroid 150, Protonix 40, ASA 81; Sorefenib 400 (Tyrosine Kinase inhibitor, oral); Keppra 500 BID

PE: RR 20; BP 144/70 HR 90; temp 37.4 C During seizure: RR 3, HR 115

LABS: (in chart) WBC 6; Hgb 8; Na 137; K 4.7; Ca++ 9.4; BUN/Cr 8/0.7

Labs available if asked:   
iSTAT ABG on NRBFM: 7.18/68/60, $9 1 \%$ , normal Ca, Na, K   
iSTAT ABG on any Nasal Cannula: 7.10/78/40, $7 1 \%$ ; normal Ca, Na, K   
EKG: NSR

# CASE TIPS:

Patient will be post-ictal when nurse first evaluates   
Make patient seize by clicking “\*INITIAL SEIZURE FOR FLOOR NURSE”. Eyes will close; convulsions will happen but are hard to see   
Sats will drop.   
If nurse/team calls for non-rebreather or $1 0 0 \%$ o2, can dial up sats or use handler “adds $\mathbf { O 2 ^ { \mathbf { 3 } } }$ .   
Seizure will get better after resident orders Midazolam; you should wait 30 seconds after correct dose of benzo and click “\*MIDAZOLAM GIVEN”.   
If they order other Benzo’s, nurse will tell them they need to be summoned from pharmacy, and there will be a delay

# Optional Case Complications:

By selecting “MAKE SICKER-ADDITIONAL RESISTANT SEIZURE”, patient will seize again.

Patient can seize again and will not respond to Benzo- should use Fosphenytoin IV. When done, click “SECOND LINE ANTI-SEIZURE MED GIVEN” 30 seconds later, and case will revert to post-ictal state.
