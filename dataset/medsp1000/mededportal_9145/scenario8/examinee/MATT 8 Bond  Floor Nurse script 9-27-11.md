# MATT Team Case 8: Elizabeth Bond Floor Nurse Script

# For Floor nurse script:

Case of Elizabeth Bond, 61 year-old lady admitted yesterday to stroke unit for a seizure. Was pleasant and cooperative according to night nurse, but your assistant thought she was very groggy while getting her vitals a few minutes ago, and has asked you to come assess her.

Bedside chart is available for additional information. MAR is in the chart.

Read over the chart, assess the patient, call for the MATT team.

Collect all of the data from the chart and your patient evaluation that you think the team would want and report to them when they arrive; take an active role during resuscitation.
