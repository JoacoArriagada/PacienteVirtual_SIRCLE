# MATT Team Case 1: Joshua Himmelberger Floor Nurse Script

# Background:

Case of Joshua Himmelberger, 40 year-old mentally challenged man from a group home with 5 days of fever and cough; admitted today, has had progressive worsening of oxygen saturation and confusion; MATT called for saturation of $8 5 \%$ . He is normally pleasant and interactive and participates in activities of daily living, but is less responsive by nursing report this morning.

Bedside chart is available for additional information. MAR is in the chart.

Read over the chart, assess the patient, call for the MATT team.

Collect all of the data from the chart and your patient evaluation that you think the team would want and report to them when they arrive; take an active role during resuscitation.
