# MATT Case 9: Springer Hypotension due to Pulmonary Embolism, Optional Level 2 PEA Arrest

Simulator Driver Guide

# Background:

Mr. Charles Springer is a 79 yo male admitted 9 days ago with acute renal failure and hydronephrosis due to fecal impaction and BPH. He is medically stable, awaiting SNF placement due to dementia.

The scenario begins with the bedside nurse being asked to obtain a routine set of vitals, which will reveal a BP of 86/40 (compared to 130/70 earlier) that should prompt a MATT call.

The patient will remain unchanged until the physician arrives, at which time Sim Driver will advance the case.

TIPS:

Case will not advance from initial vitals unless you click “\*Doc Arrives – start trend,” which will make BP fall by 20 over the next 4 minutes. Other vitals do not change.

If nothing is done, vitals will stay at that level (BP in ${ \bf 6 0 ^ { \circ } s }$ ).

If IV bolus is ordered, click $\mathbf { \mu } ^ { \mathbf { \mu } \mathbf { \mu } \mathbf { \mu } \mathbf { \Lambda } } \mathbf { I V }$ Saline bolus” to stop trend and increase BP by 40mm Hg over 2 minutes.

Simulation may end at that point after discussion of planned work-up and signout to nurse. If hypotension is not addressed within the expected time frame of simulation, Sim Driver should make patient complain of sharp chest pain and shortness of breath, with manual fall in O2 Sat.

Level 2 Optional Case Complications: Have patient complain of sharp chest pain. Click “\*Make Sicker – PEA Arrest” to begin trend where patient develops progressive hypoxemia/ hypotension to PEA and eventually asystole. Eyes will close and patient should stop responding to questions.

Sim Driver can manually adjust BP slightly for fluid boluses, but in this scenario the patient should not significantly improve until thrombolytics are considered or emergent echo for PE is ordered. Sim Driver may at any time advance case to “Stabilize” patient by clicking “\*Make Stable.” This returns BP and O2 Sat to low normal and HR to normal, and patient’s eyes will open. This can occur prior to ending scenario for debriefing.
