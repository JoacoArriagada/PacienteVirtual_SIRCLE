# MATT Case 9: Springer Hypotension due to Pulmonary Embolism, Optional Level 2 PEA Arrest

Nurse Educator Guide

# Learner preparation:

Bedside Nurse will be given script describing the change in patient status requiring an assessment and initiation of a MATT call. The Bedside Nurse will have time to briefly review the chart and be oriented to the mannequin and available equipment. They should expect a Resident Physician, an ICU Nurse, and a Respiratory Therapist to respond to the MATT call.

# Objectives:

Work as team (identify self, assign roles, use closed-loop communication, think aloud).   
Recognize and treat acute hypotension with appropriate fluids.   
Consider appropriate differential diagnosis, including pulmonary embolism.

# Level 2 objectives:

Recognize PEA and manage according to ACLS protocol.   
Recognize massive PE in differential of PEA & consider urgent echo or thrombolytics.

# Intended level:

Seasoned MATT team with junior resident leader and bedside nurse

Setting: general medical floor

# Background:

Mr. Charles Springer is a 79 yo male admitted 9 days ago with acute renal failure and hydronephrosis due to fecal impaction and BPH. He is medically stable, awaiting SNF placement due to dementia.

The scenario begins with the bedside nurse being asked to obtain a routine set of vitals, which will reveal a BP of 86/40 (compared to 130/70 earlier) that should prompt a MATT call.

The patient will remain unchanged until the physician arrives, at which time Sim Driver will advance the case.

# TIPS:

After physician arrives, Sim Driver will advance case so that BP falls 20mm Hg over 4 minutes. Other vitals do not change.

If nothing is done, vitals will stay at that level (BP in $6 0 ^ { \circ } s$ ).

IVF bolus will stop trend and increase BP by $4 0 \mathrm { m m } \mathrm { H g }$ over 2 minutes.

Simulation may end at that point after discussion of planned work-up and signout to nurse. If Leader DOES NOT address hypotension or include appropriate DDx within expected time frame of simulation, Sim Driver should make patient complain of sharp chest pain and shortness of breath, with manual fall in O2 Sat.

Level 2 Optional Case Complications: Patient should begin to complain of chest pain.

Sim Driver will initiate trend where patient develops progressive hypoxemia/ hypotension to PEA and eventually asystole. Eyes will close and patient will stop responding to questions shortly after starting trend.

\* Leader should follow ACLS algorithm for PEA. MATT Code Blue call should be simulated. (Do Not call operator.)

(CPR x 2 minutes, 30:2 ratio, epi 1 mg Q 3-5 min OR vasopressin 40 units once)

(H’s & T’s: Hypovolemia, Hypoxia, Hydrogen (acidosis), Hypo/Hyperkalemia,

Hypothermia, Tension pneumothorax, Tamponade, Toxins, Thrombosis (pulmonary), Thrombosis (coronary).

\* Leader should consider massive PE in differential. Orders for bedside echo and/ or discussion of thrombolytics should occur.

Sim Driver can manually adjust BP slightly for fluid boluses, but in this scenario the patient should not significantly improve until Leader considers thrombolytics or emergent echo for PE. Sim Driver may at any time advance case to “Stabilize” patient and end scenario for debriefing. “Stabilize” returns BP and O2 Sat to low normal and HR to normal. Patient’s eyes will open.
