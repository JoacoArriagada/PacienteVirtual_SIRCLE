# MATT Case 9: Springer Hypotension due to Pulmonary Embolism, Optional Level 2 PEA Arrest

Simulator Driver Guide

# Background:

Mr. Charles Springer is a 79 yo male admitted 9 days ago with acute renal failure and hydronephrosis due to fecal impaction and BPH. He is medically stable, awaiting SNF placement due to dementia.

Case: 79 yo male on hospital day 9 awaiting placement becomes hypotensive.

HX: Patient has dementia and cannot give history. Staff note he may be slightly drowsy today, but not much different from baseline.

On ROS he is oriented only to self but is able to answer YES / NO questions.   
ROS is completely negative.

PMH: Dementia, BPH. No known cardiac history or prior clot.

If the environment controller cues worsening later in the scene, he may complain of sharp chest pain and shortness of breath.
