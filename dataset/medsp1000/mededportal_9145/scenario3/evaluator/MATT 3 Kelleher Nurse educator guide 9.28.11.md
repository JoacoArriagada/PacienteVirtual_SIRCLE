# MATT Team Case 3: Norma Kelleher Nurse Educator Guide

Written for 3G Sim

# Learner preparation:

Bedside Nurse will be given script describing the change in patient status requiring an assessment and initiation of a MATT call. The Bedside Nurse will have time to briefly review the chart and be oriented to the mannequin and available equipment. They should expect a Resident Physician, an ICU Nurse, and a Respiratory Therapist to respond to the MATT call.

# Objectives:

Work as team (identify self, assign roles, use closed-loop communication, think aloud). Identify post cardiac catheterization hypotension; consider retroperitoneal bleed as leading cause.   
Initiate IVF resuscitation in this setting   
Optional Level 2 objectives:   
Recognize PEA and manage according to ACLS protocol.

# Intended level:

Seasoned MATT team with junior resident leader and bedside nurse

# Setting:

General medical floor

# Background:

45 year old with known CAD presented on 4 days ago with unstable angina.   
Four hours ago had a cardiac cath with new stent placed in circumflex artery.

# To prep floor nurse: (brief)

Tell them script below, ask them to assess patient as they would and call MATT. Tell them they are to assume a role on team.   
Introduce them to simulator, physical findings (pulses, lung and heart sounds, pupils, verbal responses).   
Give them bedside chart and the “floor nurse script” page that has data below:

# Bedside Nurse Script:

The pt. is 45 yo female with known CAD with her last stent placed in 1/08 in Oklahoma. She also has anxiety disorder, chronic low back pain on outpt. narcotics, HTN. She was admitted four days ago (3/23/10) for unstable angina. She had a cardiac cath four hours ago and had a stent placed. She has been complaining of increasing low back pain over the last 2-3 hours and the doctors have recently ordered morphine for this pain.

# Needs for sim room:

Nasal cannula oxygen Hospital chart IV Heptrap in place Crash Cart available Simulated hematoma R groin

# Case:

Post-cath patient complaining of increasing back pain, was given morphine one hour ago.   
BP now noted to be 85/50, which should prompt MATT call.

History: Patient noted to have increasing back pain over the last 2-3 hours. Has known chronic low back pain, but clearly worse than baseline. The blood pressures have been lower than baseline during that time frame. No CP or SOB.

PMHx: CAD with stent in 2008 in another state, anxiety disorder, HTN, DJD of LS spine with hx of spinal fusion, current tobacco use.

PE: Afeb, BP 80/58, RR 18, HR 110   
No JVD, lungs clear,   
Moderate sized groin hematoma, vague nonreproducible pain in R low back

LABs: stat Hgb is 11.5 (was 13.4 at 0600 this AM)

# Case Tips:

If no aggressive IVF by 5 minutes will have progressive hypotension.

Level 2 Optional Case Complications: Sim Driver clicks “\*PEA Arrest” to make patient decompensate. Eyes will close and patient should stop responding to questions. Leader should follow ACLS algorithm for PEA. MATT Code Blue call should be simulated. (Do Not call operator.) (CPR x 2 minutes, 30:2 ratio, epi 1 mg Q 3-5 min OR vasopressin 40 units once) (5 H’s & T’s: Hypovolemia, Hypoxia, Hydrogen (acidosis), Hypo/Hyperkalemia, Hypothermia, Tension pneumothorax, Tamponade, Toxins, Thrombosis (pulmonary), Thrombosis (coronary).

Sim Driver can click “Correct PEA Treatment” at any time to stabilize the patient.   
Vitals will improve and eyes will open.

Passing performance
