# MATT 2: Margaret Troutman Sim Driver Guide

Written for: 3GSim

# Learner preparation:

Bedside Nurse will be given script describing the change in patient status requiring an assessment and initiation of a MATT call. The Bedside Nurse will have time to briefly review the chart and be oriented to the mannequin and available equipment. They should expect a Resident Physician, an ICU Nurse, and a Respiratory Therapist to respond to the MATT call.

# Objectives:

Work as team (identify self, assign roles, use closed-loop communication, think aloud).   
Quickly diagnose and treat narcotic overdose.

# Level 2 objectives:

Recognize bradycardia progressing to cardiac arrest; manage according to ACLS protocol.

# Intended learner level:

Seasoned MATT team with junior resident leader and bedside nurse

# Setting:

General orthopedics ward (N5W)

Background:   
Case of Margaret Troutman, 91 yo patient with mild dementia in for laminectomy   
and fusion

# To prep floor nurse (prep performed by nurse educator):

Prep floor nurse for 30-60 seconds.   
Tell them script below, ask them to assess patient as they would and call MATT Tell them they are to assume a role on team   
Introduce them to simulator, physical findings (pulses, lung and heart sounds, pupils, verbal responses).   
Give them bedside chart and the “floor nurse script” page that has data below:

Floor nurse script:

Case of Margaret Troutman, 91 year old with mild dementia, one day post-op lumbar laminectomy and fusion. You received signout that she was up all night with pain. Your PCA noted her to be groggy this morning, different than baseline, and overnight she required oxygen to be turned up from 2 to 4 liters by nasal cannula for low saturations.

# CASE TIPS:

Patient will be able to give one-word answers. Hypotension, low sats from underventilation (resp rate is vey slow, but it is not obvious on sim). Pupils are very miotic, but they often don’t check- makes a good teaching point).

Hypotension will respond to IV fluids; will not wake up until Narcan.

Optional case complication : Sim driver can trend patient toward bardycardic arrest-pt may hypercapneic bracdycardic arrest; Narcan will abort it. Patient will not die.
