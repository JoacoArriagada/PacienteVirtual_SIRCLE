# CASE TIPS:

Patient will be able to give one-word answers. Hypotension, low sats from underventilation (resp rate is vey slow, but it is not obvious on sim). Pupils are very miotic.

If they call for saline, hit “\*\*500cc saline”- corrects hypotension, but you still should give one-word answers.

If they ask for higher flow O2, use sats monitor on screen right to increase (will not go above set limits for frame).

If they call for Naloxone, wait about 20 seconds, then hit “\*Naloxone” on event menu- will improve BP, pulse and pupils/eyelids immediately; also you should begain speaking (and asking for pain meds/complaining about your back and the O2 mask).

# Optional case complication :

You have option to trend patient toward bardycardic arrest- hit \*MAKE SICKERTREND TOWARD BRADY ARREST” that simulates a hypercapneic bracdycardic arrest. Patient will not die.
