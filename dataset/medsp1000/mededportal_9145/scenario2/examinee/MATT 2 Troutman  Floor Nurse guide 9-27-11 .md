# MATT 2: Margaret Troutman Floor Nurse Script

# Background:

Case of Margaret Troutman, 91 year old with mild dementia, one day post-op lumbar laminectomy and fusion. You received signout that she was up all night with pain. Your PCA noted her to be groggy this morning, different than baseline, and overnight she required oxygen to be turned up from 2 to 4 liters by nasal cannula for low saturations.

Bedside chart is available for additional information. MAR is in the chart.

Read over the chart, assess the patient, call for the MATT team.

Collect all of the data from the chart and your patient evaluation that you think the team would want and report to them when they arrive; take an active role during resuscitation.
