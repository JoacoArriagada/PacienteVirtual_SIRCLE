# MATT Team Case 10: Joseph Spencer Floor Nurse Guide

Setting: General Medical Floor with telemetry

# Background:

81 year old male with hx of CAD and alcohol abuse admitted one day prior after being found at the base of his steps with an apparent fall and probable syncope. He was found to be intoxicated on presentation with blood alcohol level of 0.22. On admission also found to have hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia and sinus tachycardia with PACs. His past medical history also includes mild COPD and hyperthyroidism treated with radioactive iodine. This morning (about two hours ago) he fell attempting to go to the bathroom in his hospital room. You are evaluating him now for noticeable change in mental status.

Bedside chart is available for additional information. MAR is in the chart.

Read over the chart, assess the patient, call for the MATT team.

Perform any interventions you normally would do.

Collect all of the data you think the team would want and report to them; take an active role during resuscitation.
