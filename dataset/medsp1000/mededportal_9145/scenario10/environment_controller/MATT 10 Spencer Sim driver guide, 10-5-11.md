# Case Tips:

The case begins two hours after a fall.   
The patient has confusion with signs of intracranial pressure increase (HTN, bradycardia, hypoventilation).   
Two minutes after “MD ARRIVES” will develop progression of HTN, bradycardia, eyes will close and should be less responsive. A Glasgow score will go to less than 8 if loses withdrawal to pain, eyes open to pain only and speaks only inappropriate words. No action will halt progression.

CT ordered one hour ago because of the fall may be available if CT scan mentioned (shows new subdural hematoma)

# Level 2 Optional case complication :

If Brady arrest is selected, the case will move to HR in low 30s and BP in 60s systolic.
