Case of Richard Brown, an 81 yo admitted a few hours ago with uncomplicated NSTEMI, pain-free, awaiting cath.

# Case:

Richard Brown is an 81yo retired executive, admitted with chest pain.

Hx: The chest pain started five days ago. Pain was a mid-chest heaviness associated with fatigue & SOB. Came on with activity & improved with rest. He didn’t want to come to the hospital, but felt very weak & his wife and PCP made him come. He had no pain when he came to the ER but was told he had had a heart attack and need to have a catheterization.

About 10 minutes ago the pressure returned while he was just lying in bed, and it quickly became severe (10/10). He feels sweaty, nauseated, and anxious & SOB. This never happened before. The nitro pill did not help.

PMHx: Hypertension, high cholesterol, diabetes on pills. He quit smoking pretty long ago. He takes 3 pills twice a day but can’t recall the names while he is in pain. No prior heart problems.

In the chart: Admitted as NSTEMI based on high troponins and ST depressions.

HTN, hyperlipidemia, DM2, former smoker. Other PMH not relevant.

No known drug allergies.

Home meds: avapro, metformin, gemfibrozil, Vit E.

Current meds in hospital: lopressor, ASA, heparin/ integrelin, avapro, lopid, NSS $\textcircled { a } 1 0 0 \mathrm { c c } / \mathrm { h r }$ .

PE: No change with SL NTG x 1 dose. 160/90, HR 80s, afeb, RR 20, O2 sat $9 0 \%$ . Labs: (in chart) WBC 11, Hgb 15, CPK 398 MB 14 Trop 16.11, BUN 30, Cr 1.09

# Case Tips:

Sim driver may respond as expected to any meds given, but patient will not significantly improve and needs to go to cath.

Patient will not spontaneously worsen. Sim Driver may initiate low BP or add PVCs if moving too slowly.

Sim Driver should be prepared to call back as the cardiologist (610-988-9106) for a hand-off from the resident.
