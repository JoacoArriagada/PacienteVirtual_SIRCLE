**Appendix B**

Moulage & Standardized Patient Support

**Moulage** included glycerine mist to simulate sweating. If activated charcoal given and simulation of vomiting is wanted: simulated vomit created by mixing oatmeal with cocoa powder and a small amount of Karo syrup. If the patient is not actively vomiting, fill a vomitus bag and smudge the patient's gown or skin with a small amount.

The **standardized patient** (SP) is a 40 year old man or woman with a history of narcotic drug abuse who presents with somnolence and delirium. The SP will present as sleepy and appearing drugged and will only say things like, “I wanted to get high so I took a lot of my mother's pain pills, they began with a ‘D,’” and “I don't feel well.” The standardized patient will deny suicidal thoughts or intent. The standardized patient will deny taking any other medications and will admit that they have never been hurt by getting high before. If pressed the SP may indicate that he or she “took 20 pills” and that he or she likes taking Oxy. The SP motivation is that she or he buys prescription pain killers on the street, prefers Oxy, but will take anything he or she can get.

During the scenario, the SP will become increasingly delirious (as if drunk or drugged) and will not be able to provide any further coherent information. The SP is unable to provide a health history.

* Family member. The family member role is just to bring in the empty bottle of Diltiazem.
