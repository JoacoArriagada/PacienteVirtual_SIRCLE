NUTRITION COUNSELING TO REDUCE CARDIOVASCULAR RISK- SESSION 2

18. A 55 yo M with CAD comes to your office for routine follow up. He has been working on incorporating a Mediterranean diet pattern into his diet for the past 8 months. You congratulate him on the changes he’s made. He replies, “Thanks, I’m feeling great! I still crave my bagel sandwich with bacon, egg, and cheese in the mornings, though.”
19. Maintenance
20. “It can be so hard to cut down on the food we have grown up to love eating and your favorite sandwich is still okay to eat occasionally. However, all processed meats, including bacon, are not only unhealthy, they are also considered carcinogenic by the WHO. And bagels, while they don’t taste sweet, still raise your blood sugar and increase your risk for diabetes. If you want to make a sandwich to eat more regularly, how about using whole grain bread instead of a bagel and avocado or tomato instead of bacon?”
