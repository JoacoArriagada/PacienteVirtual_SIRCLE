# Debrief Guide for Faculty

Topic/OSCE Topic: ERROR DISCLOSURE

Standardized Patient Case: EDNA DAVENPORT

# Key points for this Case

# Did the Resident:

o …respond to the medical error in a professional manner?   
o …express empathy for the error and its consequences?   
o …demonstrate understanding of how personal limitations and processes contribute to medical errors?   
o …display regret?   
o …listen to Edna and attend to her concerns?   
o …explain that this will be reported so processes can be altered to prevent re-occurrence
