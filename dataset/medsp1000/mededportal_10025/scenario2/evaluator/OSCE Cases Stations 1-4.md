**Station 2**

**Clinical Scenario:**

Edna Davenport, 56, African American female is in the ICU following a heart attack. The patient is stable.

You were not the admitting physician. However, you were notified that the patient needed an ABG for respiratory distress. You prepped the patient’s right wrist with the standard betadine swabs without first checking the patient’s chart.

Within a few minutes, you noted severe blistering at the prepped site. You then checked the patient’s chart and discovered she is allergic to iodine and its derivatives. To avoid infection and to be able to provide burn treatment to the affected site, you initiated burn treatment on the blistered area, and performed the ABG on the left wrist.

**Instructions:**

Please discuss the error with the patient.

Do not perform any physical exam maneuvers.

**Type of SP Case:** Formative, Communication, Interpersonal, Counseling, Difficult Encounter (No Physical Exam)

**Length of Patient Encounter:** 9 minutes

**Setting:** ICU

**Rating form:** Kalamazoo, patient-centered feedback

**Learner Level:** Post-Graduate (Residency) – Years 1 - 4/ Medical & Surgical Specialties

**Learner Objective:** Learners will be able to demonstrate compassion and understanding of the patient’s condition.

**Case overview:** Edna Davenport is a 56-year old African American woman currently in the ICU following a heart attack. Upon arrival at the hospital she made sure that her iodine and iodine derivatives allergy was clearly identified in her chart. Ms. Davenport required an arterial blood gas due to respiratory distress; this was initially attempted in her right (dominant arm). However, skin was prepped with Betadine (an iodine derivative) and the patient developed severe blistering in the area. The site now requires burn care. The ABG had to be performed on the other arm. The error will cause permanent scarring and temporary pain.

The burn area is in pain. Error happened under sedation.
