**Station 3**

**Clinical Scenario:**

You are working on the general medicine floor of the hospital. You have been assigned 71-year-old Dena Roberts, who was admitted for congestive heart failure.

The present admission is Ms. Roberts’ third in the last six months. She is on maximal medical therapy and is not showing a good response. She has no history of dementia, and is alert and oriented X3.

At the team meeting earlier today it was decided the resident needed to have a conversation with Ms. Roberts about her code status.

Ms. Roberts has no advanced care directives.

**Instructions:**

Please speak with Ms. Roberts about her code status. Do not discuss more extensive advanced directives.

Do not perform any physical exam maneuvers.

**Setting:** Patient Room

**Case overview:** Dena Roberts is a 71-year old woman currently in the hospital being treated for congestive heart failure (CHF). This is her third admission in the past six months. Despite the doctors’ best efforts, including trying many different drugs and drug combinations Dena is not getting any better, and is, in fact, worsening. Earlier today the medical team met to discuss the case and they determined that medical science could do nothing more for the patient. The resident is coming to your room to discuss the team’s findings and to discuss a “code status”.
