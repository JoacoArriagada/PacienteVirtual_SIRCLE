# Debrief Guide for Faculty

# Topic/OSCE Name: Standardized Patient Case:

INFORMED CONSENT IDA CRESWELL

# Key points for this Case

# Did the Resident:

o …listen to Ida’s concerns?   
o ...explain risks versus benefits of the transfusion?   
o …explain that treatment with transfusion is the fastest, most efficient and safest way to recovery?   
o …explain what Informed Consent means for Ida?   
o …ask Ida to restate her understanding of her current situation?   
o …attend to Ida’s concerns about transfusions?
