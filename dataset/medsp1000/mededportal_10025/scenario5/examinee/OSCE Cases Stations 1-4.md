**Station 4**

**Clinical Scenario:**

Casey Denham (age 40-60) is in for a surgical consult for a gastrectomy.

Patient had presented to the primary care physician with complaints of loss of appetite, nausea and heartburn.

The patient does not want the surgery.

**Instructions:**

Please elicit the patient’s explanation of why the patient does not want surgery and negotiate with the patient to agree to surgery.

Do not perform any part of the Physical Examination.
