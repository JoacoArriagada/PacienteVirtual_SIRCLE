# Debrief Guide for Faculty

Topic/OSCE Title: HEALTHCARE DISPARITIES (SURGICAL)

Standardized Patient Case:

CASEY DENHAM

# Key points for this Case

# Did the Resident:

o …ask open-ended questions to determine why Casey does not want surgery?   
o …listen to Casey’s story to be able to address the patient’s explanatory model for cancer metastasis?   
o …explain the benefits of performing the gastrectomy in terms Casey seemed to understand?   
o …attend to Casey’s anxieties about the surgery as a catalyst for “making the cancer spread”?
