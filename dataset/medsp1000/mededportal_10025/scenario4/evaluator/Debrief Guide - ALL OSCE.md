# Debrief Guide for Faculty

Topic/OSCE Title: HEALTHCARE DISPARITIES

Standardized Patient Case:

TERRY PHILLIPS

# Key points for this Case

# Did the Resident:

o …ask open-ended questions to determine why Terry kept refusing blood pressure medications?   
o …listen to Terry’s story to be able to address rejection of blood pressure medications?   
o …explain the benefits of blood pressure medications in terms that Terry seemed to understand?   
o …attend to Terry’s concerns about stress and the insistence on wanting to take only something to calm down?
