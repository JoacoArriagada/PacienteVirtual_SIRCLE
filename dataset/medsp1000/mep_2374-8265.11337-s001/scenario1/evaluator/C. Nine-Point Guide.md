**Appendix C**

**Template: Nine-Point Guide to Develop a Presentation and Factsheet for a Bill or Policy Advocacy Presentation to a Legislator or Policy Maker**

This document represents material that is discussed in the slide presentation (and can be distributed to participants in the form of a handout).

1. Introduce the participants, their interests and expertise.

* *Introduce yourself and others who have joined you, your expertise.*
* *State expertise related to the issue to be discussed.*
  + *Clarify role as interested citizens and healthcare professionals.*

2. Describe the bill or proposal.

* *Provide concise, factual description of specific bill or proposal.*

1. Explain the problem to address.

* *Explain proposed action generally and impacts on patients/ constituents.*
* *Connect bill or proposal to your expertise and healthcare services.*

1. Describe risks or benefits of addressing bill (focus on health impacts).

* *Indicate health challenges, vulnerable populations, and inequities, especially for patients/ constituents.*
* *Describe impacts (risks or benefits) on your healthcare services and patients.*

1. Recognize objections/challenges and costs.

* *Recognize objections and challenges, especially any claims of adverse health impacts.*
* *Discuss the costs of implementing the bill or proposal, including the costs of illnesses that could be reduced in terms of healthcare, lost work/school days, early mortality.*

1. Describe support from prior legislation/actions.

* *Highlight connections to the legislator/policy maker (such as a constituent, having attended a speech, prior meeting).*
* *Refer to previous support or co-sponsorship of this or related issue if applicable.*

         7.  Communicate relevant personal stories and supportive imagery.

* *Share personal stories about your life and work or patients and illnesses (without disclosing confidential information).*
* *Describe your perspective as a healthcare provider and what you have done to address this issue.*
* *Use Illustrative, compelling images (get permissions) or infographics.*

         8.   Ask for specific action.

* *Request support for or opposition to the bill and note the schedule for actions (such as hearings, votes).*
* *Listen to the concerns and questions.*

         9. Thank audience, offer further information, and contact information.

* *Thank the legislator/policy maker(s) for meeting with you.*
* *Provide a leave-behind factsheet.*
* *Emphasize the importance of the bill.*
