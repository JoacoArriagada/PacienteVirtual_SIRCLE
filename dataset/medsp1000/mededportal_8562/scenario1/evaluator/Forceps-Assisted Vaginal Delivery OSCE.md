Instructor’s Guide

Currently, the average graduate from residency training in Obstetrics and Gynecology in the United States has performed five forceps-assisted vaginal deliveries in the surgeon’s role during training (1). The relative rarity with which this procedure is performed limits opportunities for assessment of competence. This Objective Structured Clinical Examination was designed both to provide formative feedback to learners and to assist with summative assessment of competence in the performance of forceps-assisted vaginal delivery. In this exercise, learners move through three stations: conducting an informed consent discussion, performing a simulated forceps-assisted vaginal delivery and writing a delivery note.

Prior to the session, the “instructions for standardized patients” were reviewed by a clinical team. Two medical assistants agreed to serve as standardized patients to test the counseling station. Two faculty members agreed to serve as evaluators for the delivery stations and reviewed the task-specific checklist for this station prior to the session.

Following a didactic session on operative vaginal delivery, the room is arranged as shown in the room schematic. Each resident is given a copy of the Resident Packet. The residents begin the exercise in the “on-deck” area where they complete a pretest questionnaire, review the patient case and then as time permits may review the ACOG Practice Bulletin “Operative Vaginal Delivery.” After completing the pretest-questionnaire, two residents begin the exercise with one resident at each of two counseling stations. Each resident is provided with a case scenario and asked to conduct an informed consent discussion on forceps delivery with a standardized patient. Their performance is evaluated using the Master Interview Rating Scale (2). After completing this station, each resident moves on to a Delivery station, where he or she is given an updated case scenario prompting the resident to proceed with forceps delivery using the obstetrical simulator. While it would be preferable to have the same model at each Delivery station, many programs do not have access to duplicate models. We use a NOELLE® (Gaumard) at one delivery station and a PROMPT Birthing Simulator (Laerdal, via Limbs and Things) at the other to be able to test two residents simultaneously. One faculty member evaluates performance at each station. Following the delivery, each resident moves to the documentation station, where he or she completes a delivery note and answers a post-test questionnaire. Using the Angoff method (3), a task-specific checklist for each station was constructed such that each item on the checklists for the delivery and documentation stations would be completed by a learner minimally competent in forceps delivery. At the close of the session, each resident receives written feedback on his or her performance at each station.

Educational Objectives:

By the close of the session, the resident will be able to

-Conduct an informed consent discussion for operative vaginal delivery

-Perform a forceps-assisted vaginal delivery with the obstetrical simulator demonstrating appropriate technique

-Write a delivery note properly documenting the procedure
