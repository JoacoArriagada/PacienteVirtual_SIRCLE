|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | In this scenario, a prehospital provider is requesting orders from the learner over a radio for a male patient who has altered mental status. The prehospital provider is concerned that the patient may be having a stroke, but after the learner asks appropriate questions, it is discovered that the prehospital provider neglected to check the patient’s blood sugar, which is low. Therapy for hypoglycemia should then be initiated. |
