|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | In this scenario, a prehospital provider is requesting orders from the learner over a radio to allow a patient to refuse care. This patient is a diabetic who had altered mental status due to hypoglycemia. After receiving dextrose, he is awake and oriented and does not wish to go to the hospital. The EMS crew is requesting whether the patient may refuse transport after treatment. |

| **Facilitator script for opening radio statement** | “Medic 7 to [OLMC]” *(feel free to insert an appropriate callsign for your local institution/area)*  After the learner acknowledges transmission:  “This is Medic 7. I’m on scene with a 47-year-old male with diabetes who had a hypoglycemic episode. Initial blood sugar was 27, but after an amp of D50, it’s up to 148. The patient is alert and oriented and does not wish to come to the hospital. However, this has not happened to him in several years, and he seems mildly concerned about starting a new medication, so I wanted to call for permission to allow the patient to refuse.”  All information below must be specifically requested by the learner | | |

| **Intervention / Time point** | **Change in Case** |
| *Immediately following report* | The EMS caller signals concern about a newly started medication. |
| *After the learner learns the patient is on Glyburide* | The EMS caller should confirm whether the patient understands the risk of recurrent hypoglycemia, that it could be fatal without medical treatment, and that transport to the hospital is recommended. |
| *After determining that the patient understands the risks of refusal* | If the learner grants permission to refuse, the refusal may proceed. |
| *Other instructions to EMS regarding hypoglycemia* | If the learner gives additional refusal-disposition instructions, the EMS crew can acknowledge them. |
| *Learner requests to speak to the patient* | The facilitator may assume the role of the patient talking to the learner over the radio. The patient should understand the risks of refusal after the discussion and still state that he does not wish to go to the hospital. |
