| PROFESSIONALISM ISSUES OR CHALLENGES: | The challenges of this case are uncovering the alcohol use and cessation history which leads to the diagnosis of alcohol withdrawal. Once this diagnosis is made, the coordination of care for this individual is difficult over telemedicine and thus the patient may warrant admission to the hospital. Navigating difficult conversations about habits and addictions can be even more challenging over telemedicine as it is more difficult to exhibit empathy, this case highlights this aspect of care. |
