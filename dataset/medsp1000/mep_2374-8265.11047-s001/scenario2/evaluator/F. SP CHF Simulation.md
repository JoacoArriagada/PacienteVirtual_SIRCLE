| PROFESSIONALISM ISSUES OR CHALLENGES: | The challenge of this plan is implementing the plan in a timely fashion with a patient who is 2.5 hours from the closest academic medical center, 1.5 hours away from the closest hospital/urgent care, 45 mins away from the closest clinic and 20 mins away from the closest pharmacy. The provider needs to create a plan that is amenable to the patient’s situation. An ambulance can be called for the patient to take them to the nearest hospital and care can been coordinated with that hospital. The patient should not be allowed to drive alone to the hospital given the distance. A friend or relative can be asked to drive the patient to either the academic medical center or community hospital. Outpatient management of this condition with strict precautions can be attempted with medications sent to the pharmacy and expedited follow up with the clinic, however this is a sub-optimal plan. This coordination of care is challenging over telemedicine and must involve the patient. The other challenge of planning care for this patient is ensuring that, for the sake of accommodating the patient, the provider does not compromise their medical decision making and provide support for a plan that is not medically sound. |
