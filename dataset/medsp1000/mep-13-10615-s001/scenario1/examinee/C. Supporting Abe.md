**Module 1**

Section 3: Supporting Abe, Part A

*History of the Present Illness:*

Abe (Abraham) is a 15-year-old biracial male, with a significant past medical history for exercise-induced asthma who presents for an HME in clinic with mom. They saw a covering physician last year while their routine medical provider (who they met for the first time when Abe was 13 years old) was on vacation. While in the room together, Abe’s mom spends most of the time talking while Abe seems uninterested and rolling his eyes when his mom speaks. He appears to be taller since the visit two years ago, with an increase in BMI from 20.4 to 24.2 representing about 20 pounds of weight gained. He's wearing tight-fitting jeans and a t-shirt that has an unfamiliar brand logo on it which is over a long-sleeve thermal. His fingernails are painted dark green but he appears to be hiding them from your sight. When he spoke, his manner of speech was slightly effeminate as were subtle mannerisms. On his schoolbag, you noticed he had a rainbow patch sewed on that said “Equality” on it.

About halfway into the appointment, the medical provider asks how things are going on at home. Mom (Kathy) quickly jumps in and says “well if only Abe would do his homework like he’s supposed to, then he’d be able to be a successful engineer like his grandfather was.” Abe shrugs his shoulders and rolls his eyes but says nothing in response. The doctor looks at Abe and asks: “How are things going in school?” and mom immediately interrupts and says “He can’t just spend all of his time on Facebook sending those drawings to all those people he doesn’t even know!”

Mom subsequently reports that in the past year, Abe’s academic performance in school has declined and they sought out a therapist because he frequently became argumentative in the home setting.
