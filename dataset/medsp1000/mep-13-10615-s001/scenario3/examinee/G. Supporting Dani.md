**Supporting Dani**

**Module 3 (Handout 1)**

Section 2, Supporting Dani Part A

Dani is a 14 y/o female who presents with chief complaint of “gender issues.”

She has had a number of stressors in her life. When she was in 5th grade her mother died in a car accident. Her father, looking for more support, decided to send her to a private school outside of her community.

In the last 2 years, her father has remarried and they have since had a baby. There has been stress over her father changing jobs and needing to travel more. She describes not having much of a relationship with her stepmother but being somewhat close to her father.

She describes her school as academically rigorous with many hours of homework per night. However, she feels that teachers and other students are quite open and accepting. She is a talented athlete playing goalie on the soccer team and playing ice hockey in the winter. She has a few close friends and denies bullying.

Over the last year and a half she has begun to question her gender identity. She describes feeling like she is a boy living in a girl’s body. About a year ago she cut her long hair to a pixie cut and has begun wearing more androgynous clothing. She has not told any peers about her feelings but recently told her father who brought her here today. He reports that his initial reaction was one of confusion but as Dani has brought it up a number of times he has begun to consider her gender identity more. He wonders if this might be a stress reaction to all she has encountered and if it might be a passing thing.

On presentation, Dani is a slight, androgynous appearing youth with short light brown hair wearing a slightly oversized oxford style shirt, flat front khaki shorts and New Balance sneakers. Dani is clutching a sci-fi paperback.
