**Supporting Dani**

**Module 3 (Handout 1)**

Section 2, Supporting Dani Part A

Dani first presented with chief complaint of “gender issues.”

He has had a number of stressors in his life. When he was in 5th grade his mother died in a car accident. His father, looking for more support, decided to send him to a private school outside of his community.

In the last 2 years, his father has remarried and they have since had a baby. There has been stress over his father changing jobs and needing to travel more. He describes not having much of a relationship with his stepmother but being somewhat close to his father.

He describes his school as academically rigorous with many hours of homework per night. However, he feels that teachers and other students are quite open and accepting. He is a talented athlete playing goalie on the soccer team and playing ice hockey in the winter. He has a few close friends and denies bullying.

Over the last year and a half he has begun to question his gender identity. He describes feeling like he is a boy living in a girl’s body. About a year ago he cut his long hair to a pixie cut and has begun wearing more androgynous clothing. He had recently told his father about these feelings. His father’s initial reaction was one of confusion, but as Dani brought it up a number of times he began to consider Dani’s gender identity more. He wondered if this might be a stress reaction to all Dani has encountered and if it might be a passing thing.

At the earlier visit, Dani was a slight, androgynous appearing youth with short light brown hair wearing a slightly oversized oxford style shirt, flat front khaki shorts and New Balance sneakers. Dani was clutching a sci-fi paperback.

Section 4: Supporting Dani, Part B

Dani returns in one year for his HME. He now identifies you as the primary care provider. He feels certain that he would like to live as a male and would like to know what his options are. He is also hesitant about how any change might affect his relationship with his girlfriend of 3 months as well as his family and friends.
