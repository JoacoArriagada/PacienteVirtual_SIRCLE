**Pediatric Crisis Resource Management for Medical Students**

# Faculty Handout: Simulation Case Scenario- Respiratory/Cardiac Arrest

7. Case Narrative:
   1. Scenario background given to participants: 3 year-old male is admitted to the hospital with cough and respiratory distress.
      1. Chief complaint: Cough and respiratory distress
      2. PMH: none
      3. Meds and allergies: NKA, takes daily vitamin
      4. Family/ social history: dad has asthma, lives with mom, dad and older sister (5) at home. No pets.
   2. Scenario conditions initially
      1. History patient gives: Mom reports 3 days of cough with fever (Tm 101) with 1 day refusal to take PO and declining urine output
      2. Patient’s initial exam
         1. Patient is conscious and can speak- says he wants to go home, wants his mom
         2. **General:** Tachypneic with mild retractions
         3. **Cyanosis** at 1 minute.
         4. **Physical Exam:** Coughing with initial transient desaturations to 85%, scattered rales bilaterally with decreased breath sounds on right
