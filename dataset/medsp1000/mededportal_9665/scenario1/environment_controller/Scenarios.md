1. **Case Narrative (VERSION A)**
   1. **General Synopsis:** You are an anesthesiologist at a small community hospital taking in-house call. The colorectal surgeon calls you about an emergent exploratory laparotomy she wants to perform. The patient, Fred DeKalb, is a 77 year old farmer who presents with left lower quadrant abdominal pain with nausea, vomiting and anorexia worsening over the past 4 days. The abdominal CT scan shows dilated contrast-filled loops of bowel with compressed areas of small bowel distally. The surgical team plans for an emergency laparotomy. He is being rushed to the operating room where you meet the team.
   2. **Past Medical History**: The patient received a previous exploratory laparotomy 12 years ago for diverticulitis.
   3. **Social History:** The patient lives with his wife on the farm and has four adult children, all of whom are alive and well. He feels like he has been slowing down over the past year or so, but attributes that to “getting old.” He still farms with his 3 adult sons. They work over 300 acres.
   4. **Allergies and Medications:** The patient has no known drug allergies**.** The patient takes no routine medications. He has taken Tums® and Pepto-Bismol® since onset of illness, and reports taking about three Tylenol® per month for headaches.
   5. **Review of Systems:**
      1. GENERAL: increasing fatigue over the past 12-16 months
      2. HEAD/NECK: negative
      3. CARDIOVASCULAR: occasional chest discomfort over the past 12 months when carrying groceries up the stairs from the garage
      4. PULMONARY: gets winded easier than he used to, has shortness of breath with mild exertion, denies dyspnea at rest, negative for productive cough
      5. NEUROLOGIC: negative
      6. GASTROINTESTINAL: positive for anorexia, nausea, and vomiting worsening over the past 4 days. He has had nothing to eat or drink for 7 hours. Also, noted more frequent, darker stools over the past 12 months. Denies hematemesis and hematochezia
      7. RENAL/HEPATIC: negative
      8. ENDOCRINE: negative
      9. HEME/COAG: negative
   6. **Physical Exam:**
      1. GENERAL: thin, pale elderly male resting on hospital gurney. Appears to be in pain and ill but non-toxic
      2. HEIGHT/WEIGHT: 170cm/66kg (BMI: 22.84)
      3. HEART: distant, 2/6 systolic murmur; regular rate and rhythm
      4. LUNGS: non-smoker; distant, slightly diminished breath sounds throughout, fine crackles at bases, no wheezes
      5. AIRWAY: Mallampati I, normal mouth opening, slightly limited ROM of neck, thyromental distance > 3 finger-breadths, positive subluxation of the jaw
      6. NEUROLOGIC: Alert and oriented x 3; no focal deficits
      7. GASTROINTESTINAL: moderately distended abdomen, pain with palpation; greatest in LLQ; high-pitched bowel sounds
      8. VITALS: HR 94; BP 101/73; RR 16; Temp 37.7oC; SpO2 99% on 3 L via a nasal cannula
   7. **Laboratory, Radiology and Other Relevant Issues:**
