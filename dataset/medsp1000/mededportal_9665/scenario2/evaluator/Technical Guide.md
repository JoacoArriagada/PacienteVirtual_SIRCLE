Go to Phase IV.  < 0.5 MAC: patient proceeds with case.  **Teaching Points:**  Goals of anesthetic management for patients with AS. |
| Phase IV:  Junctional Rhythm & BP  | HR 52, junctional  BP 71/54  RR 16  SpO2 100% on vent | **Learner Actions:**  Learner may instruct surgeons to stop surgery until patient becomes more stable.  Learner may identify precipitating events: hypovolemia, onset of junctional rhythm with loss of atrial kick. Learner may call for TEE probe. | **Trigger:**  If learner correctly instructs surgeons to stop case and appropriately treats hypotension and dysrhythmia then patient progressively becomes more stable (return to baseline vitals) → *END (Debrief)*  If after 3 minutes, the learner fails to treat hypotension and the dysrhythmia → proceed to Phase V (arrest)  **Teaching Points:**  Management of aortic stenosis intra-op complications. |
| Phase V:  Cardiopulmonary  arrest | HR 33, PEA  BP 47/31  SpO2 92% | **Learner Actions:**  Learner may call for HELP.  Learner may ask the CPR and ACLS be started.  Learner may request crash cart brought into the OR and paddles placed on patient.  Learner may increase FiO2. | **Trigger:**  If learner correctly calls for help and institutes ACLS → *END (Debrief)* |
