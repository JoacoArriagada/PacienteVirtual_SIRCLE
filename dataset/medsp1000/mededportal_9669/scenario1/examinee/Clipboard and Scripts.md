Participant Instructions Coma:

You and your colleague are the residents on duty. You don’t know anything about the patient, other than that she has been unresponsive and on a ventilator for the past 2 days. Your task is to take a history from the family member, conduct a complete general and neurological examination of the patient, review her records, determine whether she meets brain death criteria, and counsel the family member.

Limitations of this simulation are as follows:

* There is not a real ventilator in the room, but the patient is intubated and connected to respiratory tubing. There is also a ventilator log.
* Deep tendon reflexes are not elicitable on the mannequin.

**Hand this to the participants playing the role of physicians and ask them to step out of the room.**
