# Case C: Mrs. Spurling

**General Instructions:** Emphasize to learners that they must describe the maneuvers they are performing as they do them. When learners incorrectly perform a technique, confirm which maneuver the learner was attempting, and give the expected outcome as outlined in the case. No feedback should be given until the resident is completely done with the exercise.

**Patient Name**: Mrs. Spurling, 63 years old

**Setting:** Outpatient clinic – exam room

**Complaint**: Acute neck and shoulder pain

**HPI**: Mrs. Spurling is a retired accountant who reports sharp pain in her **left** shoulder that began suddenly after getting out of bed two weeks ago. Over the past week she has developed concomitant left neck pain which radiates down her arm to her fingertips.. The course is intermittent and Mrs. Spurling states the pain is the worst when she is driving. Otherwise there is no temporal association or other aggravating/alleviating factors. She has taken over the counter medication with minimal relief, stating ibuprofen has worked the best. Denies fevers, chills or prior episodes. Reports occasional numbness over upper arm. Also states she feels a bit weaker when lifting up her grandson over past week. Otherwise ROS is negative. **Her left neck/shoulder pain is presently a 4 out of 10 and radiates down her arm**.

**PMHx**: HTN, DM, L knee arthroplasty 5 years ago

**Meds**: Metformin, HCTZ

**Social**: Smokes ½ pack per day for 20 years. No hx of alcohol or drugs. Worked in Miami as accountant.

**Exam:** The patient appears with L arm adducted to side. Normal gait and pleasant affect.

Vital signs stable, afebrile. The cardiac, pulmonary, GI, and skin exams are unremarkable.

Neck and Shoulder:

* Gross Inspection: Symmetric. no kyphosis or scoliosis. no muscular atrophy
* Neck ROM: Rotation 90 degrees, limited lateral flexion to L due to neck/shoulder pain, forward flexion to 80 degrees, full extension to 75 degrees
* No tenderness to palpation of paraspinal, trapezius, or upper arm muscles
* L biceps strength 4+/5; L wrist extensors 4/5; otherwise strength 5/5 bilat.
* 1+ brachioradialis reflex on left; otherwise reflexes 2+ bilat
* Spurling test produces radiculopathy down L arm to thumb and index finger
* Shoulder abduction relief sign is present
* Cervical distraction test is positive
* Hoffman’s negative. Lhermitte’s test is negative
* Strength and reflex testing in LE’s is within normal limits
