VII. Instructor Notes

A. Tips to keep scenario flowing 1. If need for further evaluation not recognized, nurse will make a suggestion for further evaluation, calling the supervising resident, giving a medication, getting the pads on the patient, or to look at the monitor for PMVT. 2. Nurse will prompt learners to consider medications if AMS due to opiates is not recognized. 3. Nurse will prompt learners to obtain baseline ECG and PMVT rhythm strip if not requested.
