**CLINICAL PROGRESSION:**

History and physical performed, IV access and monitoring requested. The patient will continue to complain of chest pain and shortness of breath.

The case will progress to worsening respiratory distress (requiring intubation) and cardiogenic shock. If appropriate therapy is not initiated, the patient will continue to decompensate with worsening respiratory status and hypotension, leading to cardiac arrest.

The case will then pause while the patient is taken to the cardiac catheterization laboratory. The case will continue following cardiac catheterization in the cardiac intensive care unit. The case will end once learners interpret the provided pulmonary artery catheter numbers and administer appropriate therapy.
