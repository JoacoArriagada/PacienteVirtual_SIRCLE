# VII. Instructor Notes

A. Tips to keep scenario flowing 1. If need for further evaluation not recognized, nurse will make a suggestion for further evaluation or Cardiology consult for initiation of antiarrhythmic. 2. Nurse will prompt learners to obtain baseline ECG if not requested.   
B. Scenario programming 1. Optimal management path  O2/IV/monitor . History and physical examination Follow rule-out MI protocol o Labs: CBC, BMP, cardiac markers, coagulation profile o Images: ECG, CXR  IV fluid resuscitation

2. Potential complications/errors path(s)

. Failure to look at baseline ECG to confirm baseline aberrant conduction   
Failure to place defibrillation pads before attempting adenosine   
. Failure to initiate appropriate treatment   
Attempt to perform unsynchronized defibrillation rather than synchronized cardioversion
