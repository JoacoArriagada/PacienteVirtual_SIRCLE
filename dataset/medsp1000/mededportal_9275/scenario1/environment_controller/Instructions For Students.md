**Additional Materials #2**

**Instructions for Students**

The faculty instructor gives the following instructions to the students:

1. You will work in the teams in which you are sitting to help the patient identify which medications s/he has been taking. You will determine medication name and dose. You will use the information the patient has already provided you. You (or one of your teammates) will call the “pharmacy”, the “doctor’s office”, and interview the patient one more time.
2. You (or your teammates) will have 4 minutes to glean information from each of your resources (the “pharmacy”, “doctor’s office”, or patient). These are busy individuals so be prepared to ask succinct questions and be brief.
3. After speaking with the “pharmacist”, “doctor’s office” and patient, you and your teammates will reconvene to prepare a medication list that identifies what the patient has been taking (medications and doses). You will have 15 minutes to accomplish this.
4. As you work with the various sources of information, keep in mind that for the purposes of this session, today’s date is July 21, 2011.
