| **Appendix A: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Scenario**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE:** Pediatric Toxidrome Simulation Curriculum: Jimson Weed Ingestion | |
| **PATIENT NAME:** Sara  **PATIENT AGE:** 15 years  **CHIEF COMPLAINT:** Altered mental status  **PHYSICAL SETTING:** Resuscitation Bay of emergency department | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Patient is a 15-year-old girl with a history of mild intermittent asthma, otherwise healthy, who presents with agitation and hallucinations secondary to intentional ingestion of jimson weed seeds.  She presents to the emergency department (ED) via ambulance with altered mentation, tachycardia, and hyperthermia. She has signs and symptoms of anticholinergic toxicity and subsequently develops a generalized tonic clonic seizure that resolves with benzodiazepines. Seizure and postictal period are complicated by airway obstruction leading to hypoxemia and respiratory failure requiring intubation. QRS widening develops in the setting of tachycardia, then progresses to ventricular tachycardia (VT) with pulse but poor perfusion. Throughout the case, the patient’s body temperature is also rising. |
