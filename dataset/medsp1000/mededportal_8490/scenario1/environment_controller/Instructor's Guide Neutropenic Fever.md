# Neutropenic Fever/Sepsis Instruction Guide

# B. Help Available for the Learner

1. Nurse – Although ideally a licensed nurse, this role can be played by one of the simulation lab personnel. The nurse should have an earpiece to hear input from the facilitator during the case. They can then offer some guidance or suggestions during the simulation.   
C. Simulation Operator – Person who is familiar with the institution’s simulation lab and can implement and control the technical aspects of the simulation. As the learners perform various actions and interventions, the simulation operator will be responsible for having the computer-enhanced mannequin (CEM) respond appropriately.   
D. Facilitator – Attending physician/faculty who is familiar with management of sepsis, septic shock, and ACLS protocol. The facilitator should be stationed with the Simulation Operator to help decide how the CEM should respond to the learners’ actions. At the end of the simulation, the facilitator will lead the debriefing session.

# III. Equipment

Case Location: Med/Surg Bed

Equipment Required (other than CEM)

1. Defibrillator   
2. Bag-valve-mask   
3. Suction Machine   
4. ET tube   
5. Laryngoscope   
6. IV pole   
7. Bags to simulate IV fluids (normal saline)

Monitors Required:

1. Non-Invasive BP Cuff   
2. Cardiac Monitor   
3. Pulse Oximeter

# IV. Documents included in this case

1. Instructions Neutropenic Fever – Summary of equipment and personnel required. Also includes overview of how to setup and run the case.   
2. Simulator Case Neutropenic Fever – Detailed description of the case including notes to the Simulation Operator and Facilitator. The Case is broken up into a series of “States,” each with a different set of vital signs that indicates a progression of the disease process. Triggers are given to designate when to progress to the next state.   
3. Case Stem – file with brief summary of the patient. This should be given to the learners at the beginning of the simulation   
4. Labs and Studies – basic labs, echo report, and EKG to be given to the team upon request.

# V. Instruction Guide and Setup

1. Simulator Equipment ii. See above for equipment requirements. iii. All monitors and equipment should be readily available to the learners. iv. All simulator settings should be entered prior to starting the case. See file “Simulator Case Neutropenic Fever.” v. Learners should be oriented to the CEM and the equipment.

3. Simulation

i. See file titled “Case Stem.” – This page should be given to the team at the beginning of the simulation. The Team Leader should read the case stem to the rest of the team.   
ii. Once the case stem has been read, initiate the simulation in State 1.   
iii. The simulator will progress through the states as outlined in the “Simulator Case Neutropenic Fever” file.   
iv. Note to Simulation Operator – denotes changes to the CEM

vi. Trigger- describes the trigger that will initiate the next step. These are meant to be guidelines only and are at the discretion of the facilitator.

vii. If the team deviates from the case as outlined in the file, the facilitator along with the simulation operator will make adjustments to react to the teams actions.

iii. Basic Labs, EKG, and Echo report are provided to the team when specifically requested. See “Labs and studies” file.

ix. At the end of State 5 when the patient expires, the team will be brought into a conference room with an actor portraying a family member. The team will then deliver the news to the family member.
