**“Devin Hughes ”**

**Authors: Elizabeth Hager, Gretchen Diemer, MD**

**CASE SUMMARY**

You are Devin Hughes, a 55+ year old person.

You have come to the hospital to sign consent forms on behalf of your mother who has dementia and coronary artery disease. Your character is at the hospital because his/her mother is in need of a medical procedure and you are the one who has been helping to facilitate and organize her care. (This case focuses on getting informed consent and most details are purely background information for your own character development.)

**History of Present Illness for MOTHER:**

Your **mother** has recently been hospitalized for an infection in the blood. She also suffers from dementia and heart disease. She has a fever and low blood pressure, is confused but awake and is has pain in her lower stomach.

**Social History:**

Occupation: You are a high school janitor at Germantown High.

You have two children. You are divorced. You have no pets and do not smoke. Alcohol or substance abuse is not an issue for you.

You completed the 9th grade but can only read at a very basic level. You would be unable to read and understand completely a newspaper article. You can sound out words but any medical jargon is not understandable. You can balance a checkbook with some effort. You do not go to the doctor yourself regularly and find it difficult to make decisions about your mother’s care because you often don’t understand what the doctors are telling you. You are reluctant to ask questions for fear of showing your lack of knowledge.

**Family History:**

Your **mother** is in the hospital for an infection in the blood. She also suffers from dementia and heart disease. You do not know any more details about her past medical history. Your children have no health problems that you know of.

**Review of Symptoms:**

None.

**What you want from this visit:**

To understand why a central line needs to be put in, what the risks are and what the other choices are instead of having it put in.

**APPEARANCE AND BEHAVIOR**

**Physical Appearance**:

You should wear casual attire.

**Behavior:**

You are worried about your mother and not entirely sure why you need to sign consent forms and what procedures they are for.

You do not understands words like “catheter”, “sepsis”, “intravenous”, “critically ill”, “pressors”. You do understand “vein”, “blood pressure”, “infection”, “very sick”, “medicine”. You understand better with shorter sentences and smaller amounts of information at a time.

If asked to read the consent form, you should be evasive to get out of showing that you cannot read it. If pushed, you should admit that you “don’t read very well” and “never understand what you doctors are talking about”.

When asked to explain back what the doctor has explained, you should only repeat things that were explained simply without jargon. Otherwise, answer “I’m not sure exactly.”

**Response to Physical Exam Maneuvers:**

NO Physical Exam.

NO History Exam.

**CHALLENGE:** At 5 min. announcement, if you haven’t brought up this question already, you should find a way to ask “DO I HAVE TO SIGN THIS FORM FOR MY MOTHER?” or “DO I HAVE ANY OTHER CHOICES?”
