A 33 yo patient presenting w/ c/o new onset R. knee pain. <u>Past Med/Sx Hx</u>:  Unremarkable for chronic diseases and hospitalizations; UTI in 2008, burning during urination followed by thick, yellow urethral/vaginal discharge; treated with antibiotics; w/ Tx complaint resolved and has not re-surfaced since. <u>Social Hx</u>: tobacco use 1 pk/2 wks; alcohol use about 5 drinks/wk; no recreational drug use; patient is sexually active with one partner for the last 2.5 yrs; unprotected heterosexual activity in a committed relationship; no extra-relationship contacts reported
