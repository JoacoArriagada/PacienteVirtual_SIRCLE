**Critical Actions Checklist: Wide Complex Tachycardia**

Leader \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

1. Y/N Recognize wide complex tachycardia on EKG
2. Y/N Place defibrillation pads on patient immediately
3. Y/N Give an appropriate antidysrhythmic medication (amiodarone, procainamide, lidocaine, or beta-blocker)
4. Y/N Give a second appropriate antidysrhythmic medication (amiodarone, procainamide, lidocaine, or beta-blocker)
5. Y/N Administer synchronized cardioversion when patient becomes unstable
6. Y/N Explain cardioversion to patient prior to shocking
7. Y/N Order labs including troponin and electrolytes
8. Y/N Monitor oxygen saturation and telemetry during cardioversion
9. Y/N Obtain a repeat EKG after cardioversion
10. Y/N Consult and admit to cardiology
