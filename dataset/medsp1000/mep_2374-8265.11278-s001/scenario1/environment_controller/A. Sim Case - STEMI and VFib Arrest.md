**Brief narrative description of case**

A 61-year-old male with a history of hypertension and diabetes presents with chest pain and is found to have ST elevations on EKG. Although initially awake and alert, he goes into ventricular fibrillation arrest during the case, requiring the participants to work together to effectively run ACLS, choose appropriate medications, and admit the patient to the cardiac catheterization lab.
