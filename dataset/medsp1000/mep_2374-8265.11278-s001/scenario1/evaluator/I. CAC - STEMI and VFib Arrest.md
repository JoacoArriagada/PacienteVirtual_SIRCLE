**Critical Actions Checklist: ST-Elevation Myocardial Infarction and Ventricular Fibrillation Arrest**

Leader \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

1. Y/N Obtain an EKG
2. Y/N Place patient on supplemental oxygen
3. Y/N Place defibrillation pads on patient (before arrest)
4. Y/N Give aspirin
5. Y/N Give heparin bolus
6. Y/N Call catheterization lab
7. Y/N Defibrillate at 200J when goes into ventricular fibrillation
8. Y/N Intubate
9. Y/N Start an appropriate pressor (epinephrine, norepinephrine, or dopamine)
10. Y/N Give appropriate antidysrhythmic medication (amiodarone or lidocaine)
