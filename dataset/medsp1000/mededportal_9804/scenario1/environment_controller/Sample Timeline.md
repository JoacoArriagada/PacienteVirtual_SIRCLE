# Sample Timeline Asthma Exacerbation Blended Simulation

I. Initial Settings

Sinus rhythm: 95 bpm   
Auscultation sounds: Bilateral wheezing   
Lung resistance: Level 2   
SpO2: $87 \%$   
Respiration rate: 36   
Blood Pressure: 144/84

II. If treatments including supplemental oxygen or a nebulizer are initiated within five minutes the manikin responds with these changes.

SpO2: $91 \%$ Respiration rate: 28 Blood pressure: 142/82

III. If the manikin is given a steroid or second nebulizer treatment, the following parameters progressively change.

Sinus rhythm: 100 bpm   
Auscultation sounds: wheezing improved   
Lung resistance: Level 1   
SpO2: $92 \%$   
Respiratory rate: 26   
Blood pressure: 134/80

IV. If the manikin is given a second nebulizer or the dose of steroids the settings progressively improve.

Sinus rhythm: 95 bpm   
Auscultation sounds: wheezing improved   
SpO2: $93 \%$   
Respiration rate: 24   
Blood pressure: 130/78

V. If no treatments are rendered in the first 5 minutes of the scenario, the manikin progressively worsens until treatment is initiated. The manikin’s condition will continue to deteriorate every three minutes there after or until oxygen is given. Once treatment is started the settings revert to II above.

Worsening conditions include three different settings.

Sinus rhythm: 95 bpm SpO2: $85 \%$ Cyanosis initiated Respiration rate: 40

Worsening condition

Sinus rhythm: 105 Eyes: half open SpO2: $83 \%$ Respiration rate: 40

Worsening condition

Sinus rhythm: 115 SpO2: $82 \%$ Respiration rate: 42 Blood pressure: 150/90

No additional changes are made to the manikin after 14 minutes.
