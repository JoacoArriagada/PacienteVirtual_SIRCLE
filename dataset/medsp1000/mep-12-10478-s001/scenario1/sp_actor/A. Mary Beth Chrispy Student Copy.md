## Bridge Program Case: Burned out medical student

Mary Beth Chrispy

Case Number 001

By: Danyelle Lee MSIII, Ranna Nash, MA, Thomas Hunt MD

Case 001

Mary Beth Chrispy

**Chief Complaint:**

Mary Beth Chrispy is a 25-year-old 2nd year medical student starting her summer studies for Step 1. Mary decided to come in and see her primary care physician due to her having trouble focusing on her studies. Mary tells you that she is concerned about her energy level. Mary has been complaining of fatigue for the past four weeks.

Case 001

Mary Beth Chrispy

**History of Present Illness:**

Mary complains of lack of energy that has increased over the past four weeks. She states that she has been sleeping more and notes that she starts falling asleep after only about an hour of studying. Mary also adds that she has not done as well as usual in her semester finals four weeks ago and is concerned that the bout of illness may have been going on longer than four weeks. Mary also complains of an increasingly sore throat and a persistent fever that is relieved by NSAIDs. You asked if she takes any other medications and/or vitamins and she states she doesn’t take any other medications and she used to take a multi-vitamin, but often times forgets to take it everyday with all her daily stress.

Case 001

Mary Beth Chrispy

**Past Medical History:**

Mary then begins to talk more about her history in medical school. She entered medical school with the ambition of becoming a surgeon. She struggled academically in her first year of medical school, but was able to get decent grades at the end. During the summer in between her 1st and 2nd year of school she took on many projects such as spending 2 weeks at the Betty Ford clinic and multiple research and preceptor projects. Needless to say, she did not give herself a summer vacation and went straight back into school. Mary started expressing slight sleep deprivation and fatigue but continued to push through the Fall semester. During Christmas break Mary started preparing for boards while shadowing a local surgeon. Again, no break. At the start of the Spring semester she started feeling fatigued and was losing energy but ignored the signs. Her focus became less and was starting to experience more frustration with her classmates and professors. She says that her study group says that they are worried about her and are being critical of her behavior. She says that her friends are worried that the lack of down time Mary is doing will affect her health.

Mary denies being allergic to any medications, any other medical issues or ever being hospitalized. When asked about her surgical history she stated that she had an old sports injury, meniscus tear in her right knee, which was operated on when she was 15.

Mary Beth Chrispy

**Past Medical History:**

When asked once more Mary denies drinking alcohol, using tobacco products, or illegal drug use. Mary admits she has recently ended a long-term relationship with her boyfriend due to the stressors of medical school. She tells you that they used condoms as protection from STDs and unwanted pregnancy. When asked about her living situation Mary states that she lives in a small apartment with another medical student, but she never really sees her roommate because she prefers to study in the quiet confines of her room.

Mary’s states that she hasn’t had much of an appetite, but she thinks that her diet has been minimal due to the stress of studying for Step 1, she has been attempting to consume oatmeal in the morning and mainly has been eating snacks rather than full meals. She also drinks large amounts of coffee and has been for many years. Past medical history seems fine. She has a family history that is negative for cancer, diabetes and heart disease. Her social history is negative for factors that would contribute to her current condition. Yet she seems a little stressed and uneasy.

Mary’s physical exam is as follows:

General: Mary is a well appearing, slightly flushed young woman.

**Diagnosis:**

Mary is diagnosed with infectious mononucleosis. She is very upset and worries about her progressing on with her Step 1 studies. Mary requests that you contact the Associate Dean for Admissions and Student Affairs to discuss that Mary will have to take 4 weeks off of her board studying to gain her strength back.
