Functions of major gastrointestinal structures14:

**Salivary glands** – secretes enzymes that break down food

**Esophagus** – muscle that allows passage of food into the stomach

**Stomach** – storage place for food, secretes acid that helps break down food

**Small intestine** – reabsorbs nutrients into the bloodstream

* Three segments – duodenum, jejunum, ileum

**Pancreas** – secretes enzymes into the duodenum that break down food

* Secretes insulin into the blood stream to help metabolize sugar

**Liver** – processes nutrients absorbed from the small intestine, breaks down drugs

**Gall bladder** – stores bile, which is released into the duodenum to break down fat

**Large intestine** – reabsorbs mostly water and some nutrients into the bloodstream

* Four segments – ascending, transverse, descending, and sigmoid colon
