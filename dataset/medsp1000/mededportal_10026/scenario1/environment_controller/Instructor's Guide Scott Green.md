**Scott Green: Depression after a Heart Attack**

**Instructor’s Guide**

**Standardized Patient Training Session:**

* During the SP training session, the Beck depression inventory should be reviewed and the implications of the score addressed. SPs should be instructed to act as if they completed the inventory after a blank one was handed to them. In actuality, the SPs will be putting their SP name and date on a pre-completed inventory.

**Small Group Session:**

**Schedule:**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | **Description** |
| 1. Review of Patient Information | The students should look over the patient information sheet (Information for Learners). |
| 1. Patient Interview | The students will interview the patient. The goal is a focused history using patient centered interviewing techniques based on the patient’s reason for the visit. |
| 1. Discussion of Assessment and Plan | The standardized patient should be temporarily excused to allow the class to discuss their assessment and plan. The SP may “complete” the Beck Depression inventory at this time. |
| 1. Sharing Information and Negotiating a Treatment Plan | The students will discuss the assessment and negotiate a treatment plan with the patient. |

**Facilitator Teaching Notes**

**30 min Review of Patient information and Standardized Patient Interview**

This session will focus on students’ abilities to obtain a focused history, exam, and counseling for a patient who was recently hospitalized for a myocardial infarction.

Prior to the standardized patient (“Scott Green”) arriving, ask students to use the “Instructions for Learners” sheet provided to review his past history. Have the student(s) interview the SP, taking a focused history.

Students are expected to take a focused history, getting the details of the patient’s hospitalization and current symptoms. It is reasonable to obtain a screening cardiac history to determine if the patient has any symptoms of active cardiac disease.

The patient clearly has symptoms of depression. If students do not fully explore the patient’s symptoms of depression, they will have another chance after further discussion.

Before the SP is excused, gauge whether the students feel they have enough information regarding the patient’s mood. If not, offer a tool that can be used to help diagnose depression called the Beck’s Depression Inventory. If the consensus is yes, hand out a blank copy to the SP and ask them to complete it while out of the room. Also give students a blank copy so they can review it. The SP will have an already-completed depression inventory to turn back in if requested. Their score will put them in the clinically depressed range.

**20 min Developing an Assessment Negotiating a Treatment Plan**

While the SP is waiting out of the room, have the students develop an assessment as well as a treatment plan.

If the student presents a reasonable argument, the SP will agree to starting an anti-depressant and going back to cardiac rehab. They will also agree to come back for a follow-up visit in the future.
