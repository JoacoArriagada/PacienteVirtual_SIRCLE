**Appendix C: Critical Actions Checklist**
Provide to SPs, observers, and faculty prior to starting simulation event to encourage structured feedback delivered to learner after simulation. May be adapted to assess and measure learner performance.

**Sexual Assault (SA) Cases Critical Actions Checklist:**

1. Identify that the patient may have experienced SA.
2. Respond appropriately to the disclosure of SA.
3. Establish a psychologically and physically safe environment for the patient.
4. Elucidate if the patient is willing to discuss next steps in managing SA from the Emergency Department.
5. Offer that the patient may meet with the Victim Advocate/Sexual Assault Medical Forensic Examiner.
6. Counsel the patient on resources available to them (if standardized patient is agreeable to further counseling).
