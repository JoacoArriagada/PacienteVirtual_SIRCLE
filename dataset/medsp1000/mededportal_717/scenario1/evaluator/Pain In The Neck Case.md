Title: “A Pain in the Neck"

Case Narrative (Brief overview describing what the learner will experience)

64 year-old female who presents via EMS with a chief complaint of “trouble breathing"and sweling in the neck. Initial vital signs stable Progresses to acute respiratory distress secondary to progressive expansion of neck mass shortly after arrival. Anesthesia/ENT consultants unavailable   
Learner must proceed to intubation of the dificult airway

# Case Narrative

Arrival time: 0445, Wednesday

Chief complaint: Trouble breathing.

HPl: 64 year-old white female complaining of swelling in the neck.   
She was hospitalized over the weekend secondary to hyponatremia and hypertension.   
Medications adjusted during hospitalization and patient discharged Monday evening.   
Went to work for 1/2 a day on Tuesday and went to bed at 2030.   
Awoke with neck pain, swelling, and difficulty breathing.   
Thought she was having a stroke so called 911.   
Also complaining of right ear ache.   
PMH: Hypothyroidism, hypertension   
Medications: Premarin,synthroid (0.075 mg PO QD increased to 0.1 mg PO QD),atenolol (increased from 25 mg PO QD to 25 mg PO BID)   
Allergies: None   
Social History: Negative for tobacco and alcohol. Married,one son who are here with patient ROS: Pertinent $( + )$ : right ear pain and neck swelling $( R > \mathsf { L } )$ , SOB,and decreased leg swelling.   
Pertinent $( - )$ : fever, chils,sore throat,chest pain, N/V/D,abdominal pain,dysuria,urinary frequency or urgency.

Authornote:This was oneofthe most difcult arways thatIhave managedinmycarer.Everythingaboutthecaseilustrates what we trainfor.It was night time,single coverage,no consultants in-house.Rapid deterioration after arrival requiring aggressive management ofa diffcult airway.Ihave included the original dictation for users to review to get a general sense of what really transpired.

In the real case,the PACS went down and the soft tissue neck film was unable to be loaded onto the system before the patient deteriorated.Thus,Ihad to manage the airway without knowledge of what the radiographs looked like and only subsequently after the intubation was lable to se the films and appreciate the extent of the sweling.

As an epilogue, the patient eventually had an MRl that was suspicious for an aneurysm and she subsequently underwent body angiography that demonstrated a 1.5 cm pseudoaneurysm arising from the inferior thyroid artery which was subsequently embolized.

She continues to do well 5 years after the visit.

There is noted a large retropharyngeal abscess. The is predominantly right sided.   
Extends from nasopharynx down into the right side of the mediastinum.   
This is displacing the airway and trachea far anteriorly.   
The greatest area of displacement is approx 4.5 cm at C7.
