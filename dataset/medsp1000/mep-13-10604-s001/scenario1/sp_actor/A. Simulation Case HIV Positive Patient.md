| **Brief narrative description of case** | Ms. Jones is a 24-40 year-old female who is pre-operative, awaiting surgery for a hysterectomy due to possible endometrial cancer. Her pastor,  Reverend Paris, accompanies her. Her mother is parking the car and trying to find her way to the preoperative area so she can see Ms. Jones before the surgery.  Ms. Jones is HIV positive. She thinks she got HIV from previous IV drug use, when she shared needles with others. Her HIV is well-controlled with medication. Neither her pastor nor her mother are aware she has HIV.  She does not want them to know.  An anesthesia resident joins Ms. Jones to obtain consent for anesthesia. She will tell the resident she prefers the pastor leave the room. If the resident neglects to ask permission, when discussing her current medications, she will hesitate and be anxious, and then tell the resident she has HIV. The pastor will be shocked. She will ask the resident and the pastor not to tell her mother.  Near the end of the consent process, Ms. Jones is adamant she wants to wait for her mother to talk to her before the surgery. Reverend Paris asks if there will be time to pray with Ms. Jones and her mother. |
