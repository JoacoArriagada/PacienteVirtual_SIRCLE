MENTORING WORKSHOP PRACTICE CASES

CASE #3

**Mentor:** You have agreed to take on an MS1 as a mentee. You will be negotiating what that relationship will be like by scheduling an appointment with this student. The only thing you know about the student is that the student has a family (one child and a spouse) and is slightly older than most of the fellow students. The student seems to be doing fine in POM and is an active participant in the group.

**Mentee:** You are an older student, having been a teacher in your previous life. You have a spouse and one 13 month old child. You are looking for mentoring around balancing life’s issues with a medical career, starting with being a student (don’t offer this unless asked). You feel you are handling your professional responsibilities well but have conflicts about what should come first, family or your medical career. (offer if asked)
