MENTORING WORKSHOP PRACTICE CASES

CASE #1

**Mentor:** The student needs significant support in adjusting to the physical diagnosis skills class. The student has done well academically but does not feel skills are their strength. The student reads the assignments in Bates and seems to understand the material but has problems applying it. You have watched the student on occasion in your POM group and sense that the student has some issues getting into self-directed learning and prefers an authoritative approach. You meet with the student to try to establish a mentoring relationship.
