MENTORING WORKSHOP PRACTICE CASES

CASE #2

**Mentee:** You have been given the opportunity of having a TALKS mentor and your major agenda is basically having the mentor ‘spoonfeed’ you. (Don’t offer this unless asked). You know the mentor is very skillful and knowledgeable and want to take advantage of their abilities. This would give you knowledge expeditiously and instantly as you are concerned about all the information you are needing to master. The mentor should be negotiating a relationship with you. Stick to your needs even if the mentor tries to steer you in another direction.
