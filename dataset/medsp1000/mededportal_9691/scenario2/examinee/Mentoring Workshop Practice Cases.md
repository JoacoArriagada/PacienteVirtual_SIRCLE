MENTORING WORKSHOP PRACTICE CASES

CASE #2

**Mentor:** You have an opportunity to mentor the student as part of their POM experience. You have seen the student work and made some assumptions about what kind of mentoring the student needed. You think that part of what you want to do includes 1) getting the student to accept feedback in a less personal way, and 2) making certain the student knows how others feel when the student makes comments perhaps not appropriate for their training level and setting. (For example, ‘I ‘m not certain you are doing that exam correctly according to what I read in Bates’. This was expressed to the faculty and TALKs students leading the session. Another example is that the student said to the group “I don’t get the importance of all this training with the readily available technology we could be using instead of this.’)

Your teaching style is to facilitate self-directed learning.
