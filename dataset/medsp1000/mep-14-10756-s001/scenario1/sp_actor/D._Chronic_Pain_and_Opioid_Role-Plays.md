**APPENDIX D: CHRONIC PAIN AND OPIOID ROLE PLAYS**

CHRONIC PAIN AND OPIOID ROLE PLAYS

**Case 1: Discussion of Risk/Benefits of Opioids and Goal Setting**

The patient is a 58-year-old morbidly obese woman (BMI=38) who presents with a several month-long history of bilateral knee pain R>L. She states that her pain is a “10 out of 10.” She has been taking ibuprofen 600 mg 1 po qid and acetaminophen 1 g po tid. She reports that she previously participated in 9 sessions of physical therapy which helped some but the pain returned to the previous state after she finished the sessions. The patient states that she took several of her husband’s hydrocodone/acetaminophen and that improved her pain some. She is requesting a prescription.

**Part 1: Goal Setting**

The participant playing the role of a patient should be instructed to focus on the goal of being able to be more interactive with her 2 young grandchildren who she watches after school several days each week.

Patient: Well, I watch my 2 young grandkids 2 days a week and I get frustrated because I can’t keep up with them. I spend most of the time watching them from my living room couch.

Patient: Well, I don’t expect to run around like a young kid, but I would like to push them on the swings, maybe throw a ball back and forth, watch them on their tricycles out front….

Patient: It doesn’t seem like long….

Patient: I guess that would be a start.
