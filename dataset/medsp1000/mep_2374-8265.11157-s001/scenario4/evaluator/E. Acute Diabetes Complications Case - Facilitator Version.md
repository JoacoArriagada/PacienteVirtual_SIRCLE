**Answer Key and Teaching Points for Module III**

**Question 1**

“Laila”

* Intermittent vomiting and diarrhea (2-3 times/day) for 1 day
* Abdominal discomfort
* Fever
* Cough/congestion

**Teaching point**

*Correct answer:* “Laila”: Expected blood glucose to be high AND ketosis present.

During acute illness, the body’s stress response triggers counterregulatory hormones, leading to hyperglycemia, peripheral insulin resistance, and ultimately ketosis.

**Question 2.a**

Results:

* BG: 500

*Correct answer:* YES, give basal insulin now, and use the same dose.

Basal insulin is required even in a fasting state.

**Question 2.b**

You are planning for the PO challenge (PB&J sandwich = 15gm of carbs).

**Teaching point**

*Correct answer:* “Laila”: Yes, give rapid-acting insulin now, and increase the dose, because of sick day management.

Additional rapid-acting insulin is needed with hyperglycemia with or without ketosis during acute illness. An additional rapid-acting insulin dose is administered every 3-4 hours during sick day management.

**Teaching point**

**Question 3**

**Teaching point**

*Correct answer:* “Laila”: Admit due to inability to tolerate oral hydration and risk of progression to DKA/dehydration.

Principles of sick day management in patients with T1D:

1. Frequent BG and ketone check every 3-4 hrs.
2. Do NOT stop basal insulin.
3. Provide rapid-acting insulin correction doses every 3-4 hours and adjust additional dosing according to BG/ketones level.
4. Monitor and maintain hydration.
