**CASE-BASED LEARNING – MODULE III**

**Clinical Case:**

You are the resident working a shift in the emergency room.

“Laila” is a **12**-year-old girl with type 1 diabetes. She is on insulin therapy with multiple daily injections (MDI) using insulin-carb ratio (ICR) for meal coverage. Her last A1C is 8.5%.

Opening presentation:

* Intermittent vomiting and diarrhea (2-3 times/day) for 1 day
* Abdominal discomfort
* Fever
* Cough/congestion

Objective results when checked:

* BG: 500
* Ketones: Moderate
* pH: 7.36

Course for the disposition phase:

* Completed a PO challenge with 15gm of carbs
* Received basal insulin and rapid acting insulin for correction
* Vomited 30 min after PO challenge
* She tried to drink water
* She feels nauseous and takes a few small sips
* BG 2 hrs. after insulin correction is 200 mg/dL
* Repeat ketones are stable at moderate
