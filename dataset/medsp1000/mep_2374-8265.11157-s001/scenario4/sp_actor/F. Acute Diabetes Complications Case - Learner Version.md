**Clinical Case:**

“Laila” is a **12**-year-old girl with type 1 diabetes.

She is on insulin therapy with multiple daily injections (MDI) using insulin-carb ratio (ICR) for meal coverage. Her last A1C is 8.5%.

She presents to the emergency room with **acute gastroenteritis**.

Current illness:

* Intermittent vomiting and diarrhea (2-3 times/day) for 1 day
* Abdominal discomfort
* Fever
* Cough/congestion
