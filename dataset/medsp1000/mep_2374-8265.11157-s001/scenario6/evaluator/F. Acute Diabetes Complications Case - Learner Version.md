**CASE-BASED LEARNING – MODULE III**

**Clinical Case:**

You are back in the ER rotation.

“Laila” is a **12**-year-old girl with type 1 diabetes. She reports switching to an insulin pump in the last 2 weeks.

Opening presentation:

* Hyperglycemia since yesterday afternoon
* Has not been able to bring her blood glucose down despite giving additional insulin correction doses through the pump every 3-4 hours
* Denies any symptoms except for cramps as her period started yesterday
* Exam is unremarkable
* Point-of-care glucose is 383 mg/dL

Objective results after ketones and blood gas are checked:

* Moderate urine ketones
* pH: 7.4

RN question:

* “Can we give a bolus through the pump instead of waiting for the pharmacy to dispense the insulin vial/subcutaneous injection?”
