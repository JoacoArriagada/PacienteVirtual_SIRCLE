**CASE-BASED LEARNING – MODULE III**

**Clinical Case:**

You are the resident working a shift in the emergency room.

“Nora” is an **11**-year-old girl with type 1 diabetes. She is on insulin therapy with multiple daily injections (MDI) using insulin-carb ratio (ICR) for meal coverage. Her last A1C is 8%.

Opening presentation:

* Poor PO intake for 2 days
* Vomiting whatever she eats for the last day
* Drinks some sips of water

Objective results when checked:

* BG: 65
* Ketones: Mild/Moderate
* pH: 7.4

Course for the disposition phase:

* Completed a successful PO challenge with 15gm of carbs
* Received a dose of basal insulin
* Tolerated drinking plenty of fluids in the last 2 hours
* BG trended between 180-200 in the last two hours
* Repeat Ketones: mild/moderate
