# CASE #3: 20 YEAR OLD IV DRUG USER CASE MATERIALS FOR DISCUSSION

# PART I: Patient Presentation

Ms. Shelly Peterson, a 20 year old Caucasian woman from Ozaukee County, who presents to the ED with nausea, vomiting, lower abdominal tenderness, vaginal discharge and dyspareunia (pain with intercourse).

Ms. Peterson is in obvious discomfort. She is doubled over with guarding. Her pain score is 10 out of 10.

Ms. Peterson is a polydrug user with intravenous heroin as her drug of choice. She exchanges sex for drugs, food and a place to sleep.

# PART III: PATIENT PERSPECTIVES: VALUES-BELIEFS Patient’s Answers to Kleinman’s Questions

73. What do you think has caused your problem?

“Sex. I always practice safe sex when I work, but I do have intimate relationships where I don’t use a condom. That must be where it came from.”

74. Why do you think it started when it did?

“I am not sure when it started (it would be very difficult to determine when it started because I am always high or sick when I’m not high). I was just in so much pain that I had to come in today.”

75. What do you think your sickness does to you? How does it work? “I think it’s eating away at my body down there. It’s destroying my body.”

76. How severe is your sickness? Will it have a short or long course?

o “Look, I wouldn’t come to you unless the situation was bad. This is messing up my ability to make money.”   
o I think I’ll probably be sick for awhile. And you’ll probably end up cutting me open and taking my insides out, and I won’t be able to work for a very long time.”

77. What kind of treatment do you think you should receive?

“Give me something that I can do here in the hospital because I am not going to spend any money on medications. Give me a shot or something quick to cure the pain.”

78. What are the most important results you hope to receive from this treatment? “I want it to go away.”

79. What are the chief problems your sickness has caused for you?

“I can’t work, so I don’t have any money, so I can’t buy my heroine. Now I am sick because I don’t have any heroine.”

80. What do you fear most about your sickness?

o “Heroin withdrawal. I am sick and I need some heroin.”   
o “Really, it wouldn’t bother me if you took my insides out as long as I could go right back to work to get my heroin.”
