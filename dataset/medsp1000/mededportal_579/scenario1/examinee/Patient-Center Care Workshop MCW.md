# CASE #1: 28 YEAR OLD PREGNANT WITH HIV CASE MATERIALS FOR DISCUSSION

# PART I: Patient Presentation

You are seeing Ms. Sadie Smith a 28 year old woman living in the city of Milwaukee in your clinic. This is Ms. Smith’s first prenatal visit at thirteen weeks. This is Ms Smith’s fourth pregnancy. You began caring for Ms. Smith after her third pregnancy which is when Ms. Smith advised you that she was HIV positive. Her third child has not tested positive for HIV. Her previous three children have all been by different fathers. As her physician, you had discussed the importance of condom use if/when she chose to be sexually active. You were not anticipating that Ms. Smith would be pregnant.

You have just recommended to Ms. Smith that she begin taking AZT to reduce the chance of HIV transmission to her baby. Ms. Smith responded by saying “God will take care of me and my baby, so I do not want any HIV medicine for me or my baby”. Ms. Smith went on to emphasize that she would not take AZT during her pregnancy, the delivery, and/or approve AZT for her baby post delivery.
