# PART I: Patient Presentation

Now assume you are seeing another 55 year old male, Mr. William Lowe, who is complaining of chest pain. Mr. Lowe presents to the emergency room where he has been seen multiple times over the past year. Mr. Lowe is clean and well dressed with his entire family with him at the bedside. His three children are present as the family was going out to dinner. The children appear pre-occupied with their cellular phones and portable Sony play stations.

Mr. Lowe has had repeated hospitalizations for chest pain: hospitalized 3 times in the last year with 2 confirmed MI's. He has severe HTN and PVD. When he leaves the hospital he voices that he will do his best to continue with the recommended treatment though he has never made a follow up appointment or refilled a prescription. He smokes 3ppd and drinks 3 beers per day. He has expressed no desire to change these habits.
