**Adolescent Case**

You are a seventeen year old boy

You are here for a physical and the doctor has just asked your parent to leave to talk over some things in private.

Your main concern is that you have acne and it’s really bothering you.

**Home Life** 

You live with your mother and little sister. Your parents split up two years ago and your dad now lives with a new girlfriend and has a baby boy now (your half-brother).

You have a lot more arguments with your mother these days about almost everything from clothes to boyfriends to your grades. You used to get along better before your dad moved out. Your sister is annoying and is always in your room trying to hang out with you.

**Education**

You go to high school. You’re a sophomore. You got left back because of your grades. You think school is just OK you get C’s and D’s. You got a couple of F’s last semester.

You have some friends in school and a best friend named Mark. You hang out with mostly juniors and seniors since you know them better and your girlfriend is a senior.

**Activities** 

You mostly go home and stay in your room

You are not on any school teams or in any clubs

You like TV

You like listening to music (pick your favorite artist or band if asked)

**Drugs**

You’ve smoked marijuana once or twice at parties

You also drink at parties-mixed drinks no beer because it is “nasty”

You’ve gotten drunk before and gotten really sick in your neighbor’s bushes. You have never tried other drugs like meth, heroin or cocaine.

**Depression**

You do feel sad sometimes. Especially when you and your mom fight or it just seems like no one listens to you. Sometimes you thought that if you died people would start to feel bad for not taking you seriously or caring about you more. Nevertheless you don’t want to die and have no plan for suicide.

**Sexuality**

You are sexually active with your girlfriend of 8 months. You use condoms sometimes. You have never had an STD. You have had 3 other relationships before this one. You are heterosexual. You feel safe in the relationship and she treats you well and with respect. You are both “serious”
