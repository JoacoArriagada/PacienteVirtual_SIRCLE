**Faculty Guide: Adolescent History Taking**

This session will include didactic instruction on adolescent history taking followed by student practice with adolescent standardized patients. This should be followed by feedback and de-brief of the session.

<u>Adolescent Standardized Patients</u>

A copy of the adolescent case will be provided for you to review. The SCAG will also be provided to help guide your feedback of the session. The students are tasked with interviewing the adolescent and obtaining an advanced social history using the previous lecture and the SCAG as guides.

Please observe your student(s) during their history taking session and pay careful attention to the following:

- Student use of open ended questions
- Student skill at explaining rational for various questions
- Student use of transitional statements as they ask about different topics
- Student use of proper language and avoidance of jargon (not talking over the adolescent)
- Student maintenance of a non-judgmental interview style
- Student avoidance of paternalistic style of interview
