Medication Histories Assessment Guide

The purpose of this document is to help facilitators run the Medication Histories Assessments.

**Medication History Assessments:**

* *Purpose:*
  + To assess a student’s ability to gather information from patients regarding a medication history
* *Equipment requirements*
  + Personnel to play the part of the patients and assess the students
  + Patient Scenarios
  + Paper & Pen
  + Assessment Rubrics
  + Med History Forms
* *Lab Setup*
  + Students assigned or allowed to choose an assessment time depending on logistics
  + Personnel playing the part of the patient should be given their scenario in advance so that they can study the part of the patient they are playing and familiarize themselves with the assessment rubric
  + The assessment venue should be set up to allow for a quiet space for the interview/assessment to take place
  + Copies of the student version of the scenario should be available to the students when they arrive for their appointment for their review.
* *Assessment organization*
  + Students will be assigned a scenario randomly. (3 case scenarios are provided)
  + The student will have ~10 minutes to review the scenario prior to entering the room
  + Upon entering the room the students will have 15 minutes to complete the interview.
  + Post interview the patient/assessor should give feedback to the student. Including if the student did not or did pass the assessment.
  + Post feedback the student will have time to rewrite their medication history form. This will be turned in with the assessment rubric.
  + Students who do not pass the assessment must be assigned or sign up for another assessment.
* *Tips for instructors*
  + If you are using the provided Medication History form make sure these are face up on the table in the interview room. Since there are specific questions that prompt the students only on the front side we find that it is a disadvantage if they are turned face down.
  + We made a version of the rubric on Qualtrics for the assessors to use. This could be done with any number of systems. This will help automating the assessment documentation process.
