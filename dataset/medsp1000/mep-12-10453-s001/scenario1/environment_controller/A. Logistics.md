**Appendix A: Logistics**

1. Location of activity
   1. Simulation Center
2. Setting for the encounter
   1. Emergency Department
3. Materials required
   1. Two 3x5 index cards
      1. One with description of maculopapular rash
      2. One with description of lymphadenopathy in cervical and axial lymph nodes
   2. Student instructions with patient vital signs
   3. Hospital gown
4. Number of encounters completed by each learner
   1. For this case, each student completes one 15-minute SP encounter, one 15 minute SOAP note, and one 3-minute encounter at the closing of the exercise in which the SP provided feedback.
5. Length of each encounter
   1. Following the 15 minutes with the SP, students had an additional 15 minutes to complete a standard SOAP note.
   2. A 3-minute SP-student feedback encounter followed the student’s written SOAP note. This brief encounter allowed the SP to provide generalized feedback to students regarding their performance.
6. Start and finish time
   1. 35 minutes per student
7. Staffing requirement
   1. One standardized patient
   2. One “time keeper”
   3. Faculty member(s) to grade students’ SOAP notes and review audio post-encounter
8. Briefing or orientation materials used
    1. Student instructions for part 1 and part 2 of this SP case can be found as an attachment.
9. Describe feedback and or debriefing methods used
    1. The SP provided generalized feedback to students following each encounter.
