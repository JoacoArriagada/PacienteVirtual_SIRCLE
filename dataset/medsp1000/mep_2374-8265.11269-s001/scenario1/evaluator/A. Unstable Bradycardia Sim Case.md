**Ideal Scenario Flow**

*One learner enters the room to assess the patient. After recognizing that patient is unresponsive and hypotensive, the learner checks for a pulse and recognizes bradycardia. The learner will name the rhythm “unstable bradycardia” and call for the help of other learners and a crash cart. Upon the arrival of the rest of the team, the first participant will inform other learners of the situation using the SBAR format and distinguish themselves as the team leader. Other learners will define their roles before proceeding with patient care. The team leader will instruct the team to prepare for Atropine administration for treatment of unstable bradycardia while also placing defibrillator pads and limb leads on the patient. The team will administer 1 mg of Atropine IV and increase the patient’s oxygen to 100%. The team will then summarize the situation and ask for the recycling of blood pressure. It will then be determined that the patient’s condition has not improved with Atropine, and unstable bradycardia persists. The team will then state their plan to proceed with transcutaneous pacing. The team leader will instruct the person operating the defibrillator to put it into manual mode and set the rate to 80 bpm. The current is then increased until capture is achieved. The team will confirm the patient has a pulse with capture. Blood pressure is recycled with vitals found to be HR 80 bpm, BP 105/65, and O2 sat 96%. The team leader will summarize the situation for the team and instruct a team member to consult cardiology. EKG and labs (including electrolytes and cardiac enzymes) will be obtained before a patient disposition is discussed.*

**Anticipated Management Mistakes**

1. *Not evaluating patient for responsiveness and pulse: The first learner to enter the room must assess the hypotensive patient for responsiveness and pulse. Failure to do so will require a pause of the case to allow for coaching of the learner. The case will then start from the beginning.*
2. *Failure to define roles and use closed loop communication: Clearly defined roles and closed loop communication will be assessed by instructors. Failure by learners to display these behaviors will lead to a pause in the simulation for micro skill improvement.*
3. *Incorrect dosage of Atropine: Many learners may not have experience with this situation and need to be taught correct dosage of Atropine.*
4. *Failure to use defibrillator in manual mode: Learners that have not used a defibrillator may require instruction in this area.*
5. *Not placing leads on patient properly: Learners should receive additional instruction in this area if instructors feel the necessity.*
