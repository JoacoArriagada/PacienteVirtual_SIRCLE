1. Title: Multiple-patient Simulation Scenarios
2. Target Audience: Upper-level emergency medicine residents (categorical third or fourth year emergency medicine [EM] residents or fourth/fifth year EM-pediatric residents or EM internal medicine residents.)
3. Learning Objectives:

**Global Objectives** (for entire curriculum):

1. Employ task switching in an efficient and timely manner in order to manage the emergency department (PC8)
2. Manage resources including other personnel in order to provide optimal patient care (ICS2, SBP2)

**Case-specific Objectives**:
*Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis (SCFE)*: 1) Formulate a differential diagnosis for pediatric limp (PC4); 2) Demonstrate professional and empathic communication during a challenging interaction with an angry family member (ICS1)

*ST-Segment Elevation Myocardial Infarction (STEMI)/Ventricular-tachycardia (V-tach)*: 1) Resuscitate a patient in ventricular fibrillation (PC1); 2) Properly manage a difficult consultant interaction, acting in the patient’s best interest (ICS2, PROF1)

*Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (CAH)*: 1) Resuscitate a hypotensive pediatric patient (PC1); 2) Formulate a differential diagnosis for refractory hypotension in the pediatric patient (PC4)

**Critical Actions**
SCFE 1. Focused brief history and exam

2. Orders appropriate images and interprets properly

3. Updates patient and mom, and discusses disposition

STEMI/V-tach 1. Defibrillates patient

2. Intubates patient

3. Transfers for catheterization rather than giving tPA

CAH 1. Resuscitate child appropriately with intravenous fluids

2. Undresses child

3. Gives steroids
