1. Title: Multiple-patient Simulation Scenarios
2. Target Audience: Upper-level emergency medicine residents (categorical third or fourth year emergency medicine [EM] residents or fourth/fifth year EM-pediatric residents or EM internal medicine residents.)
3. Learning Objectives:

**Global Objectives** (for entire curriculum):

1. Employ task switching in an efficient and timely manner in order to manage the emergency department (PC8)
2. Manage resources including other personnel in order to provide optimal patient care (ICS2, SBP2)

**Case-specific Objectives**:

*Trauma Transfer:* 1) Perform a thorough and efficient primary and secondary trauma examination (PC2); 2) Properly interpret plan film radiographs (PC3)

*Snake Bite*: 1) Demonstrate medical knowledge of snakebite treatment (MK, PC5); 2) Communicate information in an efficiency manner to consultants (ISC2); 3) Resuscitate a patient with severe anaphylaxis (PC1)

*Shoulder Dystocia*: 1) Perform a vaginal delivery in patient with shoulder dystocia (PC9); 2) Institute a plan for successfully transferring a patient to another hospital (SBP2)
**Critical Actions**

Trauma Transfer 1. Focused brief history, primary and secondary survey

2. Appropriate review and interpretation of images

3. Updates patient and discusses disposition

Snakebite 1. Gives antivenin

2. Recognizes and treats anaphylactic reaction

3. Admits patient to Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Shoulder Dystocia 1. Delivers child using appropriate maneuvers

2. Resuscitates neonate

3. Transfers mother and child
