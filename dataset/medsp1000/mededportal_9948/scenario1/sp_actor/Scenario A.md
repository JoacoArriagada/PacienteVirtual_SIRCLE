**Scenario A, Case 3 – Delivery of Shoulder Dystocia**
Initial Presentation of Patient

Admit Date: Today Hospital Day: 0

Name: Barbara Fastly Gender: Female

Age: 24 Race: African American

Weight: 172 Height: 5’8

History of Present Illness (HPI):

24 year old Gravida 1 Para 0 female at 40 weeks plus 1 day gestation presents with abdominal cramping and leaking of fluid from the vagina. Just prior to arrival, patient was showering when she had a sudden gush of fluid from her vagina. She subsequently began to experience severe abdominal pain. Pain described as a band-like cramping sensation, waxing and waning, with pain lasting 2 minutes with 1 minute between episodes. She has had some contractions during the pregnancy, but none this severe. She denies any vaginal bleeding and has continued to have fetal movement. En route, in the ambulance, she began to feel a “severe” pressure in her vaginal area. She states “I think my baby is coming”. She is scheduled for induction tomorrow.

She has not had any fever, vaginal discharge, urinary symptoms, nausea, vomiting, headaches blurry vision, extremity pain or swelling, or any other acute symptoms or concerns.

Past Medical History: gestational diabetes managed with diet only

Medications: Prenatal vitamin, docusate

Allergies: none
