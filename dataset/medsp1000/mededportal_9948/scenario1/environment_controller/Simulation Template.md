1. Environment:
* Lab set up – 3 adjoining rooms with easy behind-scenes control room movement for each scenario (A and B) being run at one time; alternatively could put more than one patient in a room or in hallways if rooms not available
* Distractors – New patient arrivals and changes in patient status built into scenarios (see attached case files)
1. Actors:
* 2 nurses per scenario; may be played by trainers or nursing students, but recommend using actual emergency department nurses
1. Moulage:
* Mannequins for the Trauma Transfer and Snakebite cases need moulage applied using stage makeup. The Trauma Transfer has simple scrapes and abrasions applied with the moulage. The Snakebite has moulage of two dark bite marks applied around a stocking on the upper extremity with red “blood” coming from the marks. A tourniquet is sitting above the wound. See attached picture of the snakebite mannequin.
