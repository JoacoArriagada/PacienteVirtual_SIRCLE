IX. Case Narrative (describes what the learner will experience)

A. Radio call from field medic alerts team that a patient is coming in 5 minutes. Patient is 15yr old male goat herder who stepped on an landmine while tending to goats. This occurred 3 hours ago and patient is being transported by pickup truck to the FST, but the medic just got in radio range. Both FST surgeons are in the village, meeting with village elders, so a driver is dispatched to bring them back. Field medic reports he applied a makeshift tourniquet to LLE.
