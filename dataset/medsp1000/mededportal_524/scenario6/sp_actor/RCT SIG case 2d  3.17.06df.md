I. Title: RCT case 2d neck and thoracoabdominal gun-shot wound (GSW) injuries (Multiple-patient scenario 4)

A. Multiple-patient scenario- It’s Ramadan and Intelligence has discovered the insurgency plans to commit a “Tet Offensive” style attack on all military bases simultaneously. The Division Surgeon has issued a FRAGO (fragmented order) to all Medical Units to be prepared for casualties, to conserve resources as much as possible and to obey the Geneva Convention with regards to EPW’s (Enemy Prisoner’s of War). The team was across the base getting extra supplies and when they return they discover the surgeon (the others are down at the Special Forces compound gathering more information through informal channels) has just started a laparotomy on a soldier who was hit with an AK-47 round which punched through his body armor (wasn’t wearing extra porcelain plates).

This patient arrives in a ground ambulance. Report from field medic: “This is a teenage Iraqi insurgent. He was an attacker who hurled himself at the defenses unsuccessfully. He got in close before being gunned down, and therefore we retrieved him from the open. He looks bad !”
