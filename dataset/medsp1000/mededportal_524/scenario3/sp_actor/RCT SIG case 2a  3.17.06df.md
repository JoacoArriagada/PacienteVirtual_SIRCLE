I. Title: RCT case 2a Rocket-Propelled Grenade (RPG) shrapnel thoracic injury (Multi-patient scenario 1)

A. Multiple-patient scenario- It’s Ramadan and Intelligence has discovered the insurgency plans to commit a “Tet Offensive” style attack on all military bases simultaneously. The Division Surgeon has issued a FRAGO (fragmented order) to all Medical Units to be prepared for casualties, to conserve resources as much as possible and to obey the Geneva Convention with regards to EPW’s (Enemy Prisoner’s of War). The team was across the base getting extra supplies, when they return they discover the surgeon has just started a laparotomy on a soldier who was hit with an AK-47 round which punched through his body armor (wasn’t wearing extra porcelain plates). The others are down at the Special Forces compound gathering more information through informal channels.

As the team arrives a radio call announces the arrival of 2 casualties, status unknown. Report from field medic: “24 y.o. male US soldier hit with shrapnel during an RPG attack on this base. He’s alert, in some discomfort but looking good. BP 110 / 70 HR 90 RR 20 SaO2 100%.”
