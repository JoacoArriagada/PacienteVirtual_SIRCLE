# Neonatal Resuscitation Simulation Scenarios

# Scenario #1:

Mother has been pushing for 6 hours. OB has failed vacuum extraction x 3. Prolonged fetal heart rate decelerations noted on monitor (in the 50’s for $> 5$ minute). Mother being taken for emergent C-section. . Prenatal history otherwise unremarkable. Clear fluids Baby is 39wks, EFW= 3kg

# Participant cues:

Physician- provide Epi at 1.5ml (1:10,000) via both ETT (0.5ml/kg) and IV (0.5ml/kg) Nurse- provide slow & shallow chest compressions using 2-finger technique

Initial state: ‘L1’ (see below) Clear fluid Term Cyanotic/Pale HR: 40 No respiratory effort Limp

# Scenarios Requires:

1. Initial steps   
2. Oxygen   
3. PPV   
4. Chest compressions   
5. $+ / -$ Intubation   
6. Umbilical catheter placement   
7. IV epinephrine

# Improvement:

Infant will remain in L1 unti IV Epi is given.   
Once IV Epi is given infant improves to ‘L3’ and scenario ends.

# SimNewB Level Chart:

Improve from L1 to L3 after IV Epi
