**Victor Billar, Acute Abdominal Pain– Instructor’s Guide**

**Resource**: Victor Billar, Acute Abdominal Pain

**Purpose of the resource**: To enrich the sources of validated case scenarios available to standardized patient trainers and medical school faculty for objective structured clinical examinations.

**Educational Objectives**:

* Establish positive working relationship with patient
* Use appropriate questioning and physical examination to assess the condition of a patient with acute abdominal pain

**Conceptual background**: This case was created for use with standardized patients, for teaching and/or evaluation purposes. The case is intended for teaching/examination of medical students, years 1 through 4 (depending on medical school’s type of curriculum), as well as residents and faculty development.

**Recommended length of session:** 15 minutes for learner in patient room, 10 minutes for patient note, 30 min for debriefing with faculty-observer (for teaching purposes only)

**Case has been successfully implemented with students at Paul Foster SOM.**
