# Assessment:

Ivan Topee is a previously healthy former full term 7mo male presenting with fever and vomiting. Given his urinalysis and urine culture results, most likely etiology is a urinary tract infection. However other causes of fever and vomiting include otitis media, pneumonia, early gastroenteritis or viral illness, though these are not supported by history or physical exam findings. With a urine culture growing gram negative rods, E. coli is a likely pathogen. Given this is Ivan’s first UTI, he will need further imaging to rule out anatomic abnormality and ascending infection to the kidneys.
