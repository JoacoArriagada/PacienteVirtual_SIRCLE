HPI: Cee Peyare is a 3 week old, former full term infant who presented after Mom noticed that he seemed to stop breathing and his lips turned blue. His lips were blue, so Mom gave him 4 rescue breaths and he started breathing again. He did not have any shaking movements of his arms or legs but he did seem a little limp while she was holding him. She called EMS. When they arrived, his oxygen saturation was reportedly $93 \%$ on room air. He otherwise looked like his normal self. Mom requested EMS bring them to the emergency room for evaluation. He has not had any fevers or cold symptoms. He has been eating well, taking his normal 3-4 ounces every 3- 4 hours. There has been no difficulty breathing. He spits up with about half of his feeds and occasionally seems to choke but he has never done anything like this before. He does not sweat or seem like he has a hard time breathing when he takes his bottles. He has been growing and developing normally.

In the ED, he was noted to be afebrile, with O2 sats of $94 \%$ on RA, RR 60, HR 180, BP 75/45. Examination was remarkable only for tachycardia. Mother fed patient in ED and infant noted to become fussy during the feed with concern that he was not tolerating the feed and “arching his back.” Patient admitted to hospital medicine further management and observation due to concerns for ALTE.
