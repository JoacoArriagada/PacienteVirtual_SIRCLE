All Cases:
1) Obtains a focused history from the patient, family, EMS or referring health care providers.
2) Initiates monitoring as appropriate for each scenario including pulse oximetry, blood pressure, and cardiac monitoring.
3) Completes a focused physical exam.
4) Assesses patient’s oxygenation status and places patient on supplemental oxygen in response to low oxygen saturation.
5) Actively participates as a team member and is respectful of other team members and their input.
6) Lists findings indicative of respiratory failure (increased/decreased respiratory rate, worsening hypoxia, mottling, bradycardia, thready pulses, altered mental status).

Case 3 Specific:

1) Orders a chest radiograph to confirm abnormalities detected on exam.

2) Describes physical findings of a right sided pneumonia (including those found on the model [crackles and decreased breath sounds on the right] and those not found [fremitus, dullness to percussion and egophony]).

3) Describes the pathophysiology of aspiration pneumonia and how it relates to the findings on the chest radiograph.

4) Lists the appropriate pharmacologic agents used to treat aspiration pneumonia.

No allergies

Operator Note: The patient is oriented to person only (he thinks that he in his mother’s house and thinks that it is 1964). Additional information that is available when the nursing home is contacted: He has had a complicated rehabilitation course. He has a dense paralysis of the whole right side and has had particular difficulty swallowing. He had a prolonged episode of coughing the night before last when the nursing aid was feeding him his dinner. You will provide this information to the students as one of the nurses at the nursing home when they ask to contact the sending facility for additional information.

# Facilitator Notes:

START SCENARIO: The students will have approximately 5 minutes to complete their history and exam of the mannequin. If they only try to get information from the patient and don’t ask for any additional sources of information, then ask them who else might have the information they need. When they ask to contact the nursing home, the operator will come on the loud speaker (speaker telephone) playing the role of one of the nursing home nurses. If the student is not obtaining a good focused history, then stop him/her after approximately 2 minutes, ask him/her to summarize and you complete a brief focused history over the next 1-2 minutes. Next direct the students to listen to the breath sounds (point out the marked areas where the sound will be best heard). Ask the students performing the exam to describe their findings so that the students in the classroom will have all the same information as those in the lab. If they do not detect the rales on the right side, then have them reexamine the mannequin and point out the difference if necessary.

PAUSE SCENARIO: Ask the students in the lab: What is the most likely cause of the patient’s condition? (Answer - Aspiration pneumonia). Guide a brief student discussion to help them discuss the differential diagnosis of a patient with hypoxia, altered mental status and fever (e.g. viral pneumonia, bacterial pneumonia, pulmonary embolus, ARDS, abscess with airway obstruction) and the pathophysiology which suggests an aspiration pneumonia.

RESUME SCENARIO: Prompt students to order a CXR if they haven’t done so. Discuss how the film findings correlate with the pathophysiology of aspiration pneumonia and the findings on physical exam. (The views show shadowing in the right lung, radiating from the right hilum and consistent with a right lower/middle lobe infiltrate. This correlates with rales heard on auscultating the right lung with normal breath sounds on the left).

PAUSE SCENARIO: Question 2: What is the most appropriate pharmacologic treatment for this patient? (Answer – A broad spectrum antibiotic combination that includes coverage of anaerobes) Guide a brief student discussion to help them evaluate the special conditions (aspiration pneumonia [providing a mix of aerobes and anaerobes] and an infection acquired in a healthcare facility [with increased risk for resistant organisms]). Piperacillin-tazobactam or ticarcillin-clavulanate would cover the aspiration concerns. The same agents would also cover resistant strains acquired in a healthcare facility with the addition of an aminoglycoside or ciprofloxacin.

RESUME SCENARIO: The patient has a coughing paroxysm and then starts to deteriorate with increasing pulse, decreasing BP, worsening tachypnea, and becomes completely unresponsive. Have each student assess one of the vital signs and the information on the monitor during this episode.

PAUSE SCENARIO: Ask each student to describe what he/she observed and then have them answer Question 3: What is your next step if the patient continues this course of deterioration? (Answer - Control the airway) Guide a brief student discussion to help them arrive at the answer if not offered initially. Also discuss the issue of advanced directives if not previously brought up during the case.

RESUME SCENARIO: The patient worsens. He loses consciousness, and stops breathing. Have two students perform 2-person bag-mask ventilation while you prepare to intubate. Demonstrate the airway anatomy with the fiberoptic scope while placing an endotracheal tube. Instruct one student on proper bagging technique (rate on 12/minute and tidal volume of about $\%$ an adult bag calibrated by chest rise). After completing intubation, instruct another student on how to connect the bag to a high flow oxygen source, talk another student through the steps involved in securing the tube with the commercial tube holder, and direct any additional students to observe the monitor for changes while these actions are occurring. Once all the tasks are completed, each can briefly share what changes he/she saw.

Next direct the students to answer Question 4: What is the most like cause of this patient’s hypoxia? (Answer - Aspirated fluid disrupts surfactant and initiates an inflammatory response leading to V/Q mismatch). Guide a brief student discussion to help them arrive at the answer if not offered initially.
