# Case 1 - Pneumothorax Tension Pneumothorax Case Frank Guest

Presenting Complaint: 18 YO tall thin male presented to triage with a chief complaint of left sided chest pain and some shortness of breath. Pain started suddenly while sitting and watching a football game. No history of previous episodes.

Vital Signs: P 105 BP 120/78 T 98.4 R 26 Pulse Ox $9 7 \%$ on Room Air

Operator Note: This is a case of a spontaneous pneumothorax. The patient’s answers to all questions regarding past medical history will be negative. Responses regarding the HPI will reflect the prototypical spontaneous pneumothorax including acute onset of sharp, pleuritic, left sided chest pain with no acute precipitating cause.

No medications No allergies
