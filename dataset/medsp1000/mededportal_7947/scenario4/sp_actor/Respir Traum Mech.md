# Appendix D

# Case 4 - Upper Airway Obstruction Sam Hall

Presenting complaint: 45 YO man presents to triage with a chief complaint of difficulty breathing which started about 30 minutes prior to arrival and has been getting progressively worse.

Vital Signs: P 114, BP 136/84, R 42, T97.6, Pulse ox is $9 6 \%$ on 2 L/min nasal oxygen

Operator Note: The patient will have stridorous breathing on presentation, but will be able to talk in short phrases to answer student questions. If asked, he has a history of a previous episode like this about 10 year earlier due to an allergic reaction to nuts. HPI information: About 10 minutes after completing a big dinner at a friend’s house, he started feeling a sensation of swelling in his mouth and throat with rapidly progressive difficulty breathing.
