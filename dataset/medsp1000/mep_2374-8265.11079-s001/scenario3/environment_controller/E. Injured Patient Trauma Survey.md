**Case 2:** Possible C-Spine Injury

*EMS phone rings*: “This is Rescue 2 coming in with an 18-year-old intoxicated patient. (S)He was at a party and fell down the stairs.”

If requested: Patient boarded and collared. Intoxicated and difficult to examine. No obvious injuries.

If requested: Vitals: 108 RR 14 BP 128 / 86 SpO2 99% RA

Vitals: HR 108 RR 14 BP 128 / 86 SpO2 99% RA

Primary survey findings:

* Airway: intoxicated, slurring speech.
* Breathing: bilateral breath sounds.
* Circulation: 2+ femoral pulses. Heart rate and blood pressure within normal limits for age.
* Disability: combative, uncooperative, intoxicated. GCS 14 for confused conversation.

Secondary survey findings:

* Head: atraumatic without tenderness.
* Face: bruise to right cheek, tenderness to palpation.
* Eyes: pupils equal, round and reactive to light – 4mm to 2mm bilaterally.
* Ears: no hemotympanum bilaterally.
* Nose: no deformities, epistaxis or nasal septal hematoma.
* Mouth: no bleeding or malocclusion. Teeth intact.
* Neck: trachea midline. C spine tenderness to palpation at C3-6.
* Chest: symmetric chest rise, no clavicular deformity or tenderness, no crepitus, no tenderness to palpation.
* Abdomen: no bruising, ecchymosis, seatbelt sign; no tenderness.
* Pelvis: pelvis stable, no blood at the meatus, no perineal bruising or lacerations.
* Back: no bruising, nontender without step-offs. Good gluteal tone.
* Extremities: no bruising, lacerations, deformities; no tenderness to upper and lower extremities. Strength 5/5 in upper and lower extremities bilaterally; sensation 5/5 in upper and lower extremities bilaterally. Full range of motion in all extremities.
