**Case 3**: Traumatic Pneumothorax

*EMS phone rings*: “This is Rescue 4 coming in with a 17-year-old restrained driver in an MVC - car vs pole.  Extensive damage to the car, + airbags deployed. Patient was moaning on scene, non-ambulatory. We’ll be there in 3 minutes.”

Patient is boarded and collared. (S)He is not opening his eyes or moving spontaneously, mumbling incoherently.

Vitals: HR 135 RR 25 BP 80 /42 SpO2 86% RA

Initial presentation:

* Unresponsive.
* Trachea deviated to left.
* Splinting with breathing.
* No breath sounds on the right.

After needle decompression:

* Opening eyes spontaneously, confused but able to answer questions, obeys commands to move extremities.
* Bruise to right chest wall and needle from needle decompression.
* Trachea midline.
* Needle in anterior chest wall.
* Repeat vitals HR 110, BP 98/58, RR 22, O2 95%.

Secondary survey findings:

* Atraumatic without tenderness.
* No trauma, no tenderness, no bruises.
* Pupils equal, round and reactive to light – 4mm to 2mm bilaterally.
* No hemotympanum bilaterally.
* No deformities, epistaxis or nasal septal hematoma.
* No bleeding or malocclusion. Teeth intact.
* No midline C-spine tenderness or step-offs.
* Symmetric chest rise, no clavicular deformity or tenderness, no crepitus, no tenderness to palpation.
* No bruising, ecchymosis, seatbelt sign; no tenderness.
* Pelvis stable, no blood at the meatus, no perineal bruising or lacerations.
* No bruising, nontender without step-offs. Good gluteal tone.
* No bruising, lacerations, deformities; no tenderness to upper and lower extremities.
* Strength 5/5 in upper and lower extremities bilaterally; sensation 5/5 in upper and lower extremities bilaterally.
* Full range of motion in all extremities.
