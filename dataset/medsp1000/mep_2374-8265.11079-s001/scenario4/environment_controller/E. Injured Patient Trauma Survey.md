**Case 3**: Traumatic Pneumothorax

*EMS phone rings*: “This is Rescue 4 coming in with a 17-year-old restrained driver in an MVC - car vs pole.  Extensive damage to the car, + airbags deployed. Patient was moaning on scene, non-ambulatory. We’ll be there in 3 minutes.”

If requested: Patient is boarded and collared. (S)He is not opening his eyes or moving spontaneously, mumbling incoherently.

If requested: Vitals: HR 135 RR 25 BP 80 /42 SpO2 86% RA

Initial primary survey findings:

* Airway: no response. No obstruction.
* Breathing: trachea deviated to left, splinting with breathing, no breath sounds on the right.

Case progression:

* If needle decompression is required, explain that appropriate needle decompression is performed.
* State need for chest tube and that trauma surgery is preparing for that procedure.
* If the participants do not stop their primary survey to perform needle decompression, stop the case for feedback and discussion.

After needle decompression:

* Repeat vitals HR 110, BP 98/58, RR 22, O2 95%.
* Disability: opening eyes spontaneously, confused but able to answer questions, obeys commands to move extremities. GCS 14.
* Exposure: bruise to right chest wall and needle from needle decompression.

Secondary survey findings:

* Head: atraumatic without tenderness.
* Face: no trauma, no tenderness, no bruises.
* Eyes: pupils equal, round and reactive to light – 4mm to 2mm bilaterally.
* Ears: no hemotympanum bilaterally.
* Nose: no deformities, epistaxis or nasal septal hematoma.
* Mouth: no bleeding or malocclusion. Teeth intact.
* Neck: trachea midline. No midline C-spine tenderness or step-offs.
* Chest: needle in anterior chest wall. Symmetric chest rise, no clavicular deformity or tenderness, no crepitus, no tenderness to palpation.
* Abdomen: no bruising, ecchymosis, seatbelt sign; no tenderness.
* Pelvis: pelvis stable, no blood at the meatus, no perineal bruising or lacerations.
* Back: no bruising, nontender without step-offs. Good gluteal tone.
* Extremities: no bruising, lacerations, deformities; no tenderness to upper and lower extremities. Strength 5/5 in upper and lower extremities bilaterally; sensation 5/5 in upper and lower extremities bilaterally. Full range of motion in all extremities.
