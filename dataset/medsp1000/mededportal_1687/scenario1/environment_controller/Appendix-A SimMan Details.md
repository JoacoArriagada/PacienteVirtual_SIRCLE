# Appendix A – SimMan details

SimMan® (Laerdal Corporation, Stockholm, Sweden; Laerdal Medical Corporation, Wappingers Falls, NY, USA):

SimMan is a portable and advanced patient simulator for team training with realistic anatomy and clinical functionality. With SimMan, educators are able to test students’ clinical and decision-making skills during realistic patient care scenarios tailored through the use of software and interactive technology. SimMan also provides highly realistic patient simulation for the practice of teamwork, leadership and communication skills.

Interactive manikin that gives immediate feedback to interventions. Airway with anatomical landmarks allowing the learner to acquire the necessary skills for accurate placement of airway management devices such as Endotracheal Tubes, Laryngeal Mask Airways and CombiTubes.   
Airway system allows accurate simulation of most relevant difficult airway management scenarios.   
Needle and Surgical Cricothyrotomy. Spontaneous breathing. Adjustable lung resistance to simulate rales, crackles, wheezes, etc.   
Pre-programmed library of heart-lung, bowel and vocal sounds with adjustable sound volume.   
Blood pressure may be taken automatically, palpated or auscultated; Korotkoff sounds are synchronized with pulse.   
IV training arm allows practice of peripheral intravenous therapy.

# SimMan Software:

The software and user interface allow for easy adjustment and management of all patient parameters during both simple and comprehensive training scenarios. An improved scenario editor program allows the educator to build advanced scenarios, allowing multiple alternatives and predefined changes to the vital signs. By using Handlers and Trends the educator can introduce multiple physiological and pharmacological changes into the simulation. Learners can experience the trends in vital signs and physical exam findings (previously programmed or spontaneously introduced) in response to the interventions instituted. Simulated patient monitor can provide snap shot of x-ray, 12-lead ECG and trends. SimMan software comes with pre-programmed scenarios; instructors can also design and save their own patient cases. Event handlers allow user to create automatic responses to actions performed by students. Trend curves on instructor’s panel control how the physiological parameters will change over time. Multiple trends can be run simultaneously with their effects added together.
