**The Basics of Health Care for Incarcerated Patients**

**Case presentation of RM**

RM is a 30-year-old male who is admitted to the hospital after an assault at the local county jail. He has several facial injuries that require surgical intervention. He is shackled to the bed with one wrist and one leg. Two correction officers are present. RM would like to tell you something in private without the correction officers overhearing him. He also requests that his shackles are removed so he can be more comfortable. He asks what would happen to him if he was to not wake up after the surgery and could not make his own medical decisions.
