**Distribute case text "Session 3, Part B"**

Summary: Taylor has missed several appointments and has lost weight.

**Discussion topics**

- Non adherence
- Family issues/challenging families
- Involving schools
- Team communication

**Potential probe questions**

- *How do you address missed appointments?*
- *What are the implications of missed appointments for providers of various disciplines and how do different disciplines handle this? How are missed appointments communicated to other members of the care team, if at all?*
- *If the outpatient does not appear to be working, what are your other options?*
- *What are you next steps?*

**Key points for discussion**

*Missed appointment policies may differ by institution, discipline, and by age of patient; learners should be aware of how missed appointments are addressed and documented within their institution. Clinicians working with individuals with eating disorders should be aware that patient may miss/cancel appointments when their symptoms are worsening to avoid potential consequences (e.g.  getting an exercise/activity restriction or being referred to a higher level of care) or because they are embarrassed that they are struggling. Because a Taylor is losing weight, a change in her current treatment plan or a higher level of care may be warranted*.
