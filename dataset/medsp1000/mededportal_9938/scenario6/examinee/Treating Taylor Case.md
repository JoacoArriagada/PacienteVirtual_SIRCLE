**Case Resource “Treating Taylor: Multidisciplinary management of eating disorders”**

<u>**Session 4, Part B**</u>      

Mom and Dad have pulled together and have presented a united front in re-feeding Taylor. She has consistently restored weight each week. Mom, Dad, Taylor, and sister Jenny attend a family meeting. When you are alone with Taylor at the scale, she admits that she has been having urges to cut. In the meeting, the family reports that Taylor has become more obsessive, is engaging in rituals around homework and eating, is more controlling about the order of her belongings, and seems more anxious.
