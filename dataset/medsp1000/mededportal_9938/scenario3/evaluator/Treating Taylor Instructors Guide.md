**Distribute case text "Session 3, Part A"**

Summary: Taylor has completed a residential and intensive outpatient program. She has gained weight, but has not had a period.

**Discussion topics**

- Amenorrhea
- Bone health
- DXA
- Weight goals
- Growth issues

**Potential probe questions**

- *What medical concerns do you have?*
- *What are your next steps?*
- *How do you set up a team for her?*
- *What role does the school play?*
- *Do you even let the school know?* 
- *What information can you share?*

**Key points for discussion**

*Menstrual functioning is an important aspect of recovery for young women struggling with eating disorders. It may take a number of months at a healthy weight to regain menses. The paper by Golden provides a good reference for discussion. Taylor may need to increase her weight close to where she was before losing weight to regain her menses. Her height should be followed closely.* 

*Medical concerns include bone loss from amenorrhea and possible other causes of amenorrhea such as elevated prolactin, thyroid disease, or ovarian insufficiency. Bone loss can occur as early as 6 months to 1 year of amenorrhea. DXA bone measurements should be considered at a year of amenorrhea.* 

*Setting up a team is an essential part of treatment. Establishing communication between team members is paramount. Signed releases need to be obtained for communication. School notification may differ from patient to patient but discussion of Taylor’s case should include elements about confidentially from various disciplines and what should or should not be shared by various team members and the school.*
