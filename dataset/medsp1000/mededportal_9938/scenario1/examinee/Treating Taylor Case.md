**Case Resource “Treating Taylor: Multidisciplinary management of eating disorders”**

<u>**Session 1, Part A**</u>

Taylor is a 13 year old with weight loss over the past 7-8 months.  Taylor presented to her pediatrician 3 months ago with complaints of intermittent abdominal pain, but Mom was more concerned about Taylor's recent weight loss.  Taylor was referred to the outpatient eating disorder program because Mom states that Taylor picks at all food and exercises too much.  Taylor has never seen a therapist or nutritionist.

Taylor reports abdominal pain for the past year--pain in around the belly button that could happen any time of the day; denies vomiting.  Taylor feels nauseous, especially in the morning, so never eats breakfast.
