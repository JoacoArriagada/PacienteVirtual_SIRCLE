**Instructors Guide to “Treating Taylor: Multidisciplinary management of eating disorders”**

<u>**Instructor Summary: Session 4**</u>

**Distribute case text "Session 4, Part A"**

Summary: Taylor and her family have started family-based treatment. After initial success, her weight plateaus. Taylor would like to start exercising. Taylor learns her mother once had an eating disorder.

**Discussion topics**

- Tenets of family-based treatment
- Refeeding
- Exercising during treatment
- Exercise as an eating disorder behavior
- Limit setting
- Genetics/Inheritablity of EDs

**Potential probe questions**

- How much should the family be re-feeding her?
- What needs to happen in this family after this meeting?
- Should she be exercising?
- What are the medical issues of exercising?
- What is the heritability of EDs?

***Key points for discussion***

*When discussing refeeding Taylor using Family Based Treatment (FBT), refer back to Harriet’s Brown’s article which discusses using high calorie foods to restore weight.  It is recommended that parents eventually feed their child up to 3000 calories a day. For further guidance, refer to the work of Lock, Le Grange, and Russell (Treatment Manual for Anorexia Nervosa, Second Edition: A Family-Based Approach). Learners can also access the following FBT-related articles:*

- *Katzman DK, Peebles R, Sawyer SM, Lock J, Le Grange D. The role of the pediatrician in family-based treatment for adolescent eating disorders: opportunities and challenges. J Adolescent Health. 2013; 53(4):433-440.*
- *Lock J.  Treatment of adolescent eating disorders: progress and challenges.  Minerva Psichiatrica. 2010; 51 (3): 207-216*
- *Lock J, LeGrange D.  Family-based treatment of eating disorders.  International Journal of Eating Disorders. 2005; 37: S64-S67.*

*Parents are often eager to research FBT to further understand the treatment and to gain extra support.  These websites may prove helpful for some parents and can be accessed during the session:* 

- [*http://www.maudsleyparents.org/*](http://www.maudsleyparents.org/)*: The Maudsley Parents website offers support and recipes that are helpful for parents who are struggling with refeeding.*  
- [*https://feast-ed.site-ym.com/*](https://feast-ed.site-ym.com/)*: This is a non-profit organization that provides information regarding research and treatment.  The goal of this organization is to empower families and provide tools and information useful for recovery.*

*In FBT, it is not recommended that patients exercise until they are weight restored to 90% median body weight. This is a topic that often generates strong and conflicting opinions.  Refer to the Mond reading for background on excessive exercise:*

- *Mond JM, Calogero RM. Excessive exercise in eating disorder patients and in healthy women. Aust NZ J Psychiatry. 2009; 43(3):227-34.* 

*Genetic factors play an important role in eating disorders. Findings from twin studies report heritability estimates of 40% to 60% of liability to anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, and binge eating disorder. Participants who want additional information on the heritability of EDs can be referred to:*

- *Scherag S, Hebebrand J, Hinney A. Eating disorders: the current status of molecular genetic research. Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry. 2010; 19:211-226.* 
- *Trace SE, Baker JH, Peñas-Lledó E, Bulik CM. The genetics of eating disorders. Annu Rev Clin Psychol. 2013;9:589-620.*
