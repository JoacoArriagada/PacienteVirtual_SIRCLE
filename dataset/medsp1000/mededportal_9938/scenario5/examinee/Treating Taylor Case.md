**Case Resource “Treating Taylor: Multidisciplinary management of eating disorders”**

<u>**Session 4, Part A**</u>                                                                

One month has passed and the family has started Family Based Treatment (FBT). The FBT therapist has instructed the family to feed Taylor whatever they think is an appropriate amount of food for her to restore weight. The family is initially very motivated to help Taylor, but after two promising meetings with the family, Taylor’s weight plateaus.

At the next family meeting with Taylor, with both parents in attendance, the family is told that Taylor needs more food and must take a leave from the track team. Taylor becomes very upset and starts to cry. Dad moves to comfort Taylor, Mom disagrees with Dad about running, Dad references Mom's past exercise during recovery, and the discussion turns into conflict about Mom's past eating disorder and Taylor's current treatment.
