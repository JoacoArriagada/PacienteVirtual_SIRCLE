**Case Resource “Treating Taylor: Multidisciplinary management of eating disorders”**

<u>**Session 2**</u>

Taylor was diagnosed with Anorexia Nervosa and the team recommendation was for her to start working with an outside therapist and continue with medical checks and nutrition counseling.  Mom is doing her best to refeed Taylor, but there is a lot of arguing at home. Dad travels often and Mom is often on her own. During your time alone with Taylor at the beginning of the visit, you notice that she has cuts on her left mid arm that are covered by her shirt. She panics when you see these and asks you not to tell her mom.
