**Instructors Guide to “Treating Taylor: Multidisciplinary management of eating disorders”**

<u>**Instructor Summary: Session 2**</u>

Summary: Taylor is diagnosed with Anorexia Nervosa and enters outpatient treatment. At her appointment, her health care provider discovers that she has started self-injuring by cutting.

<table><tr><td><p><strong><em>Script: Suicide Assessment of Taylor</em></strong></p><p><em>Therapist: Taylor, we briefly talked about your self-injury.  People cut for many reasons.  You stated that you cut with your razor because you felt bad about eating.</em></p><p><em>Taylor: Yes, I felt like I had to punish myself so I cut.  </em></p><p><em>Therapist: Have you ever cut before for any other reason?</em></p><p><em>Taylor: No, I cut because I can’t exercise and I feel bad that I ate without being able to do something to fix it.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: So the cutting fixed it?</em></p><p><em>Taylor: (smiling): No, not really, but it made sense in the moment.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: Yes I can understand that.  We have to figure out other ways that you can manage negative feelings without cutting.  It seems for you that your self-injury was used to manage your eating disordered feelings.  Some people cut for other reasons.  Have you ever felt so bad that you felt like cutting to really hurt yourself?</em></p><p><em>Taylor: What do you mean?<br/><br/>Therapist: Well, some people cut because they feel suicidal. </em></p><p><em>Taylor: I wasn’t thinking about that.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: Well, have you ever felt like...hmm, if I don’t wake up tomorrow that would be ok? (Assessing passive suicidal ideation – past and present)</em></p><p><em>Taylor: Maybe once or twice when I felt hopeless like I will never recover from my eating disorder.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: When did you feel like that?</em></p><p><em>Taylor: Well when we first moved here. It was awhile ago.  I don’t feel that way now.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: What did you do when you felt that way?<br/></em></p><p><em>Taylor: Nothing, I stayed in my bed and didn’t go to school. </em></p><p><em>Therapist: Did you tell anyone?<br/></em></p><p><em>Taylor: No.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: If you ever feel that way again you should tell your parents so that they can get you the help that you need to be safe.  Have you ever felt like you wanted to die?<br/></em></p><p><em>Taylor: Not really.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: Can you say more about that?  Have you ever thought...maybe suicide is a fall back plan in case…? Did you ever have a plan?<br/></em></p><p><em>Taylor: No plan…I just don’t want to live like this forever. </em></p><p><em>Therapist: I understand that you feel really bad.  Did you ever have the intention of hurting yourself so that you could die?</em></p><p><em><br/>Taylor: No.  I just felt like I had to do something in the moment. I don’t always feel this way, I never felt like this before last year.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: If you were feeling that way in the moment, do you have anything in the house that you would use?<br/></em></p><p><em>Taylor: No, my parents lock up the medication and the only weapons in house are the kitchen knives and I would never do something like that...who would take care of my cats?  Plus, I wouldn’t do that to my family.  I never really wanted to die; I just don’t want to be fat.</em></p><p><em>Therapist: So you never had a plan to die and you only cut a few times in your life since the eating disorder?</em></p><p><em>Taylor: Yes</em></p><p><strong><em>End of Role Play</em></strong></p></td></tr></table>
