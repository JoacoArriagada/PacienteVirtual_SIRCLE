<u>**General Suggestions for Use**</u>

1. Students have 30 minutes to complete the encounter including the history and physical exam.  Students and standardized patients must speak Spanish during the entirety of the encounter.
2. Provide students with a ten minute warning prior to the end of the encounter.
3. Announce the end of the encounter and ask the students to step out of the exam rooms and wait in the hallway.
4. Once standardized patients complete the paperwork, they should invite the students back into the exam room and provide the students with verbal feedback.
5. After receiving feedback, instruct students to begin the computer portion of the exam.
6. Students have one hour to complete the computer portion of the exam.  Announce when there are fifteen minutes remaining and again when the computer portion of the exam is finished.
