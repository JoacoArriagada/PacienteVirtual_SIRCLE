**IPV Curriculum Role-Plays**

**Scenario 5**

You are seeing Mrs. Moore in primary care follow-up. She is a 40yo stay-at-home soccer mom with chronic abdominal pain, hypertension, and obesity. You have known her for a year and have concerns about her safety at home. Her husband accompanied her to her last visit and openly expressed degrading sentiments to her about her weight. Today she says she feels more down, depressed, and stressed than usual. She mentions she is in a rush to pick her kids up at school and make dinner before her husband gets home because otherwise she will never hear the end of it. After discussing increasing her blood pressure medications, you decide to screen her for IPV. Due to your high suspicion for abuse at home, you are quite persistent about trying to discuss this topic and offer your support to Mrs. Moore.
