**Appendix D**

**IPV Curriculum Role-Plays**

**Scenario 3**

Patient: Mr. Smith

You are a 25yo man with no medical history apart from migraines. You are meeting your new primary care doctor. You work as a financial analyst and are completing your MBA at the University of Maryland. You do not smoke and you drink about 3 beers socially each weekend. You are sexually active with your girlfriend of 1 year. The two of you are happy and you consider your relationship to be a healthy one.

You have just finished discussing your headaches. Next your doctor starts to ask more personal questions. When he/she asks about domestic violence, you are surprised and offended. You and your girlfriend would never do anything to hurt each other and you do not know why any one would think there would be violence in your relationship. You are a man and you can take care of yourself. You only came to talk to a doctor about your migraines and are not sure why violence has anything to do with your health.
