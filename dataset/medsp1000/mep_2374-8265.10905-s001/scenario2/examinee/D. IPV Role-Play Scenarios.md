**Appendix D**

**IPV Curriculum Role-Plays**

**Scenario 2**

You are meeting a new patient in your primary care clinic, Lilah, and her boyfriend, Michael, for the first time. Lilah is a 27 year old woman with polycystic ovarian syndrome, hypertension, and type 2 diabetes. Both she and her boyfriend are engineers working at Lockheed-Martin, and they appear cheerful and happy during the visit. The patient answers most of your questions, and occasionally Michael adds comments. After completing the ROS, you decide you want to have Michael step out of the room so you can screen for IPV.
