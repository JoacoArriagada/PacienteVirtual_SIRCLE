| SETTING: | Outpatient clinic |
| Opening Appearance: | Position at beginning of, and throughout, the encounter: * Lie down on the exam table, holding your head as if it hurts, and covering your eyes with your hands, arms, or a pillow, as if the light bothers your eyes. * Keep your neck in one position (stiff), pointed straight head; don't’ move it side to side or up and down much at all—act like you can’t do so. Affect: act tired/fatigued. - Every now and then, act confused and disoriented (e.g., you don’t know where you are or how you got here, to the clinic; say random, nonsensical things). |
