| Brief Narrative Description of Scenario                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | You are working in the pediatric emergency department when you receive a call from EMS stating there has been an explosion at a local gathering place.  You have been designated as a hospital triage officer to sort this influx of patients for priority medical treatment in the ED. Some of these patients have been triaged in the field, but it’s important to reassess the patient and perform a secondary triage designation upon their arrival in the ED. You have 10-15 minutes to triage all patients.                                                                                                                      |
