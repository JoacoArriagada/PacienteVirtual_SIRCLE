Appendix I: Simulation Room Orientation

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Elements to Cover** | **Demonstration Description** |
| Medical License for the Day | We are granting you a medical license for the day. In providing care to today’s patient, you are a licensed physician, a first-year intern. You can order tests and medications and should lead the care of this patient until your supervising physician arrives. |
| Communicating with patient | Your patient can talk and respond to you. Be sure to communicate with him as you provide care.  Faculty: “Hello Mr. Howard, how are you today?”  Patient: “I’m doing okay.”  Faculty: “Mr. Howard, I would like to listen to your lungs, and we can discuss your symptoms in more detail when I finish.”  Patient: “Sounds good to me.” |
| Airway    Heart Sounds  Listen to lungs  Breathing | Faculty: “I am going to lift your gown and listen to your breathing.”  Patient: “Okay.”  You can listen to your patient’s heart and lung sounds. You can hear if there are normal or abnormal sounds. You can also see chest rise.  Invite learners to listen to heart/lung sounds. |
| Circulation - Pulses  Medication Administration | Our patient has carotid, radial and femoral pulses. To feel them, press lightly. (If the manikin’s eyes blink, he has both left and right radial pulses. If his eyes do not blink, he only has a left radial pulse.) |
| Oxygen | The oxygen is behind the patient’s bed on the wall. You can turn it on here. All the O2 supplies are in the room. |
| Monitor | Your patient will be on the monitor.  If you want BP, place the cuff, and hit STAT on the monitor screen (demonstrate).  You can also place the O2 probe for oxygen saturation. |
| Positioning of patient | The bed can go up and down.  Faculty: “Mr. Howard, I am going to lower your head for comfort.”  Patient: “That is fine.” |
| Chart | There is a chart with some additional information on the computer. |
| Cognitive Aids | You will find some aids on the bedside table at the foot of the bed. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Hand hygiene | There are gloves on the wall. There is hand sanitizer by the door. Always but especially with COVID precautions, you should do hand hygiene as you would in the hospital – Use the sanitizer before and after providing care and wear gloves. |
| SP RN | During the case today, you have an RN as part of you team. [Introduce RN Standardized Participant (SP)]  She can assist in carrying out interventions you order. She can take a blood sugar upon your request. She can also call for any assistance you request or order other tests or medications.  She is here to assist you. She will not try to trick you. |
