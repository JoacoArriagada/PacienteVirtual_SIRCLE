Appendix J

Role Play Scenario

This scenario involves a young male cancer patient who is in ICU. The patient is no longer lucid and does not have an advanced directive.

The Care Coordinator has called a family meeting. The objective is to negotiate a transparent plan of care. She has requested that the physicians show up 15 minutes before the actual time for the family meeting so the team can prepare. However, the oncologist gets hung up in clinic and arrives at the family meeting 10 minutes late. The negotiation must go forward without the benefit of rehearsal.

The players.

**Medical ICU Hospitalist**: Just took over from another colleague at the start of the week. Since taking over, his patient has deteriorated. He believes the medical course is now irreversible. In the past, he has experienced periodic conflicts with the Oncologist.

**Oncologist**: From her point of view, the patient’s cancer can be treated by with aggressive chemotherapy if they could reverse the deterioration.

The **Family**: Patient is in long term relationship with male partner. He confided to partner when he received his diagnosis that he doesn’t want to linger. Parents only recently found out about the diagnosis and haven’t had time to process the extent of their son’s cancer.

**Bedside Nurse**: Back on duty after being off for a few days. He was shocked by the speed of patient’s worsening condition and has been working closely with the hospitalist on several interventions today.

**Care Coordinator**: Has gotten to know the patient and his significant other very well and understands the patient’s desire for a peaceful death. She has only recently been working with the parents to help them understand what’s going on. She notes that there is tension among the family members and some confusion about the options.
