| **Appendix B: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Case Template**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Pelvic Exam Simulation Case**  **AUTHOR: Rebecca Mazurkiewicz, MD, MPH** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: Clinical Chloe**  **PATIENT AGE: 25**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Vaginal discharge** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | This is a case of a 25-year-old female who is having vaginal discharge. The objective of this case is for participants to perform an accurate pelvic exam and to discuss the differential diagnosis and evaluation of vaginal discharge. |
| **Primary Learning Objectives** | Participants will correctly perform a pelvic exam.  Participants will demonstrate how to interact with patients professionally, in a way that respects patient privacy and modesty while limiting medical jargon.  Participants will become more comfortable performing pelvic exams.  Participants will become more confident in their ability to perform pelvic exams.  Participants will become more likely to perform pelvic exams in their future practice in residency and, potentially, beyond. |
| **Critical Actions** | 1. Participants will take a thorough history of a patient presenting with vaginal discharge. 2. Participants will correctly perform a pelvic exam. 3. Participants will interact with the patient professionally. 4. Participants will respect the patient’s modesty. 5. Participants will limit jargon when interacting with the patient. 6. Participants will formulate a differential diagnosis for vaginal discharge and apply it to the patient at hand to develop a personalized plan. |
| **Learner Preparation** | **None.** |
