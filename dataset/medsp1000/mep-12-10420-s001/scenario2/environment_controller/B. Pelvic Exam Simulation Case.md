| **HPI** | All parts of the history must be elicited by the group of participants.  Onset: The discharge began 5 days ago. Symptoms: She has experience vaginal itching but no pain, bleeding, fevers, chills, dysuria, nausea, vomiting, or dyspareunia.  Timing: Her symptoms have worsened over the past few days.
