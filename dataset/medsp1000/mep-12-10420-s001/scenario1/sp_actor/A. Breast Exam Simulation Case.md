| **Initial Presentation** | | | |
| **Actors and roles in the room at case start** | The facilitator speaks for and manually manipulates the simulator in response to participant request. The current participant is the physician and all other participants should wait their turn outside the room during direct observations. During history taking and plan discussion, the facilitator speaks for the simulator and the participants act as the physician collectively. | | |
| **HPI** | All parts of the history must be elicited by the group of participants.  Onset: She first noticed it 2 weeks ago while showering.  Precipitating Factors: There was no trauma, she is not currently menstruating.  Quality: The mass is in the right breast, lateral to the nipple, nontender, and nonpruritic.  Radiation: None.  Symptoms: She doesn’t have fever, chills, weight loss, night sweats, nipple discharge, or changes to the skin of the breasts in color or texture.  Timing: The mass hasn’t changed in size or other qualities with time. There is no obvious relation to menses. This has never happened before. | | |

She consistently uses condoms. She feels safe at home and her depression screen was negative. She does not perform breast self-exams. She last saw her gynecologist a year ago and was told “everything was normal.” | Omeprazole 20 mg orally daily | None | Her maternal grandmother was diagnosed with breast cancer diagnosed at age 70 and was treated successfully. |
