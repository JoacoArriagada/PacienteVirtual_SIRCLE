| **Appendix A: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Case Template**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Breast Exam Simulation Case**  **AUTHOR: Rebecca Mazurkiewicz, MD, MPH** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: Clinical Chloe**  **PATIENT AGE: 30**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Breast lump** | |
|  | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | This is a case of a 30-year-old female who discovers a painless breast lump while showering. The objective of this case is for participants to perform an accurate breast exam and to discuss the work up of a palpable breast mass. |
| **Primary Learning Objectives** | Participants will correctly perform a breast exam.  Participants will demonstrate how to interact with patients professionally, in a way that respects patient privacy and modesty while limiting medical jargon.  Participants will become more comfortable performing breast exams.  Participants will become more confident in their ability to perform breast exams.  Participants will become more likely to perform breast exams in their future practice in residency and, potentially, beyond. |
| **Critical Actions** | 1. Participants will take a thorough history of a patient presenting with a breast lump. 2. Participants will correctly perform a breast exam. 3. Participants will interact professionally with the patient. 4. Participants will respect the patient’s modesty. 5. Participants will limit jargon when interacting with the patient. 6. Participants will formulate a differential diagnosis for a breast mass and apply it to the patient at hand to develop a personalized plan. |
| **Learner Preparation** | **None.** |
