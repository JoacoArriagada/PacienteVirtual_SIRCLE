**SCENARIO 4: THE MEDICALLY COMPLEX CHILD WITH FEVER AND AGITATION**

**Facilitator Scenario Summary**

**Age:** 6 years

**Weight:** 20 kg

**Environment:** inpatient general pediatrics ward

**Complete Scenario Progression (for facilitator):**

*\*Participants will not have all of the information listed below, but the facilitator should reveal the background information as the learners ask for it*

Background Information: 6 year old male with history of TBI due to MVA (at one year of age) s/p ventriculoperitoneal shunt (VPS) placement with global developmental delay and severe oromotor dysfunction s/p gastrostomy tube (GT) placement, seizure disorder, tracheostomy-dependent with severe spasticity s/p intrathecal baclofen pump (ITBP) placement who is post-op from spinal instrumentation for scoliosis. He has done well in initial post-op course, but now presenting with fever and increasing agitation. On exam, noted to be febrile, tachycardic, and tachypneic with muscle rigidity and agitation. Ultimately found to have ITB withdrawal syndrome. A catheter leak was diagnosed by spiral CT scan, so patient started on IV diazepam in combination with high-dose oral baclofen, and symptoms improved. He was maintained on oral baclofen until 6 months post-op when catheter was revised.

**Resolution of the Clinical Scenario:** (read out loud to participants after the end of the simulation and prior to the debriefing portion)

ITB pump malfunction was considered, particularly given patient’s recent spinal surgery. Presentation of symptoms 2 days post-operative is appropriate given that baclofen withdrawal usually presents 12-96 hours after last dose. The patient was given enteral baclofen and IV benzodiazepines with resolution of symptoms. Patient was scheduled for catheter revision with the pediatric neurosurgeons.
