**Additional Information (provide only when asked):**

**Past Medical History:** Victim of MVA at 1 year of age (unrestrained passenger), resuscitated at the scene, initial hospital course complicated by severe TBI s/p craniotomy and VPS placement; during that hospitalization had tracheostomy placement due to failure to extubate; transferred to subacute care facility for continued management where found to have severe spasticity and referred for an ITBP, which was placed 1 year ago without complication. **Allergies:** NKDA

**Medications:** Levetiracetam, Lansoprazole, Albuterol, Budesonide, Baclofen (intrathecal), Cefazolin (post-op antibiotic)

**Family/Social History:** noncontributory

**Last 24 hours (provide only when asked):** Mom reports that over the last 24 hours, he seems more agitated with increased spasticity. At baseline he has 1-2 seizures per day that last for < 30 seconds, but in the last 24 hours he has had increasing seizure frequency. He has had persistent fever that is unresponsive to anti-pyretic medications.
