**Standardized Patient Encounter Checklist**

**History of Present Illness**

The student asked me:

1. if I ever take more than was prescribed?

2. if I ever think about stopping the medicine.

3. if I ever thought that I’m dependent on medication.

4. if I ever had any withdrawal symptoms

5. if I ever had drug cravings.

6. if I was aware of the risks and problems with my current medicines.

7. if I ever considered that an alternative treatment would be effective.

8. if I ever felt like ending my life OR hurting myself OR hurting others.

The student discussed:

9. with me my treatment goals.

10. with me my treatment options.

The student asked me:

11. if I would try alternative treatments.

12. about my willingness to pursue psychotherapy.

*(Questions 1-12 are assessed on a binary scale, with a point value of 1 for “done” and 0 for “not done”.)*

**Communication**

The student:

13. conducted the encounter in a warm and friendly manner.

14. treated me like I was on the same level, never "talking down" to me or treating me like a child.

16. showed interest in me as a person, not acting bored or ignoring what I had to say.

17. encouraged me to ask questions.

18. used easily understood words and explained any technical or medical concepts in plain language.

*(Questions 13-18 are assessed on a five-point Likert scale with 1 point for “excellent”, 0.8 points for “very good”, 0.6 points for “good”, 0.4 points for “fair”, and 0 points for “poor”.)*
