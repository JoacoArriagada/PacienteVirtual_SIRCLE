PROFESSIONALISM ISSUES OR CHALLENGES:

The student is faced with the challenge of managing the treatment plan for a patient who is requesting to continue prescriptions for substantial doses of narcotic medications. Though the patient has been taking these medications for a decade, there is no current indication for these medications. The patient sees nothing wrong with this mediation regimen, and feels it has been very helpful for their symptoms. The patient is resistant to changes and is pessimistic about the odds of another treatment plan being successful. The patient shows signs of a substance use disorder related to these medication, but does not recognize this themselves, and becomes defensive when this topic is broached. The student should recognize the inappropriate nature of the patient’s previous treatment plan and use good communication and interpersonal skills to help the patient gain insight into the problems with continued use of these medications. The student should avoid disparaging the previous doctor who prescribed these medications, or undermining the patient’s relationship with that doctor. The student should work collaboratively with the patient to develop an alternative, safe, and evidenced-based approach to treating their depression, while establishing and maintaining good therapeutic rapport.
