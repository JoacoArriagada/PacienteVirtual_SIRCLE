VI. Case Narrative (describes what the learner will experience)

A. Scenario Background Given to Participants-a patient chart, consisting of the usual documents as per individual institutional protocol will be available to the participants. The `patient` will be available for a preoperative interview by perioperative team members.

1. Chief Complaint:

a) 3 month history of right upper quadrant abdominal pain isolated U/S confirms multiple gallstones. (General surgery)

2. Past Medical History: previously healthy, systems review negative

3. Medications and Allergies: no medications, no allergies

4. Family/social history: nil of significance

B. Scenario Conditions Initially

1. History patient gives: As above (chief complaint)

C. Scenario Branch Points

1. Changes in patient’s condition: Patient’s condition stable before the intravenous infusion of cephaloxin. One RN is taken to SDC and is familiarized with the patient

A few minutes after the RN begins, the patient will start to complain about generalized itching and will comment that she notices that her skin is red and that she has a rash. This will then progress to having some difficulty breathing. After a few minutes, the patient will become tachycardic, hypotensive and ultimately unconscious.
