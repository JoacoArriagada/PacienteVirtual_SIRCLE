Interdisciplinary Case Conference

2008

**Participant Guide**

**Case**:

Ms. Winters is a 35 year old student at the Duke Divinity School. She came to Duke this fall from Connecticut. She is concerned about pain in her hands.

**1. Patient Interview**:

The patient will be invited into the room to be interviewed by your group.

**2. Simulated Physical Exam**:

Your facilitator will provide information regarding the physical exam after the patient is excused.

**3. Treatment Plan**: After your group has interviewed the patient and considered the findings, you will determine as assessment and a plan of care. When you are ready, the patient will be brought back in to the room for you to communicate your findings and recommendations.
