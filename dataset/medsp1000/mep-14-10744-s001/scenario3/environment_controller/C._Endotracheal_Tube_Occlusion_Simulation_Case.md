| **Actors and roles in the room at case start** | **ICU fellow/primary learner:** Lead Fellow who will be responsible for directing the care of the patient. **Procedure/support fellow:** Optional Support Fellow to assist the Lead Fellow as needed. **The patient (manikin):** Represented by a manikin that is already intubated with ventilator tubing attached, which appears to connect to the ventilator (ventilator is connected to lung simulator). This scenario uses an ICU ventilator attached to an IngMar ASL-5000 lung simulator. **ICU intern (confederate):** A faculty or simulation staff member should perform the role of intern. The intern has additional patient history, can page staff if requested by the fellow, or leave the room to obtain additional equipment if requested. **ICU nurse (confederate):** The ICU nurse should be performed by a critical care nurse who will represent the Medical ICU nurse for the patient. The nurse will provide a brief background synopsis for the Lead Fellow using the information above. The nurse will also announce that the peak pressure alarm is going off. After extubation occurs, the nurse will disagree with the Lead Fellow’s plan to re-intubate the patient, stating that “he really wanted the tube out” and “looked good on his spontaneous breathing trial earlier”. The nurse will suggest alternate interventions such as diuresis (furosemide), albuterol, and lorazepam for anxiety. **ICU respiratory therapist (confederate):** A respiratory therapist should perform this role and assist with respiratory care needs. Once the patient is extubated, the respiratory therapist will recommend against re-intubating, stating that “he looks pretty good”, “he passed his SBT earlier” and “let’s give him a bit more time”. The respiratory therapist will suggest alternate interventions such as high-flow nasal cannula. **Observer (confederate):** A faculty member who will observe in the room and end the scenario. | | |
