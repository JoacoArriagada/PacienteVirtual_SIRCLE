| **Actors and roles in the room at case start** | **ICU fellow/primary learner:** Lead Fellow who will be responsible for directing the care of the patient. **Procedure/support fellow:** Optional Support Fellow to assist the lead fellow as needed. **ICU intern (confederate):** A faculty or simulation staff member should perform the role of intern and provide additional patient history and can page staff if requested by the fellow. **ICU nurse (confederate):** The ICU nurse should be performed by a critical care nurse. The nurse has recently admitted the patient from the floor and will call the fellow to evaluation the patient. **ICU respiratory therapist (confederate):** A respiratory therapist should perform this role and assist with respiratory care needs. **Observer (confederate):** A faculty member who will observe in the room and end the scenario. | | |
