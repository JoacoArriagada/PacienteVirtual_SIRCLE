I. Interviewing a Grandparent Caregiver with Age Related Hearing Loss

II. Environment:

A. Location: Pediatric ER   
B. Young child (4-5 years) mannequin, on monitor

III. Roles

A. ER nurse, Older Adult Caregiver (age 65 or greater if possible), voice for mannequin. The mannequin is used in this case to provide realistic distractions during the interview of the adult caregiver, as may be encountered during a pediatric visit.

IV. Case Narrative

A. Scenario Overview

1) 5 year old child has been brought to the ER by grandmother with the chief complaint that the child is really sleepy. Pediatric resident to elicit history. The historian is the child’s grandmother, who is his primary caretaker.

B. Presenting situation: Resident admission team (may include upper level resident with intern, $+ / -$ medical students) receive a call from the ER to admit a child who is suspected to have ingested a medication. The team proceeds to the ER to gather a history from the child’s guardian. The child is physiologically stable and needs to be admitted for observation.

C. Scenario conditions initially- A 5-year-old boy is present on a monitor and appears somnolent. The child was brought by his grandmother, who is seated at the right side of the child’s bed.
