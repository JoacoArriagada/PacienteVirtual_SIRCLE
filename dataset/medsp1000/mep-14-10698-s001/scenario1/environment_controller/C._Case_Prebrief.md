**History of Present Illness:** A 43-year-old patient with a history of hypertension, diabetes mellitus, hyperlipidemia, and tobacco abuse presented to the ED yesterday with spouse at 16:00 with complaints of sudden onset of unilateral numbness and weakness of the arm and face. The NIH Stroke Scale assessment was completed during ED evaluation. The patient scored a 15 which is indicative of a moderate/severe stroke (3 for palsy on lower right face; 4 for no movement of right upper extremity; 2 for leg falling to bed by 5 seconds, but some effort against gravity; 2 for absent sensation in the lower right face and distal upper extremity; 2 for severe aphasia; 2 for severe dysarthria).
