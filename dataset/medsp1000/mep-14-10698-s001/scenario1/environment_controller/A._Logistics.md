Appendix A: Logistics

1. Location of Activity
   1. Simulation center
2. Setting for the Encounter
   1. Hospital room
3. Materials Required
   1. Name tags (color-coded for student’s profession)
   2. NIH Stroke Scale marked with the preceding day’s date (example: <http://img.medscape.com/article/722/082/722082-fig1.jpg>)
   3. Two copies of the case pre-brief, posted on the door to each station. Students had received this in advance as well, through a web-based course management system.
   4. Hospital bed and gown
   5. Oxygen and nasal cannula
   6. A monitor depicting appropriate blood pressure, heart rate, respiration rate, and oxygen saturation
   7. Reflex hammer
   8. Cotton-tipped applicator
   9. Monofilaments
   10. Walker
   11. NSS/D5W bag (and tubing)
   12. Bedside table with:
       1. Towel and Washcloth
       2. Basin
       3. Soap
       4. Water pitcher
       5. Cup
       6. Apple sauce
       7. Spoon
4. Number of encounters completed by each learner
   1. For this case, each student learner completed a one-time, 25-minute standardized patient (SP) encounter as part of an interprofessional team, followed by a 25-minute team debrief session with a facilitator.
5. Length of each encounter
   1. Student teams were given 15 minutes to sign-in and meet with their teammates to discuss the case prior to the patient encounter.
   2. After the 15-minute preparation, student teams entered the patient simulation room to interview the patient and discuss a patient care plan for a total of 25 minutes.
   3. After the 25-minute interaction with the SP, a facilitator joined the students for a 25-minute debrief.
6. Total number of encounters expected in a session for the SP
   1. A maximum of three student encounters per session
7. Start and finish time
   1. 65 minutes per team in the simulation center
8. Staffing Requirement: 6 SP rooms are run simultaneously
   1. 6 standardized patients per session
   2. 6 faculty/staff facilitators per session
   3. 1 time-keeper
   4. One or two staff to assist students with signing-in and navigating the simulation center
   5. One technician to manage the audiovisual recordings of each room
