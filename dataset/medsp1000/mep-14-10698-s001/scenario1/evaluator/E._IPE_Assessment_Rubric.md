Facilitators: On the left side are behaviors that we would like our learners to achieve. Learners achieve these goals along a continuum. Occasionally, there are students at the far left (1) of the continuum. These students need to receive honest and constructive feedback about how their behaviors are perceived and can negatively impact group dynamics. Do not hesitate to provide that feedback to them. They need to hear it. How can they improve if they have never been told? Many students are progressing toward competency (2). And, some students will have mastered one or more of these competencies (3).
