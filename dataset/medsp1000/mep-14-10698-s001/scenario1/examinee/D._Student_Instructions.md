Appendix D: Student Instructions

**Part I: To be issued to all learners in advance, along with the patient case (see Appendix C: Case Pre-brief)**

**Instructions for Students**

**You are now assessing the patient as part of the acute care stroke team who is participating in interdisciplinary rounds at 08:30 the morning following patient presentation.**

* Once you enter the patient room in the Simulation Center, it will be set up as you would expect to see a patient in a hospital and there will be a standardized patient in the room. (A standardized patient is an actor who has been trained in the case. He/she will respond as you would expect a patient to respond).
* You will be representing a professional from your discipline (e.g. MD students will play the role of a physician; RN students will play the role of a nurse, etc.). With your team, you should complete the necessary assessments as if you were developing an interprofessional plan of care. Please keep in mind, you are not a student in this scenario; you are representing a member of your discipline.

***Together as an interprofessional team***, you should assess the patient and develop as much of a care plan as possible for this patient.
