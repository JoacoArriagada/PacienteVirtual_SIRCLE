Differences in Sex Development (DSD):
Team-Based Learning Module for Pediatric Residents

**Team Application Activity**

**Case 1**: A full-term newborn was born via vaginal delivery. Mom had good prenatal care and no complications before or during the delivery. She was advised that they are having a boy based on the prenatal ultrasound. The parents have been asking to speak to the doctor about the baby's genitalia, gender assignment and medical issues.
