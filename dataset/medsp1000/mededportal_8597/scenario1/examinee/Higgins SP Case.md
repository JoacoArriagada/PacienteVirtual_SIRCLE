**Standardized Patient Case:**

**RYAN HIGGINS**

Name: Ryan Higgins

Setting: ER

Student instructions: You are a student working on the psychiatry service at a military treatment facility. You are called to the emergency room to see a patient who was brought into the ER by his friend.

Presenting situation/door chart information: Today you will be seeing Ryan Higgins, a 22yo E4 who was brought in to the emergency room by his friend. His friend reports that he received a call from the patient's wife saying that she was concerned for the patient's safety because he has been "acting different" and left a worrisome message on her voicemail.

You are working in the Fort Bragg (N.C.) emergency room and the time is 2300.

Patient presentation: When clinician enters, patient is sitting in chair, looking down, punching hand in fist angrily/nervously, taking deep breaths, nostrils flaring a bit. He appropriately responds when clinician introduces him/herself. He remains seated for the interview, often looking down at floor, but occasionally making meaningful eye contact.
