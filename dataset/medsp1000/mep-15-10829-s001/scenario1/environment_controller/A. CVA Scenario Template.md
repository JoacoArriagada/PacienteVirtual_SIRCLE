| **PATIENT NAME: Steven Kreiner**  **PATIENT AGE: 55 years-old**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: Slurred speech and left sided weakness** | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | Patient is a 55-year-old male who presents for slurred speech and left-sided weakness. Symptoms started while having breakfast with his wife 90 minutes prior to arrival. Wife notes that he has facial droop and slurred speech, stating “I could barely get him to the car he seemed so weak”. He is treated with IV tPA and then decompensates due to stroke progression. He is intubated and repeat head CT shows signs of herniation. Neurosurgery is consulted and patient is treated for increased ICP. |
