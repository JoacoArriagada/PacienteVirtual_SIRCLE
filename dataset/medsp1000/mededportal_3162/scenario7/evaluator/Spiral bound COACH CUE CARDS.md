# PULLING IT ALL TOGETHER Role-playing Medical Interviews Activity 6b (90 minutes)

Purpose: To practice a first medical interview with an standardized patient (SP), gathering Chief Complaint, HPI, Past Medical History (including social & family history).

# Groups of 3:

• Students rotate as doctor and observers   
• SPs role play provided scenario   
• “Doctor” interviews SP   
• SPs and “doctor” use non-verbal behaviors to convey status of emotion   
• Observers and coaches interrupt periodically to suggest different strategies to try; SP may be rewound for student practice   
• Observers use Interview Behavior Skills Checklist to assist feedback to “doctor”   
• After one interview, SPs rotate to the next room.

# Discuss:

• What worked well, what to do differently • Identify areas for practice before next session • SPs give feedback about reactions to “doctor,” including perceptions based on “doctor’s” non-verbals • Coach gives comments/suggestions

# PULLING IT ALL TOGETHER Celebrating Successes Activity 6c (2 hours)

Purpose: To “put it all together” in terms of the opening, negotiating the connection, the technical subroutine of data gathering, and the basic medical interviewing structure.

# Large Group:

Students meet; logistics described; 1 student at a time from each room will complete taped interview with SP.

# SP Interviews:

• Students have 5-7 minutes to “connect” with the patient, get the patient’s story, including CC, HPI, PMH, as time allows • Purpose is to learn, practice, try out communication   
techniques   
• SPs complete Interview Behavior Skills checklist to assist with feedback

Feedback: In assigned room. (10-15 min per student)

• Students, coach, and SP meet to review portions of tape • Student gives self-assessment; SP gives feedback • Other students and coach give feedback.

Student Self-Assessment: Checklists will be given to students and physical diagnosis preceptors for further review. DVD’s may be reviewed again upon request. Written selfreflections may also be used.
