# GENERAL SCENARIOS

7. Billy “Bubba” Johnson, Jr. is a 16-year-old boy who is brought by his mother to the MCG Adolescent Medicine Clinic. He is required by the Richmond County Public Schools to have a physical examination before playing high school football. He has been generally healthy, but his mother tells him he has always been “big” for his age. He has had a lot of earaches and colds. Bubba is the oldest of three brothers.

6b. Practicing Medical Interviews. (In groups of 3 students, 90 minutes).

Purpose. The purpose of this session is to practice a “first” medical interview, in preparation for the recorded interview of a Standardized Patient. Students will practice with a Standardized Patient (SP) going through opening, engagement and empathy-building aspects and gather Chief Complaint, History of the Present Illness, Family, Social and Past Medical History to the extent that time allows.

Description. Each SP has a scenario to role play which includes emotional aspects of the illness and suggestions for non-verbal behaviors. They will be joined and interviewed by a “doctor.”
