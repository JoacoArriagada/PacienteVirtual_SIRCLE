3. Ruby Wright is a 44-year-old convenience store clerk living in Hephzibah, GA. Over the past 6 weeks, her right hand has gradually become weak and unusuable. Two weeks ago, upon arising from bed, the patient found her right leg too weak to support her. She stayed in bed two days and her leg improved, but it has been intermittently weak since. She refuses to make eye contact and complains angrily of her symptoms. She loudly states that she “hates doctors.”

6b. Practicing Medical Interviews. (In groups of 3 students, 90 minutes).

Purpose. The purpose of this session is to practice a “first” medical interview, in preparation for the recorded interview of a Standardized Patient. Students will practice with a Standardized Patient (SP) going through opening, engagement and empathy-building aspects and gather Chief Complaint, History of the Present Illness, Family, Social and Past Medical History to the extent that time allows.

Description. Each SP has a scenario to role play which includes emotional aspects of the illness and suggestions for non-verbal behaviors. They will be joined and interviewed by a “doctor.” Both “patients” and “doctors” will use non-verbal behaviors to convey status of emotion. Each student will interview a different SP. The remaining students and faculty will serve as “coaches” interrupting periodically with suggestions of different strategies to try. SPs will then go backward in the interview and begin again (rewound) so that the student can practice the suggested strategy. At the end of each interview, SPs will be rotated among exam rooms.

6c. Celebrating Successes (individual interviews and 6 student debriefings, 120 minutes).

Purpose. The purpose of this session is to put it all together in terms of the opening, negotiating the connection, the technical subroutine of data gathering, and the basic medical interviewing structure and to practice those skills with live patients.

Description. This entire session will be spent with mock patient interviews. Each medical student will act as a physician for a 5 - 7 minute interview that will be videotaped. Feedback will occur during playback. The objective in the interview is not to diagnose and treat but simply to connect with the patient to establish rapport and enjoy the patient’s story, to practice some of the techniques we have learned and to really listen and hear what the patient says. Students should move through the Chief Complaint and HPI parts of the medical interview to the extent possible in 5 - 7 minutes. Feel free to stop the interview if you’ve gotten stuck and begin again or add a different skill.
