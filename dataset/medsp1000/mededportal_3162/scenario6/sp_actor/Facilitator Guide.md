# GENERAL SCENARIOS

6. Thomas Washington is a 58-year-old African-American who is a railroad worker living in Camak, GA. He shows up unexpectantly at the doctor’s office, complaining of pain in his lower left jaw. He states that over the past two weeks, his jaw has gradually become sore, especially at night. He says that sometimes he wakes up at night with a throbbing pain. When this happens, he rubs some toothache medication that he bought at a local drugstore on his tooth. He admits that it usually doesn’t help very much. He fidgets with his hat as he speaks to the doctor and refuses to make eye contact. He states that he “hates dentists.”

6b. Practicing Medical Interviews. (In groups of 3 students, 90 minutes).

Description. Each SP has a scenario to role play which includes emotional aspects of the illness and suggestions for non-verbal behaviors. They will be joined and interviewed by a “doctor.” Both “patients” and “doctors” will use non-verbal behaviors to convey status of emotion. SPs will then go backward in the interview and begin again (rewound) so that the student can practice the suggested strategy.
