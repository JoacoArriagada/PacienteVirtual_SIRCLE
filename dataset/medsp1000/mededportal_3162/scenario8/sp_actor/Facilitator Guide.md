# GENERAL SCENARIOS

8. Matthew Williams is 27-year-old, happily married, and has two young children. He comes to the clinic complaining of low back pain. Matthew is from Augusta and he works for a local landscaping company where he is a crew supervisor. In his time away from work he enjoys fishing and spending time with his family. His back has been hurting for about three weeks. At first he thought the pain would go away but it seems to be getting worse. He has been doing more manual labor recently and working a lot of overtime because they are shorthanded at work. Matthew has been taking Advil, which provides a little relief, but he is still hurting, and he is beginning to think he’s done something serious to his back.

6b. Practicing Medical Interviews. (In groups of 3 students, 90 minutes).

Description. Each SP has a scenario to role play which includes emotional aspects of the illness and suggestions for non-verbal behaviors. They will be joined and interviewed by a “doctor.” Both “patients” and “doctors” will use non-verbal behaviors to convey status of emotion. SPs will then go backward in the interview and begin again (rewound) so that the student can practice the suggested strategy.
