# GENERAL SCENARIOS

1. Danita Williams is a 14-year-old of African-American descent, who is a freshman at the local high school in Washington, GA. Her grandmother brought her in with complaints of severe headache and a stomach ache. During questioning, she fidgets with her hair and avoids eye contact. Upon further questioning, Danita admits to being sexually active. She cannot remember when she had her last menstrual period. She thinks that she might be pregnant, but states that she is afraid to tell her grandmother. She lives with her grandmother, aunt, and three younger siblings.

6b. Practicing Medical Interviews. (In groups of 3 students, 90 minutes).

Description. Each SP has a scenario to role play which includes emotional aspects of the illness and suggestions for non-verbal behaviors. They will be joined and interviewed by a “doctor.” Both “patients” and “doctors” will use non-verbal behaviors to convey status of emotion. SPs will then go backward in the interview and begin again (rewound) so that the student can practice the suggested strategy.
