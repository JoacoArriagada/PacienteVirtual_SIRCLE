Multiple Casualty Scenario from Bomb/Blast Injury

# **Title: A Bomb Went off at the Bus Station**

# **II. Target Audience**: EM residents

## Environment

1. Lab Set Up –Simulation center ED, trauma bay. Could be carried out in any large open space or in the ED.

1. **Case Narrative** (describes what the learner will experience)
2. Scenario Background Given to Participants
3. Participants are divided into 3 groups (our groups were 4 / 2 / 2 – number of trainees should be less than the number of victims – between 8-10 is optimal for this casualty load) and are told that they are doing simulation stations and may have to wait their turn. No further information is provided.
4. Case starts with child with asthma exacerbation
