Multiple Casualty Scenario from Bomb/Blast Injury

# **Title: A Bomb Went off at the Bus Station**

## Actors

* Parent (1)
  + Can be played by any actor
  + Accompanies Sim youth with asthma
* Live Victims (5)
  + Can be played by any well trained actor, residents, other students
  + Roles as described in grid

**Table 1: Overall Flow of Scenario**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time** | **Action** | **Notes** |
| 5 | Initial physicians move to sim room, scenario starts. **Simyouth** with asthma, **parent** present – oxygen sats in upper 80s, working hard to breathe, one-word answers, diffuse wheezing. **Nurse 0** at bedside, room divider is closed | Room 2 |
| 8 | **Nurse 1** and **nurse 2** enter room, flustered, opening divider and say “a bomb went off at the bus station, a car just dropped three people off, I need your help!” – **Simbaby, Sim mom and HF3** in room on gurneys. | Room 1 |
| 12 | **LV1** (13 year old bleeding with open arm fracture holding with fingers) and **MAT** with amputation and femoral artery hemorrhage, moaning only, but breathing spontaneously and protecting airway | Room 2 |
| 15 | **HF1 and LV2 enter** | Room 1 |
| 18 | **LV3, HF2** arrives | Room 2 |
| 20 | Last walk-ins (**LV4 walking,** **LV5 Gurney**) arrive | Room 1 |

**Table 2: Simulator Specific Details**

| **Sim** | **Injuries** | **Initial notes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sim 1: Adolescent | None  Moulage: none | 8 year old, asthma hx, 1h hx rapid onset SOB, retracting, diffuse wheezing. One-word answers. |

| Sim 3: HF1 (amputation/open PTX) | Amputation, penetrating chest wound. Moulage: penetrating injury R chest (no remaining foreign body). | Awake, talking, exsanguinating. |
| Sim 4: HF2 (mangled extremity/ abdominal injury) | Amputation, abdominal wound. Moulage: penetrating abdominal wound with large piece of glass or shrapnel embedded, stabilized pre-hospital. | Moves and protects airway, eyes open to pain, localizes to pain, moans, amputation with exsanguination and ineffective tourniquet in place. Penetrating abdominal wound. Diffusely tender abdomen to palpation. |
| Sim 5: Simbaby (Head injury) | Open head wound. Moulage: Scalp laceration to mimic open skull fracture with hematoma. | GCS3, resp irregular, pulses present but cannot get BP, pale, L pupil blown. |
| Sim 6: Simmom (abdominal injury, head & eye injury) | 28 wks Pregnant, LUQ penetrating wound, L eye injury. Moulage: penetrating LUQ wound without foreign body embedded. | Sim 7: HF3 (cardiac injury, PTX / HTX) | Penetrating chest wound L. Moulage: penetrating L chest wound (parasternal 5th ICS) with small glass or shrapnel embedded & subcutaneous emphysema. | Confused, moaning, opens eyes to pain, localizes to pain (GCS 10). |
