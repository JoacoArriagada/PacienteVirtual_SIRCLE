**Simulated Application Case #3: Safe Discharge**

**Group Allocation and Layout**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule | Case #3 |
| Group | Medicine: 1st year residents (interns): minimum 2, maximum 3  Nursing: senior-level undergraduate nursing students: minimum 2, maximum 3 |
| Location | Clinical skills center with simulation room |
| Facilitators | Minimum of one faculty facilitator  Maximum of two faculty members |
| Duration | 1 hour |

**Materials Needed**

* Standardized patient (SP)
* Glucometer
* Diabetic literature/brochures (can substitute with printed materials from online sources)

**Step by Step Agenda: Safe Discharge**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 5 mins | Trainees read door note (scenario and tasks) |
| 25 mins | Trainees participate in the video recorded scenario with a standardized patient |
| 15 mins | Faculty facilitator(s) and standardized patient debrief trainees and provided feedback using the interprofessional team safety checklist |

**Scenario and Tasks**

*Door note*: James Banks, a 56-year-old white male, is newly diagnosed with type 2 diabetes. At 210 lbs, he has a history of hypertension and hyperlipidemia. He was admitted 2 days ago for uncontrolled blood glucose levels requiring IV insulin. His AIC is 11% and has not responded to PO diabetic medications in the past. He is now ready to be discharged. The nurse provided teaching on how to manage diabetes at home. She has given him instructional materials, including the brochures "Facts about Diabetes" and "How to manage diabetes at home." Mr. Banks has also watched videos that cover basic information about diabetes management, such as how to use the blood glucose meter.

SP: "I have a lot of questions, but I don't know where to begin. I feel overwhelmed with all this information!" (Show brochures). "I don't know if I can do all this insulin stuff, please help me, I am told to take Lantus 60 units at bedtime and Lispro 10 units before meals."
