| **Appendix A3: MedEdPORTAL Simulation Case Template**  **SIMULATION CASE TITLE: Safe Discharge**  **AUTHORS: Wei-Hsin Lu, Nirvani Goolsarran, Carine Hamo, Stacey Frawley, Colby Rowe, Susan Lane** | |
| **PATIENT NAME: James Banks**  **PATIENT AGE: 56**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT:** "I have a lot of questions about my discharge, but I don't know where to begin. I feel overwhelmed with all this information!" (Shows brochures). "I don't know if I can do all this insulin stuff, please help me, I am told to take Lantus 60 units at bedtime and Lispro 10 units before meals." | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | This is an OSCE case that requires a standardized patient actor. James Banks, a 56-year-old white male, is newly diagnosed with type 2 diabetes. At 210 lbs, he has a history of hypertension and hyperlipidemia. He was admitted 2 days ago for uncontrolled blood glucose levels requiring IV insulin. His AIC is 11% and has not responded to PO diabetic medications in the past. He is now ready to be discharged. The nurse provided teaching on how to manage diabetes at home. She has given him instructional materials, including the brochures "Facts about Diabetes" and "How to manage diabetes at home." Mr. Banks has also watched videos that cover basic information about diabetes management, such as how to use the blood glucose meter. Mr. Banks is ready to be discharged but he feels very overwhelmed by the information provided to him. You are the day team on the general medicine floor. Your task is to make sure he is all set to go home and answer any questions he may have. | |
