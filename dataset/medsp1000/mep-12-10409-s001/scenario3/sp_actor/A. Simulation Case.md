| **Brief narrative description of case** | This is an OSCE case that requires a standardized patient actor. James Banks, a 56-year-old white male, is newly diagnosed with type 2 diabetes. At 210 lbs, he has a history of hypertension and hyperlipidemia. He was admitted 2 days ago for uncontrolled blood glucose levels requiring IV insulin. His AIC is 11% and has not responded to PO diabetic medications in the past. He is now ready to be discharged. The nurse provided teaching on how to manage diabetes at home. She has given him instructional materials, including the brochures "Facts about Diabetes" and "How to manage diabetes at home." Mr. Banks has also watched videos that cover basic information about diabetes management, such as how to use the blood glucose meter. Mr. Banks is ready to be discharged but he feels very overwhelmed by the information provided to him. | |

Mr. Banks is ready to be discharged but he feels very overwhelmed by the information provided to him.  Information for the Standardized Patient/Actor:  The standardized patient (SP) is a pleasant, middle class man who wants to please his doctor. He is very non-compliant with his diabetic diet and does not routinely check his blood sugars at home. He checks only when he doesn't feel so well. He feels guilty about not routinely checking his sugar and often "forgets" his log at home on days he has an appointment even though his doctor has asked him to bring this with him every visit. He is well groomed, dressed in pants, a shirt and practical shoes. He appears healthy, with no visual signs of illness. He is pleasant, and smiling and cooperative. He makes little jokes and changes the subject when he is asked about his diet and blood glucose monitoring. The standardized patient is given the following list of questions to ask:  Q: "I don't understand why all of a sudden I need insulin, I have been trying very hard to eat right and exercise and I don't know what I am doing wrong"  Q: "Should I be concerned about any side effects of taking insulin?"  Q: "I am not sure how to use this machine (shows the glucometer)"  Q: "Do I continue to take my metformin with the insulin?" | | |
