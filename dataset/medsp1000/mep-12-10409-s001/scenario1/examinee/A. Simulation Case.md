| **PATIENT NAME: Albert Smith**  **PATIENT AGE: 45**  **CHIEF COMPLAINT: no complaint, patient is unresponsive** | |
| **Brief narrative description of case** | This case requires high fidelity simulation mannequin. This is a 45-year-old male who was admitted for shortness of breath. He was found to have a right main stem pulmonary artery embolism. He is now in PEA arrest. Your team is being called into a code blue/cardiac arrest. Your task is to follow ACLS protocol with your team. Following this, you will conduct a root cause analysis of the event. |
