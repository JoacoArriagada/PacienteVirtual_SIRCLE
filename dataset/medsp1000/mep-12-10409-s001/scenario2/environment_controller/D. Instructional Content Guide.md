**Scenario and Tasks**

*Door note and handout*: You are the day team and about to end your shift and hand-off to the night float team. Please review the following patient case with interval events that occurred throughout the day.

**Patient case**

76-year-old patient with past medical history of severe COPD, CAD s/p CABG, Diabetes type 2, peripheral artery disease, and hypertension, who was admitted early this morning for decompensating respiratory failure due to progression of severe COPD. Patient was placed on albuterol/ipratropium nebulizers every 4 hours and given IV solumedrol (methylprednisolone). He appears to be stable for now, but is very sleepy and has been deferring all questions to his wife. He has had a complicated stay thus far with the following significant events:

**Interval events/nursing info provided**

**10 am**: breathing slightly worse, respiratory therapist placed patient on facemask 60% oxygen with some improvement noted. ABG revealed: 7.31/60/70/34 chest x-ray was unchanged with interpretation of severe emphysematous disease

**12 pm**: Rapid response was called for hypoxia with O2 saturation drop to 60%, patient returned to baseline after aggressive suctioning and chest PT; since then has been difficult to wean off 60% face mask

**3 pm**: Given worsening respiratory status and possible intubation requirement, code status was discussed with wife; she is thinking about DNR/DNI but needs time to discuss with her children. Repeat ABG is pending and chest x-ray that was done at 6pm has not yet been read back.
