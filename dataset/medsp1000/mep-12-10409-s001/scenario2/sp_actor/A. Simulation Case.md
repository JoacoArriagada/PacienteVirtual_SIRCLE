Patient was placed on supplemental oxygen via venti-mask, albuterol/ipratropium nebulizers every 4 hours and given IV solumedrol (methylprednisolone). He appears to be stable for now, but is very sleepy and has been deferring all questions to his wife. | |

**Ideal Scenario Flow**

The hand-off provider team will first enter the room to find a stabilized mannequin patient with end stage COPD. They examine and question the patient but he defers all questions to his wife who is not present in the room. The wife is purposely excluded from the scenario at the time of hand-off to create a hand-off scenario of an unresolved code status.
