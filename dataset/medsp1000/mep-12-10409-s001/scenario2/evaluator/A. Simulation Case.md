**Ideal Scenario Flow**

The hand-off provider team will first enter the room to find a stabilized mannequin patient with end stage COPD. They examine and question the patient but he defers all questions to his wife who is not present in the room. The wife is purposely excluded from the scenario at the time of hand-off to create a hand-off scenario of an unresolved code status. The team discusses the handout of the patient case and interval events with each other. Learners generate a written IPASS hand-off using the handout template as a guide. After 10 minutes, the hand-off receiver team enters the room; the hand-off provider team is ready to conduct the hand-off exchange. The provider team presents a succinct written and verbal hand-off to the receiving team using the IPASS format. At the end of the hand-off exchange, faculty facilitator conducts a debrief session using the safety critical action checklist and review the written IPASS hand-off generated.
