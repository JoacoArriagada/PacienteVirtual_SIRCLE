| **Actors and roles in the room at case start** | The faculty facilitator splits the group into 2 interprofessional small teams: hand-off provider and hand-off receiver teams. The hand-off provider team first enters the room and uses their handout to discuss the case and interval events, and generate a hand-off using the blank IPASS template provided. After 15 minutes, the faculty facilitator lets the hand-off receiver team into the room and observes the verbal hand-off from the provider to the receiver team. | | |
| **HPI** | The faculty facilitator is present in the room and observes the generation and exchange of hand-off between both teams.
