# OSCE Question # 14

Question: “A four month old male named John comes into your office for a well child visit. On exam you notice that he has a heart murmur. He has normal vital signs, an otherwise normal exam, and no significant past medical history; parents have no complaints and say that the John is doing well. You think he has an innocent murmur and decide to tell the parents about the murmur (which they were never told about before). Remember you are explaining the diagnosis to the parents.”
