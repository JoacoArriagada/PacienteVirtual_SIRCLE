Anticipated Management Mistakes

This simulation has three main areas of difficulty built in:

1. The first area of difficulty is the maintenance of sterility. The simulation operating room is small and cramped and there are a number of students who contaminate the instrument table or themselves. At this point they are made to rescrub or stopped immediately and any break in equipment sterility is acknowledged.
2. The first opportunity for a management mistake is at the point where the anesthesia provider attempts to administer cefazolin to the patient. Students may not feel comfortable speaking up about their uncertainties, however this is designed to have negative consequences for the patient should they not share their concern.
3. The scrub technologist/nurse should intentionally drop a laparotomy sponge onto the floor in a discrete manner. Students should acknowledge an instrument count should take place. When this does happen, a sponge will be missing and students must maintain sterility and assist in reconciliation of the instrument count.
