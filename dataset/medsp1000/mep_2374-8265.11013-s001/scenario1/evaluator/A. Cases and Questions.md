Introduction to LGBTQ+ Health

Cases


**Case 1**: John is a 16 year old cis-gender male who comes to clinic for his annual well-child visit. Mid-way through the visit, you ask his parent to leave the room and go through your HEADS exam. He states that he is sexually attracted to both men and women. He currently is in a relationship with a woman, but he admits to “fooling around” with men on the side.

1. What barriers typically exist for providers and patients that impede high-quality care for patients who identify as LGBTQ?
2. What questions should you ask him regarding his sexual practices?
3. What STIs does the CDC recommend testing for?
