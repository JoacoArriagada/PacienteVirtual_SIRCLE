**Case 4:** A 6 year old girl named Mary comes in as a walk-in with distressed parents because she has been dressing like a boy recently. They are worried she is going to be bullied, so they have been forcing her to wear dresses and pink bows. They wonder if she is transgender but assume it is “just a phase” since the mom was a “tomboy” as a child and grew out of it.

1. What is the difference between gender identity and gender expression?
2. How do you define transgender and gender-expansivity?
3. Is this just a phase? What age do children start to reveal they are transgender?
