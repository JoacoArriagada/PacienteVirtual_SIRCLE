**Appendix A: Student Instructions Handout**

**Patient name:** Leslie Davis

**Chief Complaint:** Rash

**Background:** The patient is a 45-year-old with a confirmed diagnosis of **shingles (herpes zoster)** and is in clinic today to discuss the treatment for this rash.

**Your tasks:**

1. First, complete the pre-case worksheet *before* meeting with the patient. This worksheet will help you to prepare for the patient encounter by identifying the information needed to address the following patient discussion tasks.

* Be sure to ***save a copy of your notes from this worksheet*** to use during the patient discussion.

2. Next, provide the following information to the patient, ***in words that the patient can understand:***

* **Tell the patient about the treatment options for shingles.**
* **Recommend to them the specific medication you think is best** for treating the patient’s shingles (be prepared to **explain its mechanism of action**).
* **Provide instructions for** **how the patient should take this medication** (i.e., for how many days, route of administration—oral, intravenous, etc.).
* **Provide information on important** **side effects** that may occur with this medication.
* Given these side effects, **determine which lab test(s) you should obtain** (if any) before prescribing this drug to this patient.
* **Answer the patient’s questions** about this medication, to the best of your ability. Be prepared to explain things such as how it works (i.e., its mechanism of action).
* Also be sure to **briefly review the other antivirals** **used for other herpes infections** (i.e., HSV, CMV).
