# Alex Corason - Abnormal Heart Sounds – Instructor’s Guide

Resource: Alex Corason - Abnormal Heart Sounds

How to use the resource file: The resource file contains information necessary for training standardized patients, presented in a table format. It describes the case, the way used/validated, contains SP training notes and additional details.

Conceptual background: This case was created for use with standardized patients, for teaching and/or evaluation purposes.

Practical implementation advice: This is a hybrid case, which means that heart sounds simulator will be necessary to implement the case (e.g. Harvey, Cardionics or Ventriculoscope). All original materials/patient exam room needs are described in the case, users are free to change the original case to better suit their needs, as long as due credit is applied.

Use of section “Standardizing the Unknown”: This section is intended for use when training multiple SPs for portraying the same case. Its purpose is two-prong:

(1) It is very hard to predict and standardize the answers to each and every question learners might decide to ask of the SP. Having more details to the portrait makes the character more complete, and the SPs can easier associate with and think as the patient portrayed.
(2) The details referred to in this section might be quite different for different states, cities and schools. These questions are intended for the group of SPs to decide on. This gives them the chance to partake in the creation of the portrayal, bring local detail to the role, and take ownership of the role. The SP trainers should feel free to add/delete questions in this section to make it most applicable to their local specific circumstances.

Recommended length of teaching session: 1 hour 15 minutes: The students have 10 minutes with the patient, next go out to examine a manikin with pre-set heart [and lung] sounds (10 minutes) and return to the room to deliver the news to the patient, present a closing summary and discuss future plans (5 minutes). When the patient encounter is complete, the students have 10 minutes for patient note, and a 30-min debriefing with faculty-observer (for teaching purposes only). Additional 10 minutes are factored in for transitioning between task locations.
