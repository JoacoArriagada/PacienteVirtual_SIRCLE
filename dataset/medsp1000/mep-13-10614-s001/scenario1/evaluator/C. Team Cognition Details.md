**Appendix C — Team Cognition Details**

1. Team Leadership
   1. Identify the leader prior to entering patient room
   2. Identify who they are to the nurse and their collective role
   3. One person emerges as leading the team
      1. Delegates roles
      2. Receives information from individual roles
      3. Shares information to the team
      4. Recognizes when a team member is underperforming and assists or send helps
      5. Debriefs team on critical event at the conclusion
2. Closed-loop communication
   1. Requests information, supplies, procedures to an individual
   2. #A request is confirmed by recipient
   3. #A sender confirms receipt of request
   4. likely examples:
      1. History and Physical
      2. Infusions
      3. Nurse history
      4. Physical status decompensation
      5. ACLS intervention
         1. Airway, suction, oxygen
         2. Chest compressions
         3. Drug delivery
         4. Lab orders
3. Shared mental model
   1. Does the leader share newly discovered information with the entire team?
   2. Do the individual team members share the unique information from entry paragraphs with the team/leader?
   3. Do the team members “think aloud”?
      1. Changing status of the patient
      2. Drug infusions
   4. Does the team debrief on the critical event?
4. Mutual performance monitoring
   1. Are all the information sources analyzed?
      1. History and physical
      2. Patient status
      3. Drug infusions
      4. Nurse’s understanding
   2. ACLS
      1. Are chest compressions appropriate?
      2. Is minute ventilation appropriate?
      3. Is the nurse being managed appropriately?
