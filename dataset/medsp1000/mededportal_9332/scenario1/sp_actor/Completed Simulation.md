# Title: Medicine Across The Barricade

# Environment:

## Distracters: Screaming from the bystander(s) out of fear on the other end of the phone.

# Actors:

## Roles:

## Bystander is an uninjured, adult hostage but referred to as a bystander for ease of nomenclature.

## Who can play them: The bystander can be anyone with no medical training.

# Case Narrative:

## The TMP is on-scene with a local SWAT team at a barricade situation with multiple hostages (number unknown). There is an injured hostage requiring medical care.

### CC: GSW

### HPI: Hostage tried to run when bank robbers first came in and he sustained a GSW.

### PMH: Unknown

* + 1. FH: Unknown
    2. ROS: Unable to attain secondary to decreased mental status

## PE

### Tachycardic, tachypneic, diaphoretic, cool clammy skin

### On the RLE there is an anterior GSW just medial to midline of the mid-thigh with a posterior GSW.

### There is active bleeding coming from the posterior GSW wound.

### Distal pulses in the RLE are decreased compared to the left.

* 1. Scenario Branch Points:
     1. Failure to remain calm will cause the bystander to become hysterical.
     2. Failure to use lay-terms will result in the bystander becoming confused and angry.

1. Instructor Notes:
   1. Tips to keep the scenario moving:
      1. The bystander can also ask multiple questions about next steps.
   2. Tips for actors
      2. Bystander
         1. Will follow commands.
         2. Does not understand medical jargon.
         3. Cannot anticipate next steps.
   5. The bystander is ideally being played by someone with no special medical knowledge or training and no knowledge of the victim’s injury(s). Therefore, in order for the medic to assess the condition of the victim, he/she will request information which a lay person could reasonably be expected to provide such as whether the patient is breathing, has a pulse, can talk or has active external bleeding. Furthermore, he/she may request the bystander to relay changes in these initial findings after medical therapy is initiated or after a delay in treatment occurs.
