Attachment 1

Tactical Case Debriefing Points

1. What went well and what would you do differently if you were going to repeat the exercise?
2. What was your thought process for the key actions taken or not taken? (Direct question modified to address specific actions)
3. Discuss simulation events related to the specific learning objectives (Use a group discussion format led by the instructor):
4. Perform an appropriate primary survey
   1. Exsanguinating Hemorrhage
   2. Airway Breathing
   3. Circulation
   4. Disability
   5. Exposure
   6. Pain control
5. Perform a timely and appropriate secondary survey
   1. After the primary survey and interventions, a head-to-toe secondary survey should be performed to identify all injuries.
6. Recognize and treat hypovolemia secondary to hemorrhage
   1. Tachycardia is usually the first sign of hypovolemia but vital signs initially can be normal due to cardiovascular reserve.
   2. Other signs are mental status changes, delayed capillary refill, skin pallor and hypothermia.
7. Demonstrate the correct sequence of steps to apply a tourniquet
   1. Access a prefabricated CAT
      1. LE:
         1. Pass the self-adhering band through the inside slit of the friction adaptor buckle
         2. Pass the band through the outside slit of the buckle
         3. Pull the self-adhering band tight
         4. Turn the windlass in either direction until there is no active bleeding
         5. Secure the windlass in the clip and secure the self-adhering strip over the top of the clip
         6. Check to ensure there is no pulse distally, if there is then tighten the windlass further (repeat steps 4-6)
      2. UE
         1. Pass the band through the outside slit of the buckle
         2. Pull the self-adhering band tight
         3. Turn the windlass in either direction until there is no active bleeding
         4. Secure the windlass in the clip and secure the self-adhering strip over the top of the clip
         5. Check to ensure there is no pulse distally, if there is then tighten the windlass further (repeat steps 4-5)
   2. Gather items to make a tourniquet if a prefabricated one is not available
      1. Wrap fabric around the limb so that you can fit your hand under the loop and tie a square knot
      2. Slide the windlass under the loop and twist in either direction until cessation of active bleeding
      3. Secure the windlass to the main loop with another tie
      4. Check for a distal pulse, if there is one then repeat steps 2-4.
8. Reassess casualty if there are any changes or after the casualty is moved
9. Initiate a timely request for evacuation
