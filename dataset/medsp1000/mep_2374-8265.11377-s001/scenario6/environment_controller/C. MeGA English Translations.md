1) What is it

Comic vignette: Doctor speaking

Doctor: “Asthma is a chronic lung problem, and people who have asthma sometimes have asthma flares. An asthma flare, or asthma attack, is when the small airways inside your lungs become swollen or inflamed. As a result of this swelling or inflammation, the airways become more narrow or tight. If this happens, it is harder to breathe, you may notice sounds called wheezing, you may have a cough, and you might also feel chest tightness.”

The diagnosis is typically made after:

Listening to your symptoms and your medical history Doing a physical exam, including listening to your lungs In some cases, we might do other tests like chest x-ray (radiograph), but these tests are not always recommended.

Yellow diamond: Remember! Please do not leave without asking any questions you may have. We want to help you understand and improve your health

1) Qué es

La doctora le explica el diagnóstico a Clara.

“El asma es una enfermedad crónica de los pulmones, y la persona con asma en ocasiones sufre ataques de asma. Un ataque de asma es cuando las vías respiratorias dentro de los pulmones se in!aman o se hinchan. Debido a la inf!amación, las vías que llevan aire se hacen más estrechas. La persona puede sentir que es más difícil respirar, oír sonidos como sibilancias o pitidos, tener tos y sentir el pecho apretado.”

El diagnóstico de asma se hace después de:

Escuchar sus síntomas y su historial médico Examinar los pulmones y otras partes del cuerpo A veces se hacen otras pruebas como una radiografía (placa) de los pulmones, pero no siempre se recomiendan.

2) How is it treated

Comic vignette: Patient practicing how to use the inhaler while following the instructions.

Caption: Albuterol is a rescue medication that works quickly during an asthma flare. You breathe it in through a device called an inhaler like the one Clara is using. The doctor teaches Clara to use the rescue inhaler properly so it can be as effective as possible.

Doctor: “(1) Shake the bottle and connect it to the inhaler with spacer, (2) Breathe out to empty out your lungs, (3) Place the inhaler mouthpiece in your mouth, squeezing your lips around it to make a good seal, (4) Press the container to release the medication, (5) Take a deep breath and hold it for 10 seconds.”

2) Cómo se trata

El albuterol es un medicamento de rescate que funciona rápido para mejorar el ataque. Se respira con un aparato como el inhalador de Clara. La doctora le enseña a Clara a usar el inhalador de forma que haga el mayor efecto:

“(1) Agite el frasco y conecte el espaciador, (2) Saque el aire de los pulmones, (3) Coloque el inhalador en la boca, apretando los labios, (4) Presione el frasco, (5) Respire profundo y aguante por 10 segundos.”

Comic vignette: Picture of medications

Caption: A steroid medication pill might be needed to reduce the swelling in your lungs. Depending on how severe the asthma flare is, sometimes other treatments might be recommended as well.

Un medicamento esteroide puede ser necesario para bajar la in!amación en los pulmones. Dependiendo de la gravedad del ataque, a veces se hacen otros tratamientos también.

Patient: “If I still feel badly tomorrow, should I return to the hospital?”

Doctor: “We expect that symptoms from an asthma flare will last several more days. But, if you notice that you are getting worse or develop concerning symptoms, you should use your rescue inhaler right away, and call your doctor or return to the hospital immediately.”
