1) What is it

Comic vignette: Doctor speaking

Doctor: “You have a heart problem called congestive heart failure. This means that your heart is not pumping or pushing your blood strongly enough. When your heart does not pump well, fluid can build up in some parts of the body, for example, in your legs or in your lungs. When fluid builds up in your legs, you can get leg swelling, and the fluid in your lungs causes shortness of breath and getting tired easily after activity.”

The diagnosis is typically made after:

Listening to your symptoms and your medical history Doing a physical exam Running some tests, which might include a chest x-ray or radiograph, an echocardiogram (an ultrasound of your heart), and/or blood tests.

2) How is it treated

Comic vignette: Patient listening to treatment information

Caption: To treat congestive heart failure, it is important to understand how diuretic medications work and what you can expect when you begin this treatment.

1) Qué es

La doctora le explica el diagnóstico a Roberto.

“Usted tiene un problema del corazón que se llama insu!ciencia cardíaca congestiva. Esto quiere decir que su corazón no está bombeando o empujando la sangre lo bastante fuerte. Cuando el corazón no bombea bien, se acumula líquido en algunas partes del cuerpo, como por ejemplo en las piernas o en los pulmones. El líquido en las piernas causa hinchazón y el líquido en los pulmones causa falta de aire o fatiga.”

El diagnóstico de insuficiencia cardíaca se hace después de:

Escuchar sus síntomas y su historial médico Hacer un examen físico Hacer algunas pruebas como una radiografía (placa) del pecho, un ultrasonido (sonograma, ecografía) del corazón y análisis de sangre.

¡Recuerde! No se vaya sin expresar sus preguntas. Queremos ayudarle a entender y mejorar su salud

2) Cómo se trata

Para tratar la insu!ciencia cardíaca congestiva, es importante entender cómo funcionan los medicamentos diuréticos y lo que usted puede esperar con este tratamiento.

Doctor: “We are going to prescribe a diuretic. This is a pill that you need to take every day. This medication is going to help you get rid of the built-up fluid in your body. It is going to cause you to urinate more frequently, but your breathing and leg swelling will get better. After one month of taking the diuretic, you will need to have blood tests to check how the medicine is working.”

Comic vignette: Picture of IV medications

Caption: Sometimes, treating congestive heart failure can involve getting admitted to the hospital in order to get urgent tests done and to get the treatment started rapidly through intravenous (IV) medication.

3) When to seek medical attention

Comic vignette: Patient asking question about follow-up and doctor providing information   
Dialogue

Caption: Roberto is feeling better and is being discharged from the hospital. The doctor explains what he should do to take care of himself at home. But, if he notices certain concerning symptoms, he should seek medical attention immediately.

Patient: “If I still feel badly tomorrow, should I return to the hospital?”

Doctor: “At home, you need to start the routine of taking your medication every day and keeping track of your symptoms. You should also make an appointment with your primary care doctor and with the heart specialist, the cardiologist, in one to two weeks.”

Possible concerning symptoms:

Chest pain   
Difficulty breathing Dizziness or fainting Confusion

3) Cuándo pedir ayuda

Comic vignette: Doctor and patient dialoguing about preventive health and social aspects of care

Caption: Congestive heart failure is a chronic condition. The doctor speaks with Roberto about how to prevent his congestive heart failure from getting worse or flaring up. Some prevention tips include maintaining regular physical activity, eating a low salt diet, taking your medications as prescribed, and keeping all your medical appointments.

“Le vamos a recetar un diurético. Es una pastilla que debe tomar todos los días. Le va a ayudar a eliminar el líquido acumulado. Va a orinar con más frecuencia debido a la pastilla, pero su respiración e hinchazón van a mejorar. Después de un mes de empezar el diurético, vamos a repetir los análisis de sangre.”

A veces el tratamiento para la insu!ciencia cardíaca congestiva requiere ingresarle en el hospital para hacer pruebas y para empezar el tratamiento urgentemente por vía intravenosa.

Roberto ha mejorado y le van a dar de alta. La doctora le explica lo que debe hacer en casa. Pero si nota síntomas de alarma, debe buscar atención médica inmediatamente.

Dolor de pecho Di!cultad para respirar Mareos o desmayos Confusión
