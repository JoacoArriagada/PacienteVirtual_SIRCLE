1. Case 1: Low risk chest pain with an overbearing family member

1. Environment
   1. Lab Set Up: Emergency department triage area and emergency department treatment room

2. Actors
   1. Roles: Physician, Nurses, medical technician, patient, patient’s wife
   2. Action Role: The medical staff are involved in the care of the patient and addressing the concerns of the wife.

3. Case Narrative
   1. Scenario Background: The patient and his wife are sitting in an emergency department triage area. The first person to enter is an emergency department technician.
      1. Throughout these encounters the patient’s wife answers first for the patient with every question asked. Feeling insecure and worried for her spouse, she is intrusive and expresses skepticism of the medical care.
   2. Initial Scenario Conditions
      1. HPI: 50 year old male with chest pain that started earlier in the day, is gone now.
