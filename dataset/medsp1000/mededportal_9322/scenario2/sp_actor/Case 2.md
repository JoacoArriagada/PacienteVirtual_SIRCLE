1. Case 2: Ventricular fibrillation in DNR/DNI Patient

7. Actors
   1. Roles: Family members
   2. Who May Play Them: Trained actors play the family members.
   3. Action Role: The family members arrive after their mother has been intubated. They are distraught.

8. Case Narrative
   1. After intubation, the patient’s family members enter the room. They are very upset because their mother has been made DNR/DNI on her last admission to the hospital, and they contend that this information should have been available to the providers.
   2. If the caregivers and the team follow the steps for Conflict Resolution, then the family has understanding, becomes less upset and agrees to continue the resuscitation.
   3. The family could also agree to terminate resuscitation based on the wishes of the mother.
   4. If the caregivers become defensive about why the patient was intubated, the family members become more upset and accuse the care team of ignoring their mother’s wishes.
   5. Ultimately, the family is asked whether care should continue. Regardless of how the previous interaction has proceeded, the family makes a second decision to either defibrillate or to allow the patient to die.

9. Instructor’s Notes
   1. The second part of the scenario involves actors portraying upset family members. The family enters just after the patient has been intubated.
   2. The actors should be trained to recognize empathy, clinical explanation and inclusion of them in the care of their parent.
   3. If the team is successful in this, then the actors will relax and express understanding of the care team’s actions in the face of the patient’s unknown code status.
   4. If the care team becomes defensive and fails to recognize and acknowledge the family members’ concerns, the family members become more irate and are unable to come to a decision on whether to continue the resuscitation or not.
   5. The ultimate decision that the family members make can be flexible in regards to the teaching institution’s policy on DNR orders and medical power of decision.
