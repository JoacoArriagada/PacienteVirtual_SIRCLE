1. Case 2: Ventricular fibrillation in DNR/DNI Patient

6. Environment
   1. Lab Set Up: Emergency department trauma bay
   2. Manikin Set Up: The manikin is on a stretcher, fully clothed with CPR in progress by EMT transport.

7. Actors
   1. Roles: Physician, Nurses (2-4), EMTs, Family members
   2. Action Role: The medical staff are involved in the medical care of the patient.

8. Case Narrative
   1. Scenario background: Physicians, nurses and EMTs are standing in an empty trauma bay and have no indication as to the incoming case. A phone rings and is picked up by a staff member. The phone call is from EMS with a report of a 75 year-old female picked up for respiratory distress. ETA is 2 minutes.
      1. The EMTs arrive with a patient on the stretcher with CPR in progress, having lost a pulse just prior to arrival.
   2. Initial Scenario conditions
      1. The patient is unresponsive with no pulse
      2. EMTs give report after handoff that the patient was in respiratory distress upon arrival and she had an oxygen saturation of 84% on room air. Oxygen and 40 mg furosemide were given before she lost her pulse in the ambulance.
      3. Nothing is known about the patient’s past medical history and she was alone in the house.
