# **Case No1** *(what learners will see if they are given a schedule ahead of time )*

# **Case Title / Topic: TPA Stroke Case**

## Environment

1. Venue Set Up
2. Type of Room / Area: ED room / critical care bay
3. Beds / stretchers: usual ED stretcher
4. Other large equipment: Cardiac/BP monitor, crash cart

Electronic/computer screen

## Actors

10. Neurology residents in groups of 3 (one resident running stroke code and other 2 acting as team members), arrive once stroke code is called
11. Additional nurses and medical students can be learner team members when desired.
12. **Case Information**
13. **Case Narrative:** Scenario background given to participants
14. Scene introduction for Neurology resident: You are rounding at your hospital as G2 resident with your attending and your pager goes off indicating a stroke code. You are about to enter the critical care bay in the ED.
15. Chief complaint: Cannot talk/speech is garbled, is not moving right side.
