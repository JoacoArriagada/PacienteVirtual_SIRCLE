**Case 1:** You are seeing a 54-year-old man with a chief complaint of atraumatic right shoulder pain. He appears comfortable. His pain is described as sharp and worse with shoulder abduction. He has been followed by an orthopedic surgeon. His shoulder pain is not new or changed. He has no sensory or motor deficits. He states that he was told to come to the emergency department by his orthopedist for pain control.

1. How would you appropriately set expectations for this patient’s visit?
2. How would you treat the patient’s pain while in the emergency department? At home?
3. How would your treatment plan change if this patient has a “pain contract” with a pain management specialist?
4. What would your discharge instructions be for him?

(Facilitators: Discuss how and when to establish patient expectations, especially when instructed by another provider to seek care in the ED. Discuss chronic pain and how to manage acute exacerbations of chronic pain in the ED in the age of opiate overuse.)
