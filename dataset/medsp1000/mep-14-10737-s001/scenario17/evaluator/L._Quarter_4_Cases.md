**Case 4**

PATIENT: You are an elderly patient and have been told that you are being admitted because you passed out in the grocery store today. You have been in the ED for 6 hours. You have not seen your doctor in over an hour, and you are alone in your ED room. You are not sure you want to stay to be admitted.

DOCTOR: It’s Saturday night at 3AM and your elderly patient is awaiting an inpatient bed and is boarded in the ED. They are here for syncope and have multiple comorbidities. They have been in the ED for 6 hours. The nurse asks you to come update the patient.

1. What do you tell the patient about the delays?
2. How do you communicate that they are receiving quality care?

(Facilitators: Discuss how you might avoid these situations when you know that inpatient beds are sparse and a patient is going to be waiting in the ED for several hours. Talk about how to reassess and update patients with a prolonged ED stay who are too unwell to be discharged home. Discuss the importance of reassessment and how patient status may change [for better or for worse] during their ED stay.)
