She tried to wake him up to take a bottle but he wouldn’t latch. She tried to suction his nose and that did not appear to help either.  If asked:   * No recent sick symptoms such as fever, cough, congestion, vomiting * Takes formula only * His mother thinks he is a fussy baby at baseline * He wakes at least twice a night for feeds * Mother recently went back to work. Her mother comes and care for him from 6AM until the mother wakes up in the afternoon * The grandmother reports that he was a little more sleepy than normal this morning and did not want to take his normal bottle. | Instructor Notes - Changes and CASE Branch Points | | |
| **Intervention / Time point** | **Change in Case** | **Additional Information** |
| 5 minutes into the case | BP decreases to 70/38 and HR increases to 190, RR decreases to 10 with irregular pattern, oxygen saturation decreases to 82% if on room air, 89% if on oxygen. Infant stops crying | If not noticed by team within 2 minutes, RN or confederate alerts the provider: “Doctor, the vital signs are changing” |
| Participant provides bag mask ventilation or intubates the patient and provides manual ventilation | Oxygen saturation increases to 97% | Some learner groups may elect to bag the patient and not proceed to intubation. Ending the scenario does not require that they have intubated |
| Participant requests paralytic agent to be given | RR 0 | Oxygen saturations decrease unless patient is bagged prior to intubation attempt |
| 10 minutes into the case | Infant develops tonic-clonic movements of all 4 extremities. | Upper airway obstruction occurs as demonstrated by stridor with inspiration. Pupils remain 4 mm and sluggish |
| Participant requests finger stick blood glucose. | Glucose level is 110 |  |
| Participant request isotonic fluid bolus | Blood pressure increases to 75/40 and heart rate decreases to 175 | A second bolus with result in BP of 78/40 and HR of 170 |
| Participant requests blood gas | If on oxygen: 7.11/70/95/-10  If not on oxygen: 7.11/70/50/-10  If being bagged: 7.3/50/95/-6 |  |
| Participant requests bedside electrolytes and or hematocrit | Sodium 139  Potassium 4  Chloride 110  Bicarb 16  iCa 1.12  Hct 40 | Only provide these labs if they would actually be available at your site |
| Participant requests benzodiazepine to be given by IN, IM, IV or PR route | Seizure stops | Vitals return to pre-seizure vitals |
| Participant attempts to intubate during the seizure WITHOUT a paralytic | The endotracheal tube cannot be passed through the cords |  |
| Nasal airway is placed at anytime stridor is present | Stridor resolves |  |
