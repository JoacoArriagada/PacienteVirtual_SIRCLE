**Simulated Patient (SP) Training: Cases Information Provided to SPs**

# Case 3: Birth Control

You are a Deafblind patient and have come to the clinic to discuss birth control options. You are considering different methods and want to understand the pros and cons of each option. You are particularly interested in learning about long-term methods like IUDs and arm implants (Nexplanon), as well as how to take oral contraceptives if you choose that option. You use an FM system to communicate and have access to ClearSpeech Interpreters.

**Simulation Setup:**

• A ClearSpeech Interpreter is present.

**Instructions To Patient**

**Patient Background:**

• **Name**: You may use your own name or create one

• **Age**: 35

• **Sex:** Female or assigned female at birth

• **Living Situation:** You live alone in a subsidized apartment, have family in the area, and receive in-home assistance several times per week.

**How to Act During the Simulation:**

• You are eager to discuss birth control options. You are attentive and want to make an informed decision about your health.

• Demonstrate openness to learning about different birth control methods but show hesitation or uncertainty about taking a pill every day, weight gain (Depo shot), or options that may cause heavy bleeding (copper IUD).

*• “I have heavy bleeding and I’ve learned to deal with it but if there’s any birth control option that can not only be effective but also help lessen heavy bleeding, that would be ideal.”*

• Ask questions or express concerns when something is unclear or when you need more information.

• Show appreciation when the provider uses tactile explanations or hands-on demonstrations (e.g., using a basket of contraception options).

• **Facial expressions**: Show understanding (nodding, smiling), confusion (tilting head, furrowed brow), or concern (serious expression).

• **Body language:** Sit attentively, maintain focus on the provider and interpreter.

**Health Information**

**• Medical history:** Someone told you that you may have fibroids. You are not too concerned about this currently and are focused on seeking care for birth control.

**• Menstrual history:** Regular, heavy bleeding that occurs every month for about 7 days. You do NOT soak through a pad every 1-2 hours. Periods are associated with moderate crampy pain (does not occur outside of the period) that is manageable with tylenol and ibuprofen. Your last period was about a week ago.

• **Surgical history:** None.

• **Medications**: None.

• **Tobacco use**: None. **Alcohol use:** Occasional, in moderation. **Other drug use:** None

**Key Questions You Might Ask the Doctor:**

• “What are the different birth control options available to me?”

• “How do IUDs and arm implants work, and what are the risks and benefits?”

• “If I choose oral contraceptives, how do I take them correctly?”

• “Are there any side effects I should be aware of?”

• “What should I do if I experience side effects?”

• “How do I know which method is right for me?”
