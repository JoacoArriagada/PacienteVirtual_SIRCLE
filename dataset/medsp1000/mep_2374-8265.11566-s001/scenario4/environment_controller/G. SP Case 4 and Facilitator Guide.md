| THE PATIENT’S STORY: | Parent: “My child seems more tired than usual, even after sleeping. They are struggling to keep up with schoolwork and daily activities. They aren’t as physically active as they used to be and they aren’t eating as much lately.”  Child: “I don’t know, I’m just tired”  Upon further expansion:  “I don’t really know when it started… maybe a few weeks ago? I just feel tired all the time. Even when I get a full night’s sleep, I wake up feeling like I didn’t really rest. Sometimes I feel lightheaded and feel my heart pounding when I’m just sitting.”  “It’s been hard to get through the day at school because I’m always sleepy, and I can’t really focus during class. I used to hang out with my friends after school or ride my bike, but now I just want to lie down when I get home.”  “It’s been really frustrating. I used to love playing soccer with friends, but now I just don’t have the energy. It makes me sad that I can’t keep up with them like I used to. And I think my friends are starting to notice—like, they stopped asking me to come out and play as much. Right now I feel like I’m missing out on stuff, and it’s kind of lonely.” |

| DIAGNOSIS AND DIFFERENTIAL | |
| Diagnosis with support from positive and negative history and PE findings | Anemia  Positive history of fatigue, decreased activity (no longer playing sports due to low energy), difficulty concentrating, tachycardia at rest, lightheadedness without clear triggers, pallor, and pale conjunctiva. Patient also does not eat meat, and has low iron intake. |
| Differential with support from positive and negative history and PE findings | Differential diagnosis includes   * Hypothyroidism, given fatigue and decreased concentration. Less likely without constipation, skin changes, cold intolerance, or family history of thyroid disease. * Depression, given fatigue, withdrawal from usual activities, difficulty concentrating. Less likely without feelings of sadness/hopelessness. * Vitamin deficiency (B12) deficiency: Low intake of animal products, fatigue, pallor. Less likely without neurologic complaints like numbness/tingling in extremities. |
| MANAGEMENT OR DIAGNOSTIC PLAN | Order a CBC, Ferritin, Iron, TIBC, BMP, TSH, T3, T4.  Start an iron supplement or multivitamin.  Encourage a varied diet. |
