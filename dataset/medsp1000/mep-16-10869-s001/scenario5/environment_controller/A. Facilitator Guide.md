**Case A. 22q11.21 deletion syndrome**

**Case 3A. A 20-year-old pregnant woman presents at 23 weeks gestation for genetic counseling due to her personal history of childhood repair of interrupted aorta type B, hearing loss and mild learning issues. The genetic counselor notes that the patient is quite small and has some atypical facial features. The patient attends on her own and appears quite anxious and you note she stopped her anxiety medication for her pregnancy.**

* *Use interrupted aorta type B, hearing loss, learning issues, and anxiety to search for possible diagnoses.*
* + *The cause of 22q11.21 syndrome is a microdeletion due to unequal crossover at meiosis due to duplicons.*
  + *We can detect most cases via fluorescent in situ hybridization (FISH), but some smaller deletions cannot be picked up. For this reason as well as broader testing, we often start with a microarray unless results are needed quickly.*
  + *Testing options for the fetus if desired include concurrent FISH for the patient and fetus and/or fetal echo if decisions might be based on complex cardiac malformations.*
* *22qdel-87% sporadic, 13% inherited as AD trait.*
