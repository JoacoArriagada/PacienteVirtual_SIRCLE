**Case 3A. A 20- year-old pregnant woman presents at 23 weeks gestation for genetic counseling due to her personal history of childhood repair of interrupted aorta type B, hearing loss and mild learning issues. The genetic counselor notes that the patient is quite small and has some atypical facial features. The patient attends on her own and appears quite anxious and you note she stopped her anxiety medication for her pregnancy.**

-Assign one person to play the role of the physician and one to be the pregnant patient; role play delivery of the information to the patient.
