**Case 2A. An 11- year-old boy was referred to genetics clinic from a local comprehensive cleft palate clinic. He had his cleft palate repaired early in life. He has residual hypernasal speech due to velopharyngeal insufficiency, is petite, and is on an individualized learning plan (IEP) for learning issues. He struggles in school academically and is frequently bullied. He is examined in genetics clinic where his physical exam shows growth parameters at the 5%, long face, tubular nose, squared ears, loss of nasolabial folds, and slender, tapered fingers. His mother shows a photo of his father which shows very similar features.**

-Assign one person to play the role of the physician and one to be the mother; role play delivery of the information to the family.
