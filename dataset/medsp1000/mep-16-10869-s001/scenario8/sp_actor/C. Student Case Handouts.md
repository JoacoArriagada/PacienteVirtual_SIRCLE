**Case 4B. A very active, 3- year-old boy with mild hypotonia comes for genetics evaluation due to his diagnosis of autism spectrum disorder.  Due to what appears to be isolated autism spectrum disorder (ASD), he undergoes standard testing for genetic causes of ASD including fragile X syndrome and microarray analysis.  He is found to have a deletion of the Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) gene to the surprise of all.**

●      Assign one person to play the role of the physician and one to be the mother. Role play delivery of the information to the family.

* + *How might the family react to this news in terms of the understanding these unexpected results? How do they feel about physical disability with shortened lifespan versus autism spectrum disorder?*
