**Case 1A. A 4 month-old baby girl is admitted to the pediatric service for failure to thrive (FTT). On physical exam she is noted to be petite, have somewhat over-folded ear helices and has a murmur. An echocardiogram shows a small cardiac ventricular septal defect. The baby is in the maternal grandmother's custody. The family history reveals baby was removed from maternal custody due to her recent history of heroin use and rapidly cycling bipolar disorder. In an effort to understand the cause for the baby’s issues, genetics is consulted.**

-Assign one person to play the role of the physician and one to be the grandmother; role play delivery of the information to the family and lead students to consider the following:

* *How might the family react to this news in terms of the new understanding of the mother's issues and baby's future?*
* *How might they feel about the chance of physical issues? mental health issues?*
* *Do you think the family will want to know everything at once or not?*
* *Might they feel grief, relief, overwhelmed, guilty, empowered?*
