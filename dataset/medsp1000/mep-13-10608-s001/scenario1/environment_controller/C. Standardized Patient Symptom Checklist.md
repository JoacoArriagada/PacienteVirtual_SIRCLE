**PTSD STANDARD PATIENT SCENARIO: BOB ROBERTS**

**(THIS IS GIVEN TO THE SP)**

**BOB’S CURRENT SYMPTOMS (THAT HE INITIALLY DISCLOSES TO STUDENT):**

* Poor sleep: Trouble with falling asleep and staying asleep due to memories and thoughts (related to trauma), nightmares, tossing/turning, and waking up yelling/screaming (which upsets wife)
* Alcohol Use: Bob drinks 2-3 beers each weekday night and a six-pack each weekend night. He does not think that his alcohol use is a problem, and states that he drinks to help him sleep.

**ADDITIONAL SYMPTOMS (THAT HE MAY DISCLOSE AS INTERVIEW PROGRESSES):**

**INTRUSION SYMPTOMS:**

* Frequent thoughts/memories about the trauma (“film replaying in my head”)
* Nightmares about the traumatic event (1 x per week) after which he often wakes up screaming

**AVOIDANCE SYMPTOMS:**

* Bob avoids talking about the traumatic event, especially with his wife who often pressures him to talk about it.

**NEGATIVE ALTERATIONS IN COGNITION AND MOOD:**

* Feelings of guilt related to the accident and that it was “his fault” because he had worked on the generator a week prior to the explosion.
* Bob does not really get enjoyment out of life, and often feels detached from his wife and children.

**HYPERAROUSAL SYMPTOMS**:

* Hypervigilance: Bob is anxious when he is not in a familiar area, and is constantly scanning and “on guard”.
* Hyperstartle: Bob startles easily when he hears loud noises (e.g., tools being dropped at work, alarms, etc.).
* Irritability: Bob is easily irritated and angered. He gets into frequent arguments with others, and has lost jobs as a result. He often feels that other people are “stupid”.

**DEPRESSION SYMPTOMS:**

* Bob does endorse the following symptoms of depression (that overlap with PTSD symptoms):
  + Lack of enjoyment in activities
  + Excessive Guilt
  + Poor sleep
* Bob does not currently have any suicidal ideation, plan, or intent. He does not have a history of suicide attempts or any past history of mental health treatment.
