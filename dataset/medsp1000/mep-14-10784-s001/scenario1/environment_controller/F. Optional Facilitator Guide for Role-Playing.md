**Appendix F – Optional Facilitator’s Guide for Role Playing**

**In the Spirituality Session**

**Role Play Number 1: The Spiritual History**

Instructions to Chaplain

In this role play, the student will practice taking a spiritual history using the FICA model. You should provide the student with the background that the student is meeting you, the patient, for the first time the day after an uncomplicated cholecystectomy.

In playing the patient, we suggest that you use the spiritual and faith background with which you are most familiar. General parameters for your story should be:

* You consider yourself a spiritual person. You pray regularly, and consider your faith to be a “big part” of your life.
* You do identify as a member of your faith community.
* You do have a concern, about which you never felt comfortable asking your surgical team. Keep the concern relatively simple.
* You are worried and also disappointed in yourself for not bringing up these concerns earlier. You just did not think the doctor would listen, or be able to help.
* You should at first be hesitant to respond to the student, but open up to the student as the history progresses.
