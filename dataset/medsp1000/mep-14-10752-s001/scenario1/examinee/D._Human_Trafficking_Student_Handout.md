You are an attending physician working in the ER. An 18-year-old Asian female arrives with the chief complaint of “rash on leg.” When you meet her, she is thin and appears pale and uncomfortable. She is accompanied by her boyfriend who seems very protective of her. You ask her why she is here, and the patient looks at her boyfriend quickly, then stares at the floor without answering your question. Her boyfriend explains that she speaks very little English and can’t understand you well. He immediately states that he is willing to serve as the interpreter. He says that she is afraid of doctors so she will be more comfortable if he stays. You notice that while the boyfriend is talking, the patient shifts her gaze between the boyfriend and the floor, but never looks anywhere else.
