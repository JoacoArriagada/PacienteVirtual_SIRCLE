A 22-year old Latina woman, Ms. Garcia, is a recent immigrant to Houston. She primarily speaks Spanish and lives with her boyfriend, since most of her family is abroad. She doesn’t have health insurance, and after a fight with her boyfriend she is unsure where to go for her dislocated elbow. She ends up at a Harris Health county hospital, Ben Taub.

There are multiple bruises on her torso and arms in addition to her dislocated elbow.

As a resident attempts to take a history in broken Spanish instead of calling language services, she tearfully asks what her options might be when she returns home to her boyfriend. When it becomes clear that the resident does not actually understand her, she becomes uncomfortable, does not trust the physician enough to reveal how she hurt her elbow, limits herself to one-word answers, and says she “fell down the stairs.”
