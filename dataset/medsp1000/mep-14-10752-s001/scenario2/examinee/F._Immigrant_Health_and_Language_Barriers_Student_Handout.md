A 22-year old Latina woman, Ms. Garcia, is a recent immigrant to Houston. She primarily speaks Spanish and lives with her boyfriend, since most of her family is abroad. Ms. Garcia doesn’t have health insurance, so after a fight with her boyfriend, she is unsure where to go for her dislocated elbow. She ends up at a Harris Health county hospital, Ben Taub, where she receives treatment under EMTALA (the Emergency Medical Treatment and Labor Act).

In the ED, the nurse notes the presence of multiple bruises on Ms. Garcia’s torso and arms, in addition to her dislocated elbow.
