# Station B: New-onset Atrial Fibrillation Teaching Points

1. Evaluate reversible causes of new onset atrial fibrillation (A fib) before initiating other interventions. Most common non-cardiac causes of A fib include: subclinical hyperthyroidism, alcohol consumption, post-operative complications, and pericarditis.   
2. Goals of A fib management include rate and rhythm control plus anticoagulation.   
3. CHADS2-VASc score assesses risk of stroke in patients with A fib and determines need for anticoagulation.
