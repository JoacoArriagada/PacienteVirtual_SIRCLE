B. Scenario conditions initially

1. Ms. Heffernan is a 75 year old woman who had presented to her local urgent care center with cold-like symptoms. There, she was discovered to have a very rapid heartbeat on exam. The urgent care doctor wanted to immediately shock her heart, but the patient herself felt this was drastic, and wanted to see her PCPs (the learners) first to get their opinion.
