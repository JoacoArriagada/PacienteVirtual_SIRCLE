Progression:

With oxygen via non-rebreather, sats may rise to the mid 90s while moderate / severe respiratory distress continues.

If respiratory failure is not definitively managed, at 4 minutes after oxygen placement a worsening trend begins:
- saturation drops
- respiratory rate then drops
- impending respiratory failure progresses toward respiratory / cardiac arrest
- VS may become HR 199, BP 81/46, RR 55, Sats 90%

If intubated, a recovery trend begins:
- oxygen saturation rises back to the high 90s
- heart rate falls
- patient becomes stable for transfer to the PICU once tube placement is confirmed
