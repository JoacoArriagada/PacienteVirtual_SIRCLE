Patient is groggy, so history of prior contrast reaction is available only on questioning husband after reaction starts. Proper patient assessment, treatment, and resource utilization stabilizes patient’s anaphylactoid reaction and contrast aspiration events. Inadequate treatment results in cardiac arrest and “rescue” by simulated resuscitation team. Patient care needs to be transferred to, then from Radiology staff.
