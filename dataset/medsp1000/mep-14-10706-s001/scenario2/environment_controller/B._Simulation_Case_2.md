| **Brief narrative description of case**  *Include the presenting patient chief complaint and overall learner goals for this case* | 3 month old infant with unrepaired tetralogy of Fallot admitted for viral illness, with diarrhea and mild dehydration. The patient had poor enteral intake and increased losses over the 24 hours prior to admission. Baby fed well in the ED and was admitted for observation and enteral resuscitation. Overnight the baby continued to feed but had increased stool output. This morning during a venipuncture for labs, the patient becomes agitated and more cyanotic. |
