**Debriefing Ground Rules**

This is a safe environment to reflect on and discuss individual and team performance so we can learn and improve from what went well and from our mistakes.

We acknowledge that you might perform differently in a real situation than in the simulated environment.

In order to maintain this safe environment we ask that:

* You are polite and respectful about others’ actions and remarks.
* You don’t discuss the performance of your colleagues outside of this debriefing.

We expect everyone to participate in the discussion.

If you feel distressed about a particular experience, please do not hesitate to contact one of the facilitators.
