| **Brief narrative description of case**  *Include the presenting patient chief complaint and overall learner goals for this case* | 3 hour old infant born at full term with a pre-natal diagnosis of TGA (no ventricular septal defect). Maternal history is unremarkable. Baby was born by spontaneous vaginal delivery with normal resuscitation in the delivery room by the NICU team. Apgars were 7 and 8. NICU team placed umbilical venous and arterial lines, as well as a peripheral IV (PIV). Pulse oximetry (sPO2) on room air was 81%. Prostaglandin infusion was started. Baby was then transferred to the ICU one hour ago. |
