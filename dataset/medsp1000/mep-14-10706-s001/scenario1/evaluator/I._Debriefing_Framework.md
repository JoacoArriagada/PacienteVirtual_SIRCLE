**Debriefing Framework**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Phase** | **Inquiry/Script** |
| **Introduction** | Facilitator: We are going to debrief together for about XX minutes. During this time, I would like you to reflect on the scenario you just completed and most importantly, I would like everyone to participate in the discussion. My role is to help facilitate, but not dominate the discussion. Before we begin, we should acknowledge that this was simulation, and we may have acted differently in real life. Now, let’s focus our discussion on our team interactions and the process of care delivered to the patient. |
| **Reactions** | Facilitator: How are you all feeling now that’s its over? Or tell me your reactions to this event?  Facilitator: Great. Other thoughts? Let’s talk about teamwork and communication during the situation. |
| **Descriptive of Case** | Facilitator: Can someone please give me a short summary of the case? |
| **Analysis**  **(Crisis Resource)** | Facilitator: (open-ended questions)  First let’s talk about how the team worked together.  What went well?  What didn’t go well?  Let’s explore how the team interacted with each other?  Tell me how you decided on your roles assigned clearly and early?  Let’s talk about how the leader get identified?  Let’s focus on communication in the situation?  Facilitator: Great, let’s move on to discussing the medical management |
| **Analysis**  **(Medical Management)** | Facilitator to the Participant #1,  You were the code leader, why don’t you walk us through your thought process around the medical management of this patient? |
| **Summary** | Facilitator: So to conclude, let’s identify the key learning points from this scenario.  To the group- What would you say are the 2 or 3 main take-home points or messages from the scenario?  OR  Individually- Let’s go around the room and have each of you describe your main take-home message/point from this scenario. |
