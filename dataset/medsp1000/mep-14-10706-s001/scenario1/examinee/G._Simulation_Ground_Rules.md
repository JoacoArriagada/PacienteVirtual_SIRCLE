**Simulation Ground Rules**

We believe that everyone participating in simulation activities wants to learn, improve their clinical skills and cares about doing their best.

We ask you suspend disbelief and temporarily allow yourself to believe something that isn’t true.

Suspending disbelief is how you will get the most value from your time spent in simulation activities.

Recognizing the limitations of simulation, we will do our best to help make the simulation seem as realistic as we can.

Please act in a professional manner with your colleagues, facilitators and the simulator.
