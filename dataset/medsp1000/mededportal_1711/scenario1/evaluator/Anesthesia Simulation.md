# I. Title: Anesthesia Simulation Case 6 - 22 year old for appendectomy

Topic: Anaphylaxis Last Modified: 12/24/2008

II. Target Audience: All levels of anesthesia students and providers

# III. Learning Objectives or Assessment Objectives

A. Learning Objectives 1. Demonstrate recognition of the clinical manifestations of anaphylaxis. 2. Demonstrate knowledge of the goals of treatment. 3. Demonstrate knowledge of the first line management for anaphylaxis. 4. Demonstrate knowledge of the appropriate dose of epinephrine for anaphylaxis and risk of administering too much. 5. Demonstrate knowledge of the second line management for anaphylaxis.

VI. Pilot Testing and Revisions

A. Numbers of participants – this scenario has been simulated by over 300 CA-1 anesthesia residents at the University of Washington over the last 16 years. Numerous changes to the scenario have been made over the years.   
B. Performance expectations, anticipated management mistakes: 1. Many residents take 5 or more minutes to diagnose anaphylaxis. 2. Many residents fail to examine the skin or breath sounds when they observe hypotension.

3. About $1 0 \%$ of residents diagnose SVT for hypotension with reflex tachycardia. 4. Many residents administer an excessive dose of epinephrine. C. Evaluation form for participants: Participants receive a detailed printed record of the case simulation and printed debriefing of their case management.
