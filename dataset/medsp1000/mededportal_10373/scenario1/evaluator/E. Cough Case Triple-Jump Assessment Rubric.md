Student Post-Encounter Triple-Jump Assessment Rubric

Accepted antibiotic regimens based on IDSA or ATS guidelines and points were awarded based on the grading scheme in the table below.

* + Azithromycin 500mg PO x1 then 250mg PO once a day for 4 days
  + Azithromycin 500mg PO once a day for 3 days
  + Clarithromycin 500mg PO every 12 hours for 5 days
  + Clarithromycin XL 1000mg PO once a day for 5 days
  + Doxycycline 100mg PO every 12 hours

*Grading Notes:*

* *Students who entered 2 antibiotics or 2 antibiotic regimens, they received 0 points unless they specifically said OR. For example:*
  + *A student who wrote azithromycin 500mg po qday x3 OR clarithromycin 500mg po bid x5 received 5 points.*
  + *A student who wrote azithromycin 500mg po qday x3 and clarithromycin 500mg po bid x5 received 0 points.*
* *Students who selected the wrong antibiotic regardless of the correctness of the regimen received 0 points.*
