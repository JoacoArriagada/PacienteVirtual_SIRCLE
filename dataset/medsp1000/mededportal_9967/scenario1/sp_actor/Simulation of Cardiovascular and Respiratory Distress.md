Parent will arrive five minutes in the scenario and provide medical history including allergies, medications, PMH, FH, SH. Patient will provide brief HPI.  **Past Medical History:** History of asthma since infancy with multiple emergency visits for asthma related illness within the past year. She was hospitalized as a child for asthma exacerbation but did not require PICU admission.  **Family History:** Father has asthma.  **Surgical History:** None.  **Social History:** High school student who participates in golf. She does not smoke or use ETOH. She quit basketball due to her asthma symptoms. | |

Dialogue and Reveal Boundaries

- Dialogue statements: hard to breath, shortness of breath, 2-3 word sentences; still cannot catch breath; breathing improving and able to speak in 4-5 word sentences; breathing continuing to improve.
- Information given upon request: increased dust at school due to new construction.

Portrayal Notes

- Patient will not discuss the construction at school unless asked.
- The parent arrives five minutes into the scenario and provides history upon request.
