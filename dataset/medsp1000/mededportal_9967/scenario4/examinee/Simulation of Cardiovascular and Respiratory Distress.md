**Title: Simulation of Cardiovascular and Respiratory Distress for the Pediatric Clerkship**

Actors and Starting Situation

- Roles present: parent, nurse.
- The simulated parent will provide patient history to students.
- The simulated nurse will provide IV access, medications, oxygen or other tasks requested by students.
- Students are oriented to the simulation lab including the mannequin, monitor, equipment, medications, and nursing staff. They are provided a telephone to call for help if needed. Vital signs are not given initially. Blood pressures are not automated throughout the simulation.
