Dialogue and Reveal Boundaries

- Dialogue statements: HPI provided by mother; baby cried all day and nothing helped; not eating well.
- Information given upon request: mother noticed retractions approximately 1 hour ago and describes it as funny breathing; only 2 wet diapers today.

Portrayal Notes

- The mother is present throughout the entire scenario and provides history. She is anxious.
- The infant has copious secretions over the nose, face, and hands.
