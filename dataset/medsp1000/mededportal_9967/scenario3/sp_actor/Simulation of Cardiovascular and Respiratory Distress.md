Dialogue and Reveal Boundaries

- Dialogue statements: patient is light-headed and has difficulty speaking due to tongue swelling; she can only describe being at track practice and the leg pain; she may have been bit by something; later she describes being less dizzy and tongue swelling; once the sting is discovered, she confirms allergy to bee stings and remembers the sting at track practice.
- Information given upon request: patient is allergic to bee stings; the parent gives patient history; she did not have her EpiPen with her.

Portrayal Notes

- The bee sting is over the left leg and may be covered by pants.
- The patient is dizzy/light-headed from hypotension and has difficulty talking due to tongue swelling. She does not describe the bite until it is discovered on exam; after that, she recalls the bite and gives the HPI.
- The parent arrives five minutes into the scenario and provides history upon request.
