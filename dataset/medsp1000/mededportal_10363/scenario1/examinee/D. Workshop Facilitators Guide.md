# INTRODUCTION TO CLINICAL MEDICINE THE CLINICAL EXAMINATION

Examination	of	the	Infant Workshop

# Description	of	Workshop	Activity:

This	half-day	“Examination	of	the	Infant”	Workshop	is	a	component	of	the	pre-clerkship medica students’	Introduction	to	Clinical	Medicine course. Pediatric	faculty	facilitators	lead	an	interactive demonstration	of	the	infant	physical	exam	with	small	groups	of	students.

# Detailed	Workshop	Schedule:

# 2:45	– 4:10	pm			Examination	of	the	Infant

1. 		Introduce	the	parent/s	and	infant	to	the	group.	Do	not	reveal	the	age	of	the child.

2. Ask	students	to	observe	the	child’s	behaviors.

3. Examine	the	patient. Students	may	participate	in some aspects of the examination.

4. 		Students	may	ask	parents	questions	about	the	child.
