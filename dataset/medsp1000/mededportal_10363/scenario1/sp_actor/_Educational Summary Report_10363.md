# Methods

Recruitment of infants, toddlers, and faculty is done at least 6 to 8 weeks in advance. There should be one infant (0 to 12 months) with a parent assigned to each group. Children are recruited from within the medical school community (medical students, residents, and attendings) for the workshop through $\textstyle \in$ -mail (Appendix A). Parents are asked to bring the child’s most recent weight and length measurements to the workshop.

The workshop takes place in our clinical skills center, a simulation space with multiple examination rooms. An exam room with enough space for an exam table and chairs for all students, the parent, and the faculty member is needed for each group.

When a parent and child enter the room, they are introduced to the group without revealing the age of the child.

The exam may be done on the exam table or the parent’s lap. Students may participate at the discretion of the facilitator and parent. Students may ask parents questions about the child. Facilitators ask the parent to report the child’s most recent weight and length. Parents are often sensitive and sometimes naturally defensive about their child’s growth and development.
