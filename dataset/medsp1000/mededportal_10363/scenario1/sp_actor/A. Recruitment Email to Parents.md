# Recruitment Email Communication to Parents of Infants

Dear Parents:

We would like to invite any of you with infants from 0 to 12 months old to participate in the "Examination of the Infant" workshop. The session is held in the clinical skills center.

This session would involve about 1 hour of your time. The purpose of this workshop is to demonstrate for preclerkship medical students how to approach an infant differently than they might approach an adult for a physical examination. A pediatric attending will perform a physical examination on the infant, with you present, for a group of 12 to 14 students. If okay with you and the infant is enjoying the experience, students might perform some basic aspects of the physical examination, such as listening to the infant’s heart rate.

The pediatrician will also want to emphasize how rapidly an infant grows and develops in the first year of life, so please come equipped with the infant’s height and weight measurements (and head circumference, if you have it) from the most recent doctor’s visit. Students in the group will be learning how to document this information on growth and developmental charts, so this information would contribute greatly to the session.
