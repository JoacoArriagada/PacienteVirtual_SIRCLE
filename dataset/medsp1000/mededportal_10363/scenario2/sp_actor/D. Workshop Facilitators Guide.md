# Detailed	Workshop	Schedule:

# 2:45	– 4:10	pm			Examination	of	the	Toddler

1. 		Introduce	the	parent/s	and	toddler	to	the	group.	Do	not	reveal	the	age	of	the child.

2. Ask	students	to	observe	the	child’s	behaviors. Milestones may be reported by the parent when asked.

4. 		Students	may	ask	parents	questions	about	the	child.

5. 		Ask	parent	to	report	the	child’s most	recent	weight	and	length.
