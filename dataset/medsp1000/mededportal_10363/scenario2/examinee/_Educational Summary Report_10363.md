# Methods

The workshop takes place on a single afternoon for the entire medical school class. The class is divided into groups of 12 to 14 students. One facilitator is assigned to each student group.

There should be one toddler (13 to 24 months) with a parent assigned to each group.

Our workshop takes place in our clinical skills center, a simulation space with multiple examination rooms. An exam room with enough space for an exam table and chairs for all students, the parent, and the faculty member is needed for each group.

When a parent and child enter the room, they are introduced to the group without revealing the age of the child.

Students may ask parents questions about the child.
