<u>**Chief Complaint:**</u>  

"You called me to come in immediately to talk about my mother."

<u>**Scenario:**</u>

Your mother, Sarah Dunn, called you earlier today at work stating that she was experiencing chest pain. You immediately told her to call 911 and get to the hospital. You met your mom in the emergency room and stayed for several hours. After talking with the ER doctor, a decision was made to admit your mom to the cardiology service for further evaluation and care. Once your mom got a room upstairs, you went home to get some clothes and eat dinner before heading back to the hospital. You then got a phone call from a healthcare worker involved in the care of your mother stating that you needed to come in immediately for an update.  Her status had changed. She is stable but needs to be moved to the Coronary Care Unit/Intensive Care Unit.  You are not provided with any further details.

<u>**Profile:**</u>  

Your mother’s medical problems include:

- HTN – controlled with medications
- Diabetes – controlled with insulin
- Heart attack – 10 years ago. She takes multiple medications for it.
- Kidney problems – told she had problems, but you are just monitoring right now

Your mom lives alone, and you and your family live close by. You have a good relationship with your mother, and you were the one who helped take care of her during her first heart attack. You make all of her medical decisions. You also know from your mom’s doctor that any chest pain should be taken seriously.
