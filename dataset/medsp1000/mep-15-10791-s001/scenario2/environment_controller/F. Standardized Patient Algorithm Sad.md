| The team will tell you that they made a medication error that resulted in the need to transfer your mom to an intensive care unit (ICU) setting for careful monitoring. You immediately break down. You are sad. Your mom is so important to you and such a part of your daily life. You feel incredibly responsible and guilty. You are upset and start to cry. You can’t believe these people almost killed your parent. You really want answers but it’s hard to focus and ask questions. Whether you stay SAD/UPSET or begin to de-escalate to acceptance and to calmer concern depends on how team responds. | ***Student Team Behavior:***  *The group will introduce themselves (hopefully) and explain that a medical error was committed.*  **SP Sadness level = Very high anxiety and guilt; SP Vocal tone = tearful; unable to speak easily; crying; punctuated by guilt and exclamations of  “No” and “Oh God,” etc.**  **SP Action to a failure to introduce all team members:** “I’m so sorry, I don’t understand what’s going on. Who are all of you?” (Students should introduce everyone.)  **SP Action AFTER Introductions**: “How could you all make this mistake? Who is responsible? Maybe I caused this by leaving her here!” | Students should try to answer your last two questions.  **SP Sadness level = Very high anxiety and guilt; SP Vocal tone = tearful, slightly calmer; punctuated by guilt and exclamations of “No” and “Oh God,” etc.**  SP Action if only one person responds for team:  “What do you think? How could have let this happen?” (directed to those not speaking)  **NOTE: Teams may respond differently. Two general response patterns are:**  **RESPONSIVE:**   * Takes responsibility for mistake * Is calm in voice and demeanor * Is empathetic to your sadness, worry, anxiety and guilt.   **DISMISSIVE OR NON-EMPATHETIC:**   * Blames individual team members or fails to take responsibility as a team * Voice and demeanor are dismissive of your concerns, unable to understand your feelings * Does not acknowledge your feelings in a genuine way | SP ACTION will depend on student response pattern. | Students should try to answer your last questions.  SP ACTION will depend on student response pattern. | Students should try to answer your last questions.  SP DISPOSITION will depend on student response pattern OVER THE COURSE OF THE ENCOUNTER. |
| IF STUDENT TEAM RESPONSE PATTERN IS: RESPONSIVE  **SP Sadness level = Slightly lower;**  **SP Vocal tone = tearful; still punctuated by guilt, concern and exclamations.**  SP Action to Responses:   * Is my mother going to die? * Will this affect her heart? * How long does she have to be in the ICU? | IF STUDENT TEAM RESPONSE PATTERN IS: RESPONSIVE  **SP Sadness level = Slightly lower;**  **SP Vocal tone = Calmer delivery with fewer exclamations; You are beginning to calm down, accept that you are not responsible.**  SP Action to Responses:  Gather information   * How are we going to make sure this doesn’t happen again? | IF STUDENT TEAM RESPONSE PATTERN IS: RESPONSIVE  **SP’s sadness should have dissipated to just concern for mother. SP’s tone should be calm, acceptance that you are not responsible, but worried.**  Student team may or may not attempt to close the encounter, depending on how the exchanges go, and the students’ use of time. |
| IF STUDENT TEAM RESPONSE PATTERN IS: DISMISSIVE, NON-EMPATHETIC  **SP Sadness level = Remains very high anxiety and guilt;**  **SP Vocal tone = still tearful; unable to speak easily; crying; punctuated by guilt and exclamations of “No” and “Oh God,” etc.**  SP Action to Responses:   * Is my mother going to die? * Will this affect her heart? * How long does she have to be in the ICU? | IF STUDENT TEAM RESPONSE PATTERN IS: DISMISSIVE, NON-EMPATHETIC  **SP Sadness level = Remains very high anxiety and guilt;**  **SP Vocal tone = still tearful; unable to speak easily; crying; punctuated by guilt and exclamations of “No” and “Oh God,” etc.**  SP Action to Responses:  Gather information   * How am I going to make sure this doesn’t happen again * Ask to have your parent transferred or given another team. | IF STUDENT TEAM RESPONSE PATTERN IS: DISMISSIVE, NON-EMPATHETIC  **SP’s sadness, guilt and tearfulness will not dissipate very much, and SP will want another team caring for her. May shift to concern for mother. SP’s tone should be upset and guilty.**  Student team may or may not attempt to close the encounter, depending on how the exchanges go, and the student’s use of time. |
