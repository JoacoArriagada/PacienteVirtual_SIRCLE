**Learner Case 1.**

A 77-year-old male with hypertension presents to the Emergency Department with dyspnea and bilateral leg edema. Blood pressure 155/85, heart rate 115 beats per minute. Creatinine clearance is 55 mL/min. EKG reveals atrial fibrillation (afib). An echocardiogram shows a normal ejection fraction with grade 1 diastolic dysfunction with normal valve function.
