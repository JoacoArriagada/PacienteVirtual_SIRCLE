**Faculty Teaching Points: Case 1 – Model for behavior change: Tobacco Cessation**

1. Help the patient understand that smoking is directly linked to his current illness (COPD, current hospitalization).
2. If a patient is pre-contemplative, you should recommend that he quit smoking without being too “pushy”. It may be more useful to explore with the patient his reasons for smoking and his concerns about quitting. Then you can start to target motivational reasons for cessation.
