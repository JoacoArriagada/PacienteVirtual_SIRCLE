**Faculty Teaching Points: Case 4 – Delivering Bad News**

1. Assess the patient’s understanding of their current illness as well as the patient’s social support system.
2. Ensure the students use simple, straight-forward language.
3. One big challenge of this case is student comfort with a limited knowledge/data set (ie they do not have all of the answers). It is important to help students be comfortable with the limits of their knowledge and defining a path forward for the patient.
