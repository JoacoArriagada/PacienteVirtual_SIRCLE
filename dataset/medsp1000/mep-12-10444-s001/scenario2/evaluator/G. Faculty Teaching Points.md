**Faculty Teaching Points: Case 2 – Difficult Doctor-Patient Encounter**

1. Try to address and show empathy for the reasons behind the patient’s anger (stress about work demands) rather than just reacting to patient’s anger.
2. Help patient understand that antiobiotics can be harmful if used inappropriately.
3. In dealing with an angry patient, it is helpful to focus on plan of action “what we are going to do for your symptoms” rather than “what we are *not* going to do”
