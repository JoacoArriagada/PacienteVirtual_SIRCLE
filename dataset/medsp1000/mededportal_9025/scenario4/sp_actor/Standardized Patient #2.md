Standardized Patient Scenario 2 -- CC

**CC**: Right arm pain

**HPI**: CC is a 45 year-old male with a significant past medical history for alcoholism and depression who first noted that his right arm hurt three months for no apparent reason. He works as an auto mechanic and wonders if the tasks at work may have something to do with it. The pain has been slowly progressive since that time and is described as a burning/stabbing pain down his arm from the shoulder to his right elbow, occasionally the hand. His neck also hurts but not as badly. The pain is especially notable when using his right arm after a day of work and he seems to notice that looking up can trigger a jolt down the arm. Nothing really seems to help except rest. The pain score ranges from 6-10/10. He thinks his hands get numb sometimes, especially at night. He also complains of occasional urinary urgency and even claims to have had an episode of incontinence a week ago. He has fallen a few times since this has all begun as well. He hasn’t had any significant systematic symptoms associated with this such as fevers, night sweats, chills, weight loss, swelling, shortness of breath, or fatigue.

Since this pain has begun he has been using ibuprofen or naproxen and this seems to help but only mildly so. His PCP gave him some muscle relaxant and those also provide mild relief. Otherwise he has not gone through any other treatment modalities or had any diagnostic testing.

His sleep has been affected by the pain in that he gets up with discomfort occasionally. He continues to work as a mechanic. He is considering pursuing disability and even wonders aloud if he can initiate a worker’s compensation claims. He denies any additional sadness or thoughts of harming himself because of this situation but admits to a history of depression and alcoholism. He went through an alcohol rehabilitation program five years ago and hasn’t drank since.

**Past medical history**: alcoholism, depression

**Past Surgical History**: left shoulder arthroscopy, right hip arthroscopy

**Medications**: naltrexone, paroxetine

**Allergies**: celebrex – he tried this during his hip pain experience

**Family History**: He doesn’t know his biologic father but has been told he was a raging alcoholic. His mother is alive and well but has depression. His sister is alive and well. His maternal grandparents each had heart attacks and high cholesterol. His grandmother died of breast cancer.

**Social History**: He admits to trying most illicit substances when he was younger but hasn’t in a long time. He smokes tobacco cigarettes, about one pack per day. He admits to having been an alcoholic as above. He is single and has one teenage son that lives with the child’s mother. He denies any domestic issues and any history of being the victim of verbal, physical, or sexual abuse.

**Review of Systems**: Negative for fever, weight change, altered mental status, headache, visual change, chest pain, shortness of breath, abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, constipation, diarrhea, dysuria, unusual bleeding, edema, rash, suicidal ideation.
