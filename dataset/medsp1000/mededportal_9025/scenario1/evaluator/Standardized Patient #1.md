Standardized Patient Scenario 1 -- DB

<u>**CC**</u>: Low back pain

<u>**HPI**</u>:  DB is a 45 year-old male with no significant past medical illness who first noted that his back hurt three months ago while playing softball.  The pain has been slowly progressive since that time and is described as a deep ache focused over the right low back and upper buttock area.  The pain score ranges from 2-6/10.  It is especially notable in the morning while trying to get his shoes on and while sitting. Recently, he developed a cold.  With every cough he gets a jolt of pain and this seems to have further worsened his pain and now he notices a pins and needles sensation over the right lateral shin and occasional aching there as well.  He doesn’t note any other neurologic or voiding dysfunction.  Furthermore, he hasn’t had any significant systematic symptoms associated with this such as fevers, night sweats, chills, weight loss, swelling, shortness of breath, or fatigue.

Since this pain has begun he has been using ibuprofen or naproxen and this seems to help.  Otherwise he has not gone through any other treatment modalities or had any diagnostic testing.

His sleep has been relatively unaffected by the pain, but he has had to stop many of the extracurricular activities he enjoys, such a golf and softball.  He works at a local hospital as a lab technician and denies pursuing any disability or workers compensation claims.  He denies any sadness or thoughts of harming himself because of this situation.

<u>**Past medical history**</u>:  seasonal allergies and high cholesterol

<u>**Past Surgical History**</u>: vasectomy

<u>**Medications**</u>: atorvastatin 20mg daily, loratidine 10mg prn

<u>**Allergies**</u>:  NKDA (except seasonal)

<u>**Family History**</u>:  His father had a similar episode of back pain two years ago that was due to a disc herniation, and was recently diagnosed with a blocked blood vessel in his heart (no heart attack).  His mother has seasonal allergies.  His maternal grandfather had a heart bypass operation in his sixties.  His maternal grandmother has something wrong with her nerves (she can’t feel her feet to walk properly).  An aunt has had melanoma twice (both seem to have been resected successfully)

<u>**Social History**</u>:  He denies any history of illicit substance abuse or tobacco use.  He admits to drinking alcohol, especially on weekends while watching sporting events.  He is currently married and has three young boys.  He denies any domestic issues and any history of being the victim of verbal, physical, or sexual abuse.

<u>**Review of Systems**</u>:  Negative for fever, weight change, altered mental status, headache, visual change, chest pain, shortness of breath, abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, constipation, diarrhea, dysuria, unusual bleeding, edema, rash, depression, suicidal ideation.

Vitals are normal

Mental Status is grossly normal.  His mood is pleasant and affect appropriate.

Heart, Lungs, and Abdomen are normal except for mild central obesity
