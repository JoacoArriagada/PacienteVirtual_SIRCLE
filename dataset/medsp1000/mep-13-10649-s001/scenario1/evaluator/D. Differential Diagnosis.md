Appendix D: Differential Diagnoses

After the initial interview, the in-room facilitator interjects a brief time-out to discuss key aspects of the case with the students. Before ordering laboratory tests, the group is encouraged to synthesize available information and consider a broad differential. This is a list of targeted diagnoses students are expected to consider:

* Alcohol withdrawal
* Lithium toxicity
* Diabetes insipidus (central or nephrogenic)
* Hyperglycemia and diabetic ketoacidosis
* Other drug intoxication or withdrawal
* Metabolic derangement
* Thyroid dysfunction
* Subdural hematoma
* Liver failure
