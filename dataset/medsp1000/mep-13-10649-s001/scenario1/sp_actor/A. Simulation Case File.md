**Ideal Scenario Flow**

*The patient also reports anxiety and a tremor. If asked about alcohol use, he has been drinking daily for the past several months. He has no history of medically complicated withdrawal. He continues to feel tremulous, tired, and thirsty. The patient complains of right-sided rib pain and tells a story of falling off a ladder 2 weeks ago and then being prescribed naproxen, which he has been taking. Since then, he has also been noticing increased thirst and urination as well as a tremor and general malaise. The patient asks if the team thinks he has a problem with alcohol.*
