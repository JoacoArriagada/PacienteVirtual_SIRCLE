**Appendix A: Teaching Scenario Instructors Guide**

**Case 2**

Set-up: The student in this scenario has been on shift for a couple of hours, has picked up 6 patients already and is struggling to provide adequate care to the patients. The nurse on shift brings it to the simulated supervisor’s attention that several patients and family members are concerned about things the student has said or done. The student then presents several patients, both with inappropriate plans and no inkling on the student’s part that they are not on the right track.
