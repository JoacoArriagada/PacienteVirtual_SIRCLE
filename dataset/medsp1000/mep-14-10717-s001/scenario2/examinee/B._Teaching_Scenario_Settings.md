**Appendix B: Teaching Scenario Settings**

**Case 2**

The setting: The RN working the low acuity side pulls you aside and asks “Who’s that terrible medical student working today?” He goes on to say that two families have complained to him already in the first two hours about the medical student failing to discuss what was going on, and another patient was in tears because she believed the student had said that she was too fat and needed to lose weight to solve her knee pain. The nurse also is upset because the MS failed to throw away their exam gloves 3 times, leaving them on the supply cart in the room. You have just arrived to staff, and note that this student is already carrying six patients, most of which have been in the ED for over an hour.
