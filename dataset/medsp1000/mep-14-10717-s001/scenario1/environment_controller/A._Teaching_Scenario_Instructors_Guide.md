**Appendix A: Teaching Scenario Instructors Guide**

**Case 1**

Set-up: The student in this scenario has already matched in radiation oncology and is not putting his full effort into the rotation as a result. The choice of radiation oncology was intentional to minimize the overlap in practice between the ED and planned specialty. This also allows the simulated supervisor to identify interactions with consultants as a possible point of interest to the student. At the beginning of the scenario the student was late to the shift and is questioned by the simulated supervisor about why they are late and provide feedback.
