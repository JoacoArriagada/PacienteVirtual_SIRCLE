**Appendix A: Teaching Scenario Instructors Guide**

**Case 4**

Set-up: The student in this scenario picks up an older patient presenting with syncope who has a significant GI bleed. The student has taken a good amount of time before presenting the patient so the supervisor briefly looked at the patient and it is clear they have melena by the smell but they note that the patient is still fully clothed. While presenting the patient the resident is likely to ask about the findings on a rectal exam, which the student has not performed but in an effort to not look bad they reply that it was normal. The resident may challenge the student on that based on the fact that the patient remains in their clothes and if they do the student will admit to not performing the exam. If the resident doesn’t then the facilitator can intervene by interjecting and asking the resident when they are planning to do a rectal exam.

* A nurse may come to the resident asking if they are going to do a rectal exam.
* If the student mentions they did the rectal exam and the resident takes the student to the bedside then have the patient mention that the nurse warned them that you would be coming to do a rectal exam.
