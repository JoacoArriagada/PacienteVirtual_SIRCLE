**Appendix A: Teaching Scenario Instructors Guide**

**Case 4**

Set-up: The student in this scenario picks up an older patient presenting with syncope who has a significant GI bleed. The student has taken a good amount of time before presenting the patient so the supervisor briefly looked at the patient and it is clear they have melena by the smell but they note that the patient is still fully clothed. While presenting the patient the resident is likely to ask about the findings on a rectal exam, which the student has not performed but in an effort to not look bad they reply that it was normal. The resident may challenge the student on that based on the fact that the patient remains in their clothes and if they do the student will admit to not performing the exam.
