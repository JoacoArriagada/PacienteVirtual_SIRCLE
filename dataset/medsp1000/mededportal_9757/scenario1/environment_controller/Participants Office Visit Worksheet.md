# Scenario:

You are Mr. Scott’s primary care physician. He presents to you today for a follow-up visit post discharge. He has been hospitalized 5 times over the last 6 months. Wt today is 235lbs, BP 130/80, HR 90.

With your knowledge of his medical, social and functional history as indicated on the PPT slides, please perform the following tasks:

# A. Goals for the Post Discharge Visit

1. List the goals of a post discharge visit? Which of these goals would you like to accomplish together with Mr. Scott during his post discharge visit with you today?

2. Keeping in mind these goals, list activities that you would perform during this visit to help you gather information and develop a care plan to accomplish these goals.

# B. Discharge Summary Review

List the key elements of a discharge summary that the PCP or interprofessional team should look for at the post-discharge visit?

# C. Appropriate medication reconciliation and management

Reconcile his admission and discharge medications. Are there medications that you would like to start, discontinue or change the dose? Indicate a reason for the change you would like to make. In addition, reconcile the medications that Mr. Scott presents with to the office today. Are they the same as his discharge medication list?

# D. Support Services

1. In the light of Mr. Scott’s frequent re-hospitalization, identify his care needs that may be contributory to his readmissions and also the barriers to addressing these care needs.

a. Medical Needs

b. Social Needs

c. Functional Needs (ADLs and IADLs)

2. List any ancillary services that you think he would benefit from?

# E. Post Discharge Care Plan

What are the elements of a care plan that you would develop, either individually or with an interprofessional team, for Mr. Scott during his visit with you today?
