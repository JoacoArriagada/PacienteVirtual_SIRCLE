**Health Promotion and Disease Prevention: Health Behavior Counseling Case**

Case 3: T. is a 48 year old single mother of 3 school aged children. She has a history of heart disease and hyperlipidemia. Her primary care doctor and cardiologist have mentioned diet and exercise in addition to her medication regimen. T. mentions being too busy with work and virtually home schooling her children to be able to take care of her own health.
