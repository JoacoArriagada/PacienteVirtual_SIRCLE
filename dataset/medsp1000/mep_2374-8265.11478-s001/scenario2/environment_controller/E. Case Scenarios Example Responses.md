**Health Promotion and Disease Prevention: Health Behavior Counseling Case**

Case 2: J. is a 70-year-old male with a history of myocardial infarction. He insists on following the popular ketogenic diet that his daughter has convinced him to try. His cardiologist has referred him to a nutritionist who doesn’t recommend this diet in his case. J. is talkative and strongly opinionated. He has told his doctors that his goal is to get off all his medications in a year and to manage his health through diet only.

Doctor: Is it ok for us to discuss your diet?

Patient: I suppose so.

Doctor: How do you view your diet?

Patient: My daughter encouraged me to try the keto diet. I have never felt better! I have more energy and am already losing weight. It is a great diet!

Doctor: Your health is important to you!

Doctor: What do you view as some pros and cons about the keto diet?

Patient: Pros are that it will help me lose weight easily. I am bent on sticking with it. Cons are that my nutritionist doesn’t like the keto diet and neither does my wife.

Doctor: Tell me more…

Patient: They think it is not a heart-healthy diet. I think about that from time to time.

Doctor: You have lingering doubts about the effectiveness of this diet.

Patient: I guess so.

Doctor: What I am hearing is that: although you are enjoying what the keto diet is doing for you in the short term, you have concerns about your long term health.

Patient: I hate to admit it.

Doctor: How does following the keto diet fit in with your desire to be around to see your grandchild grow up?

Patient: It doesn’t.

Doctor: Would it be all right to share some information and options for heart healthy diets?

Patient: I think I might be willing to consider it.

Doctor: I wouldn’t recommend the keto diet as studies have shown that although it may be appropriate in some cases, it is detrimental to heart health. You might consider the Mediterranean diet or the DASH diet or increasing plant-based foods with small portions of proteins low in saturated foods. What are your thoughts about that?

Patient: Well, you have given me something to think about. I will talk it out with my wife.
