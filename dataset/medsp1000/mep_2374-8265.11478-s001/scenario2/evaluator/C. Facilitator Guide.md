**Objectives and methods**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | Methods | Measurement |
| Recognize the importance of lifestyle counseling in patient care | Presentation of data regarding counseling as a physician responsibility and an effective way to help patients | Pre and post survey item: “health behavior counseling is important to me”, likert scale |
| Apply and develop confidence in evidence-based brief counseling interventions to assist patients with health behavior change | 1. Faculty role play and group debriefing 2. Student role play and group debriefing 3. Flipped classroom model with pre-reading, so that classroom time is used for role play and group discussion. | 1. Pre and post survey item: “I am confident about counseling patients regarding health behaviors”, Likert scale 2. Post-evaluation item: “ I made gains in the following objective: apply evidence-based brief physician counseling interventions to assist patients with health behavior change in a clinical setting”, Likert scale 3. Scored role play transcripts using rubric |
| List and address obstacles to health promotion in a clinical setting | Group discussion, review of presented data | Post-evaluation item: “I made gains in the following objective: List and address obstacles to health promotion/physician counseling in a clinical setting”, Likert scale |

**Scoring Rubric for student role play transcript assignment**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Criteria** | **Does not meet expectations** | **Meets expectations** | **Exceeds expectations** |
| Answers all questions thoroughly | 0 points  Some questions left blank | 3 points  Basic descriptions | 4 points  Detailed descriptions |
| Applies counseling interventions appropriately | 0 points  Inappropriate use of counseling interventions | 3 points  Key points included but no examples given | 4 points  Further developed points with example statements |
| Uses empathetic and respectful language | 0 points  Disrespectful or unempathetic language used | 3 points  Some empathy and respect demonstrated. | 4 points  Empathy and respect fully demonstrated |
