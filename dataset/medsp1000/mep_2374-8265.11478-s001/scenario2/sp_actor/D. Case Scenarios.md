**Health Promotion and Disease Prevention: Health Behavior Counseling Case**

Case 2: J. is a 70 year old male with a history of myocardial infarction. He insists on following the popular ketogenic diet that his daughter has convinced him to try. His cardiologist has referred him to a nutritionist who doesn’t recommend this diet in his case. J. is talkative and strongly opinionated. He has told his doctors that his goal is to get off all his medications in a year and to manage his health through diet only.
