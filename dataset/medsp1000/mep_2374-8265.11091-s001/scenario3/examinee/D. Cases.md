**Appendix D. Health Literacy Cases (Facilitator Version)**

**3. Case of Mrs. Zimdahl**

**Scenario:** Mrs. Zimdahl called the clinic to make an appointment because her baby was having continuous diarrhea. The person working in the phone bank making the appointment told her to give the baby oral electrolyte solution. Three weeks later Mrs. Zimdahl took the baby to the emergency room because the baby would not stop crying and was still sick.

**Directions:** Role play this scenario where the provider explains to Mrs. Zimdahl how to treat her baby for diarrhea.
