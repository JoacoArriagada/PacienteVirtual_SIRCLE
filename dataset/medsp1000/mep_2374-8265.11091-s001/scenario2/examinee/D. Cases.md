**Appendix D. Health Literacy Cases (Facilitator Version)**

**2. Case of Mrs. Slovak**

**Scenario:** Mrs. Slovak received a prescription for a blood glucose meter to check her sugar for diabetes. When Mrs. Slovak got home, she called the clinic because she said the meter did not work. The staff ordered her a new meter. When the new meter came in, Mrs. Slovak went to the clinic and asked the staff to show her how to use it. She also took the first meter with her to return it.

**Directions:** Role play this scenario where the provider gives Mrs. Slovak instructions on how to use her blood glucose meter.
