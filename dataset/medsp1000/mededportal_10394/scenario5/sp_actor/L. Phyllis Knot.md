# Patient: Phyllis Knot

CC: Wheezing and difficulty breathing

HPI: Phyllis Knot is a previously healthy 9 month old full term female presenting with concern for wheezing and difficulty breathing. Parents say that she started having a runny nose and cough a couple of days ago. These have not gotten better and her cough is getting worse. She is coughing all night and not sleeping well. She is also having a hard time eating because of her cough and runny nose. Her parents say that she started “wheezing” today and she seems like she can’t breathe so they brought her to the emergency room. She is fussier than normal and not eating well. She has had 4 wet diapers in the last 24 hours. Sometimes she coughs so hard she throws up. Parents also noticed that she felt warm so they took her temperature which was 101F. They gave her Tylenol and her fever got better, though they think it might be coming back. She does not go to daycare and other people in her family have cold symptoms.

In the ED: when she first arrived to the ED, Phyllis was noted to have increased work of breathing with moderate subcostal and supraclavicular retractions and nasal flaring. She was also breathing fast with a respiratory rate in the 60s. Suctioning produced copious thick secretions. She continued to have respiratory rate in the 50s and mild retractions and wheezing, which did not improve with albuterol so the decision was made to admit to Hospital Medicine for observation and suctioning. Chest x-ray was read as viral illness versus reactive airways disease.
