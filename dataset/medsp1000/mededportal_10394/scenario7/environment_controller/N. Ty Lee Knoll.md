# Patient: Ty Lee Knoll

CC: Fever

HPI: Ty Lee Knoll is a 3 week old former 36 week gestation male presenting with a fever. Mom noticed that he felt warm earlier today and checked his temperature and it was 101F. They checked it rectally. He has been coughing and congested for the last 3 days but didn’t feel warm until today. He seemed a little fussier than normal and parents had a hard time calming him down, but he did console. He is still eating his normal amounts, breastfeeding every 3 hours. He spits up a lot normally. He has had his normal number of wet diapers per day and parents have not noticed a change in his stools. Parents remembered that their pediatrician told them to not give him Tylenol until after he is 2 months old, so they didn’t give him any medicine and brought him to the emergency room.

In the ED: Ty was noted to be febrile to 100.8F rectally and had a runny nose but otherwise looked well. Because of his age, the ED did a full evaluation for serious bacterial infection. His CBC showed WBC of 11 ( $6 0 \%$ segs, no bands) and normal H/H and platelets. UA did not show signs of a UTI. Blood and urine cultures were sent. He also had an LP that had clear fluid and only 2 WBC. There were 5 RBC, glucose 67, and protein 30. CSF was sent for culture. Chest x-ray was normal. No other labs were sent. He was given antibiotics in the ED and was admitted to the general pediatrics service for further management.
