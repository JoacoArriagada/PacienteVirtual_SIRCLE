# Interprofessional Education in a Pediatric Simulation: Case of an Infant with Fever

# Faculty Facilitators’ Guide

# Case Presentation:

The case presentation is best completed as a Standardized Patient (SP) exercise.

The SP plays the role of the parent, presenting to the emergency department (ED) with a 28 day-old female for complaints of fever, irritability, and poor feeding. As the overall simulation is designed to follow the patient-care aspects of this pediatric case from initial presentation through treatment, students are first asked to participate in the “triage” of the patient via interaction with the SP. Physical exam findings are provided by the faculty facilitators, as prompted by the students’ questions. Then the students can complete a detailed clinical history from the SP.
