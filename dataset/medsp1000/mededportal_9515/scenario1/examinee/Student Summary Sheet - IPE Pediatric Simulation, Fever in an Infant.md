**Interprofessional Education in a Pediatric Simulation: Case of an Infant with Fever**

**Students’ Guide**

**Overview:**

You will be participating in a simulated pediatric patient-care exercise as part of an interprofessional healthcare team. In the setting of a local emergency department, you will be asked to evaluate and treat an infant who is brought-in by her parent with a complaint of fever. Be prepared to participate fully in this exercise by engaging in many different roles, in order to provide care for this patient, as part of the team.
