VI. ACTORS: Roles can vary depending on the number of participants in the simulation session and on potential actor availability. This case can be implemented using a single participant and one facilitator/operator who can provide oral feedback via overhead audio and play additional historical or consultant roles via voice only. Realism can be enhanced by using physical actors, such as nursing staff members, but these are not required to successfully implement this case. Roles may be played by resident or faculty physicians, nurses, or medical students.

a. Primary physician: The main scenario participant will act as the primary physician and
perform the primary evaluation including obtaining a history, conducting a physical exam,
and ordering any necessary medications or other interventions. The primary physician can
perform any needed procedures, or can delegate these to other providers if multiple
participants are present.

b. Secondary physicians: Other participants in the scenario will serve as collaborators and
assistants for any necessary procedures and consultants.

c. Nursing staff: The role of the nurse will be to administer medications, verify orders, and perform other tasks as directed by the physicians. The nurse can also make observations as needed to stimulate case progression.

Initial and Prior ECG:

Previous ECG on request: Nonspecific T wave changes

Changes in patient condition: Patient will initially present without chest pain. As evaluation and testing begin (basic labs, ECG, and chest XR) patient will develop new chest pain.

If participants have recognized Wellens’ anterior T waves, chest pain may develop after they report recognition and as they are calling Cardiology.

If the patient is reassessed, the patient will report worsening chest pain.

If the patient is not reassessed, nursing may prompt participants by reporting that patient has new chest pain.

Repeat ECG:

If participant fails to order repeat ECG with development of chest pain the nurse will prompt the participant by asking, “Do you want a repeat ECG?”

Repeat ECG shows ST elevations in the anterior leads.

If participants do not act on the repeat ECG changes, prompting may include “What do you think the ECG shows, doctor?” and “Those look like ST Elevations. Do you want me to activate the cath lab?”

If stress testing is ordered, nurse may prompt participant by asking, “Are you sure you want a stress test with an ECG that looks like that?” If participant continues to order stress test, the simulation will end with report that patient went into ventricular fibrillation arrest.
