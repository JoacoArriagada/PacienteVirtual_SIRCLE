2. Setting: Outpatient VA clinic
3. Materials required:
   1. Fred Benson case: lab results sheet; MOCA score sheet; blood pressure cuff set to 156/94; New York Times magazine crossword puzzle from more than 6 months prior, very incompletely and inaccurately filled out

   1. Fred Benson case: one with SP, three with SIs

   2. Inter encounter activity: consultation sheet

8. Staffing requirement:
   1. SPs trained by SP staff trainer
   2. SIs trained by faculty trainer
   3. Day of OSCE: 1-2 staff people to check in SPs, SIs, and to shepherd learners from station to station
9. Briefing or orientation materials used: none, except for door instructions
