**Context for Standardized Interprofessionals:**

Fred Benson is an 80 yo with poorly controlled blood pressure, CAD, status post-NSTEMI (2002), alcohol abuse, & low back pain here for an evaluation of cognitive decline and possible depression.

The patient lives alone in an apartment. He does not cook, rather eats/drinks meals at Hafferty’s Tavern. He is able to ambulate but wants to be issued a scooter for improved mobility. His wife died 2-3 years ago and since that time his health has worsened, and he’s become socially isolated. No children or family members. His only social contact is the bartender at Hafferty’s (Henry, who is also a veteran).

The trainee will meet with you after he/she sees Fred. They will likely present the case and ask for your advice. We would like you to treat the presentation as if it were real, and give your advice and plan as you would for a true patient at the VA.

**STANDARDIZED INTERPROFESSIONAL: NURSING**

**Overview:**

The standardized interprofessional nurse for this case will be the one who saw him at his last visit and makes an offhanded comment about her perception of the patient’s inappropriate behavior during his last appointment, but then tries to take it back after saying it. At that last visit, Fred made what s/he felt were extremely inappropriate comments about some of the other nurses he had seen at the clinic: ‘Some of the nurses here are so beautiful, especially that brunette with the long hair. She’s a real looker…’

**Emotional Tone:**

The nurse feels frustrated because Mr. Benson not only makes what she thinks are inappropriate comments, but he also continues after she has tried to redirect him. In the last encounter she had with him, she felt like it was impossible to get him to listen to anything she was asking or telling him regarding his health. She therefore felt not only that the visit was wasted time for her, but that his continued commentary on the physical attributes of her colleagues was extremely disrespectful & made her feel extremely uncomfortable being in the room with him.

Despite this, others have apparently not had the same problem when working with him, so she will try to take back her statement after making it to the trainee.

The nurse’s goal is to give the trainee a ‘heads up’ about the patient’s past problematic behavior. Something like: “I’m ok with it in general, because you know, these guys are lonely and everything, but this guy creeps me out a little bit. And Martha’s just beside herself. I feel like I need to protect her.”

**Note:** As the standardized interprofessional and someone who is typically empathic and helpful, you may need to overemphasize some of the elements of negative emotional tone in order to convincingly portray the case.

**Key Line:**

‘So you're seeing Mr. Benson! You know, every time he’s in here, all he does is talk about how pretty all the nurses are. Martha’s his favorite, he just goes *on* about her. Did you notice that?’

**Further potential lines:**

If you feel scolded by the trainee, say, "I was just trying to give you a heads up."
