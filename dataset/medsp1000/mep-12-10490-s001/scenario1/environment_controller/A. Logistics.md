**APPENDIX A: Logistics**

1. Location: Simulation center
2. Setting: Outpatient VA clinic
3. Materials required:
   1. Tony Beckett case: accessory lamp, monofilament to test sensation
4. Number of encounters completed by each learner:
   1. Tony Beckett case: one with SP, four with SIs
5. Length of each encounter: 25 min with SP, 5 min with SIs
   1. No post encounter activity
   2. Inter encounter activity: consultation sheet
6. Total number of encounters expected in a session
   1. For SPs: 4
   2. For SIs: 8 (pharmacy SI) to 16 (all other SIs)
7. Start and finish time: for afternoon sessions, we start at 12:45 and end by 5 pm
8. Staffing requirement:
   1. SPs trained by SP staff trainer
   2. SIs trained by faculty trainer
   3. Day of OSCE: 1-2 staff people to check in SPs, SIs, and to shepherd learners from station to station
9. Briefing or orientation materials used: none, except for door instructions
