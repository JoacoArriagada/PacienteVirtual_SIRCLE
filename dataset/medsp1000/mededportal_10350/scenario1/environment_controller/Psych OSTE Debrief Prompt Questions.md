**Psychiatry OSTE Post-Discussion Debrief Questions**

**Small Group Debrief**

* Content debrief to interviewer
  + How did he/she feel to conduct a MSE with a psychiatric patient?
  + What went well?
  + What would you want to do differently?
* Content debrief to observers
  + Was the MSE complete?
    - Anything you would like to ask the patient to collect additional data?
* Process debrief to interviewer:
  + How did it feel to interview a psych patient?
* Process debrief to observers:
  + What did you see in the interview that made the patient more comfortable?
* What did you observe that was uncomfortable for the interviewer and/or patient?

**Large Group Debrief**

* What did you observe the faculty preceptor do that promoted learning for the student interviewing? Student reporting MSE?
* Did the preceptor involve the observing student(s)? If yes give an example of how?
* What else can be done to involve students in their learning post observing an interview?
