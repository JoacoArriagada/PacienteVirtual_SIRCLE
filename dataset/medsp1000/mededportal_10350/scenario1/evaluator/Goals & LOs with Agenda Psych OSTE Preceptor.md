**Psychiatry Objective Structured Teaching Exercise (OSTE)**

Goals: Upon completion of the OSTE session participants will be able to:

1. Conduct and debrief 3 student-psych patient interviews in one session.
2. Create a learning environment that engages both the student interviewing as well as the students observing the psychiatric interview.
3. Facilitate students learning the diagnostic interview with a focus on the History of Present Illness (HPI) and the Mental Status Exam (MSE).

**Learning Objectives:** Upon completion of the OSTE session, preceptors will be able to:

1. Observe a student conduct a psychiatric interview with a standardized patient
2. Observe a preceptor debrief (content and process) of the psychiatric interview with a group of students
3. Identify small group facilitation strategies to create a learning environment that is inclusive of all students
