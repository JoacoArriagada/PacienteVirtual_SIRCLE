**Virtual Hospital Field Trip**

**SIMS - Diabetic Nephropathy** 

**Doorway Information/Brief Introduction to Patient:** David Jackson is a 43 year-old African-American male that presents with a chief complaint of not feeling well and being unable to urinate much for the last week. He is 6’0  210lbs

<u>**Sim Patients would need stats of Pulse of 90, Resp of 16 BP of 138/85**</u>

In our case the patient would have fluid overload due to low urine output.

<u>**Clinical Signs & Symptoms:**</u>

    - Little or no urine when you urinate.
    - Swelling, especially in your legs and feet.
    - Nausea and vomiting.
    - Flank Pain; pain in the back just below the rib cage~~.~~
    - Fatigue, Lethargy
    - Foamy appearance or excessive frothing of the urine
    - General ill feeling
    - Generalized itching
    - Headache
    - Poor appetite; Not feeling like eating.
    - Unintentional weight gain (from fluid buildup)

Swelling in the legs and feet known as edema.
