**SIMS - Diabetic Ketoacidosis**

**Background:** Mike Doser is a 24yo Type 1 diabetic who presents with stomach pain, nausea and a fruity smelling breath. He is 5’9 175. Black male

Diabetic ketoacidosis is often accompanied by abdominal pain. Nausea and vomiting are often prominent. Abdominal pain may be severe. Fast respirations and a fruity odor on the patient's breath are classic signs of the disorder. Lethargy and central nervous system depression may evolve into coma with severe DKA.
