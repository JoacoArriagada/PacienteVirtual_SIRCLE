**“Brown Bag” medication reconciliation simulation: Activity and Learner Instructions**

**Group Work**: “Brown Bag” patient case example

Patient AB is a 77-year-old male who comes in for medication review and reconciliation. He brings with him a bag that contains all of his medications and his pillbox that he currently uses. He fills his pillbox mostly on his own. He fills his pillbox on Thursdays. Today is **Monday**.

**Instructions:** Your job is to reconcile the patient’s medication list below, his pillbox, and the medicines that he brought in his “Brown Bag” to see if everything matches up and to identify errors in his medication management.

His most recent med list includes the following:

**Oral medications:**

1. Aspirin 81 mg; take 1 tablet once daily
2. Furosemide 20 mg; take 1 tablet twice daily
3. Lisinopril 20 mg; take 1 tablet once daily
4. Metoprolol tartrate 25 mg; take 1 tablet twice daily
5. Glipizide 5 mg; take 1 tablet three times daily with meals
6. Potassium 40 mEq XR; take 1 tablet once daily

**Inhalers:**

1. Albuterol 90 mcg; inhale 1 puff by mouth every 4-6 hours as needed for breathing
2. Budesonide 160mcg/formoterol 4.5mcg; inhale 2 puffs by mouth twice daily
3. Tiotropium 1.25mcg/1 actuation; inhale 2 puffs by mouth once daily
