**Recommended Standardized Patient Case Outline**

PART B

**Identifying case factors**

**Presenting complaint:** Patient Nancy Roberts, 92 y.o. female patient with moderate dementia and mild hypertension, arrived at the UC Davis ED from a local assisted living residence at 2000. Before admission, she became increasing SOB, with yellow-greenish sputum, and febrile to 38.5 with pulse oximetry = 88. The assisted living residence staff were concerned Mrs. Roberts had developed pneumonia and, after receiving permission from the patient's son/daughter, called an ambulance for transport to the ED.

Length of Patient Encounter: 10 minutes

**Learning objectives/Case Purpose**:

1. After completing this session, each participant will demonstrate understanding of effective critical incident communication by disclosing a medical error
2. After completing this session, each participant will identify two strategies for restoring trust after a medical error
3. During this session, each participant will demonstrate empathy and respect when communicating a clinical error
4. During this session, each participant will elicit family member concerns and allow them to express emotions related to a medical error

**Diagnosis**: anaphylactic reaction to a cephalosporin

**Type of case**: teaching, communication
