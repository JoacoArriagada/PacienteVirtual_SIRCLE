**Effective Interprofessional Communication Rating Scale (I-CIRT Scale)**

*Please score each of the component items on a scale of* ***1 – 3: 1 (not performed); 2 (attempted but incomplete or not always effective), 3 (performed completely and effectively)****. Raters score OVERALL item based on their general impression using a 1 to 5 score, 5 being highest*

**Prepare for communication**

\_\_ Evidence that “huddle” took place with care team

**General communication skills**

\_\_ Uses language at a level understood by patient/family

\_\_ Responds to patient/family member needs

\_\_ Checked on patient/family understanding of the information clinician provided

\_\_ Shows respect

\_\_ Tailors conversation to what patient/family understands

\_\_ **Overall impression of general communication skills**

**Assessing the Patient/Family members perception**

\_\_ Asks patient/family member if he/she may talk to them about what has happened

\_\_ Uses open-ended questions to determine how the patient/family member perceive the situation

\_\_ Warns that he/she is about to give “bad” news

\_\_ Explores patient/family expectations

\_\_ **Overall impression of assessing perceptions early in conversation**

**Explanation of facts**

\_\_ Provides honest explanation of events as known

\_\_ Clearly states how event affects patient and the specific actions taken to care for the patient

\_\_ Express contributing factors as best understood currently

\_\_ Appropriately takes responsibility for event

\_\_ Avoids speculation

\_\_ Checks patient/family member understanding of event

\_\_ Clarifies explanation based on patient/family questions/responses

\_\_ **Overall impression of honest explanation of facts**

**Address patient/family emotions with empathetic responses**

**\_\_** Expresses clear and honest communication of regret that the event occurred

**\_\_** Assures patient/family incident is taken seriously

**\_\_** Tells patient/family member who will provide ongoing care and who will provide follow up

**\_\_** Offers support

**\_\_** Allows for silence

**\_\_** Closes with sincere expression of support, empathy and concern

**\_\_ Overall impression of empathy**
