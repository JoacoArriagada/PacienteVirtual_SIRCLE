Standardized Parents:

The potential for child abuse can elicit visceral reactions both in medical personnel caring for such patients and in those acting in simulation case scenarios. The overall theme conveyed during this case should be that we, as physicians, are not judge, jury, and executioner. Especially in the emergency department, we act on incomplete data. The investigation into child abuse is just that: an investigation, not an accusation. It is encouraged that the instructors review relevant state laws prior to using this case. For the advanced learner, we have incorporated a hostile family member taking the child out of the ED. Standardized parents can be presented in many ways. We have often found significant learning opportunities in presenting the same case on the same day with only minor variations. For example, you can present the same mother who doesn’t know much of the HPI, the same concerning boyfriend, and have an undiagnosed coagulopathy with no abuse noted.
