**Ideal Scenario Flow**

*As the students enter the room, they find the patient coughing intermittently, but in no apparent distress. If the students ask the nurse to connect the patient to the heart monitor and obtain a full set of vital signs, the preceptor in the simulation control room would respond as the nurse. Upon identification of hypoxemia along with the complaint of being short of breath, supplemental oxygen by 2 L NC raises the oxygen saturation from 91% on room air to 97%. If the patient is placed on a high flow oxygen face mask, that is also acceptable; after a few minutes the patient can report that they are breathing easier and ask whether the students can try oxygen through the nose because they do not like the mask on their face. A physical examination reveals minimal accessory muscle use and rales on the right base. If a chest radiograph is ordered, it demonstrates a right lower lobe infiltrate. If laboratory studies are obtained: CBC – WBC 14.5, 76% segs, 5% bands, 18% lymphocytes, and a BMP – normal. ABG results: not performed/not available.*
