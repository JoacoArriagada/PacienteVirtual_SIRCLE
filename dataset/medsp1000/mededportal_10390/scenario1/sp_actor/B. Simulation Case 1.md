**Ideal Scenario Flow**

*As the students enter the room, they find the patient coughing intermittently, but in no apparent distress. If the patient is placed on a high flow oxygen face mask, that is also acceptable; after a few minutes the patient can report that they are breathing easier and ask whether the students can try oxygen through the nose because they do not like the mask on their face.*
