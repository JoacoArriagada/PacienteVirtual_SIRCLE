PAGE: Please call RN re: patient Smith; PM vitals with BP 82/38, complaining of back pain. (###-###- ####)

# Notes for nursing:

Other vitals at the time: BP 82/38, HR 110, RR 18, sat $94 \%$ on RA, T 98.4F   
Patient has been complaining of worsening back pain for the past 2 hours, given acetaminophen without resolution; cardiac cath was about 6 hours ago.   
Patient had a repeat hemoglobin done, you aren’t sure why, about an hour ago which was Hb 7.4 from pre-cath when it was 10.0.   
Fresh set of vitals if asked for: 84/40, P114, RR 18, sat $9 5 \%$ on RA, still afebrile

# Desired learner actions:

Request fresh set of vitals   
Ask about anticoagulation—request to have the heparin gtt stopped. Inquire about character of back pain – onset, severity   
Ask about most recent hemoglobin; if asked:

o Ensure adequate access

# Teaching points:

In post procedure patients who become unstable, consider bleeding as a potential etiology - in patients $\mathsf { s / p }$ cardiac catheterization, one of your concerns should be retroperitoneal bleeding. Verbal orders appropriate to given the nurse in this case include those for a repeat hemoglobin; type and screen, additional peripheral access and to notify the cardiologist of record.
