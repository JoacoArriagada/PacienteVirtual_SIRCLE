# 9. Nurse wants clarification of the plan from cross cover intern: 1 student (5 minutes)

D6/558: DNR/DNI. Mr. Rick Jackson 80-year-old man with Alzheimer’s dementia admitted following fall.   
Developed HCAP, briefly requiring NIPPV. Stable now, awaiting placement.

PAGE: Please call RN regarding plan for patient Jackson. (###-###-####)

# Notes for nurse:

Be persistent regarding your desire to know the plan despite this being a night float intern Patient is stable, you just were reviewing the chart and interested in clarification of the longterm plan   
Discuss his pain regimen, he has chronic (old) mild pain from arthritis, how you feel he needs more opioid and ask for Dilaudid for patient   
Patient has been delirious, on top of his dementia; has not had a BMP in five days and has been too nauseated to eat well.

# Desired learner actions:

Be clear about your role in the medical team – you are cross cover and are not intimately aware of the details; don’t speculate   
When asked regarding his pain regimen, assess for any contraindications to increasing opioid therapy (mental status, GI side effects)   
If there is concern for increase in pain acutely; intern must notify nurse they will assess patient – should not increase pain regimen without doing so

# Teaching points:

Some people are going to expect you to know all the details despite only knowing a patient peripherally; it is important to think of a tactful way to defer this to the morning.   
While pain control is very important, must balance adverse effects & assessment of etiology, chronicity of pain   
Any call for increased pain should be followed up with a clinical assessment, particularly if you are concerned enough to increase medications.
