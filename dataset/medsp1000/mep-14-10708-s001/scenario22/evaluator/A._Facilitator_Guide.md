7. Dyspnea (secondary to congestive heart failure) (1 students) 10 minutes

F4/534: FULL. Mr. James John 72 year old man with history of CAD and heart failure with reduced ejection fraction admitted today for community acquired pneumonia. On ceftri/azithro.

PAGE: Please call RN re: patient John, complaining of shortness of breath. (###-###-####)

# Notes for nurse:

Vitals: T 99.9F, P 80, BP 146/88, RR 30, sat $84 \%$ on RA – if recheck they are about the same Dyspnea started within the last four hours – since last assessment   
Got 3 L fluid bolus in the ED as he was tachycardic, also on NS 125 m/hr for the past six hours – has $2 0 \mathsf { g }$ PIV   
Does not complain of chest pain   
Weight at the time of admission was his dry weight – 210 lbs.   
If student doesn’t volunteer – ask what he/she is thinking about for etiology.

# Desired learner actions:

Ask for a fresh set of vitals

Ask about time course of dyspnea   
Inquire about IV fluid resuscitation   
Agree to assess the patient urgently   
Verbal order for stat CXR $+ / .$ - ECG; supplemental oxygen   
Clear, closed-loop communication when relaying orders to nursing staff   
Tell nurse you believe this could be due to heart failure

# Teaching points:

Patients with initial sepsis should be fluid resuscitated if they require it, regardless of their   
cardiac status though consider smaller boluses of fluid in patients with heart failure; $5 0 0 ~ \mathsf { m } \mathsf { I }$ at a   
time   
Obtain a stat CXR for sudden change in respiratory status; ECG, ABG, troponin are often   
indicated, as well   
For acute dyspnea secondary to pulmonary edema, loop diuretics (furosemide) should be   
administered IV
