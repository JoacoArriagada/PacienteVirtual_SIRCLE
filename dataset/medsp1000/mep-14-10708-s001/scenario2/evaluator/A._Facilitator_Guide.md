# Desired learner actions:

Ask for fresh set of vitals   
Verbal orders for stat ECG, CXR and ABG; advise nurse to place patient on supplemental oxygen   
$( + / -$ coags, CBC, BMP as thinking about treatment) using clear, closed-loop communication   
Agree to come evaluate the patient urgently   
IV access

# Teaching points:

Consider the most life threatening causes of hypoxia when a patient’s status changes abruptly. It is appropriate to provide immediate verbal orders for the nurses to execute while you are en route to evaluate the patient – fresh set of vitals, chest x-ray, ECG, supplemental oxygen, ABG Pulmonary embolism can occur when patients have been immobilized for a long period of time; which is why we often give pharmacologic DVT prophylaxis for patients in the hospital.
