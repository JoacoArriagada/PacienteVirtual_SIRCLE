# Teaching points:

Hyponatremia must not be corrected too quickly to avoid risk of osmotic demyelination syndrome.   
Risk factors for osmotic demyelination syndrome include low presentation serum sodium $( < 1 2 0 ,$ but greatest risk if $\mathtt { < } 1 0 5$ ), longer duration of hyponatremia and too rapid rate of correction. Main goal is you need to relower the Na for the goal of no more than 10mEq increase in 24h: could do with D5W and desmopressin.   
Need to frequently recheck Na in severe hyponatremia, especially with rapid correction Clear, closed-loop communication must be used when relaying orders to nursing staff
