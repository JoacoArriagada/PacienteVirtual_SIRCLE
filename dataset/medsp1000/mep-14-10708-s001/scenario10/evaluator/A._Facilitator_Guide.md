# Teaching points:

Patients with ESRD on HD very frequently have potassium at the upper limits of normal to mildly elevated, particularly if they are coming due for HD (as he is this AM)   
The most serious manifestations of hyperkalemia, ( ie cardiac rhythm disturbances), often don’t manifest until potassium is greater than 7.0 in patients with chronic hyperkalemia – of course, this threshold is lower in those with an acute rise.   
COULD give kayexelate if concerned, but not without risk: cases of intestinal necrosis.
