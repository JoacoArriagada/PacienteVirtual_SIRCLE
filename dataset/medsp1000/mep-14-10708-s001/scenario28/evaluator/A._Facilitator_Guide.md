# Teaching points:

Recognize the risk of neutropenia in patient recently treated with chemotherapy.   
Neutropenic fever is defined as one temp 38.4 or higher or 38.0 for greater than one hour. Blood cultures and other directed testing should be performed and appropriate antibiotics should be administered promptly.   
Prompt IVF for hypotension
