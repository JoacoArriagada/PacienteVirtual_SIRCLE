# Teaching points:

There are lots of options for constipation, both from above and below; recognize that patients with a large stool burden often need enema or suppository in order to clear stool burden Phosphate & Mg containing products should be avoided in patients with CKD   
While in this case the nausea will likely be relieved by relieving the constipation, reasonable to treat it symptomatically until resolution of the constipation   
Phenergan causes sedation, and risk of thrombophlebitis, tissue necrosis if extravasation – not great for repeated PIV dosing, especially in elderly patients.
