# 2. Family wants to talk to the doctor (defining your role as a cross cover intern, managing family

expectations); 1 student; 10 minutes

Sara K. Johnson, MD

D4/442: DNR/DNI. Mr. Ryan Black is a 68 year old man with ESLD admitted with hepatic encephalopathy, found to have community acquired pneumonia. Transferred to the ICU as he needs BiPAP.

PAGE: Family of patient Black requesting update from MD. Call RN at ###-###-#### if questions.

# Notes for nursing:

Family visiting for the first time in 4 day hospitalization; surprised to see he has been transferred to the ICU. Family is requesting an update from an MD taking care of the patient. His clinical status has been stable – still requiring BiPAP to maintain saturation in 90s.

# Desired learner actions:

Define your role as cross cover to the nursing staff.   
Attempt to arrange alternate time for family meeting – ideally, during working hours for the day time.   
Agree to speak with the family briefly

# Teaching points:

Don’t speculate on patient’s status; be clear about your role as a cross cover intern. Attempt to arrange alternative time for family to have their questions asked by the primary team; their concerns are important.   
Remain professional
