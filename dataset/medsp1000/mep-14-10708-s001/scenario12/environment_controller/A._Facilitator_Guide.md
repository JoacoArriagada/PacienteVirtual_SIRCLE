# 11a. Hyperkalemia case: 2 students needed for this case (one to answer page, one to call a consult); (12 minutes)

D4/417: FULL. Mr. Bob Jones is a 66-year-old man with chronic back pain and CKD stage IV admitted for acute kidney injury; checking potassium overnight, admission $K = 5 . 5$ .

PAGE: Please call RN – Patient Jones repeat potassium is 6.9. (###-###-####)

# Notes for nurse:

Patient has not received intervention yet for his hyperkalemia   
When asked to resend specimen, notify provider you have already done this   
Patient is stable, not complaining of any cardiopulmonary symptoms   
Patient is not on telemetry; provide some resistance to request for “stat” ECG – (“ECG techs are   
no longer in house and I have three other patients, could this wait until after my break?”)   
If asked, other labs were Na 132 CO2 14 BUN 85 Cr 3.95 with this last lab check

# Nephrology Consultant: (played by NP or if no NP, then the facilitator can play consultant role)

o Goal to guide students to have a clear question. If you are not getting that in the first 30 seconds, then ask “So what’s your question?”   
o Goal for students to give you a succinct overview of the medical situation. If they are rambling on with medical details try to guide them to be more succinct: “just tell me the one liner”   
o If it is an urgent need for a consult (like in this case), but the student is not clear about that in the call, you could prompt them with saying “ok we will see him tomorrow” and see if they will ask for more urgent timeline.
