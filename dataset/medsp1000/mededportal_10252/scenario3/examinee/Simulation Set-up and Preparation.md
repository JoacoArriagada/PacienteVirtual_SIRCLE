# Stimulation 3:

1. Title: “Tongue Thruster”

4. Environment

1. Set Up: Office or clinic room   
2. Props: 3D dental mold   
3. Distractors: none   
5. Role: 10-year old girl

Scenario Background Given Freely to Participants

I am 10 years old and my dentist says that I need braces. My mother took me to the orthodontist and he said that he would not put braces on me until my tongue acts more normal or goes back or something. He said if he puts braces on and I don’t fix my tongue then my teeth may move again and I would need braces again!
