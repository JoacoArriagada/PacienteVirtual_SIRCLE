| **Brief narrative description of case** | A 23-year-old patient is brought to the Emergency Department after vomiting in the dorm room. The patient ingested an uncertain number of aspirin tablets three hours prior to arrival and subsequently presents with nausea, vomiting, tinnitus, tachypnea, hyperthermia, diaphoresis, tachycardia, and agitation.
