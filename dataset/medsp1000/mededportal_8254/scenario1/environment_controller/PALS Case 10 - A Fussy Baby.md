1. Title: Pediatric Advanced Life Support Case 10 – A fussy baby

Topic: Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT)

Last Modified: 1.15.12

12. Case Narrative
13. Scenario Background Given to Participants
14. Chief complaint, triage note, medic report—A 1 month old infant arrives in ED. Mother tells you that the baby was well until 2 days ago when she began to have periods of inconsolable fussiness and poor feeding. At times the infant’s color has been somewhat dusky during these episodes. The infant is now dusky and very fussy.
15. Past medical history—Term infant, uncomplicated pregnancy, labor, and delivery. No history of fever. The infant’s diet consists of both breast milk and commercial infant formula. Mother is on no medications.
16. Past surgical history--None
17. Meds and allergies—None
18. Family/social history—Noncontributory
19. Physical Exam:

Well developed, well nourished infant who is fussy and pale.

Wt: 4 kg T: 36.5C HR: 228 BP: 66/44 RR: 55/undistressed SpO2: 89%

Airway: Unobstructed

Lungs: Clear breath sounds

CV: Regular rapid rate; a strong pulse is present.

1. Labs:

Hematocrit: 38%

White blood cell count: 12,000

Electrolytes, BUN, Creatinine: all normal. Glucose 122
