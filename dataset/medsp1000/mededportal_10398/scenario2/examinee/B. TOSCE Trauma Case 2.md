# TOSCE CASE #2

**Simulated patient name**: Mrs. Jones

**Setting**: Quiet room for family meetings in an ED

**BACKGROUND INFORMATION FOR RESIDENTS**

You are the trauma resident on call at University Hospital. You are asked to evaluate an 88 year-old female who was brought in after a fall. The patient, Mrs. Jones was found by her daughter at the bottom of the stairs, minimally responsive. The patient has atrial fibrillation and is on Coumadin (Warfarin). She required intubation in the field after becoming obtunded.

The nurse informs you that that family is in the waiting room and would like to an update on the patient’s condition.

**TASK**:

1. Disclose information about the patient’s condition
2. Respond to questions and concerns that the family may have.
