**Orientation to the Breastfeeding & Lactation Medicine OSCE**

Welcome to the Breastfeeding & Lactation Medicine OSCE.

You will get information about your patient and your tasks for this patient in the form of a Door Card when the session formally begins.

In this encounter – YOU are the provider, not ‘just a student’ on the team. Thus, you should counsel the patient, tell them the plan, explain findings or diagnoses as such.

Forbidden physical exam maneuvers include corneal or gag reflex, breast, rectal, GU, pelvic exams or anything that would require you to remove a patient’s mask.
