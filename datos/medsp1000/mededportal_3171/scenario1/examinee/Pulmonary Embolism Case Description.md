# VI. Case Narrative

A. Background for students – given freely a few minutes before allowing them in the exam room.

# Dan Sparks

Identification: Dan Sparks is a 43 year old white male   
Chief Complaint: "I can't seem to catch my breath" for the last 4 hours   
History of Present Illness: Mr. Sparks was in his normal state of health until 4 hours ago when he began noticing shortness of breath. The dyspnea was worse with exertion and with deep breathing. The dyspnea became severe enough that he called 911 and paramedics have now transported him to the ER with the above history. Paramedics have placed a heplock IV and gave him a breathing treatment with nebulized albuterol en route.

Past Medical History: Appendectomy at age 13

Allergies: NKDA

Medications: None

B. Scenario conditions initially

2. Patients initial exam –

initial vital signs should be sinus tachycardia in the 110’s to low 120’s. BP should be low 90’s systolic, . Pulse oximetry should be in mid 80’s on room air. Respiratory rate should be about 30 per minute. Temperature – normal. No other abnormalities are necessary. Alarms should be turned OFF.
